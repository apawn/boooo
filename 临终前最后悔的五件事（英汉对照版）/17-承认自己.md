# Regret 5: I wish I had let myself be happier · 承认自己

Being an executive in a global corporation, Rosemary was a woman ahead of her time. She had moved up the ranks long before women were ever seen in these types of roles. Prior to this though, she had lived according to society’s expectations in those days, and had married young. Unfortunately, with her marriage came physical and mental abuse. When she was left almost dying after one particular beating, it was time to escape for good.

> 作为一名全球业务公司的总经理，罗斯玛丽在她那个年代里是妇女中的顶尖人物。在社会接纳妇女担当这类职务的很久之前，她就已经升迁到了这个位置。在此之前，她都是按照社会对她的期望而活着，年纪轻轻就结婚了。不幸的是，她在婚姻中遭受了身体和精神上的虐待。当她在一次被丈夫凶狠残暴的对待差一点丢掉性命的时候，她决定是时候该永远逃离这种生活了。

Despite it being a very valid reason to leave a marriage, divorce was still a scandal in those days. So to uphold the family reputation in a town where their name was well known, Rosemary had moved to the city and started afresh. Life had hardened her heart and her way of thinking. Self-validation and family approval were now found through her success in a male-dominated world. Consideration of another relationship never crossed her mind again.

> 在那个年代，尽管有非常合理的理由了断一段婚姻，但离婚依然是一种丑闻。在他们当时居住的那个城市里，她的家庭非常有名望，因此，了维护家族名誉，罗斯玛丽没有离婚，但离开了这座城市，并开始了自己崭新的生活。生活的磨练造就她有了一副铁石心肠和顽固的自我思维方式。由于在男人主导的世界领域里取得了成功，如今，她那自我合理化的认同和家庭的赞许已经显示出来。在她的脑海里从未想过要去考虑所谓的另外一种人际关系。

Instead, Rosemary moved up the ranks with fierce determination, a high IQ, and a lot of hard work, until she was the first woman in her state to hold the high level of management she did. Used to telling people what to do, Rosemary enjoyed the power her intimidating manner gave her. This behaviour then carried over to her treatment of the carers. She was going through one after another, never happy with any of them, until I arrived.

> 相反，罗斯玛丽靠着强硬的决心、高智商和加倍的努力工作把自己提升到公司高层，直到后来，在她工作的那个州，她成为了第一个获得高级管理层职位的女性。罗斯玛丽习惯指挥人们去做事，享受着由她的威严和强硬作风给予她的那种威力。然后，她把这种行为习惯带到了对待她的照顾者身上。她换了一个又一个的看护员，从来没有哪一个让她感到满意过，直到我的到来。

She liked me because I had a banking background, which in her eyes exempted me from being a fool. This way of thinking was certainly not one I resonated with but I had nothing to prove anymore either way, so I figured she could judge me in whatever way made her happy. After all, she was in her eighties and dying. Rosemary then insisted on keeping me as her primary carer.

> 她喜欢我的原因是因为我有银行工作的背景，在她的眼里，我的这个背景使我免遭被视为一个傻瓜。这个想法当然不是我跟谁产生共鸣而得来的，不管怎么样，我也拿不出证据证明它，所以，我想她是可能以让她感到愉快的方式来评判我吧。归根结底她现在已经是八十多岁又临近死亡的人，我不用去在意她的想法。随后，罗斯玛丽坚持要我留下来作为她的主管看护员。

The mornings were particularly bad with bossy moods and nastiness. Having a strong sense of my own self by now, I tolerated it to a point but knew there would be a limit. When her mood turned especially nasty and personal one day, I gave Rosemary the ultimatum. Be kinder or I was out of there. To this she screamed for me to go, to get out of her house, saying even nastier things than before, while sitting on the side of her bed.

> 早上尤其地糟糕，罗斯玛丽专横的情绪和脏话连天让人受不了。由于现在我已经有强烈的自我生活感，所以，我可以忍受她到一定的程度，而我明白我的忍受会有一个极限。某一天，当她的情绪转向非常的恶劣和进行人身攻击的时候，我给罗斯玛丽下了最后通牒，要么她变得友善，要么我就离开这里。对此，她坐在她的床边对我大吼大叫，要我离开，指令我滚出她的家，还骂出比以前更难听的脏话。

While she was screaming at me, I just went and sat next to her. “Go then. Get out,” she kept yelling, pointing to the door. I just sat there looking at her, sending her loving kindness, waiting for her outburst to subside. Silence followed. We both sat there for another minute or so, saying nothing, but sitting close enough to be leaning on each other. “Finished?” I asked, smiling gently.

> 在她对我吼叫的时候，我只是后退几步，还是坐在她的旁边。“滚！滚出去！”她手指向门口一直叫喊着。而我只是坐在那里望着她，并向她投以仁慈和友好的目光，等待她的异常发作消退。沉默接着出现了。我们两个都坐在那里，一声不吭。过了一会儿，我还是走过去接近她，差不多到了相互挨着对方的距离。我温柔地对她微笑着问道：“完了吗？”

“For now,” she huffed. I nodded, saying nothing. Silence continued. Finally, I just put my arm around her, kissed her on the cheek and went out the kitchen, returning a few minutes later with a pot of tea. Rosemary was still sitting in the same position, looking like a lost little girl.

> “暂时完了。”她怒气冲冲地说。我点点头，没有说话。又沉默了一会儿。最后，我只是拥抱了她，在她的脸颊上亲吻了一下，离开去了厨房。几分钟后，我端了一杯茶回到房间。罗斯玛丽依然原封不动地坐在那里，看上去像一个迷失的小女孩。

Helping her up off the bed, we moved across to the settee in her room. The tea was waiting on the table beside it. Rosemary sat, looking up at me smiling as I placed a lovely rug over her legs, then I sat myself down too. “I’m so scared and lonely. Please don’t leave me,” she said. “I feel safe with you.”

> 我帮助她下了床，我们向房间另一边的沙发走过去。我已经先把茶杯放在旁边的桌子上，罗斯玛丽坐了下来。当我将一张可爱的毯子盖在她双腿上的时候，她抬头看着我，微笑了。这时我也在她旁边坐下来。“我感到害怕和孤独，请不要离开我。”她伤感地说。“和你在一起我觉得有安全感。”

“I’m not going anywhere. It’s OK. As long as you treat me respectfully, I’m here for you,” I told her sincerely. Rosemary smiled like a little girl in need of love. “Stay then please. I want you to stay.” Nodding, I kissed her on the cheek again, which made her smile hugely.

> 我很诚挚地告诉她：“我不会离开。不必担心，只要你很尊重地对待我，我就为你留在这里。”罗斯玛丽笑了，如同一个需要爱的小女孩那样微笑。“请留下吧，我希望你留下。”我点点头，再次亲吻了她的脸颊，这让她笑得更加灿烂。

From this point, things turned better between us immediately. She spoke of her past, which helped me to understand her more, and of how she had always pushed people away. Having known that pattern in myself for a long time, and the benefits of breaking free of it, I explained how it wasn’t too late to let people in. Rosemary said she didn’t know how but she wanted to try and be nicer.

> 从此，我们之间的关系变得好一些。她对我讲述了她的过去，帮助我更加了解她，她也讲述了她怎样总是把人们从她身边推开，让别人有距离感。我明白那种状况，因我自己很长一段时间也是这样，我也明白摆脱这种状况的好处，所以，我向她解释了现在让人们进入她的生活还来得及。罗斯玛丽表示说她想去尝试，也希望做一个友善的人，可不知道怎样去做。

Her illness was slow to take hold but there were definitely signs of it spreading daily, particularly with her increasing weakness. It was a slow change at first and while I could see it, Rosemary was still in occasional denial. Making plans for me to do her books, and get all of her investment portfolios in order, she would talk in detail about this and that.

> 她的疾病控制得很慢，很明显，她的病情一天天在加重，她越来越虚弱。开始的时候，这种改变很慢，后来我能辨认出病情的确在发展，罗斯玛丽偶尔也否认她的病情。她计划着要我帮她整理她的书籍和文档，并要求把她的所有投资组合文件按序编排，她会很仔细地告诉我该做这些或者那些。

I just listened, knowing it would never happen. Rosemary explained how she was going to spend a few hours with me to get started on it all, when she had the energy. I had seen this before, where people continued to make plans towards their future, while in the meantime their strength was disappearing more each day.

> 我只是听着，心里明白这都永远不可能做到。罗斯玛丽解释说，等她精神好些的时候，她将如何跟我一起花一些时间开始去完成这些事情。以前我也遇到过这种类型的人，他们为他们的将来继续制订种种计划，而与此同时，他们的能量在一天天地逐渐消失，体力一天不如一天。

She also insisted on me making her appointments around town, ensuring I made the calls from her bedroom phone, where she could listen to every word I said and butt in constantly, controlling the whole conversation. I’d then have to reschedule them, not cancel them, one at a time. There was no denying Rosemary had a controlling personality. While I was happy to do certain unnecessary things for her, I refused outright at other times; like being unwilling to waste my time and energy looking for things we had already looked for in every single inch of the home.

> 她还坚持要我帮她在城里周围预约各式的会面，要求我在她的卧房里打电话，以便她能够听到我说的每个字，同时她还不时地插嘴打岔，想控制整个的谈话内容。到后来，我不得不一个一个地打电话重新安排时间。不可否认，罗斯玛丽有“控制人格”。我乐意为她做一些不必要的事，但我断然拒绝在其他的时候帮她做一些事，比如我不愿意浪费我的时间和精力帮她寻找我们已经在这个家的每个角落找遍了的某些东西。

Each day saw her emotional walls breaking down more and our closeness increasing. Rosemary’s relatives lived away, though did telephone regularly. Quite a few friends were frequent visitors, as were former business associates. Mostly though, it was a pretty quiet home with a lovely garden we would enjoy together. While watching me from her wheelchair nearby as I put some linen away one afternoon, Rosemary told me to stop humming. “I hate that you’re happy all the time and always humming,” she declared miserably. I finished what I was doing, closed the linen cupboard door, turned around and looked at her with amusement. “Well, it’s true. You’re always humming and you’re always happy. I wish you’d just be miserable sometimes.”

> 每一天我都看到她情感上的那堵围墙一点点在拆除，我们也变得越来越亲密。罗斯玛丽要我停止哼歌。“我讨厌你随时都是快乐的，总是哼歌。”她以悲哀的口气对我下令。我完成了手上做的事情，关上了家庭日用织品小柜子的门，然后转过身，惊愕地望着她。“嗯，是真的。你总是在哼歌，你永远都是快乐的。我希望有时候你悲哀点。”

This was such a typical viewpoint from Rosemary that I wasn’t surprised by it at all. I wasn’t always happy, but when I was it gave her something to moan about. Instead of responding verbally though, I just looked at her, then did a pirouette, poked my tongue out at her, and left the room laughing. She loved me for this, as when I came back into the room shortly after, she was smiling with mischief and acceptance. Never did she condemn my positive moods again in such a way.

> 这就是罗斯玛丽的典型的看问题的视角，我完全不会感到惊讶。我并非永远快乐，可是当我表现出快乐的时候却得到她的抱怨和悲叹。我只是盯着她，而不是反唇相讥，随后，我做了一个像芭蕾舞那样的竖趾旋转动作，对她伸了伸舌头，笑着离开了房间。她喜欢我这样，当我很快再次回到房间的时候，她面带调皮和包容的微笑。我以这种方式阻止了她，后来她再也没有谴责我的乐观心境。

“Why are you happy?” Rosemary asked one morning soon after. “I mean, not just today, but in general. Why are you happy?” I smiled at the question, thinking how far I had come in myself to even be asked such a question. Considering what I had been going through in my own life while caring for Rosemary, it was a rather poignant question.

> “为什么你是快乐的？”不久后的一个早上她这样问我。“我的意思是不仅是今天，而是平时，为什么你总是那么快乐？”我被这个问题弄笑了，想到了自己从开始快乐到被问及这个问题中间经历了多么漫长的岁月。想想为了此刻照顾罗斯玛丽，在我自己的人生道路上我所经历的一切，这是个多么凄凉的问题啊。

“Because happiness is a choice Rosemary and one I try to make every day. Some days I can’t. Like you, I’ve had a hard life too, in different ways but still hard. But rather than dwelling on what’s wrong and how hard I’ve done it, I am trying to find the blessings in each day and appreciating the moment I am in, as much as I can,” I told her honestly. “We have the freedom to choose what we focus on. I try to choose the positive stuff, like getting to know you, like doing work I love, not being under pressure to reach sales targets, and appreciating my health and every day of being alive.” Rosemary smiled, looking at me intensely as she absorbed my words.

> “罗斯玛丽，因为快乐是一种选择，我每天都在尽量让自己快乐。有的时候，我也跟你一样不能够快乐起来，我也有很艰难的时候，形式不同，但真的很难。然而，我尽量在每一天里去寻求上帝的祝福，尽自己最大可能欣赏此刻的我，而不是整天纠缠于到底是怎么啦以及我要做到这样有多么的难。”我非常诚实地告诉了她。“我们有自由去选择我们需专注什么。我尽量选择乐观的态度，如同你知道的那样，这就好像我正在做我喜爱的工作，没有任何压力去达到销售目标，同时，我很感激我有健康的身体，每天都活着。”罗斯玛丽微笑了，她在听我说话的时候，很认真地盯着我。

What she didn’t know though, was that while I was caring for her I had also been dealing with illness of my own. Some time prior, I had had a minor operation. When the specialist called me with the results, he told me they were open to doubt and a larger operation had to be done immediately. I told him I would think about it.

> 她有所不知的是，在我正在照顾她的同时，我也在跟自己的疾病进行着搏斗。前段时间，我还动了一个小手术。当那个专科医生打电话告诉我结果之时，他告诉我他们不敢肯定我的诊断，需要马上进行一个更大的手术。我回答他们会考虑这个问题。

“There is nothing to think about,“ he had stated adamantly, “you must have this operation or you could be dead in a year.” Again, I told him I would think about it. I had already done some big learning through my body, which isn’t surprising since the body is where our past is stored. All of our pain and joy manifests within the body in one way or another.

> “没有考虑的余地！”那位医生坚决地说。“你必须动这个手术，否则在一年之内你可能会死。”我再次告诉他我会考虑的。通过我的身体，我已经学到了一些经验，过去身体的种种不适让我处变不惊。我们所有的痛苦与欢乐都以这样或者那样的方式体现在我们的身体之中。

Having managed to relieve myself of smaller ailments previously, by healing various painful emotions, I decided a huge gift of healing was now on offer to me. So I would approach my illness from that perspective. Having enough of my own fear to deal with though, I was only able to share the situation with one or two people. It was going to take all of my strength to get through it and to stay focused on what I wanted, which was health.

> 以前我有依靠疗愈不同的情感痛苦来指导自己缓解一些较小病痛的经验，我决定现在给自己提供一个巨大的疗愈福音。所以，我会从这种视角来处理我的疾病。我自己的恐惧感已经够我去应对了，因此，我只能够将我的情形告诉一两个人。我需要拿出我的全部力量来战胜疾病，并且保持我想要的是身体健康的信念。

So I couldn’t risk taking on other people’s opinions or fears. These may have been intended with love, but there was not one inch of space for anyone else’s fear on this healing journey. Having the courage to express myself emotionally, to release things from some very deep levels, became even more important and things certainly turned dark for a while. A lot of past stuff surfaced from the depths.

> 我不可以冒险接纳别人的意见或者别人的恐惧心理。他人的意见也许包含了爱，但是，在我的疗伤旅途中不能有半点的其他人的恐慌和害怕。拥有勇气去表达自己的情感和从内心深处释放信念变得更加重要，其结果，有些事情当然暂时转为灰暗起来。许多陈旧的思想此时也从心底深处冒了出来。

At one stage, it became so difficult and emotionally painful I ended up welcoming the thought of dying, and asked the illness to take me away. When I had to seriously contemplate my whole life and accept that despite my efforts, I may actually pass on from this illness and not live through to an old age, I reached a point where an amazing peace was found. Realising I had already lived an incredible life and I’d had the courage to honour my own heart and calling, allowed me to look my death in the face and accept either outcome.

> 这种情形一度让我的生活变得非常艰难和情绪上饱受痛苦，以至于最后我想到了死，请求疾病把我带走。我不得不慎重思索我的整个人生，并承认不管我有多努力，实际上我也许会因这种疾病而去世，承认自己活不到老年。认清了现实之后我却达到了令人惊叹的平静层面。当我意识到自己已经拥有了他人难以置信的生活经历，我鼓起了勇气以我自己的心和召唤为荣，允许我面对自己的死亡，接受任何后果。

The peace that followed this acceptance was beautiful. While continuing with my usual meditation practice though, I also worked through various healing books and visualisation techniques, in addition to releasing the emotions that wanted out. Various shifts started to occur within me. Eventually, I reached a stage where I felt the worst was behind me, and that I was on the road to wellness.

> 我突然发现，接受一切的那种平静是多么的美好。随着我继续进行常规冥想练习，并通过不同的疗伤书籍和心理学方面的形象化技术，我在情感上得到了释放。同时，各种各样的轮班工作也开始出现了。最终，我进入了一个新阶段，我感觉人生最坏的那段路我已经跨越过去，现在我正走在恢复健康的路途之中。

A lovely house-sit was offered in a rambling little cottage, covered in vines and hidden away behind a high fence. It was in a fairly affluent suburb, but was almost invisible and I loved it. Soaking in a bath had always been a bit of a lifesaver for me too, and this house had an enormous one. Being in such a congenial environment, I decided to do a juice fast, as I had numerous times before, and a couple of days of silence and meditation.

> 有次，工作是是看管一栋杂乱无章的小村舍，这对我而言是非常愉快的工作。小村舍被藤本植物所覆盖，隐藏在高篱笆墙后面。虽然它坐落在一个相当富裕的郊区，但几乎是不被外人所见，我喜欢这个村舍。对我而言，在澡盆里浸泡永远让自己感觉好像即将有大救星降临，恰好这栋房子有一个巨大的澡盆。置身于和我如此投缘的环境，我决定去制作一些果汁，随后的几天，我进行自己的冥想练习。

My body had always been a great indicator of where my emotions had been. If a minor ailment would present itself, I could recognise where my thoughts or activities had been in the days or weeks leading up to this. As a result, over time I had come to enjoy a very clear and honest communication channel from my body, always now listening to what it was saying and doing my best to adhere to methods of improvement. Often clients or friends would admit to knowing there was something wrong in their body, long before they did anything about it.

> 我的身体永远都是一个很棒的指示器，总会显示出我的情绪状况。如果我的身体出现一点小毛病，我就能凭此确认出这几天或者这几周我是被什么样的思想或者行动所“挟持”。结果，随着时间的推移，我享受于生理与心理的清晰和诚实的沟通。现在我完全可以听从我的身体向我发出的某些指令，然后尽我最大的努力去坚持改进的方法。我的有些客户或者朋友们承认他们知道自己的身体不健康，而很久之前他们并不在意身体的问题，即使有点小毛病，也不懂得如何改善。

But having seen the lack of quality that life offers once health is gone, I had learned to act on any signs from my body as soon and as best as I could. Health offers amazing freedom that once gone, is often gone forever. One of the meditations I did while in the cottage was guidance from a book I had recently bought. There were many stages leading up to this point though, and much work had already been done.

> 由于见过很多人因缺乏生活品质，使得曾经的健康一去不复返，我学到了一旦我的身体出现任何不良迹象，我一定要尽我的最大可能性，尽快采取应对措施。人的健康有着惊人的自由度，不是以你的意志转移，一旦失去健康，通常是永远一去不复返。我在这个村舍进行的冥想方式是我从最近买来的一本书中学来的。虽然整个过程它包含了许多的步骤，但是，在完成这些步骤的同时其中的很多功效随之已经体现出来。

This particular book was looking at the intelligence of our cells, how they worked together, and offered guidance in asking them to eradicate the illness from the body. It was healing at a cell level. So mid-morning, I sat myself down on my meditation cushion and slipped into a deep, peaceful place within.
