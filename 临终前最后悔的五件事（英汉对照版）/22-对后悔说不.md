# No regrets · 对后悔说不

The speed in which things changed after this was phenomenal. Depression slipped away in the night, taking its heavy cloud of darkness with it. It had only ever been waiting for love to arrive and when that happened, it knew its role was completed and left. I spent the next few days restoring my energy through meditation, gratitude, and reverence for my beautiful self.

> 痛定思痛之后，一切发生改变的速度令人非常惊讶。一夜之间，抑郁症带着它那沉重的层层乌云烟消云散了。我此刻唯一要做的就是静静地等待着爱的到来，就在当下，抑郁症明白了它的使命已经完成，将永久地离开。随后几天里，我通过冥想练习和感恩祈祷来恢复我的能量和对自我的美丽表示崇敬。

This nurtured my heart, while soaking in the bath nurtured my body. I took long, easy walks over the hills, not pushing myself, just walking gently as I marvelled at life through the eyes of someone reborn. It was like waking up in a world so beautiful that it was difficult to remember the world before.

> 我在浴室里泡澡呵护我那躯体的同时，也呵护着我的心灵。我在旷野的山丘之间长时间地轻松散步，坦然自若，很温和地慢步行走，以一个脱胎换骨之人的眼光，惊叹着生命和周围的一切。这就好像一觉醒来发现眼前的世界非常的美丽，跟以往记忆中的那个世界截然不同。

To signify the beginning of my new life I decided to carry out a formal farewell and welcoming ceremony. I collected wood from the paddocks and lit a beautiful fire. There were things that needed a proper farewell from my life, aspects of my old self and the circumstances that had risen from that. So I wrote these things down as well as things being welcomed.

> 为了庆祝新生命的开始，我决定进行一个正式的告别和欢迎仪式。我从农场的围场里捡来许多杂木，堆积并点燃起了一个漂亮的篝火。此刻，我需要跟我的生活进行一次适当的告别，告别以前的自我，告别以前曾经出现过的种种遭遇和不幸。因此，我用笔和纸写下所有要告别的内容，以及需要欢迎新生活的词句。

Then as the sun went down and the first evening stars came out, I happily stood by the healing, warming fire. Old parts of myself were thanked and given farewell as I then dropped that piece of paper into the fire. Each welcome was also acknowledged. Sitting out under that country sky after, staring into the fire, I felt immense love for myself and for life. I also felt incredible gratitude.

> 当太阳落山，新生命的第一个夜晚开始降临的时刻，我独自高兴地站在可以疗愈伤痛的温暖的篝火旁。当我把写满内容的纸丢进火中时，旧日的我带着感激和告别之情也随之而去了，每个欢迎新生的感受也得到了确认。随后，我坐在乡村的夜空下，凝视着篝火，我感觉对自己和对生命的那种无边无际的爱悄然而生。同时，我也感到难以置信的感恩。

The firelight continued to burn warmly. Grinning, I looked at the vast cover of stars above me and acknowledged that through all of this, someone new had indeed been born. The person I had been working towards being for years was now who I was. She had finally been allowed through. The person who had made too many excuses for others, had carried decades of pain, and who hadn’t accepted that she too deserved every happiness was no longer needed. Her role was now complete. She was gently thanked for her part in my evolution, and was now gone.

> 篝火继续在温暖地燃烧着。我仰望着浩瀚星空不由得咧嘴笑了，并承认通过一切难关之后，一个新的生命的确诞生了。我曾经努力了很多年想成为的那个人，如今做到了，她就是现在的我。她最终允许自己达到了目标。曾经太多的原谅别人，背负着几十年的痛苦，以及曾经不能接受自己也值得快乐和幸福的那个她再也没有存在的必要了，她的历史使命已经完结。她温柔的感谢了进化中的那部分自我，现在她放心地离去。

Every day following continued to reveal delight on fresh levels. It was almost like discovering life for the first time. Never had I felt so free. Happiness, as I had never known happiness before, completely unhindered, joyous, and guilt-free, became my more natural state. New birds came to sit on the fence and sing to me.

> 随后我继续每天沉浸在崭新的喜悦之中，这几乎就像一个人第一次发现了生命那样充满新鲜感和好奇心。我感觉从未有过的自由。以往从未体验过的那种十分顺心、令人喜气洋洋和毫无愧疚的快乐感是我目前的心理状态。各种刚刚飞来的小鸟站在农舍的篱笆上为我歌唱。

Old ones followed me when I walked the paddocks in a state of bliss. All of my senses became more heightened, feeling like I had just finished weeks of silent meditation, except that this more alert state remained. Natural sounds were clearer, and colours brighter and more alive. I noticed as least thirty different shades of green in the country surrounding the cottage.

> 当我幸福无比地漫步在围场的时候，那些小鸟跟随在我的身后。除了我的警觉系统保持原状之外，我的所有感官都变得更加敏感和高亢，感觉仿佛自己刚好完成了几周的默默冥想练习一样。大自然的各种声音变得更加清晰，各种色彩更加明亮，我眼中的大自然更有活力。在这间农舍周围的乡村里，我注意到了至少有三十种不同的绿色色调。

There was space and clarity within that I had always believed was there, but had never quite known. My past was now of little relevance either. The wisdom gained along the way was a part of me. The past has served as an incredible learning tool and none of the learning had been wasted. But the suffering that had come to shape me had played its role and had now disintegrated.

> 我一直相信我的内心其实对事物有着很强的清晰认识，只是从来都不完全的明白而已。我的过去跟现在也一笔勾销，没有了关联。我在前进的道路上所获得的智慧是我生命的一部分。我的那些过去成为了自我成长过程中最不可思议的学习内容，以前所有的经验学习如今都派上了用场。不过，曾经塑造了我的那些痛苦也发挥其作用，如今最终走向消失。

There was nothing to prove, nothing to explain, nothing to justify. My face ached from smiling. Almost overnight, life shifted onto a completely different plane. Living presently had also now become a way of life, after years of practice. The doors of opportunity then swung open. All of the past efforts on my creative journey, of focus, resilience and sacrifices, started to be rewarded.

> 这里没有什么需要证明，没有什么需要解释以及需要再去辩护。我按捺不住一直在微笑。几乎一夜之间，我的生活好像转入一个完全不同的地质平面。多年的磨练之后，活在当下也成为了我现在的生活方式。从此，机遇之门为我打开。我过去在创作之旅中的所有努力，以及应变能力和牺牲精神方面的付出开始带给我种种的福报。

My work gained huge momentum and new writing opportunities arrived from unimagined sources. Love for myself had opened the doors, allowing great things to flow my way. It had all been waiting patiently for years, simply for my own readiness. As time has since unfolded, the natural flow of goodness continues to grow. New support systems too have built themselves around me, both professionally and personally. Of course, there will always be new things to learn about myself but I never take even the tiniest blessing for granted now.

> 我的作品获得了巨大影响，新的创作邀请从意想不到的各种机构来到我的身边。爱护自己的各式大门已经打开，我让一切的福报随意出现在我的生活之中。我为准备好这一切而耐心地等待了很多年。白从那一晚以来，友好和仁慈不断地在我心中滋长。在我周围无论是专业团队还是个体的新的支持系统自然地建立起来。当然.我需要永远不断地了解自己，如今我决不要把上帝赐予的一点点福音不当回事，我要珍惜出现在我眼前的一切美好的事物。

Over the previous years I had been consciously creating the life I imagined, by releasing my hindrances one layer at a time. Getting very clear about the life I wanted to be living and the person I wanted to be, was also a necessary part of this process. If occasional blockages now surface, I am patient and loving with myself whilst working through them. Self-discovery is a joyful process and I can smile at my humanness.

> 过去这些年以来，通过一次次逐步化解自我的重重障碍，我已经有意识的去创造自己想象的那种生活。其中最有必要的是要更加清楚地明白自己希望过什么样的生活，要成为什么样的人。如果我现在在创造新生活的道路上遇到什么阻碍，我会耐心地面对，以爱自己的方式去化解。挖掘自我是一个非常愉悦的过程，我现在可以为自己的为人而微笑了。

With all that happened, I found myself feeling closer than ever to every one of the beautiful people I had cared for in their passing. This new life unravelling for me was the kind of life they had each glimpsed as having once been possible, when they had looked back and spoken of their regrets. In their final weeks and days, when all else had fallen away, they could see what potential joy life had offered them, if only they had lived differently.

> 随着眼前发生的一切，我发现对那些我曾经照顾过的每一个优秀的临终人来讲，我感觉比以前任何时候都更加接近他们。我明白了，现在为我展开的新生活正是跟他们当时的生活大致相同，比如，他们在回首往事并对我述说他们那些遗憾的时候，领悟到了以前一直未能领悟到的人生哲理。他们在生命的最后几个星期和几天里，当一切都无法挽回之时，他们能够明白以前曾经拥有过潜在的愉悦生活，所以，他们后悔的感叹要是从前早些意识到这一点就好了。

Not every person spoke of regrets though. Some people said they would have done some things differently, but were not consumed with any actual regret. Some were beautifully content with the life they had led. Or in the least, beautifully accepting of the life they had led. Many others did have regrets though and had a strong desire to be heard, to have their thoughts known. The length of time I spent with each client was possibly a catalyst for the honesty that each relationship came to know. For those large amounts of time, I will always be very grateful.

> 然而，也不是每个人都有表明后悔。有些人说如果时间倒流，他们可能会有不一样的生活，但实际上还谈不上后悔什么。有的已经拥有了漂亮的人生，生活一直按照自己的愿望在进行，或者至少他们满意地接受自己的那种生活。而其他的许多人的确有一些遗憾和后悔，他们很希望把他们的生活教训讲给别人听，也让人知道他们的想法。我将永远感激那些年来与他们共度的那些时光。

The regrets they shared left me determined to not feel the same at the end of my own time, whenever that was. There was no way I was going to be given the gift of this wisdom and not learn by it. But having now endured the biggest tests, I understood just how difficult the challenges could be. I also now understood though, how joyous the rewards were by making it through.

> 他们的那些懊悔让我下定决心无论我的生命在何时结束，在那一天到来之际，我不要有他们那种感觉，不要有任何的后悔。我不想有这样的后悔智慧，不想成为事后诸葛亮，也不希望从自身的教训中学习什么。然而，经受了这次最大的人生考验，我懂得了生活中的挑战可能会是多么的困难，我还懂得了在经受考验的过程中也有怎样令人喜悦的福报。

The potential of fulfillment and pleasure, that each of these dear people caught a glimpse of towards their passing, is what is on offer to every one of us now, before our own time to pass arrives. Each new day that unfolds leaves me even more enchanted with the natural flow of goodness. It wants to come through and it does so when you learn how to allow it, through faith and self-love. It awaits everyone. You just need to get out of your own way first and that is where the real work is – learning to own your own thoughts by clearing away the debris that stops you from letting it all flow.

> 在我们每个人即将走到生命终点之前，那些可爱的人们在他们临终之时领悟到的潜在满足感和喜悦感对我们每个活着的人都有参考作用。展现在我面前的每一个新日子让我更加陶醉在自我感觉良好的自然流露之中。当你充满信心和自爱，同时学会了如何允许善待自己，你对美好生活的向往就会心想事成。每个人都有相同的机会拥有自我感觉良好的生活。要等到这一天，你必须首先从你原有的生活框架中脱离出来，学会通过清除你通往美好生活道路上的一切障碍来拥有自己真正的思想，而不是被别人的思想所左右。

Learning will always continue. It’s not like you’ll reach a stage of growth and say, “Great. Now I can sit back, know everything, and cruise through every day without ever having to learn another thing”. Even Stella, who had done so much work on her inner journey, was reminded of the need to let go and surrender at times. In doing so, she was able to become more peaceful in her remaining days, before leaving with a radiant smile on her face when the time to pass over arrived.

> 学习将永远不会停止。这不像你到达一定的成长阶段，然后说：“很好。现在我可以坐下来歇歇脚，我知道了一切，我不需要再学习其他的东西也可以航行远洋。”就拿斯特拉来说吧，她已经修炼了内心很多年也依然提醒自己要经常地放下和屈服。正因如此，她在生命的最后时期能够变得更加的平和，当她的大限日子到来之际，她是面带灿烂的微笑离开人世的。

So if the learning never stops, we may as well embrace it rather than resist it. Not one day goes by that I don’t learn something new about myself. But I can do so now with loving kindness, by loving myself in an unconditional way, with no self-judgment. Laughing gently and lovingly also allows the growth process to be smoother. When Grace spoke the words, “I wish I’d had the courage to live a life true to myself, not the life others expected of me,” she carried such sadness about her life turning out the way it did.

> 因此，如果我们不断地在学习，我们也许也会乐意地接受学习，而不是抵制学习。我每天必须从自己身上挖掘新的东西。然而，我只能依靠无条件和不加任何评判地爱自己，在自爱中才能做到这一点。温柔而亲切的欢笑也能使成长的过程更加顺畅“但愿我有拿出勇气过自己真正想要的生活，而不是过别人期望的生活”。当格雷斯说这话的时候，她对自己的生活感到无比的悲伤，事实上也的确如此。

It is a pity that being who you truly are requires so much courage. But it does. It takes enormous courage at times. Being who you are, whoever that is, sometimes cannot even be articulated at first, not even to yourself. All you know is there is a yearning within that is not being fulfilled by the life you are currently living. Having to explain this to others, who have not walked in your shoes, may just leave you questioning yourself even further.

> 然而令人遗憾的是，做真正的自己需要太多的勇气。这一点毫无疑问，的确需要时时拿出巨大的勇气。不管你是谁，做你自己有的时候完全不能用语言表达清楚，甚至你自己都不完全清楚到底该做些什么。你唯一可以知道的是心中蕴藏着一种渴望，那就是你不满足你现有的生活。假如你想对别人解释你为什么不满足也许只能让你更进一步的质疑自己。

But as the wise man Buddha said more than two millennia ago, “The mind knows no answers. The heart knows no questions”. It is the heart that guides you to joy, not the mind. Overcoming the mind and letting go of other’s expectations allows you to hear your own heart.

> 根据两千多年前的圣人佛陀所说：“有我的思想得不到答案，无我的心才能明澈。”只有你的心才能引导你到达愉悦，而不是你的有我思索。战胜了你的有我和放下别人的期望使得你听从于自己内心的召唤。

Having the courage to then follow it is where true happiness lies. In the meantime, keep on cultivating the heart while mastering the mind. As the heart grows, life brings more joy and peace your way. A happy life wants you as much as you want it.

> 随后，你必须拥有勇气去追寻你的幸福和快乐到底在哪里。与此同时，在无我的心掌控着有我思想的时候，继续保持耕耘和培养你的无我之心。当你的无我之心成长了，你的生活才会更加的喜悦和平静。一种幸福生活的获得需要就像你所期望的那样去生活。

When Anthony lay is the nursing home and admitted to not having the courage to try for a better life, he sadly showed the consequences of being ruled by such fear. It doesn’t mean that you too will end up in a nursing home before your time. But the lack of both stimulus and happiness that became a part of his life are no different to the lives of millions amongst us. Each day was just a mind numbing routine, safe and secure, but never satisfying.

> 当安东尼躺在养老院，并承认没有勇气去尝试一种更好的生活的时候，他令人遗憾的显示出被极度害怕所控制之下的悲惨结局。这并不意味着你去世之前也一定会最终在养老院度过余生。但是，缺乏外界的刺激和快乐感这两个因素成为了他们生活的主要组成部分，这与我们当中成千上万人的生活没有两样。每一天只是考虑麻木的日常事务，考虑安全和有保障，绝对没有满足而言。

It takes fortitude to create big changes. The longer you stay in the wrong environment and remain its product though, the longer you deny yourself the opportunity to know true happiness and satisfaction. Life is too short to watch it go by, just because of fear that can be conquered if faced.

> 你若要想有很大的改变必须有坚韧的意志力。你越长时间地呆在不适合的环境和持续成力该环境的产物，你就越长时间地拒绝自己去了解真正的快乐和满足的含义。生命如此短暂，眨眼而过，如果你不能面对只是因为害怕而放弃追求幸福和快乐的话，相信你能战胜这种胆怯心理。

Like the vines trapping beautiful flowers in the garden of Florence’s mansion, we are all capable of creating our own trappings. Obviously, many of these are not quite so easy to remove as her vine was. Most trappings have the strength of decades of growth behind them and do not take kindly to being removed. They will hang on for dear life, strangling your beauty if you let them.

> 如同弗洛伦斯那豪宅花园中的那些藤本植物，慢慢缠绕上美丽的花卉，我们都是自己给自己制造了那些缠绕物。很显然，就像她的那些藤本植物一样，有的很难被斩草除根。几乎所有的藤本植物经过了几十年的生长后都变得很强大，用温柔的手段不可能把它们根除掉。如果你任凭它们自由发展，它们将紧紧缠着你不放并扼杀你的美丽。

Just as they were created over time though, they can be undone over time too. It is a delicate process of determination, bravery and at times, of letting go. It is having the courage to stop unhealthy relationships in their tracks and say “Enough”. It is treating yourself with respect and kindness, both of which you deserve.

> 犹如它们随着时间的推移而生长那样，它们也需要时间的推移而灭亡。这是一个微妙的需决心、勇气和时间的过程，也是放下的过程。这是需要有勇气结束不健康的关联网，然后表明“足够了”。这是需要尊重自己和善待自己，两者都是值得你拥有的。

Mostly though, to break free of your own trappings, it is about becoming an observer of your own thoughts and habits. This awareness helps solutions become more apparent. Life is your own, not someone else’s. If you are not finding some element of happiness in what you have created and are doing nothing to improve on it, then the gift of every new day is wasted.

> 但更重要的是，你要摆脱自己去制造那些缠绕物的束缚，时时刻刻观察你自己的思想和行为。这种警觉性帮助你更加明显的找到解决问题的方法。你的生活你做主，与别人无关。如果你在你所创造的生活中找不到幸福和快乐的元素，也没有努力去改善你现有的生活，那么，每一天的新生活就被你白白地浪费掉了。

A tiny step or a small decision are great starting points, those and taking responsibility for your own happiness. A happy life can also be found without moving house or doing anything drastic in your physical world. It is about changing your perception and being brave enough to honour some of your own desires too. No one else can make you happy or unhappy, unless you allow him or her to.

> 每迈出一小步或者一个细微的决定都将是非常好的起点，都是为你自己的幸福承担起责任。一种幸福的生活并非一定是从你搬迁大房子或者在你的物质世界里超级的消费中获得。只要你改变你的审视视角和有足够的勇气去履行你的某些欲望和打算，你就会找到你的幸福和快乐。没有人可以让你开心或者不开心，除非你自己依赖于别人。

Yes, having the courage to be yourself and not who others expect you to be may take a lot of strength and honesty. But so does lying on your deathbed and admitting that you wished you had done it differently. There were many other clients in between those mentioned. This regret, wishing they had been true to themselves, was the most common one of all.

> 拥有勇气去做你自己而不是做别人所期待的那个你也许的确需要很多的力量和诚实。然而，当你躺在临终的床上承认你是多么希望你曾经拥有一种不一样的生活也是需要勇气和诚实的。除了我提到的那些临终之人外，我还有很多其他的客户。但愿曾经真实地做他们自己是他们所有后悔中最普遍的后悔。

When John said he wished he hadn’t worked so hard, he was also speaking some of the most common words I would hear throughout those years. During his final weeks, sitting out on the balcony watching life on the harbour unfold, John was burdened with regret. There is nothing at all wrong in loving the work you do. In fact, this is how it should be. But it’s about finding balance so that work is not your whole life. I can still hear that dear man sighing deeply, as he came to terms with the choices he had made.

> 当约翰说他希望以前没有拼命工作的时候，也说出了那些年我常常听到过的最为普遍的一些话。在他最后的几个星期里，他坐在房屋外面的阳台，观望着出现在海湾的人来人往，约翰的心里充满了后悔的包袱。热爱你的工作没有半点的错，事实上，你是多么的应该热爱自己的工作。问题是你需要找到平衡，毕竟工作不是你的整个生活。至今我依然听得见这位可爱的男人在勉强接受他曾经作出的人生选择时所发出的深深的叹气声。

Listening then to Charlie’s insistence on the benefits of a simple life, I had to agree with his wisdom and life experience. True value is not on what you own, but on who you are. Dying people know this. Their belongings are of no consequence whatsoever at the end. What other people think of them, or what they have achieved in belongings, does not even enter their thinking at such a time.

> 随后，我聆听到查利那坚持认为简单生活带来益处的声音。我不得不认同他的智慧和生活经验。真正的人生价值不在于你拥有了什么，而在于你是什么。临终的人都明白这个道理。在他们的生命快要结束之际，没有任何东西是属于他们的。无论别人以往怎样地评价他们，无论他们曾经取得了什么样的成就，在这样的时刻，所有的一切甚至都不会引起他们的注意，一切都不重要了。

In the end, what matters to people is how much happiness they have brought to those they love and how much time they spent doing things they themselves loved. Trying to ensure that those they left behind don’t end up with the same regrets also became critical for many people. None of the life reviews I witnessed from their deathbeds were spent on thoughts of wishing they had bought or owned more, not even one. Instead, what most occupies the thoughts of dying people are how they lived their lives, what they did, and if they had made a positive difference to those they left behind, whether that was family, community or whoever.

> 人将至死，人们最在乎的还是给自己所爱的人曾经带去了多少快乐和幸福，他们曾经拥有多少时间做某些事来善待和爱护自己。对很多人而言，尽量去确定不要让他们的亲朋好友们带着跟他们一样的悔恨走向死亡也成了他们临终关心的重点。我在那些临终之人的床边亲眼所见，没有任何人在回顾自己的人生时希望自己以前买得更多或者拥有更多，真的是一个人都没有。相反地，这些临终的人想得最多的是他们以前的生活是怎样过的，他们曾经做了些什么，以及他们是否可以给他们的后人带来积极正面的影响，那些后人可以是家人、社区朋友或者任何一个人。

The things you often think you need are sometimes the things that keep you trapped in an unfulfilled life. Simplicity is the key to changing this, that and letting go of the need for validation through ownership or through other’s expectations of you. Taking risks also requires courage. But you cannot control everything.

> 你时常认为你需要的那些东西有的时候实际上让你一直在永不满足的人生中被它们所缠绕和困扰。简单是改变这种困扰的关键，放下那些希望自己拥有一切来获得别人认同的需求，或者放下别人以为你该有的那些需求。承担风险也需要勇气。你不可能控制生活中的一切。

Staying in a seemingly secure environment does not guarantee that life’s lessons will pass you by unnoticed. They can still come out of the blue, when you least expect them. So can life’s rewards though, for those with courage to honour their hearts. The clock ticks for every one of us. It is your own choice how you spend your remaining days.

> 持续生活在看似安全和有保障的环境之中并不能保证你所有的生活中的种种经验教训都将会无声无息的顺利而过。至少当你预计它们会出现的时候，它们还是可以突然出现在你的面前。对于那些拥有勇气，忠实地遵守自己内心的人们来说，生活的奖励或者好处也是遵循这种定律。时钟的嘀嗒声在我们每一个人的生命中一致敲响。不同的是，我们各自都有如何度过自己余生的选择。

As Pearl understood, things flow when you need them. She believed that the most important thing is to work towards finding your purpose, to do your work, whatever it is, with the right intention and not get trapped into unhappy work situations by a fear of lack. It is about learning and daring to think without limitations and not trying to control how things will flow to you.

> 如同珀尔理解的那样，当你需要它们的时候，一切都会如你所愿。她相信，最重要的一点是朝着你自己的人生目标去工作，无论何种工作只要有正确的意图就该努力做好，千万不要因担心失去而被不愉快的工作环境所困扰。这与学会无边无际的大胆设想有关，千万不要试图控制事情该怎样地出现在你的面前。

Life is over so quickly, she said. It is. Some of us will live a long life, many of us won’t. But if you can know happiness and fulfillment in this brief time, there is no need for regret when the end comes, as it inevitably will.

> 珀尔说生命瞬间即过。事实的确如此。我们当中有的人也许将活得很长久，但大多数不会这样。如果你在短暂的人生里可以懂得快乐和知足，当你不可避免地走到生命尽头的时候，你不必有任何的遗憾和后悔。

Learning how to express feelings is unfortunately a challenge for too many adults. It was also a deep frustration and regret for dying people, including Jozsef. He wanted to express himself but didn’t know how, as he’d had no practice. The heartache this brought to the lovely man was his greatest regret, as he died feeling like his family had never truly known him. Other clients developed illnesses associated with the bitterness they carried, as they had also never learned to express themselves.

> 对太多的成年人而言，学会如何表达感受是一种很不幸的巨大挑战。这也是让很多的临终之人，包括约瑟夫在内所感到深深的沮丧和后悔的地方。约瑟夫曾经希望自我表达，可他不知道如何去做，因他从来没有向别人表达过心里的感受。在他去世的时候，好像他的家人从未真正的了解过他，不能向他们表露出内心感受给这位可爱的男人带来无比的心痛，这也是他临终时的最大懊悔。我的其他有严重疾病的客户们也是因为从来没有学会向亲人述说内心而背负着这种痛苦。

Like anything, you get better with practice. So by starting with small acts of bravery in expressing yourself, you become more at ease with opening up and even begin to enjoy sharing such honesty. You’ll never be able to control the reactions of others. However, although people may initially react when you change the way you are by speaking honestly, in the end it raises the relationship to a whole new and healthier level. Either that or it releases the unhealthy relationship from your life completely. Whichever way, you win.

> 如同我们做任何事情那样，你需要不断地练习才能做得更好。因此，在自我表达方面，一旦你勇敢地迈出开始的第一小步，你在以后的自我表达中会变得更加的轻松自如，甚至开始享受与他人分享这样的诚实。当你诚实地表达了内心，你也永远不可能控制对方的回应。然而，当你改变了你的习惯作风，很真实地说出心里话，虽然开始人们也许会出现不同的反应，但是，最终你们的关系会得到提升，到达崭新和更健康的水平，或许还会使你们那种不健康的关系从你的生活中完全地释放出来。无论怎样，你都赢得了胜利。

We can never know how long we are here for or how long those we love will be. So rather than have to live with the regrets before your dying time, ensure those you value know how you feel now. As dear Jude said, guilt is a toxic emotion to accompany your remaining years. Expressing your feelings feels good too, when you get used to it. It is just the fear of how it will be received that holds you back. So knock the fear on the head and dare to reveal your beautiful self to others, before it is too late either way.

> 我们永远不可能知道我们自己或者我们所爱的人能在世上活多久。所以，你需要现在就确定你所看重的人是否懂得你的感受，要及时地向他们表白，而不是等到你临终之前才觉得后悔，就像亲爱的朱迪所说的那样，愧疚是一种毒性情感将伴随你的余生。当你习惯了表达你的感受，这种感觉也是非常的好。害怕如何去表达将使得你永远退缩不前。因此，无论如何，在力时已晚之前，你要阻止害怕心理，勇敢地向别人揭示你的美丽自我。

If you are already carrying guilt from things left unsaid to someone already passed, it is now time to forgive yourself. You are not honouring your life by carrying guilt forward. It is time to be gentle with yourself. That was who you were then. It does not need to be who you are now. Compassion for who you used to be, given from who you are now is the first seed of kindness towards self-forgiveness.

> 如果你已经因为没有向某个已经过世的人表达需要表达的感受而背负着愧疚，那么现在请你放下你的愧疚，勇敢地原谅自己吧！背负着愧疚的生活是不尊重你的生命。现在该是你温和地对待自己的时候了。你是原来的你，不必做现在充满内疚的你。对曾经过去的你表示出同情之心，做原本的你是播下仁慈的宽恕自己的第一粒种子。

If people in your life don’t appear to respond to your honest expression, it doesn’t mean they haven’t heard you or that you should not have expressed yourself. Nanci with Alzheimer’s disease was a great example of this. Other relationships in my life though have also been transformed through consistency of kindness and honesty. For a long time, it appeared that my words were not heard. But when the other people were ready to express their feelings, it was obvious that every word had been heard along the way. It wouldn’t have mattered in the end though.

> 在你的生活中，如果人们对你的诚实表达不做任何的回应，这并不意味着他们还没有听见你的心声或者你不应该表白自已。患有老年痴呆症的南茜就是最好的例子。在我自己的生活中，经过仁慈和诚实的一致性，其他的有些人际关系也已经发生了转变。很长一段时间，似乎我的自我诚实表白没有被他人所听见，但是，当那个人准备好了回应我的时候，很显然，在整个过程中，你的每句话对方都明白了。最终，你已经不在乎对方为什么没有及时回应，或者不作回应。

I was peaceful knowing I’d had the courage to express myself honestly. If any of us had been taken unexpectedly, I would have been without guilt. There was no one taken for granted, no one who didn’t know that I loved them, even if they were not able to express themselves in return so honestly. Tell people how you feel. Life is short.

> 我心平气和地知道我已经拿出勇气去诚实地表达自我。假如我们当中任何人意外的离开人世，我也不会感到愧疚。没有人是理所当然要回应你，人人都知道我有爱他们，纵然他们不能够像我那样诚实地回报我和对我表达他们自己的感受。没有关系，你依然要告诉他们你的内心感受。生命真的很短暂，千万要把握时间。

Locating her friend for Doris brought me true enjoyment and fulfillment. When she spoke of regretting not staying in touch with her friends, I had no idea how often I would also hear this regret through other clients to follow. Now having been through what I have and knowing how valuable old and loyal friends were in carrying me through, it is even less difficult to understand this regret.

> 就桃瑞丝的情况而言，查找到她朋友的住址给我带了最真实的享受和满足感。当她说出没有跟老朋友保持联络的遗憾之时，我不知道从我的其他客户们那里听到过多少次相同的后悔。现在我自己也有了类似的体验，也知道了老朋友是多么的珍贵，所以，我更加不难理解这种后悔的含义。

Most people have friends but when it comes to the crunch, there are not a lot of friends who can be there through the absolute hardest times. When someone is dying, this is one such time. History and understanding are what friendships offer. As the clients were looking back over their lives, it was often friends they missed to reminisce with.

> 几乎每个人都有各种朋友，但是只有当紧要关头才能看出哪些是真正值得珍惜的朋友，不是所有的朋友都可以在绝对危难时刻出现在你的面前。当一个人临近死亡的时候，就是最能辨别真情的紧要关头。友谊给予了人们理解，也增加了人生的历史。我的那些客户们在回顾他们以往的生活时，他们经常追忆与他们失去了联络的朋友。

Life gets busy and friendships fade away. There will always be people who come and go in life, friends included. But those who truly matter, those who you love most dearly, are worth every ounce of effort to stay in touch with. They are the ones who will be there for you when you most need them, just as you will be there for them. Sometimes it is not possible to physically be there, but even phone contact gives people a lot of strength and comfort during hard times.

> 人们忙碌地为生活奔波，友谊渐渐消失。生活中永远都是人来人往，推陈出新，朋友也是如此。然而，那些真正与你息息相关和你深深最爱的人值得你不惜一切代价与之保持联络，因他们在你最需要的时候将与你同在，正如他们需要你的时候，你也毫不迟疑地来到他们的身边。有的时候，虽然不可能亲自到场，但是，在危难时期，一通关怀的电话也会给人们极大的力量和安慰。

Acceptance and forgiveness from friends, particularly when dying, assisted Elizabeth in finding her peace after years of alcoholism. In the end, it is just about love and relationships. But not everyone was so lucky to track down friends at the end, despite the desire to. That’s why not losing touch in the first place is important. No one knows what lies ahead or when the time will come that you are longing for your friends and in between, you still have the gift of them in your life.

> 当伊丽莎白临终之际，那些朋友们的接受和原谅帮助她在多年的酗酒成瘾之后找到了平和。最终留给她的是真爱和友谊。可是，不是每个人在临终的时候都很幸运的找到失去多年的朋友，尽管他们也非常渴望找到。那就是为什么当初不要随意跟你的朋友失去联系是如此的重要。没有人能够知道未来，或者说没有人知道什么时候你需要寻找你的朋友，所以，你依然需要随时随地与朋友保持联络。

Watching the roster of Harry’s support team only continued to emphasise the importance of this at the end. While it can be a gloomy time of sadness for others, the person actually dying wants to enjoy their remaining time as much as possible. Friends bring humour to sad times and this humour brings happiness to the dying person. Whether you are dying or not, friends are the ones capable of making you laugh through the worst of times.

> 哈里那些朋友们的探访花名册正好继续强调了人到临终之时有朋友在身边的重要性。对别人来说，生命快要结束的时候是最悲伤的阴霾之际，而实际上对哈里而言，他希望尽可能地享受最后不多的愉快时光。他的朋友们此时带给他许多的幽默，这让他拥有了足够的快乐和幸福。无论你是否正处在临终或者危难时期，朋友们都能让你在笑声中愉快地度过。

Sitting beside me on her bed after she had screamed at me to go, Rosemary’s admission to having never allowed herself to be happy was an honest one. It also improved her remaining time immensely. Rosemary hadn’t believed that she deserved happiness, due to not being what her family had expected her to be. When she realised it was a choice, she learned to allow happiness through and was able to find a part of herself that had been dormant for most of her adult life. It was a beautiful smile that sometimes then came to escape her in her last weeks.

> 在罗斯玛丽向我吼叫要我滚出去之后，我们俩平静地坐在她的床边，她坦诚地承认了从来没有允许自己成为一个快乐的人。她在生命的最后时期也明显改变了观念。由于以前她的家庭不希望她成为随心所欲的快乐人，她一直相信自己不值得拥有快乐和幸福。当她意识到快乐是一种选择的时候，她学会了让快乐时时伴随着自己，并挖掘出已经潜伏在她几乎整个成年时期那些快乐属性。那就是她的美丽笑容。

Appreciating every single step along the way is one of the keys to such happiness. As Cath contemplated her final time she spoke of missing a lot of potential happiness by focusing too much on the results, rather than on the time along the way as well. It is so easy to think that happiness depends on something falling into place, when it is the other way around. Things fall into place when happiness is already found.

> 注重和享受人生旅途中的每一个时光也是获得快乐和幸福的关键之一。凯西在临终时审视了她的一生之后表示，自己以前太注重人生的种种结果而忽略达到这些结果的过程，因此她失去了很多潜在的快乐。话又说回来，我们很容易认是否快乐与否取决于事情是否达到恰到好处。而事实证明，当你感觉快乐了的时候，一切的事情才会顺眼顺心，才会有头绪。

While it may not be possible to be happy every day, learning to steer the mind towards that direction is still possible. Acknowledging something beautiful outside of the sadness is one example, something that helped me to move back towards a place of peace myself. The mind may cause great suffering. But it can also be used to create a beautiful life, once mastered and used properly. Every single of us has reasons to feel sorry for ourselves. Every one of us has suffered. But life doesn’t owe us anything. We only owe ourselves, to make the most of the life we are living, of the time we have left, and to live in gratitude.

> 在不可能每天都是快乐的时候，学会改变心态方向也是有可能的。比如，学会发现悲伤的外表下也有美丽动人的地方，这就是“否极泰来”，极度的悲伤帮助我冷静下来，平心静气地审视人生。心智也许可以制造巨大的悲痛，而一旦掌控好心智并能合理应用，它也可以用来创造美好的生活。我们每一个人都有理由为自己而感到遗憾，我们每个人都有痛苦。然而，生活没有亏欠我们，只有我们自己亏欠自己，在我们大部分的时间中，我们没有心怀感激地去生活。

When we accept that there will always be learning and that some of this will bring suffering and some will bring happiness, we reach a place of better equanimity. From this perspective, happiness becomes a more conscious choice and the waves are no longer so tumultuous. Some that may have once left you crushed and wounded may now be ridden with the skills that come from experience and wisdom.

> 当我们接受我们一辈子永远是活到老学到老，接受生活中既有痛苦也有快乐的时候，我们的心境才会达到更加平和的状态。从这个视角来看，快乐就是更加主观的选择，痛苦与快乐再也不会如此的动荡不定。过去某些曾经让你受伤和摧毁你的经历使得你从生活体验中获得了智慧，而如今这些经验和智慧可能让你具备了各种人生技能。

It is also perfectly OK to be silly and playful sometimes. You just have to give yourself permission. It is also more than possible to have fun without drugs or alcohol. There is no rule in place that says adults have to be serious and cannot have silly fun. Taking life too critically or being concerned how you will appear to others, are going to be regrets you face at the end of your life, if you let such thoughts hinder your happiness now.

> 有的时候，犯傻和贪玩也还是相当令人愉悦的。你最好应当允许你自己偶尔犯傻和贪玩。另外，不必通过酗酒和吸毒也同样可以获得生活的乐趣。没有什么规定成年人必须要很严肃地生活，不应该有犯傻的娱乐。如果你现在把生活中的有些事情看得太严重以及太注重自己在别人眼里的形象，并让这些思想阻碍着你获得快乐，那么，你在临终的时候必将感到遗憾和后悔。

Of course, your perspective makes a huge difference to happiness, as beautiful Lenny showed. Despite the losses in his life, he focused on the gifts he had received and saw his life as a good one. The same view you look at every day, the same life, can become something brand new by focusing on its gifts rather than the negative aspects. Perspective is your own choice and the best way to shift that perspective is through gratitude, by acknowledging and appreciating the positives.

> 当然，如同美丽的莱尼所表示的那样，你的视角对快乐有明显不同的诠释。尽管他在生活中经历了很多的失去，但他依然在乎他得到的上帝赐予的礼物，认为自己拥有一个好的生活。通过把注意力集中在生活中好的一面而不是坏的一面可以让你感觉以往相同的生活和每天所看到的同样环境变得有所不同，似乎一切都焕然一新。拥有什么样的视角由你自己选择，而转换视角的最佳方法是心存感激，承认现实，以乐观态度应对人生。

Despite the many regrets dying people shared with me, in the very end, each of them found their peace. Some were not able to forgive themselves until their last couple of days but they did manage it before they passed. Many experienced a variety of emotions leading up to this, including denial, fear, anger, remorse, and the worst, self-condemnation. Many also experienced positive feelings though, of love and immense joy for memories that surfaced as they lived through their final weeks.

> 尽管我所接触的那些临终之人与我分享了他们很多的遗憾和悔恨，然而，到了最后，他们每一个人都找到了各自的平静。有些人一直不能原谅自己，直到生命的最后几天，经过他们的努力，在他们过世以前终于做到了自我原谅。很多人在临终之前经历了各种不同的情绪，其中包括否认、害怕、愤怒、悔恨以及最严重的自我谴责。但仍有许多人经历了乐观、充满爱的感受，在生命的最后阶段享受着往日的回忆给他们带来的巨大喜悦。

Before the very end though, they found a peaceful acceptance that their time had come and they were able to forgive themselves for the regrets they expressed, regardless of how tormented they had been. It was imperative to many of these clients however, that others learned through their regrets. They were all people who had been given the time to contemplate their lives. Those who go suddenly do not have such a luxury, and many of us will be amongst those too.

> 在他们最后几日里，无论他们以前饱受了怎样的人间折磨，他们最后都能够平和地接受离开人世的时间已经来临，也能原谅自己流露出的种种遗憾。不过，他们说出遗憾或者悔恨也是很有必要的，其他的人可以从他们的教训中学到东西，可以引以为戒。他们不是在瞬间结束自己的生命，都是有一定的时间来审视自己的人生。那些在意外中突然去世的人们没有这种福分，我们当中很多人也会成为他们中的一员。

It is so important to consider the life you are living now, as there may be little time given at your passing to find your peace or for any contemplation. Instead, you will die knowing that you have spent your whole life chasing happiness through the wrong channels; with it always eluding you, always just out of your grasp, always depending on the right things or situations to come your way. You will die knowing that the opportunity to change your direction well before it was too late just slipped on by.

> 因此，现在认真思考你的生活显得非常的重要，因，也许你在离开人世的时候没有时间寻找你的平静或者没有任何的人生反省机会。反倒是你将带着许多的迷惑和后悔而离去，你只知道你在一些虚假的通道里用毕生精力去追逐快乐，而始终未能达成心愿，永远得不到你想得到的快乐和幸福；你只知道本来在时已晚之前有机会改变自己的人生方向，只是自己不知不觉地错过了良机。

The peace each of these dear people found before their passing is available now, without having to wait until your final hours. You have the choice to change your life, to be courageous and to live a life true to your heart, one that will see you pass without regret. Kindness and forgiveness are a great starting point. Not just to others, but to yourself as well.

> 每一位可爱的人在他们去世之前所寻找到的那种平静其实现在就摆在我们的面前，我们不必等到临终的时候才觉得应该拥有它。你有改变生活的选择，你要拿出勇气，按照自己的真正愿望去生活，做一个没有遗憾而离去的人。仁慈和谅解是最重要的起点，不仅针对别人，也是针对你自己。

Forgiving yourself is also such a necessary component for this process. Without it, you continue to add fertiliser to the existing bad seeds in your mind by being hard on yourself, as I once did too. But self-forgiveness and kindness weakens the strength of these seeds. Healthier ones replace them and grow stronger, in time overshadowing the old seeds until there is nothing left to sustain their growth.

> 原谅自己也是寻求快乐的必要组成部分，否则的话，由于对自己太苛刻，你将继续在你的大脑土壤为不良种子施肥，我曾经就是这样的。自我谅解和仁慈削弱了那些不良种子的势力。从而，更加健康的新种子及时地让旧种子枯萎，并取代了旧种子而茁壮成长，直到最后那些老旧种子完全无法维持生长。

The bravery needed to change your life is easier to find when you are kind on yourself. Good things take time too, so patience is also required. Every single one of us is an amazing person with a potential limited only by our own thinking. We are all amazing.

> 当你善待自己的时候，你更加容易寻找到改变自己生活所需的那份勇敢。美好成果的获得也需要一定的时间，因此，心急吃不了热豆腐，耐心也是需要的。我们每一个人都具有惊人的潜力，我们只有想不到的，没有做不到的。我们都是很棒的人。

When you think of the numerous environmental and genetic influences that have shaped you, including the genes that have come to you through your own unique biology, it makes you a pretty amazing and special person. All of your life experiences so far, both good and bad, also contribute to you being unlike any other person on this planet. You are already special. You are already unique.

> 环境和遗传因素的巨大影响塑造了你，其中遗传基因造就了你的唯一生物特性，使得你成为一个令人惊叹和特殊的人。到目前止，你的所有人生经历，不管是好的或者是不好的都造就了你在这个地球上是独一无二的。你天生已经具有某种特殊性和唯一性。

It is time to realise your own worth and to realise the worth of others. Lay your judgments down. Be kind on yourself and be kind on others. As no one has ever truly walked in another’s shoes, seen through another’s eyes, or felt through another’s heart for their whole life, no one knows just how much each other has suffered either. A little bit of empathy goes a long way.

> 现在你该知道了你的自我价值，也知道了别人的价值。放下你的那些主观评判吧，善待自己也善待别人。世上从来没有人一生里能够完全理解别人的处境；能够以别人的眼光看问题，或者能够感受到别人的内心，也没有人知道彼此之间遭受了多少的痛苦。一点点的同情心会给人们带来很大的帮助。

By being kind to others and tossing your judgment out the window, you are being kind to yourself too by planting better seeds. Forgive yourself for blaming others for your unhappiness. Learn to be gentle on yourself, accepting your own humanness and frailty. Forgive others too who have blamed you for their unhappiness. We are all human. We have all said and done things that could have been otherwise done in a kinder way.

> 通过善待别人和抛开你的主观评判等于在你的心田里播种了更美好的种子，最终也就等于善待了你自己。原谅自己责怪了别人，原谅自己没有快乐感。学会对自己温和，接受自己的本性和缺点。也原谅别人对你的责怪，原谅别人的不快乐。我们都是普通的人。我们原本所说过的话和所做过的事都可以以另外更加友善的方式去完成。

Life is over so quickly. It is possible to reach the end with no regrets. It takes some bravery to live it right, to honour the life you are here to live but the choice is yours. So will be the rewards. Appreciate the time you have left by valuing all of the gifts in your life and that includes especially, your own, amazing self.

> 光阴似箭，无怨无悔地度过一生也不是不可能的。要完全做到这一点需要一些勇气，遵守你的生活原则也需要勇气，然而，一切的选择权都在你的手中，所有的福报也在你的手中。重视你生活中的所有福音，珍惜你的生命，尤其珍惜独一无二、令人惊叹的自我。
