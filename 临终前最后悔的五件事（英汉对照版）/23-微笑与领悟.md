# Smile and know · 微笑与领悟

When I look at my life now, there are moments that still blow me away. The life I imagined becomes more reality every day. The person I had imagined is now me. It came about through courage, resilience, discipline, and through learning to love my own heart. Life can actually be easy and joyous. It can in fact flow well. Even better is that as I have continued to adjust and grow, continuing to accept that I deserve all that comes my way, the more easily things keep flowing.

> 当我审视我现在的生活，发现依然经常有遭受打击的时候。我原来想象的那种生活如今一天天变得更加的实实在在。我曾经想象的那个人现正是我自己。通过鼓起勇气、提高应变能力、自我训练以及学会爱护自己的内心等方法的应用，我找寻到了自我。我发现，生活实际上并不是那么的艰难，也充满愉悦，而且生活也可以做到一帆风顺。甚为令人满意的是，随着我继续进行自我调节和获得自我成长以及继续接受自己值得拥有，我生活中的一切都更加持续和顺利。

One little phrase kept my faith strong through that final, dark period: Smile and Know. On one particularly hard day, my old thinking was holding on for dear life and telling me I didn’t deserve all I had dreamed of. My new thinking meanwhile was trying to move in permanently, reassuring me I did. So I prayed for some very simple and clear guidance, something that would not be hard to remember in my tearful state, in order to conquer the hard days.

> 短语“微笑与领悟”让我在最黑暗时期坚守了信念和信心。在某个特别难熬的一天，我的陈旧思想拼命地拽住我不放，还对我说我不值得拥有我所有的梦想。与其同时，我的新思想正试图永久性控制局面，并安抚我说我值得拥有。因此，为了征服黑暗，我拼命祈祷希望得到某些非常简单而明了的指导。

I needed something to keep me strong and with hope whenever I was able. The words Smile and Know came to me. I wrote these words down and put them in obvious places around the home. Whenever I walked past them, a commitment with myself was honoured and I would Smile and Know that this time would pass and good would follow.

> 我需要一些东西让我保持坚强，保持某种希望——无论何时我希望能够征服危难，我就能做到。“微笑与领悟”随之就这样产生了。我把这几个字写下来，把它们贴在家里显眼的地方。无论何时当我看到它们，我就自觉地遵守我的这个承诺，我会“微笑与领悟”这样的黑暗将会过去，美好的日子将会到来。

It is also much easier to be strong in faith when you are smiling. So it would automatically lift my mood and reassure me that I would indeed find more reasons to smile again. There was no point reading the words without actually smiling though, as the smiling itself allowed the knowing to be easier. So smile I did.

> 当你保持微笑，你的自信心也非常容易得到提升。我的情绪自动变得高亢，安抚自己的确可以再次寻找到更多微笑因子。没有实际的微笑不可能读懂这几个字的含义，只有在微笑的时候才更加容易获得领悟。所以，我笑了。

Later, I added underneath Thank and Know, ensuring prayers of gratitude were said in advance, with confidence and faith that it was all coming to me. Smile and Know, Thank and Know, became my mantra as I went about my days smiling and knowing when I could. As I did so, I was walking in complete faith, which left me naturally wanting to thank as well. My prayers, dreams and intentions had already been heard. My only job was to Smile and Know and to Thank and Know. Naturally this enabled me to smile much more than I would have otherwise.

> 后来我又在“微笑与领悟”的下面添加了“感恩与领悟”，已确定人们所说的祈祷之前先要感恩，随后的自信和信念才会通通向我走来。“微笑与领悟”和“感恩与领悟”成为了我的座右铭，我微笑着度过每一天，并在可能的时候领悟事理。当我这样做到了，我轻易达到完全的自信，这也让我自然地想要去感恩。我所有的祈祷、梦想和意图已经被上帝听到了。我唯一要做的就是“微笑与领悟”“感恩与领悟”。

Of course, there were times when I was not strong enough to draw on these words, including that final day of utter sadness and resignation. But that time of surrender was the ultimate turning point. It was true I could not live with the pain of my past anymore and I was right in a sense. It was the end of my life, as I knew it at least. But I didn’t have to die physically. Only that old part of myself died, spiritually.

> 当然，我时常也有不够坚强去执行这些座右铭的时候，比如那一天因极度悲伤准备书写遗书。然而，在我屈服的那一刻是我人生的终极转折点。千真万确，我不可能再背负着过去的伤痛继续生活下去了，我的感觉是正确的。我明白，那一天是我生命终结的日子，我的意思不是说我的生物躯体必定死亡，而仅仅是指我精神上那部分陈旧的自我死去了。

Those old ideas of myself could not survive the bright light of my own love. The new life that had been quietly manifesting for years was finally able to be born. While I was smiling and knowing, my dreams felt real and became even more a part of me. That’s why the doors of opportunity swung open when I was finally able to realise my own worth.

> 在强烈的自爱明亮之光的照射之下，我的那些只能在阴暗角落里才能存活的自我观念上的旧框架必死无疑。我的新生命经过几年悄悄的孕育期之后终于降临于世。当我一边微笑和一边领悟的同时，我感觉到我的那些梦想成为了现实，并更加纳入真实自我的一部分。那就是为什么说当我终于能够意识到自己的价值所在的时候，机遇的大门突然向我打开。

The dreams had already arrived and were simply waiting for me to let them come through. So it was with a joyous heart that I opened up, allowing things to flow. They did in many and various forms: personally and professionally. Some time later as I sat in delighted shock at the suggestion of my dear, beautiful parents to have a vegan Christmas, I smiled wholeheartedly, having just been given the best Christmas present in the world.

> 我的梦想已经在我心中酝酿出来，只是等待着我将把它们一个个变成现实。因此，我喜悦地敞开胸襟，心怀坦荡，打开思路，任凭思维无限奔放。它们以个人特性和专业技术等各种不同的形式充分展现了出来。一段时间之后，当我得知我可爱美丽的父母建议今年过一个素食主义的圣诞节的消息时，我高兴得快要昏倒了，我真心实意地微笑了，因为我得到了这个世界上最为珍贵的圣诞礼物。

For more than two decades I had dreamed of at least a vegetarian Christmas. When it finally came about, it did so with such natural ease that we all agreed it was one of the most beautiful Christmas days we’d ever experienced. With my mum chopping vegetables beside me, sharing cheek and laughter, my dad sorted out the music. Country tunes from the 1950’s floated through the home as we all laughed, chatted, and prepared a great feast. It was joyous and easy.

> 我梦想有一个全素食的圣诞节至少已经超过二十年了。当这一天终于到来的时候，我们一家人都非常自然地准备圣诞晚餐，都感觉这是我们所经历的最美好的圣诞节之一。我和妈妈在一起边切蔬菜，边讲悄悄话并一同大笑，我爸爸选出一些音乐来播放。五十年代的乡村音乐曲调在整个家里回响着，我们都在谈笑声中准备了一顿丰富的圣诞晚餐。那真的是轻松和喜悦的一天。

My work continues to grow and thrive, bringing satisfaction and enjoyment. While it is possible to find work you love in being employed by others, in the times we live in, the best way forward for me is to work for myself. That is what I needed and wanted most anyway, to live life my way, including my work-life. High levels of motivation and amazing clarity accompanied me onto my new plane of existence, along with the best from the old life, including self-discipline.

> 我的作品继续得到提高和发展，给我带来满足感和无比的享受。尽管你有可能找到一份自己喜爱的为别人打工的工作，但在我们生活的那个年代，对我来说最好的工作方式是给自己打工。那是我最需要和最希望的自我工作和生活方式。连同旧生命中最好的优点，比如自律一起，高标准的行为动机和惊人的清晰思维陪伴我登上了我自己的新的人生飞船。

Making contacts in the local area, meetings were set up all over the place. Inspiration and ideas poured forth. Excitement built as I re-entered the world, creating new and positive opportunities for myself. Through a couple of community groups, I taught some songwriting workshops to disadvantaged sectors of society. Teaching again, and being my own boss, was lovely and of course, watching the transformation of the people in the classes was hugely rewarding.

> 我在当地联络了一些人，并在各个地点设定了几个见面会。灵感和各种点子像打开闸门的洪水喷涌而出。极度的兴奋使我重新融入了这个世界，为自己创造出新的积极机会。通过几个社区团体的帮助，我为一些社会弱势阶层举办了一些歌曲创作工作坊。重新教课并成为老板是非常美妙的，当然，看到课堂上人们的那些良好的转变也给我带来巨大的奖励和鼓舞。

After the seriousness of my past, it was time for more joy in my work as well. So I created a children’s show, performing to children under five years old. Watching these delightful, uninhibited, little humans dancing and jumping around to my new songs has been delightful. Writing opportunities also flowed, as did a new album’s worth of adult songs. It amazes me of what we are truly capable of creatively and physically when we let go of all that has held us back.

> 我过去的工作和生活太过于严肃，现在我该尽情地享受工作中的轻松愉快。因此，我创办了一个儿童表演秀，专门对五岁以下的儿童进行表演时使用。望着那些讨人喜欢、天真可爱的小孩子们在我的新歌曲的伴奏之下蹦蹦跳跳，我感到无比的喜悦。当我出版发行了另外一张有价值的新的成人歌曲专辑时，我写歌的邀约更加频繁。令我感到惊讶的是当我们放下了所有那些曾经阻挡过我们进步的种种障碍的时候，我们真的具有无限的创作能力。

My blog experienced further massive waves, bringing more people to my work. I also created a happy and positive range of T-shirts, bumper stickers, and tote bags, drawing on lines from my songs and articles. Not only did the ideas flow forth, but motivated action also accompanied.

> 我的网络博客刮起了更巨大的一波高潮，更多的人阅读了我的作品。我也创作了一系列具有快乐和正面积极意义的T恤衫、车子保险杠贴纸、大手提袋，上面画上我的歌谱和文章片断。我的创作灵感源源不断地迸发。

As I now share my autumn nights snuggled up with a beautiful man, I smile at how much life can change. He is a dear person. There were things we both had to let go of, before we could find each other but timing is an amazing thing. Life is now lived from new perspectives.

> 在这深秋的夜晚里，我与一位漂亮的男人相互依偎在一起，我笑看自己的生活到底还有多少可能的改变。他是一位和蔼可亲的人。我们俩在认识之前，双方都有丰富的人生经历，都放下了许多陈旧的东西，我们相识的时间点非常的奇妙。我们现在以新的心态和视角对待自己的生活。

In the very best of ways I have been reminded about the cycles of life. Death has certainly been shown to me directly through others. I have known my own kind of death too now though, by watching that old part of me finally cease to exist.

> 而所幸的是，我已经明白人类生命的周期。通过以往照顾临终客户，死亡已经很明确地直接展现在我面前。通过见证，我的陈旧自我部分终究不复存在，我也已经领教到自身死亡的过程。

It was a spiritual death, a death of a part of me that had controlled me for decades. It was also the birth of a new spirit, one that I had always suspected was there, one that I wished to be. It was a painful death, yet it was what truly freed me from the conditioning of my past, of unnecessary burdens, of all that held me back.

> 这是一种灵性死亡，是已经控制了我几十年的那部分精神的消失。同时也是一种崭新精神的诞生，那是我总猜想早就已存在我心，并一直希望表现于我身的一种高境界灵魂。推陈出新是一个痛苦过程，然而却使我从我的过去、从那些不必要的精神包袱以及从所有阻挡我前进的种种障碍中彻底地解放了出来。

With the real me now allowed to live unhindered, I continue to evolve into who I really am. It is only through shedding what I have that I am able to actually know who that is and I love her. I love her courage. I love her heart. I love her creativity. I love her mind. I love her body. I love her kindness. I love all of her.

> 如今，随着真实的自我引导我顺利地生活，逐渐让自己变得更加真实。只有通过脱胎换骨的我才能够实际认识到我是谁，才能够达到爱自己的这个她。我爱她的勇气、爱她的心怀、爱她的创造力、爱她的心智、爱她的躯体、爱她的善良，爱她的一切的一切。

Life moves in new directions. It is a new beginning, a new birth of myself. In the best possible way I have also been reminded of other new beginnings. A precious baby now grows within me. I have been blessed with the opportunity to become a mother.

> 生活向着崭新的方向迈进。这是我新生命的开端。有一种最好的好事提醒了我，我的另外一段新的人生开端已经出现在我的面前。一个珍贵的胎儿正在我的身体中孕育着。上帝赐予了我将会成为一个母亲。

As my womb expands and my body swells in the divinity of womanhood, I am in bliss and overwhelming gratitude to know such an experience. It is a world away from the life I once knew – the isolation, the sadness, the hopelessness. Yet again, I am reminded of just how much we can fit into one lifetime. Thank goodness I did not end my life when I thought to. Thank goodness.

> 随着子宫的膨胀，我的整个身体像完美女神那样丰满起来。我很幸福和十足的感恩能有这样一种母性的体验。我曾经熟悉的那种孤单、悲伤和没有希望的生活将离我远去。我再一次明白了人的一生中可以有很多适合自己的改变。谢天谢地，幸亏当初我没有结束自己的生命。

The bond between mother and child grows daily. I have also been blessed with great health throughout, much to the curse of other poor pregnant women suffering morning sickness. I absolutely love being pregnant and soon will be guiding another soul through their human journey until they are old enough to fly in directions of their own choice. Life may certainly have its share of death and endings but it has its share of birth and beginnings too. I am grateful to have been exposed to both, literally and symbolically, on so many occasions.

> 日复一日，母亲与胎儿一起茁壮成长。我很庆幸，整个怀孕过程还算顺利，比那些有严重妊娠反应的可怜孕妇们要好得多。我绝对的喜爱成为一名孕妇，并很乐意马上就要引导另外一个灵魂，在他的人生旅途初期给予必要的教导，直到他长大成人能足够为自己的人生方向作出选择为止。你的生活确实与你分享了你的死亡和结束的过程，但也分享了你的出生和新生命的开始过程。从某种实际和象征意义来说，我感激我在许多情况下经历和分享了这两种过程。

Whenever out on a leap of faith, things have never turned out as I had imagined, but in the long run, things have always turned out better. Faith is a powerful force and one that creates incredible blessings. Letting go of limitations and of trying to control how things will flow is an immense gift to one’s self.

> 当无论何时信仰猛增的时候，也许事情看起来从未按照自己所想象的那样发生改变，但是从长远来看，事情最终总是变得更好。信仰有着巨大的力量，它能创造令人不可思议的种种福音。能舍弃生活中的那些条条框框和试图控制事物如何自然流露的障碍是一个人获得的天赐的巨大礼物。

Strangely one of the hardest things for many, as it was for me, is to learn how to receive, to realise you deserve it, then allow the goodness to flow. Most of the miraculous solutions that have flowed to me throughout my life have also come via other people. We are all much more inter-connected than we acknowledge, and play bigger roles in each other’s lives than we know.

> 我跟很多人一样，对我们来说最难以做到的事情之一就是学会如何去接受，去明白自己值得受人恩惠，然后允许这种好事自然地出现在你的面前。不可思议的是，我在生活中已经获得的大多数神奇的解决问题的办法也都是在别人的帮助下产生的。我们人与人之间的相互关联性其实远远比我们所意识到的那样还多很多，这种相互关联在人们彼此的生活中所扮演的角色比我们所知道的也更加重要。

So learning to receive is a necessity if you are truly open to seeing your dreams realised. As any person who is a natural giver knows, there is great pleasure in giving. But if you are a giver without allowing yourself to also receive, then not only are you blocking the natural flow of things to you and creating an imbalance, you are robbing someone else of the pleasure of giving. So allow others to give too. It is only pride or lack of self-worth that stops anyone from being able to receive and every single one of us deserves such goodness.

> 因此，如果你真的睁开眼睛想看到自己的梦想是否得以实现，那么，学会接纳别人的帮助是必不可少的。如同任何一位施舍者都懂得的那样，给予是一种巨大的快乐。但是，如果你只知道当一名施舍者，而不让自己接纳别人的恩惠，那么，你不仅阻挡了你生活中某些自然事物的流通，制造了施与受的不平衡，同时也掠夺了别人施舍的那种愉快感。所以，我们也要允许别人去施舍。只有傲慢或缺乏自我价值感的人才会阻挡任何人接纳别人的恩惠，事实上，我们每个人都值得接纳别人的慈悲关怀。

If you are one who does not know how to actually give, then keep practicing. Just try it without expectation. It feels good. Just give for the pleasure of giving. To do so with accountability is not true giving though, nor is giving then reminding people of it later in anger. Waiting for the good to flow back to you after giving is not it in its truest sense either.

> 如果你还不知道如何实际的施舍，就请你多在实践中学习。尽量不要希望得到任何回报的去施舍。那将给你带来美好的感觉。尽量做到施舍的快乐而施舍。带着某种责任感的施舍不是真正的施舍，虽然你施舍了，但后来在生气时又向接纳人提起你的施舍也不是真正的施舍。你在施舍之后，又等待着别人给予好的回报当然也不是真正意义上的施舍。

But giving with the sole intention of giving, whether that is in love, kindness, or action is where true pleasure comes in. And yes, those who give with this intention are rewarded, but not always immediately and not necessarily in ways you imagine. But you also need to know how to receive too, allowing the flow to go both ways. Of course, this includes giving and receiving with your own self too.

> 无论付出的是爱、善良或者任何行动，只有纯粹的施舍才能获得真正的快乐。是的，那些只是为施舍而施舍的人将会得到很多的好处和回报，那些好处不一定都会立即显示出来，也不是以你想象方式回报与你。尽管这样，你依然需要懂得如何接纳别人的施舍，要允许自己施与受并进。当然，这里面也包括了你对自己的施与受。

It is possible to change the world and us. As we improve our own lives, and work toward having no regrets, we naturally improve the lives of all around us. It is possible to reverse the segregation and disharmony we have created in society. It is possible to be happy. It is possible to work towards dying without regrets while still alive and well.

> 我们有可能改变这个世界和我们自己。每当我们改善了我们自己的生活，对人生无怨无悔，自然地我们也就改善了我们周围人的生活。我们有可能消除种族隔离和我们自己建造的社会不和谐。我们有可能成快乐的人。我们也有可能在还活得好好的时候，不会带着任何后悔而走向死亡。

We are all fragile in our own way, like globes of delicate glass. Imagine the old light bulb with the roundish glass surrounding the globe. (It doesn’t quite create the same image if you imagine the new energy-saving tube-like light globes, though either will suffice). A part of all of us is like a delicate light globe. A beautiful light shines from within, one that can remove darkness from any place.

> 我们每个人都有自己脆弱的一面，就像一些易碎的玻璃球。想象那老式的电灯泡，周围是圆形的玻璃（如果你想象的是现代那种节能灯管的电灯泡就不符合这个比喻）。我们每个人身上的一部分如同一个脆弱的电灯泡。一道道美丽的光从灯泡里面发出，可以照亮每个黑暗的角落。

When we are born, we are shining bright, bringing great light and happiness to all. People marvel at our beauty and light. Then over time, muck starts being thrown at us. This muck is not about us. It is theirs, the people who are throwing it.

> 当我们出生的时候，我们光芒四射，给我们周围的所有人带来光明和幸福。人们因我们的美丽和光彩照人而感到惊叹。然后，随着时间的推移，淤泥或污物开始向我们身上涌来。那些淤泥不是我们自己的，是别人的，是别人扔给我们的。

But they hurl it at us anyway. After a while, it is not only those close to us who throw muck on us. It is school friends, workmates, society, and many of whom we come across. It affects us all differently, some become victims, some bullies, some take it in and it stays within for a long time, some appear to let it go naturally. Regardless of how it appears to affect each of us, it still hinders our original light and goodness from shining at its absolute best.

> 他们用力猛向我们投来淤泥。久而久之，不仅是接近我们的人往我们身上扔淤泥，而且我们学校的朋友、工作的同事、社会人士，以及许多我们所遇见的各色人等都向我们身上扔淤泥。这使得我们成为了各不相同的人，有的成为了受害者，有的成了横行霸道的人，有的人接纳了淤泥，并让它们保存在自己身上很长时间，还有的似乎就让淤泥自然的放下。无论淤泥怎样地影响着我们每个人的生活，它们依然妨碍了我们在最光彩照人的时刻展现我们原有的光辉和美丽。

With so many people throwing muck on us, we figure that they must be right. So we join in, throwing muck on ourselves too. Why not? All of the muck throwers can’t be wrong after all. If I am going to throw muck on myself, then it must be normal and OK to throw muck on others too.

> 随着无数的人都向我们扔来淤泥，我们推测他们必定是正确的。因此，我们也加入他们的行列，也向自己身上扔淤泥。力什么不呢？毕竟所有那些扔淤泥的人不可能错嘛。如果我向自己扔淤泥，那才必定是正常的行为，同样的，我也可以向别人扔淤泥。

Yes, I’ll throw some more and I’ll continue to let others throw it on me. Eventually, you are carrying so much muck that not only are you weighed down by it, but also your light cannot be seen at all anymore. Every inch of you is covered in muck, a lot of it from what others have thrown and some from when you joined in and started throwing it on yourself too.

> 结果，我将对自己扔来更多的淤泥，也继续让别人对我扔淤泥。最终，你身上携带了无数的淤泥，以至于你不仅因此而被拖垮了身体，而且你仅有的一丝光芒也完全地荡然无存。你身上的每一寸地方都被淤泥所覆盖，大多数的淤泥是别人扔给你的，有些则是你后来加入到他们的行列，自己扔给自己的。

Then one day you remember there was once a beautiful light shining in you. But things have been dark for so long, you hardly remember that part of yourself. It can still be felt sometimes though, when you are quiet and alone. The warm glow has remained shining all this time, regardless of the darkness surrounding it. You realise that you want to shine again. You want to remember who you are when you’re not carrying other people’s muck on you, or your own.

> 随后，某一天你猛然想起你身上曾经有过灿烂的光辉，美丽动人。但是，你成为一个淤泥之人已经有太长的时间，你几乎记不得曾经辉煌的那部分你。有的时候当你在安静地独处之时，你可以感觉得到自己具备那些耀眼的光芒。在这段时间里，无论它的周围有多么的黑暗，那温暖的热光还在里面持续地闪烁。当你不再携带别人或者自己扔给你的那些淤泥的时候，你希望恢复你的记忆，回忆起你到底是谁。

So you start saying you’ve had enough. You stop allowing anyone else to throw muck on you. People don’t like this. But you are determined and move out of reach of the muck throwers. Slowly you start rubbing very gently to remove some of it off you. But it has to be done very tenderly, as you are incredibly fragile underneath. If you try to do it too roughly or hurriedly, you will shatter and never know your light again.

> 因此，你开始大声喊出你身上的淤泥已经够多的了。你停止让别人对你扔淤泥。虽然他们反对，但是你下定了决心要远离那些扔淤泥的人。你开始缓慢而温和地擦洗你的身子，把某些淤泥清除掉。然而，你必须很温柔地清洗淤泥，因为那些淤泥的下面是非常脆弱的玻璃球。假如你试图很粗莽或者匆忙的清洗淤泥，你将粉碎那个玻璃球，再也不可能了解到你的光芒所在。

So you slowly and patiently work away at cleaning it off. A tiny ray of light breaks through and you are given a glimpse of your own beauty again. It feels good. Then someone throws some more muck at you and you have to start removing it again. So you wipe that bit off and clean some more away.

> 所以，你要慢慢地、有耐心地、持续不断地一点一点清洗它们。一道微光开始穿透出来，你又重新窥见到自己的美丽。这样的感觉美妙极了。随后，某些人会扔更多的淤泥给你，你必须开始再次清洗它们。所以，你擦去一点点，更多干净的地方出现了。

Frightened by what you see though, you then throw some at yourself. You don’t deserve to shine this brightly. Here have some more muck. But the light has caught a glimpse of the outside again and starts to shine brighter. It wants to be seen.

> 于是你被所见的光芒吓坏了，赶快向自己扔些淤泥。你觉得自己不值得闪烁光芒。你又扔更多的淤泥。但是，今日已不同于往日，那耀眼的光芒已经重见天日，并开始闪亮登场。它希望被人所见。

With each bit of light that starts shining out, you start feeling better. It gives you a taste of how great it would feel to be free of all that you are carrying. This makes you recognise just how much everyone else is also carrying and you feel compassion. You decide that from now on you are not going to throw any more muck on others.

> 随着每一道道光束开始光芒四射，你的自我感觉开始变得好一些。你身上散发出的光亮让你尝到了没有那些淤泥携带，你的感觉是多么的美好。同时，也使得你认识到了还有很多的人们依然携带各自的淤泥，你为此非常同情他们。于是，你决定从现在开始你再也不向任何人扔淤泥。

After all, how can we all be shining at our best if we keep throwing it all over place and on each other? So you get back to work on yourself and very gently rub a little more off. It takes a lot of patience and gentleness, working on a little bit at a time. But excitement builds each time another pocket of light breaks through and you catch another glimpse of your own beauty and radiance.

> 毕竟，如果我们不断处处互相扔淤泥，我们又怎么可以达到最光亮的闪烁？因此，你应该回头多清洗自己身上的淤泥，非常温柔地一点一点的擦洗。这需要相当有耐性和动作温和，一次一点地擦洗，不能急躁。从而，每一次新的光束穿透而出都会让你兴奋不已，你再次目睹了自己的美丽和光辉。

At times you are tempted to throw some more back on yourself or others, as you have been in the habit of it for almost all of your life. But now you see how the little bits of light shining out of you are helping others, who are also becoming more courageous. They start cleaning off some of their own muck. They have to be very gentle too, as everyone is delicate and fragile underneath and can shatter very easily. You also want to help others clean theirs off. But they have to do it themselves, as no one but them knows just how fragile they are underneath.

> 由于你几乎在整个一生中已经习惯了被别人或者自己扔淤泥，时常你又抵挡不住诱惑，对自己或者他人扔淤泥。而现在你看到了你一点点的光芒重现是如何的帮助别人，也让他们变得更加的勇政。从而他们开始清洗自己身上的淤泥。他们也必须很温和地擦洗，因为每个人都是脆弱的，淤泥下面都是易碎的玻璃球，很容易破碎。虽然你希望去帮助别人擦洗他们身上的淤泥，但是，他们必须靠自己去完成，因除了他们自己没有人可以知道在那些淤泥之下，他们的玻璃球有多么的脆弱。

You can show others how you did it and maybe that will help them. But they have to do the work themselves, at their own pace, and in their own way. And of course, not everyone has the courage or strength to do it all at once. So you are patient, respectful, and compassionate, as you now understand that it can sometimes be a very painful and frightening experience.

> 你可以展示给他们看，你是如何擦洗淤泥的，也许这样才会帮助到他们。但是他们还是得靠自己去做，以他们自己的方式和步调来完成。当然，不是每个人都有勇气和力量马上做得到这一点。所以，你要有耐心，尊重和同情他们，因你现在懂得了有的时候，这将是非常痛苦和可怕的经历。

You feel good in yourself. It is a new feeling but you like it a lot. So you give up throwing muck on yourself forever, because you are beginning to love the beauty you have discovered, as your light continues to shine brighter. There are rays of light coming out of you at all angles now.

> 你自我感觉良好。虽然这是新感受，可你非常喜欢这种感觉。结果你永远不再对自己扔淤泥，因为你开始爱上你所发现的这种美丽，你身上的玻璃球继续明亮闪烁。那些光线从你身上不同的角度向外反射。

But some of the oldest muck is still very stuck on you, making it the hardest to remove. It had grown very comfy here over the decades, thank you very much. It doesn’t want to go anywhere. The closer you are to the glass, the more delicately you have to rub. Yet the more stubborn and determined the muck is at this level.

> 然而，某些陈旧的淤泥依然牢牢地粘在你的身上，很难把它们清除掉。它们很舒服的依附在那里几十年，对你感激不尽。它们哪儿都不想去，只想跟你永远在一起。你越是接近这个玻璃球表面，越是更多的淤泥需要你小心翼翼地擦洗，因为更加顽固和坚硬的淤泥就在这个层面。

It has been such a big job and you are very weary. You are already an improvement on who you were, definitely. Maybe this will be enough. Maybe I can live with this last layer of muck and just shine as much as I am now. But the light is strong and determined too. It wants you to shine at your brightest. So it gives you even more strength and you keep cleaning away the last of it.

> 这是一件大工程，你感到非常的疲惫。不过，你已经比较清楚地看到了你是谁。也许你觉得这就足够了。也许你可以与最后一层淤泥共同生活，尽量按你现有的光芒而闪烁。但是，那玻璃球的热光也很强烈和坚定，它们希望你发出最耀眼的光芒。因此，它们给予你更多的力量，让你继续清洗下去直到除掉最后一点淤泥。

Finally, you have done it and your brightness astonishes everyone, especially yourself. You had no idea you could be so beautiful and shine with such radiance. Now when you hang out with other light globes, they too want to shine brightly as they can see your beauty and it reminds them that they too have such potential inside of them. They had just forgotten it with all of the muck they were also carrying.

> 你终于做到了，把身上的淤泥全部擦洗干净，你发出来的光辉令每个人都大为惊讶，也包括你自己。你原来都不知道你可以如此的美丽和如此的光彩照人。当你现在跟其他电灯泡相聚的时候，你在它们面前展现的美丽也提醒了它们，原来在它们玻璃球里面也有如此的潜能，只是因为淤泥缠身而忽略了。它们也希望像你那样闪烁光芒。

Some light globes think it is too hard to let their light show, so they stick together in the darkness, trying to convince themselves and each other that they are happy this way. Who needs all that hard work when we have become used to carrying our muck? Why, I like it this way, they say and I am going to throw some more muck around. I am going out there right now to throw some on those bright lights that are happy and having a good time out there. How dare they have so much enjoyment?

> 有些电灯泡认为要让它们展示出光亮太艰难，所以，它们宁愿在黑暗中相互粘在一起，并尽量说服自己它们这样也是快乐的。当它们已经习惯了携带淤泥，谁又需要大费周折去伤脑筋和伤体力呢？它们说：“我为什么要改变，我喜欢这样，我就是要向周围的灯泡扔淤泥。我现在就来向那些光芒四射、很快乐的灯泡扔淤泥，我会在扔淤泥中度过愉快的时光”。它们岂敢以这为乐？

So the dark globes head outside with all of the muck they can find and start throwing it. They work better in a team too, safety in numbers and all that. They can’t see as clearly anymore though, as everything is so bright with all of this cleaning going on. But they spot a few light globes that are now shining brightly and happily, as they have almost finished removing their muck. So the dark globes throw heaps of it at those bright ones. It doesn’t stick though. What’s going on? It used to always stick.

> 于是这些黑灯泡走了出来，携带所有它们可能寻找到的淤泥，开始向别的灯泡扔去。它们也知道作为一个团队能干得更好，更安全。它们再也看不明白周围的一切，看不明白每个灯泡在洗心革面之后非常的明亮。它们发现了几个几乎完成了清洗自身淤泥而变得光彩照人和快乐的亮灯泡。这些黑暗灯泡向那些亮灯泡扔去大堆的淤泥。淤泥完全没有粘上去，到底怎么呢？以前可不是这样的。

What they didn’t know was that even though the light was hidden for all of these years, it had still been growing inside. Now it shines so warmly and brightly that the muck will never stick again. It just slides off, without even leaving a mark or an impression anymore. Your own glow is like that. You have a light within you that is beautiful and potentially radiant.

> 这些黑灯泡们哪里知道尽管那些亮灯泡以前被淤泥覆盖了很多年，但它们的内心依然在成长。而如今，它们放射出的光芒非常的炽热和明亮，以至于淤泥将永远不再会黏附在它们身上。淤泥很快从灯泡的表面滑落，甚至再也不留一点点的痕迹或者印记。你自身就是这样。在你的体内存有一道美丽而具有潜能的辉煌亮光。

But you need patience and tenderness with yourself to remove all of the muck you have been carrying for decades. As each little bit is removed, a little more of your true self will shine through. Each of the regrets shared from the bedsides of those now dearly departed people, takes courage and love to conquer. But the choice is yours. Like a light that wants to shine brightly and cheerfully, you have a guidance within that will lead you through, one step at a time.

> 但是，你需要耐心和温柔慢慢地把已经依附在你身上几十年的淤泥彻底清洗掉。每擦掉一点淤泥，你的真实自我的光亮度就会增加一点。从那些如今已经去世的可爱人的床边所分享到的每一个后悔给了我勇气，使我喜爱征服黑暗。人生的所有选择完全在于你自己。如同一个希望明亮闪烁的电灯泡，你拥有了内心的指导，它将指引你逐次逐点的发出灿烂的光辉，一步一个脚印地向理想迈进。

Be who you are, find balance, speak honestly, value those you love, and allow yourself to be happy. If you do this, then you will not only be honouring yourself, but all of those who despaired in their final weeks, by not having had the courage to do so previously in their own lives. The choice is yours. Your life is your own.

> 请记住：做你自己、达到平衡、诚实表达、重视你所爱的人以及允许自己快乐。如果你做到了这些，那么，你不仅是尊重你自已，也是尊重那些在生命最后时期而感到绝望的人们，因为他们在自己以前的人生旅途中没有勇气做到这些。而你可以做得到，你有选择的可能性。你的生活你做主。

When the challenges are thrown your way and you are wondering how on Earth it will all come together, how you will find peace about a particular relationship, when the contacts you need will arrive, or how you will find the money to make something happen, just remember that what your heart wants, wants you too. You just have to get out of the way sometimes. Take what action you can and then let go. Get out of your own way.

> 当种种挑战绑架了你，而你纳闷究竟怎么啦，所有的不幸都集中到一起的时候，当你将如何从一段特别的人际关系中找到平和的时候，当你需要联络的人将来到你的身边，或者你将寻找到资金去实现你的梦想的时候，谨请记住，你心里所希望的也就是你所想要的。而有些时候你必须学会放弃坚持，尽可能采取行动，然后放下。不要固执，要学会舍弃你的陈旧方式。

Then when you find yourself in this position, stand tall, put your shoulders back, and take a deep and loving breath. Walk on being proud of who you already are, with full faith and confidence you deserve it, that your prayers have been heard, and that they are already on their way to you. And simply remember one little phrase. Smile and know. Just smile and know.

> 当你在这种状态之下发现了真正自我的时候，请昂首挺胸，双肩朝向后背，做一次深而轻松的呼吸。然后，带着自豪、十足的信心向前迈进。你的一切祈祷已经应验了，它们已经向着你走来。最后，请你记住这个短句：微笑与领悟。只有微笑才能真正的领悟人生。
