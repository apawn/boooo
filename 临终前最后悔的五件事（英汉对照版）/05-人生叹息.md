# Regret 1: I wish I’d had the courage to live a life true to myself, not the life others expected of me · 人生叹息

It took no time at all for Grace to become one of my favourite palliative clients. She was a tiny woman with a huge heart. This flowed on to her children; all of who were parents themselves by now, and equally beautiful people.

> 毫不费力，格雷斯很快就成为了我喜爱的姑息护理客户之一。她是一个情感极丰富的小女人。她把所有的爱都给了她的孩子们，现在他们都已经为人父母，个个都是很有出息的人。

Grace lived in a totally different part of the city, which was unusual for our clients. It was a suburban street like many others with no mansions looming on either side. My first impression was it would be a good street for a television series, as it oozed with family energy. The thing I most liked about Grace herself, and her family, is they were very down to earth and genuinely welcoming.

> 格雷斯生活在与这个城市截然不同的区域，跟我们的其他客户相比那是一个不寻常的地方。这是一条位于郊区的街道，如同其他的郊区街道一样，两边没有赫然耸立的高楼大厦。由于这里洋溢着浓厚的家庭生活气息，我对这条街的第一印象是这里可以是拍电视连续剧的一个好场景。我最喜欢格雷斯和她家人的原因是他们都脚踏实地、非常的平凡和真诚地欢迎我。

My early days with her began as usually happened with clients, sharing stories to get to know each other. Familiar comments were heard in the bathroom about the loss of dignity Grace was experiencing, with someone else having to wipe her bum, and how a nice young thing like me shouldn’t have to do such an awful job. I became used to that part of my work though and tried to lighten up the situation for Grace and for all of my clients by making no fuss of it.

> 跟以往其他客户一样，我刚开始与格雷斯相处时也都是以分享彼此的往事来增进相互的了解。在她的卫浴间里常常听到那些熟悉的抱怨，例如，因行动不便，格雷斯失去尊严不得不由别人帮她擦屁股、像我这样年轻的女孩如何不应该做这种糟糕的工作等等。然而，我已经习惯了做这些事，那是我分内的工作，我也尽量付出更多的关爱来安慰格雷斯以及我的所有客户们，让她们不必为此事担忧。

Being sick is certainly a way to dissolve the ego. Dignity disappears into the past forever when you are terminally ill. Acceptance of the situation and of someone else wiping your backside becomes inevitable, as clients become too sick to worry about such things after a while.

> 人一旦生病必然导致没有了自我。当你病入膏盲之际，你的尊严也将不复存在，永远成为了过去式。当客户们的病情进入严重阶段后不久，他们就会担忧自己的尊严问题，接受现状和接受别人为你擦屁股已是不可避免的了。

Married for more than fifty years, Grace had led the life expected of her. She had raised lovely children and now rejoiced in the lives of her grandchildren as they lived through their teenage years. Her husband had apparently been a bit of a tyrant though, making married life for Grace very unpleasant for decades. It was a relief for everyone, especially Grace, when he had been admitted into a nursing home permanently only a few months before.

> 结婚超过了五十年，格雷斯的生活不负众望，尽心尽责。她养育了可爱的孩子，如今又拥有了孙子，他们都长成为十几岁的青少年。可是她的丈夫显然是一个暴君，这使得格雷斯几十年的婚姻生活很不愉快。就在几个月之前，当她的丈夫被接收可永远入住一所养老院的时候，对每一个人，尤其对格雷斯来说，那简直就是一种解脱。

Grace had spent her married life dreaming of living independently from her husband, of travelling, of not living under his dictatorship and mostly, just of living a simple, happy life. Although she was in her eighties, she had still been fit for her age and healthy. Good health gives freedom of mobility and this had been present for her when he was admitted into the nursing home.

> 格雷斯在整个的婚姻生活中都在梦想着脱离丈夫独自生活：梦想出去旅行，梦想不要在丈夫的独裁专制下过日子，而最为梦想的是过一种简单、快乐的生活。虽然她已经八十多岁了，但依然很健康，身体状况符合她的年龄。健康的身体使得她具有自由行动的能力。对她而言，丈夫住进了养老院等于是上帝赐予她的一份礼物。

Within a short time of her newly found and long awaited freedom however, Grace began to feel very ill. A few days after this turning point, she was diagnosed with terminal illness, already quite advanced. What made this even more heartbreaking was that her illness was due to her husband’s long-term smoking habit within the home.

> 然而，长时间地期盼自由却在很短时间内发现有了疾病，这使得格雷斯开始感到非常的伤感和身体不适。在这个逆转时刻的几天之后，她被诊断患有不治之症，而且已到了晚期。令她更为心碎的是她的疾病还是她丈夫长时间在家里吸烟造成的。

The illness was aggressive and by the time a month had passed, Grace had lost all of her strength and was bedridden, other than hobbling slowly to the bathroom with a walking frame while assisted. Dreams she had waited all of her life to live were now never going to happen. It was too late. The anguish she suffered over this was ongoing and tormented her enormously.

> 该疾病对身体是侵蚀性的，一个月过后格雷斯失去了所有力量，变得卧床不起，除了有时候依靠助行架慢慢一瘸一拐地走进卫生间。她一生中所期盼的所有梦想如今绝不可能实现了，犹如梦幻泡影，虚无渺茫。真的是太晚了。疾病的极大痛苦正在持续地折磨着她。

“Why didn’t I just do what I wanted? Why did I let him rule me? Why wasn’t I strong enough?” were questions I heard regularly. She was so angry with herself for not having found the courage. Her children confirmed the hard life she had experienced and their hearts felt for her, as did mine.

> “力什么我以前不按照自己的想法去生活？为什么我让他来管治我？为什么那时我不够坚强？我不断地听她这样地发问。她非常怨恨自己当初怎么没有找到勇气。她的孩子们也承认她经历过了艰难的岁月，以前的日子真的不容易。他们跟我一样很同情及体谅她。

“Don’t you ever let anyone stop you doing what you want Bronnie,” she said. “Promise that to this dying woman please.” I promised and went on to explain how I was fortunate to have an amazing mother who had taught me independence by example.

> “邦妮，千万不要让任何人阻止你正在做、你想要做的事。”她对我说。“请答应我这个快要死的人。“我答应了她，并跟她讲了我是如何的幸运有一位伟大的母亲，她以身作则地教我要成为独立的女人。

“Look at me now,” Grace continued. “Dying. Dying! How can it be possible I have waited all of these years to be free and independent and now it is too late?” There was no denying this was a tragic situation and one that was going to be a constant reminder to live my own way. In her bedroom, dotted with sentimental artifacts and photos of her family, we shared hours of conversations over those first weeks. Her decline was happening quite fast though.

> “看看我现在，”格雷斯继续地说，“临近死亡，走向死亡！这怎么可能呢，我为自由和独立等待了这么多年，而现在什么都力之已晚。”不可否认，这是一个悲剧，也是一个警钟，将不断地提醒我要按照自己的意愿去生活。在布满了寓有情感的各式手工艺品和家庭照片的格雷斯卧房里，先前的几个星期我们交谈着，分享了许多往事。可她的病情正在迅速地恶化。

Grace explained that she wasn’t against marriage, not at all. She thought it could be a beautiful thing and a great opportunity to grow, through shared learning. What she was against was the doctrine of her generation, stating that you had to stay in a marriage regardless of anything. And so she had, all the while forfeiting her own happiness. She had dedicated her life to her husband who took her love completely for granted.

> 格雷斯解释说她并不是完全地抵抗这段婚姻。通过共同领悟，她认这样的婚姻可能是一件好事，是一次使人成长的好机会。而她所抵抗的是他们这一代人的那种教旨，无论婚姻状况如何，你都得维持下去。正因如此，她丧失了所有自己应有的幸福。她把自己的生命都奉献给了她的丈夫，可他理所当然地完全地霸占了她的爱。

Now that she was dying, she didn’t care what people thought of her and anguished over why she hadn’t worked this out sooner. Grace had kept up appearances and lived the way others expected her to, only now realising the choice to do so had always been her own and was based on fear. Although I offered support, including the need to forgive herself, the fact that it was all now too late, continued to overwhelm her.

> 现在她临近死亡，再也不在乎别人如何看她，如何认她为什么不尽快摆脱这种痛苦。她总是好面子，总是按照别人期望的那样去生活，到现在才意识到这样的生活选择总是基于害怕而决定的。尽管我努力去支持和安慰她，包括帮助她原谅自己的过去，可事实上这一切都时已晚，悔恨的痛苦继续在打压着她。

Most of my assignments were of this one-on-one kind, longer-term clients who I would care for until they passed. There came to be many others dotted in between these though over the years, clients who I would see just a few times in between their own regular carers. These words from Grace, filled with anguish, despair and frustration, became familiar words from many others I also came to meet. Of all of the regrets and lessons shared with me as I sat beside their beds, the regret of not having lived a life true to themselves was the most common one of all. It was also the one that caused the most frustration, as the client’s realisation came too late.

> 我大部分的工作都是这样固定性的长时期照顾一个客户，一直到该客户最后去世。过去的几年里，我在定期照顾客户中间也有临时性像钟点工那样去照顾另外一些病人。从格雷斯的谈话中，流露出了苦闷、绝望和沮丧，在我后来照顾的客户中也有一些常表达这些熟悉的心理感受。当我坐在他们的床边与他们交谈，我分享了他们所有的遗憾、悔恨和教训，最为常见的遗憾之一就是后悔没有真正的为自己而活。当他们的醒悟姗姗来迟的时候也就导致了他们极度的沮丧。

“It’s not like I wanted to live a grand life,” Grace explained in one of many conversations from her bed. “I am a good person and I didn’t wish to harm anyone.” Grace was one of the sweetest people I have ever met and wouldn’t have been capable of harming anyone anyway. It just wasn’t in her. “But I wanted to do things for me too and I just didn’t have the courage.”

> “我并不是想要一种辉煌的生活，”在床上闲聊时格雷斯解释道。“我是一个好人，我不愿意伤害任何人。”格雷斯是我认识的人中最甜美的人，也不会有能力以任何方式伤害任何人。她的重点还不在这里。“但是我也想为自己谋利益，我只是没有勇气这样去做。”

Grace now understood it would have been better for everyone had she been brave enough to honour this desire. “Well, everyone except for my husband,” she said with disgust towards herself. “I would have been happier and would not have let this misery permeate our family for decades. Why did I put up with him? Why Bronnie, why?” Her heartbreaking sobs burst out and continued to flow as I held the dear lady close.

> 现在格雷斯终于明白了，要是她能够有足够的胆量去兑现她的愿望，那对每个人都会更好。“嗯，除我丈夫之外。”她以怨恨自己的口气这样说。“我本来会是快乐的人，本来不会让这种痛苦渗透到我的家庭几十年。为什么我要容忍他？为什么？邦妮，为什么呀？”当我紧紧地抱住这位亲爱的女人时，她再也忍不住爆发出令人心碎的呜咽声并持续了好一阵。

When her tears subsided, she looked at me with fierce determination. “I mean it. Promise this dying woman that you will always be true to yourself, that you will be brave enough to live the way you want to, regardless of what other people say.” The lace curtains blew softly, allowing the day outside to enter the bedroom, as we looked at each other with love, clarity, and determination.

> 等她的哭泣停止之后，她用强烈而果断的眼神看着我说，“我是认真的。答应我这个快死的人，不管别人怎么说你，你都要坚持真实地对待自己，这样你才会有足够的胆量去过自己想要的生活。”格雷斯说这话的时候，蕾丝窗帘被风轻柔地吹拂起来。让那窗外的新鲜空气吹进房里。此刻，我和格雷斯彼此看着对方，心中都充满着爱意、明智和決心。

“I promise Grace. I am already trying to. But I promise you now that I will always continue to do so,” I replied with heartfelt truth. Holding my hand, she smiled knowing that at least her learning wasn’t going to be totally wasted. Explaining to Grace that for over a decade of my adult life I had worked in those unsatisfying banking, administration and management roles, she started to understand me more and listened with great interest.

> “我答应你，格雷斯。我总是试图这样做的。而今天我向你保证我将一如既往地按照你说的那样去做，”我发自内心地回答了她。她握住我的手，微笑了，她知道至少她的经验教训将不会被彻底地浪费掉。我对她讲述了我过去超过十年的成人生活，曾经在那些不令人满意的银行业工作，担当一些行政和管理职务。她开始更多地了解我，并以极大的兴趣听我讲述。

Further years in banking had been added to this tally later, when I had returned from overseas. But I called those my weaning years, weaning myself out of the industry. The first couple of years out of school were fun. There were many trainees and work was a social thing above all else. All of the trainees were seventeen or eighteen years old.

> 当我从海外流浪归来，我再次回到银行业工作，那几年里我是变本加厉地厌烦这种工作。然而，我把这些年称之为我的“断奶年”，将自已与企业彻底断绝关系。离开学校的头几年是愉快的。在企业培训中有很多的学员，工作的社交性高于一切。所有的学员都是十七八岁的青少年。

So work was really just about catching up with friends and earning money to fund our weekends. The work itself was very easy for me at first and may have stayed that way, if my heart had been in the job. It never was though. After those first years, I quickly became restless and began questioning life. Yet I carried on with the life expected of me for a decade more, the whole time knowing there was something else waiting but not having the courage to seek it.

> 因此，上班真的只是为了与朋友交往和为周末的消费挣点零用钱。刚开始工作，只要我用心去做，这份工作对我来说可以很容易做好，也许我永远不会被淘汰。可是，几年之后，我很快变得烦躁不安并开始质疑人生，但我依然按人们期望的那样生活了十几年之久。整个那段时期我都明白有某种另外的生活在等着我，只是我还没有勇气去追寻它。

What kept me there most was the fear of ridicule I would face from some family members, if I broke out of the mould they expected me to fit. I was living someone else’s life through my shoes and it was never going to work. Yet I carried on, changing bank jobs, uniforms, and locations regularly. As a result, I found myself on an accelerated career path, due to having worked for most of the banks and in more roles than a normal person my age would have. I was a success by default. Desperately unhappy, I continued to give my working week to an industry that did nothing for my soul.

> 之所以我仍然保持原状的很大部分原因是害怕面对一些家庭成员，假如我打碎了他们对我的那种期望模式，就会引来他们的嘲笑、奚落。我自己的鞋却一直被别人穿在脚下，从未合脚舒服过。可是我还是坚持着，并时常地转换银行工作、更换制服和搬迁住所。结果，我发现自己踏入了一条快速超负荷的职业之路，先后在大家银行工作过，所担当过的多种角色不是正常同龄人都能做的。我因做好别人不愿干的工作而成为成功者。尽管极度的不快乐，我还是继续在没有灵性的银行业里上班。

There are plenty of people who love their jobs in banking and I am happy for them. Banking needs such people. These days too there are opportunities to work in areas where you are giving back to the community and other noble avenues. But like Grace, I had been living the life others expected of me, not the life I wanted to.

> 银行业中也有许多人在热爱他们的工作，我为他们感到高兴。该行业也需要这样热诚的人。那段时间的工作也让我有了机会回馈社区和为当地有钱人服务。然而，跟格雷斯一样，我也是为别人的期望而活，这种生活并不是我想要的。

As I could do no right with some of my family and was struggling to be who they wanted me to be, staying in a ‘good job’ would at least keep them off my back in that area of my life. I was trapped with fear and the potential pain that would be created by setting myself up for even more judgment than I had already endured. Being the black sheep in any family is never an easy task. Black sheep have a different role to play in family dynamics.

> 我可以做一些我的家人没有权力干涉我想要做的事，也正在抗争他们要求我成为他们期望的人。保持一份“体面的工作”至少我不会在那些时间经常回去打扰他们。我被恐惧和潜在的痛苦所阻扰，那是由于自己给自己设置了比已经忍受的还要多的别人如何评判自己的假想状态。在任何家庭里，作为一个败家子日子都是不好过的。败家子在家庭互动中都扮演一个异类的角色。

But it is not always easy. When some of the main players in the unit gain their power by reducing the strength of others, it is a hard road upwards. But working amongst so many families in this line of work, I came to observe that very few families are free of conflict on some level. Every family has its learning, every one of them. Mine was no different, though this realisation didn’t necessarily ease my own pain at the time.

> 这非常不容易啊。当家庭联盟中的主要成员拥有削减另外成员力量的势力时，作异类想要在家里抬起头门都没有。我观察到很多家庭都是这样，只有极少部分家庭在某种程度上没有这种冲突。每个家庭及家庭成员都有自己的一个领悟过程。我的家庭也不例外。而意识到这一点也未必减轻一点自己内心的痛苦。

Making jokes of me had been a family sport for as long as I could remember. I was a swimmer amongst a family of horse riders, a vegetarian from a sheep farming family, a nomad in a family of settlers, and on it went. Often things said were in jest and the person saying so may not have realised the pain they were causing at the time.

> 从我有记忆开始，取笑我就一直是我们家的一项集体活动。我们家里的人都善于骑马，而我擅长游泳；在这个饲养羊的农场家庭中，我却是个素食者，是一个定居在家庭里面的流浪者等等。事情往往都是这样，开玩笑的人在说笑话的那一刻并没有意识到会给别人带来怎样的痛苦。

But jokes tend to wear thin after a few decades of hearing them. At other times though, too often, things said were intentional and just outright cruel. Even if you had the strength of a thousand people, it does wear you down after years. Particularly when you have trouble even recalling a period of your life when being ridiculed, yelled at, or told you were hopeless, were not a part of it.

> 于是几十年之后，有些笑话往往会被人淡忘。但是某些时候，事情并不总是这样，说笑话的人毫无疑问是故意中伤你。即使你具有千万人那般强大的力量，数年之后，那种玩笑话依然会削弱你的意志，让你难以振作起来。尤其是当你遇到麻烦，甚至回忆你的一些生活往事时，被家人嘲笑，大声训斥，或者告诉你是一个没有希望的人，不属于他们的一部分的时候，你真的感到自己低人一等。

Consequently, up until this time I had never particularly enjoyed family dynamics. So the easiest way to handle this back then was just to keep living the life expected of me. Eventually though, I did begin to withdraw and close down around them. It was my own coping mechanism.

> 结果直到现在，我仍然从未享受过家庭的快乐。因此，要想挽回大家对我的看法，最简单的办法只有保持按照他们的期望去生活。然而，最终我还是开始从家庭中撤退出来，并不再跟他们来往。这是我自己的应对机制之一。

Artists the world over are also a misunderstood lot, and I was an artist. I just hadn’t realised it yet. All I knew was that selling insurance products to people just wanting to bank their pay cheques was really not up my alley. The branch sales at the end of the month held absolutely no relevance to me. I didn’t care about anything except giving the customers friendly, warm service, which I did very well. But that was not enough in the changing face of the banking industry. It was all sell, sell, sell.

> 全世界的艺术家都被人误解很多。我也是一名艺术者，只是那时还没有意识到而已。我所知道的就是向人们推销保险产品，只是希望他们把薪金支票放进银行里，这真的不符合我对人生的要求。该支行到了月底统计销售业绩绝对与我无关。除了为我的客户提供友好而热情周到的服务之外，我不在乎其他的任何东西，我对客户非常好。但是，我这样做并不足以改变银行老板对我的看法，他们要我做的就是销售、销售、再销售。

They say though that we do more to avoid pain than we do to gain pleasure. So it is when the pain becomes too much that we finally find the courage to make changes. Until then, the pain within me was just continuing to fester until it did reach breaking point.

> 然而人们常说，我们需要做得更多的是避免痛苦而不是获得快乐。因此，只有当我们的痛苦变得无法忍受的时候才最终鼓起勇气去改变。在那之前，我依然忍辱负重，内心痛楚不堪，以至于达到爆发点。

When I left yet another ‘good job’ to go and live on the island, confusion reigned. ‘Why would she do that? Where is she going to this time?’ And through all of this I was just thinking with excitement, ‘I’m going to live on an island!’ The further away, the happier I was. My life was my own there and it was a good life. Any contact I did have with the mainland was to my dear mother, who was my rock and treasured friend.

> 当我再一次离开一份“好工作”并打算到岛上去生活的时候，家里出现一片混乱。“为什么她会这样？”“这个时候她要去哪里？”无论他们怎样地大呼小叫，我心里只管兴奋地想着“我要到一个岛上去生活了！”离他们远一点，我会更快乐。在那个地方我的生活我做主，这样的生活感觉很好。我与澳大利亚本土大陆唯一联络的人就是我亲爱的母亲，她是我的坚强后盾，是我的珍贵朋友。

It was during these years on the island that I first dabbled in meditation. Later I found my way to the path that would offer me the opportunity to connect with my own goodness in ways no other had. Through this path, I began to understand and experience compassion. It is such a beautiful and powerful force.

> 在岛上生活的那些年间，我开始进行冥想练习。稍后，我发觉通过冥想方式会我自己提供机会去连接自身潜在的仁慈特质，其他的什么方式都不能达到。通过冥想，我开始去理解和体验同理心。这是一种如此美好及强大的力量。

The pain I had accepted from others had been their own suffering projected onto me. Happy people do not treat other people that way. They don’t judge others for living a life true to his or her self. If anything, they respect it. Recognising the pain carried into my generation from previous ones, I had the choice to break free of it in my own life.

> 别人的痛苦能投射到我的心里，我可以设身处地地感受到。往往快乐的人不会像那样对待他人。他们为了过好自己真实的生活才不去理会别人。假如需要的话，他们也是尊重别人而已。从前辈身上我看清了我们这一代人正经受同样的痛苦，所以我选择了打破枷锁，让自己的生活自由自在。

I was never going to be able to control another and had no desire to. People change because they want to and when they are ready. Learning to view life compassionately, and accept that I may never have the understanding or loving relationships I had once yearned for, was liberating. It transformed my life on so many levels. Knowing the ongoing pain of my own healing, I accepted that not everyone has the courage to face his or her past, at least not until it becomes unbearable.

> 我从未获得能力去掌控别人，也不想要这样。人要转变那是因为他们想要转变，并在万事俱备之时实现转变。学会以同情的眼光去观察生活和承认自己以前可能从未获得过曾经渴望的理解或者爱其实是一种解脱，这使我的生活在很多方面发生了转变。懂得了靠自己去愈合持续出现的心理创伤，我承认不是每个人都有勇气去面对自己的过去，也许是要等到过去的一切到了无法忍受的地步才拿出勇气面对。

To a degree, the same dynamics persisted for some years after, but began to affect me less and less. It took strength and time, but I now saw that it was not about me. It was about whichever person was trying to give me their criticism or judgment. A Buddhist story is that a man came shouting angrily at Buddha, who remained unaffected by him.

> 这些多少有点大同小异的家庭压力持续了几年后，在强度和时间上，开始对我的影响越来越小。现在我看出来了，有压力感的不是我自己，而是任何一个正在试图对我加以批评或评判的人。有一个佛家故事：一位先生对着佛陀愤怒大吼，而佛陀坦然自若不受他的影响。

When questioned by others as to how he remained calm and unaffected, Buddha answered with a question. “If someone gives you a gift and you choose not to receive it, to whom then does the gift belong?” Of course it stays with the giver. So it was with words that were still unjustly dumped onto me sometimes. I stopped taking them on and instead I felt compassion. After all, those words were not coming from a place of happiness.

> 当有人请教佛陀是如何保持冷静，不被激怒的时候，佛陀以提问的方式来回答：“如果有人送给你一个礼物，你选择拒不收这份礼，那么，这份礼又归谁了呢？”当然，它还留在送礼的人手上。尽管有时候他们依然对我说一些不公平的话，我不再去理会这些话，相反，我感到同情他们。毕竟，他们不是在心情愉快的时候说出这些话的，他们心里也不好受。

The most important thing I have ever learnt in life though, the most, most important thing, is that compassion starts with yourself. Developing compassion for others allowed the healing to begin and continue. It removed me from the equation somewhat when the old behavioural patterns still tried to reign. I could recognise the suffering and see it wasn’t about me at all. It was someone else’s own pain coming up and out.

> 然而，我在以往的生活中领悟到最重要，甚至最最重要的一点是，慈悲心始于善待自己。培养同情别人的心态使得我开始并继续愈合自己的伤痛。当我过去的一些陈旧行为仍然试图在我的生活中占上风的时候，慈悲心怀将我在某种程度上拉了回来。我可以辨认出痛苦是什么，并看清了那痛苦的人已经完全不再是我，而是别人的伤痛在来来去去迂回。

This didn’t just apply to family relationships of course. It was relevant to all relationships, personal, public, and professional. We all suffer at some time. We all have pain, every one of us. But learning how to develop compassion for myself was much harder and though I didn’t know at the time, it was going to take years. We are all so hard on ourselves, unfairly so.

> 当然，这种方法不仅仅适用于建立家庭内部良好关系，也适用于个人关系、公共关系等所有人际关系的建立。我们每个人在某些时候都可能遇到不幸，每个人都有痛苦的经历。我以前根本不知道这些，直到历练了许多年之后才领悟过来，其实学会如何善待自己是相当的不容易。我们都太苛刻自己，这非常不公平。

Learning to give myself loving kindness and acknowledge that I too had suffered enormously was such a difficult change to make. It was almost easier to listen to unfair opinions from others and take them on, as it was so familiar. It may not have brought happiness, but learning to be kind to myself and give myself compassion above everything else, was certainly a process I had to grow into. But the healing had at least now begun.

> 学会给自己爱的关怀和承认自己也有极大的痛苦其实是极其难以做到的改变。我们几乎很容易听取别人不公平的意见，并采纳，因那些意见是多么的熟悉，在你耳边时常回响。这也许不会给我带来快乐感，但是，学会善待自己和给予自己高于一切的慈悲，的确是我必须成长的一个过程。无论如何，现在愈合自己伤痛的过程已经启动了。

With this new intention of self-love, self-respect and self-compassion, the old family dynamics started losing power. I found the strength to speak back, allowing myself to finally be heard, rather than continuing to withdraw. Of course, this was my own pain now being expressed and not really about the people I addressed at all.

> 由于心中怀有爱自己、尊重自己和同情自己的意图，原先那种家庭压力开始在我身上失去了威力。我发现大声驳回，让他们最终能听到我的声音远比持续性地撤离、回避要好得多。当然，我现在所表达的是我自己的痛苦，而非完全是他们的痛苦。

We all interpret things that happen to us in our own way. So it was about me expressing and releasing my own suffering. Breaking the patterns of decades took a lot of guts. But my pain gave me that and I had nothing to lose anymore. As it was, I just couldn’t carry the pain of silence any longer.

> 我们每个人都是以自己的方式去诠释发生在自己身上的事。因，自己的伤痛由自己去表达和释放。我鼓起了巨大勇气去打碎多年的生活模式。是我的痛苦逼着我这样做，我再没有什么东西可失去的了。事实上，我不能扛着我的痛苦再沉默下去。

In the end though, it was really only the desire to be loved, accepted, and understood by each other that was truly fuelling the pain in us all. So compassion was the only way forward: compassion and patience. In spite of everything, love, in its own fragile disguise, still existed between us.

> 不过到头来，真的只有希望能得到别人的爱、得到别人的接纳和相互间的理解，才真正地激发我们所有的痛，才能将之转换爱。所以，仁慈才是唯一的出路，要有爱心加耐心。尽管爱很脆弱，但在我与家人之间依然存在。

It was as if I had been swimming down the same river over and over, and each time, I would come across a large rock blocking my natural flow. It was always there. One day though, I realised that it may always be there. So rather than have to face that same rock, that same blockage repeatedly, I was choosing somewhere different to swim, somewhere that would allow me to move forward freely and naturally. I didn’t have to set myself up for that hurdle over and over, one that hindered my natural progress, causing blockages and pain every time without fail.

> 这就如同我在同一条河里反复地游泳，每次游泳我都会绕过那块挡在河当中的大石头。它总在那里，不会移动。有一天，我终于意识到了，这块巨石可能永远在那里。因此，与其不得不反复面对同样的石头，同样的障碍物，倒不如，我选择另外的游泳地点，在那里没有阻碍物，我可以自由自在地向前游去。我没有必要给自己一次又一次的设置那种障碍物，它将妨碍我的活动，引起道路堵塞和每次带来必定的痛苦。

It was time to do things differently. It was time to choose a different way, to speak up and say ‘enough’. I wasn’t willing to tolerate the same patterns anymore. Even if it turned out lonelier, at least it may lead to peace. The other path certainly wasn’t peaceful.

> 是该采取不同行动的时候了，是该选择一种不同的方式对他们呐喊说“够了”，我再也不愿意忍受相同的局面。你的反抗即使会使你变得孤独，但至少带来宁静。其他的办法肯定不会是平静的。

After speaking up, things started to change within myself. I grew stronger in self-respect and clearer in self-expression. Some new and healthier seeds had finally been sown. I didn’t yet know how to nurture them, but they were planted at least. It was time to start living as who I wanted to be, one small step at a time.

> 敞开喉咙说出心里话之后，我自身的许多事情发生了改变。我成长了，有了更强的自尊及更加清晰的自我表达。一些新的更健康的思想种子终于在我身上播种下去。虽然我还不知道如何去培育它们，但至少它们已经被种植了。它们正在开始按照我所想要成为什么样的人那样生长发芽，那一刻，我跨出了一小步。

After sharing all of this with Grace, we grew closer without effort. She agreed that all families have their learning. She couldn’t think of one family that hadn’t had its challenges and believed that families bring the greatest gifts of learning for most people. We discussed how the only way to experience love is to accept people totally as who they are and have no expectations of them. While it may be much easier said than done, it was the most loving approach possible.

> 与格雷斯分享了我的所有故事之后，我们很容易就变得亲近起来。她赞同家家都有一本难念的经。她不认一个家庭完全从未遇到过挑战，她相信，对大多数人而言，家庭是学习和成长的最好地方。我们探讨了唯一体验爱的方法是完全地接受别人，不希望得到回报。当然，或许是说得容易做到难，可是，那是爱最有可能的表达方式。

Grace shared many stories with me; reflecting on her life, of the children growing up, the neighbourhood changing, then often back to her dying regret. She wished she’d had the courage to live a life true to her own heart, not the life others expected of her. When there is limited time left, there is little to lose by being totally honest. What we shared with each other now was straight to the core of important things.

> 格雷斯也跟我分享了她的很多故事，包括她的生活、她的孩子们的成长、邻居的更换，然后常常又回到她临终后悔的话题。她希望当初有勇气真实地过自己想要的生活，而不是为别人而活。这样的话，当有限的生命为时不久，个体会是彻底的坦诚，没有什么好后悔的了。我和格雷斯现在彼此共享的是端正重要事情的核心。

There was no idle chatter anymore as all of the subjects covered were deeply personal. Opening up to Grace was unexpectedly very healing for me and my listening ear became healing for her. Eventually we also came onto the subject of where my life was now, my musical directions, and how I had started to write songs and perform. Over a cup of tea, Grace insisted I bring my guitar to work the next day and play her something, which was an absolute pleasure for me.

> 我们再也没有无聊的闲扯，所有的话题都是围绕着个人内心最深邃的东西。出乎意外的，对格雷斯敞开心扉为我自己愈合了伤痛，而我忠实的倾听也为她缓解了痛苦。最终，我们的讨论主题还包含了目前音乐是我的生活方向，以及我是如何开始歌曲创作和唱歌表演。喝完一杯茶之后，格雷斯坚持要我第二天带上我的吉他来上班，并为她弹奏点什么，我高兴得不得了。

With a heart of happiness, I sang to Grace as she smiled and hummed, sitting up in her bed. She embraced every song I shared, receiving them as if each one was the best song in the world. Her family too came to hear some of them and was equally beautiful and supportive. One song in particular Grace absolutely loved, as she had always wanted to travel. It was called ‘Beneath Australian Skies’.

> 带着发自内心的快乐，我唱歌给格雷斯听，她躺在床上微笑着，并跟随哼唱。她向我的每首歌都伸出热情的手拥抱它，仿佛每一首都是这个世界上最好听的歌。她的家人有时也来听我弹唱歌曲，同样对我来说是美好的事，也是对我莫大的支持。特别是有一首格雷斯最喜爱的歌，她总是要求我力她弹唱。这首歌叫做《在澳大利亚的天空下》。

After that day, she would ask me to sing to her regularly. There was no need for a guitar Grace had said. So I’d sit there in her bedroom, singing to this delightful little lady, as she closed her eyes smiling, absorbing everything I sang. Songs were requested over and over and I never tired of singing them to her.

> 自从那天以后，她会定期要我为她唱歌。后来她也不要求我带吉他来了，只是清唱。所以，我会坐在她的床边，为这个愉快的小女人唱歌。每当我唱歌的时候，她闭着双眼，微笑着感受和吸收歌中的每个音符和歌词。虽然，我一次又一次地为她歌唱，但我从未感觉到累。

Each day Grace’s health deteriorated. Her tiny size reduced even more. Old friends came to say their goodbyes. Relatives sat by her bed chatting and fighting back their tears. Her family was a hands-on one, very involved, and their visits regular. I liked that. There was also gentleness in them that I was drawn to. When they were all gone though, it was just Grace and I again and the request for more singing resurfaced. They were special times.

> 每一天格雷斯的病情都在恶化，她那瘦小身躯变得越来越小。老朋友们过来向她道别。亲属们强忍着眼泪坐在她的床边聊天。她的家庭很团结，非常在意每个人，家庭成员之间经常互访。我喜欢这个家庭。他们吸引我的还有一点就是他们都很温和。等他们都离开了，屋内再次只有我和格雷斯，格雷斯又会再请我唱一首歌。我们就这样度过了难忘的时光。

She was not able to walk well now and though Grace had accepted the use of the commode by the bed, she refused when it came to her bowel movements. She wanted to use a proper toilet so I didn’t have to clean the commode. There was no budging her on this, even when I tried to assure Grace it was no big deal for me to do.

> 目前，她已不能很好地走路了，虽然她已经接受在床边用便盆，但是她还是拒绝坐在上面解大便。她想要用一个正式的厕所，那样我就不必清洗便盆了。尽管我再三向她保证，清洗便盆算不了什么，我乐意去做，但完全没有商量的余地，她很坚持。

So ages passed as we made our way to the bathroom, which thankfully was next to her bedroom. She was very weak. When the business was done and she was clean, I assisted her with standing up then pulled her underpants back up. Balancing her while doing the undies thing had to be quite a swift move.

> 从我们走进卫生间的方式就可以看出她在衰老，好在她的卫生间就在卧房旁边。她现在很虚弱了。当上完厕所后，我协助她站起来，然后帮她提拉内裤。这一切动作还必须快速进行，以免她失衡倒下。

As we then started our hike back to the bedroom, Grace leaning on her walking frame and me following, holding her hips, I noticed that in the rush I had tucked a little of her nightdress into the back of her underpants. Smiling at this dear little woman in her last days, tottering back to bed, I was then overwhelmed with joy as she started to sing ‘Beneath Australian Skies’ as she walked. A few of the words were in the wrong places, but that just made the moment even more endearing.

> 在我们开始回到卧房的时候，她靠着她的助行器走路，我扶着她的腰跟在后面，我注意到了有时需要快速穿裤子时，我把她的睡衣的一部分塞进了她后面的内裤里。在她的最后几天里，这位可爱的小女人都是这样摇摇晃晃地微笑着走回卧房，然后，当她边走边哼着那首《在澳大利亚的天空下》的歌时，我沉浸在无比的喜悦之中。尽管有几个词没有唱准确，但那一刻反而更加招人喜欢。

I knew then that I had just experienced the high point of my musical career. Nothing that could ever happen again would ever top the joy I experienced at that moment. If I never wrote another song it would not have bothered me. To have brought this dear person so much pleasure through my music, and to then receive that pleasure back by hearing her singing my song in her final days, opened my heart more than anything I had ever hoped for musically.

> 我明白，我正体验着我音乐生涯的制高点。以后我再也不可能获得当时那样的喜悦感。倘若我停止写另外的歌也不会令我不安了。在格雷斯最后的几天里，我的音乐给这位可爱的人带来了很多的快乐，然而，反过来听她唱我的歌又使得我获得了快乐。敞开心灵比我在音乐事业上所希望的任何成就都更加重要。

Arriving at work a couple of days later, it was obvious that today was to be the last for Grace. Explaining that I was going to call her family, she initially shook her head no. Weak and exhausted, she reached up and hugged me. To save her little arms the effort, I lay on the bed and held her in my own.

> 之后几天一切正常。显然，是格雷斯的最后一天了。我跟她表明说，我要给她家人打电话，她最初摇摇头表示不要。尽管虚弱、疲惫，她还是伸出手来拥抱我。为了不让她的瘦小的手臂受累，我俯下身，躺在床上用我的双臂抱住了她。

She liked that and we lay talking softly for a while, as her fingers stroked my arm. Asking why she didn’t want the family there, she said she didn’t want to cause them any more pain. She loved them too much. But they needed to say goodbye, I said, and not giving them that choice may end up causing them both pain and guilt that they would have to live with.

> 她喜欢这样，我们俩躺在床上轻声地交谈了一会儿，同时她用手指抚摸着我的胳膊。在我问她为什么不要家人过来时，她说她再也不想给他们造成任何的痛苦。她太爱他们了。我对她说，他们也需要去跟你道别呀，假如不给他们选择的机会，可能到头来会使他们在今后的生活中永远伴随着痛苦和内疚。

She understood and agreed, accepting that she didn’t want them to feel guilty for not being there. So phone calls were made and the family soon arrived. But just before they did, she said to me through her exhaustion, “You remember our promise Bronnie, don’t you?” Nodding through my tears I said “Yes”. “Live true to your own heart. Don’t ever worry what others think. Promise me Bronnie,” her voice now a barely audible whisper.

> 她理解和同意了，并承认她不要他们因这个时候没有到来而感到永久地内疚。然而，就在他们快要赶到之前，她用很虚脱的语气对我说：“邦妮，你记住了我们的承诺，是吗？”我眼含泪水说：“我记住了。”“为自己真实的想法去生活。千万不要在意别人的想法。答应我，邦妮。”她的声音几乎是听不见的耳语。

“I promise you Grace,” I said gently. Squeezing my hand she drifted off to sleep, waking again only for brief moments to acknowledge her lovely family who then sat by her bedside until the end. Within a few hours, Grace drifted off. Her time had come. Sitting quietly in the kitchen afterwards, my promise to her was still fresh in my ears. But it was not just Grace I had made the promise to. It was also to myself.

> 我轻柔的回答她：“我答应你，格雷斯。”她捏捏我的手，迷迷糊糊地入睡，仅几分钟后又醒过来，以确定她的家人是否来了。后来，他们都坐在她的床边直到最后她离去。在最后的几个小时，格雷斯昏迷了。她离开人世的时间已经来临。在这之后，我静静地坐在厨房里，我对她的承诺一直回响在我的耳边。然而，那不仅仅是我对格雷斯的承诺，也是对自己的承诺。

Standing on stage launching my album a few months later, I dedicated that song to her. Grace’s family was in the audience. The spotlight blacked out most of the faces but I didn’t need to see them. I could feel the love they were sharing as I remembered that dear little woman who hadn’t lived as she wanted, but who had inspired me to instead.

> 几个月以后，站在我的专辑发行表演舞台上，我把《在澳大利亚的天空下》那首歌献给了她。格雷斯的家人也在听众席上。投射在舞台上的聚光灯使台下一片漆黑，不过，我并不需要看清听众的脸。我可以感受得到他们正在与我分享这份爱，那是我在缅怀那位可爱的小女人。她没有得到过她真正想要的生活，但她却一直激励我代替她去善待自己，真实地过自己想要的生活。
