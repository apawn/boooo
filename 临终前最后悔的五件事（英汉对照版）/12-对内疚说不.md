# No guilt · 对内疚说不

The buzzer rang, bringing me out of a snug sleep in my latest abode. Slipping some cover on my feet and wrapping myself in a robe, I headed upstairs to attend to Jude. Words that may appear as a grunt to an untrained ear were uttered, indicating she needed repositioning, as her leg was in pain. Once Jude was comfortable and smiling again, I turned off her lamp, wished her sweet dreams again, and headed back down to the comfort of a beautiful bed.

> 一阵阵蜂音器铃声响了，把我从新的逗留处舒适的睡梦中吵醒了。我急忙起床，把睡袍滑下去盖住我的脚，然后把身体裹起来，往楼上跑去看望朱迪。因一只耳朵听力不行，她大声地咕噜着想说出话来，我明白这表明她需要改变一下姿势，因为她的一只腿在疼痛。一旦朱迪感到舒适了和再次出现微笑，我就为她关掉台灯，再次祝她有个美梦，然后下楼重新回到我那舒适而漂亮的床上。

Jude and I had been brought together through word of mouth. Someone in the songwriting circuit knew I worked as a carer and lived as a house-sitter, so passed my phone number on. The majority of my palliative clients so far had been elderly or past middle age, and most were dying of cancer related illnesses, but not all. Jude’s illness though was a motor neurone disease and she was only forty-four. Her husband and daughter, a delightful nine-year old with curly auburn hair and a precious smile, were loving and beautiful people, as was Jude.

> 还是通过口碑相传才把我和朱迪带到了一起。歌曲编写圈内有人知道我曾经干过护理员和入住帮人看房子的工作，所以，他们把我的电话号码转给相关的人。到目前为止，大多数我的姑息护理客户们都是中老年人，其中几乎都是因患癌症等相关疾病而临近死亡的人。但也有例外，朱迪的病属于一种运动神经性疾病，她只有四十四岁。如同朱迪一样，她的丈夫和女儿都是可爱而漂亮的人，她女儿是一个讨人喜欢的九岁女孩，有着漂亮的褐色卷发和甜美的微笑。

By the time I came to be her carer, they were fed up entirely with agencies who sent different people all the time. Jude’s needs were many and quite specific, particularly in understanding her comfort and her deteriorating speech. So the desire for one main carer became the priority.

> 在这个时候我成为了她的护理员，他们对那些职业中介总是给他们派去不同的护理人员感到彻底的厌倦了。朱迪的需求很多也很特别，尤其是要理解她的舒适度和日益恶化的说话功能。所以，对一名主要护理员来讲，有意愿做这份工作成为优先考虑条件。

Other carers were employed to cover my time off and thankfully I now had enough experience to train them. No longer able to support her own weight, we used a hydraulic hoist to move Jude to her wheelchair and bed. Each day I saw her abilities lessen and was glad to have arrived while she could still communicate reasonably well, as it enabled me to translate the grunts that were to come later.

> 另外的看护员是雇来替代我的休息时间。谢天谢地，我现在有了足够的经验去训练他们。由于她再也不能支撑自己的重量，我们使用一个液压起重机来把她搬动到她的轮椅和床上。每天我都看得出她的运动功能在降低，很欣慰的是我在她还有一定的沟通能力时就来到了她的身边，这样使得我可以沩后来的看护员翻译她的“咕噜语”。

Jude came from a very wealthy family and had extreme pressure on her as a young adult to marry well and live the life expected of her. Her first car was a luxury model, costing more than most people’s annual salary. She had not stepped foot inside a regular department store until she was in her mid twenties. Designer clothes were all she knew. Her upbringing had guaranteed this.

> 朱迪出身于一个非常富裕的家庭，她的家庭给了她极大的压力，希望她年轻时就嫁得很好，并按照他们的期望去生活。她的第一部车是豪华型的，价值超过大多数人的年薪。直到二十五岁左右，她才开始到普通的百货商店买东西。她认识所有的私人服装设计师。她就是在这样的环境之下成长的。

Yet she had always been a creative and very down-to-earth person. The simple life was all she wanted, she told me. But her parents insisted she go to university, giving her the choice of studying economics or law. There was no other choice, despite her brief mention of wanting to study art. So under pressure and expectation, Jude chose law.

> 然而，她却成为了一个富有创新和务实的人。她告诉我，过简单的生活是她真正所要追求的目标。可是她的父母坚持要她上大学，并要她选择学习经济或者法律。尽管她想要学习艺术，但最终不由得她作任何选择。结果在压力和期望之下，她选择了法律。

Her choice was based on the idea that one day her parents would die and she may be able to then put her knowledge into a better cause, either the arts or community welfare. Things didn’t work out that way though. Her father had passed on by now and it was most likely she would die before her mother. Regardless, she was no longer capable of working anyway.

> 她的选择是基于万一有一天她的父母去世了，那她就可以把她的法律知识应用到更好的自己所爱的事业上，这要么是从事艺术，要么是从事社区福利。然而，一切并非如她所愿。她的父亲刚刚去世了，而就目前情况来看，最有可能的是她会在她母亲之前离开人世。无论怎样，她再也不可能工作了。

Her love of the arts saw her falling in love with Edward, an artist himself. They both told of an instant attraction that had obviously not dwindled at all in the years since. Although both had been a little shy at first, the strength of their mutual attraction gave each of them the confidence to be brave.

> 她对艺术的热爱见证了她如何爱上艺术家爱德华。他们俩都告诉我他们是一见钟情。看得出来，从那以后他们的爱情并没有随着时间的推移而减少。虽然刚开始他们俩都有点腼腆，但是双方相互吸引的力量让他们彼此增强了信心，更加勇敢地去爱。

In no time, they were in love and the whole world fell away as they became each other’s world. Jude’s family was horrified by her choice, as Edward was raised in a lower-class family and was happy with a simple life, pursuing his art. He was actually quite a successful artist. But he was not a white-collar worker and this was never going to be good enough for Jude’s parents.

> 很快他们就深爱了，外面的整个世界消失了，他们只活在他们俩自己的世界之中。朱迪的选择让她的家人感到惊慌，因爱德华生长在低阶层家庭，他喜欢简单的生活和只追求他的艺术。他实际上是一位很成功的艺术家。然而，他不是白领阶层，仅凭这一点将永远不会令朱迪的父母满意。

Sadly she was made to choose between her parents and Edward, so she chose Edward. Of course, she laughed. It was never a decision. She loved Edward with all of her heart, as he did her. Jude was then completely ostracised from her family.

> 令她伤心的是她要在家庭和爱德华之间做出选择，结果她选择了爱德华。那是当然的。讲到这里她笑了。这是从来就不需要做决定的事情。她全身心地爱着爱德华，爱德华也是深爱着她。到后来，朱迪完全跟她的家庭疏远了。

A few close friends remained from earlier years. But she was moving in a different, happier, and more accepting world now, so enjoyed the new friendships that entered her life too. A few years later, Jude and Edward welcomed their little girl, Layla into the world. Every effort was made again to reconcile with her parents, as she wanted them to know their granddaughter.

> 开头几年还跟一些紧密的朋友保持联系。而如今她搬迁到一个跟她以前的生活截然不同的地方，一个更加快乐和包容的世界，她也享受着这里的新友情。几年以后，朱迪和爱德华迎来了他们的第一个女儿莱拉。为了希望她的父母知道他们有了外孙女，她再次做出种种努力跟她的父母和解。

Jude’s father finally conceded and came to enjoy a relationship of love and quality with his dear granddaughter before he passed. His relationship with Jude also improved. While he was polite to Edward, Jude’s father still struggled with the knowledge that an artist had won his daughter’s heart. So it was not a close relationship. However, it was as a result of his relationship with Layla, Jude’s father purchased this harbour-side mansion for them all, much to her mother’s disgust.

> 最后，朱迪的父亲认同了她，在他去世之前，经常过来跟他可爱的外孙女玩耍以享受天伦之乐，他跟外孙女建立起了充满爱的亲密关系。当然他跟朱迪的关系也有所改善。不过朱迪的父亲对爱德华只是礼节性的应付，他仍然纠结着，总是对一个艺术家赢得了女儿的心有所介怀，所以他不愿与他有紧密的关系。然而，由于疼爱莱拉，朱迪的父亲在港湾边他们买了一栋豪宅，她的母亲非常反感这件事。

Things had been moving along well, they told me, until Jude had started to become clumsy, to the point it could not be ignored anymore. These stories were told in unison from Jude and Edward and I suspected this would have been the case, even if she were not struggling with the illness. They were just so close as a couple. Their love was both inspiring and heartbreaking for me to witness. These were people of my own generation.

> 他们告诉我，他们的生活一切都很顺利，直到朱迪的行动开始变得笨拙到了不可忽视的地步。朱迪和爱德华分别给我讲的这些故事是那么的相似，我猜想，即使朱迪没有跟疾病搏斗，他们依然会是这样，他们之间非常的亲密。他们的爱情既激励了我，又让我见证了心碎的感觉。他们跟我属于同辈人。

Hours of deep and honest conversation unfolded between us all. The acceptance of death at such an age was one we spoke of too. It is easy for us all to assume we will live forever. But life doesn’t work that way.

> 我们三人之间花了很多时间展开了许多开诚布公且有深度的谈话。在这样的年龄接受死亡也是我们谈话的内容。对我们每个人来讲，假设我们将会永远活着是不难的。但是，生活不会以人们的意志转移。

Through the storms of life, some young ones will always go. Like flowers blossoming, not yet ripened into fruit, they will be taken away before they can ever realise their full potential. Others will make it through to full maturity and go out at their best. Still others will live past their prime and slowly degenerate over the years.

> 经过生活暴风雨的洗涤，某些年轻人将会永远地离去。就像花朵开放一样，不是每朵花都会成为果实，在他们能够意识到自己的各种潜力之前就将花渐凋谢、零落成泥。而另外的则可以顺利生长，达到完全的成熟并以最佳状态走向社会。还有一些虽可以正常度过风华正茂的少年时期，却在以后的日子里机体慢慢地衰退。

While it is often referred to as dying before their time, it isn’t really. We all go when it is our own time. Millions of people are not destined to live a long life. It is the assumption we will all live forever, or at least until a very advanced age, that brings so much shock and despair when someone young passes. But this is actually a natural part of life in all species. Some young ones die, some middle aged ones die, and others don’t die until old age.

> 人们通常把这种状况称为“提前死亡”或者“英年早逝”并力此感到遗憾，其实亦非尽然。我们每个人都会在人生的不同时间离开人世。并不是所有人注定会活得很长久。我们总是假设我们将会活到永远或者至少活到非常高的年龄，当出现年英早逝就会给人们带来非常大的震惊和绝望。地球上的所有生物都是这样的，这事实上是生命的自然属性。有些人年纪轻轻就去世了，有的人到了中年去世，而多数人到了老年才去世。

Of course, it is heartbreaking to see young ones go when it appears they had their whole lives ahead of them. Some of my own friends have lost young children and I have been there through their heartache, some of which has never left. But these children or young adults were not here for such a long life. They come in, shine bright, and are remembered with purity for all they gave during their brief time.

> 当然，看到那些年轻人在他们的前面还有很多的生活需要去享受的时候就离开了人世的确令人心碎。我自己的一些朋友就有失去年幼孩子的经历，有些孩子刚一出生就没有活下来，在他们心痛的时候，我陪伴着他们。然而，这些孩子和青年人并不是了长寿才来到这个世界上。他们降临到世上，在其短暂的生命中，散发出耀眼的光芒，给他们周围的所有人留下纯洁的印象。

Even though Jude had made it all the way through to her forties with good health, it also would have been easy to think how wrong it was that such a good woman was now dying at only forty-four. But she and Edward had come to accept this, both just grateful they had met and known the love they did. They had also been blessed by bringing Layla into the world.

> 朱迪四十岁以前的身体一直都是很健康的，如今突然病成这个样子不难觉得这是上帝所犯的多么大的错误啊，一位如此美丽的女人怎么可以在四十四岁就临近死亡。然而，她和爱德华都能接受这个现实，双方都感谢他们遇上了对方和懂得他们付出的爱情，并感谢上帝赐给了他们莱拉。

In that regard Jude was peaceful in a way, knowing she’d had the honour of guiding this delightful little girl through her first nine years. Naturally though, there was also heartache as she would not be around to watch her little girl become a woman, and for the pain Layla may suffer in losing her mum. But it helped Jude a lot knowing that her daughter had a loving dad to help her further on her way.

> 在这方面，朱迪始终保持一种平和的心态，她很庆幸她能指引这个令人喜欢的小女孩走过她的第一个九年时间。同时，很自然的，这件事也让她心痛，她不能在女儿身边看着她从一个小女孩长大成为一个女人。莱拉也会因为失去母亲而痛苦。不过，朱迪也知道，她的女儿在今后的人生道路上有爱她的父亲帮助她，陪伴她。

By now Jude had lost her independence and mobility completely, but her greatest frustration was that she was losing her speech. The thing she feared most, she told me one night as I repositioned her in bed, was when she would no longer be able to convey she was in pain and would have to lie there tolerating it. I thought about how difficult life can be and how different all of our lessons are.

> 现在，朱迪已经完全失去了独立生活能力和肢体行动能力，而最让她沮丧的是她正在失去说话能力。有一天晚上，当我帮助她在床上重新调整姿势的时候，她告诉我，最让她感到害怕的是在她再也不能够向我们传达她的疼痛之时，她还得躺在床上忍受疼痛。我想象得到这可是多么艰难的日子，与我们那些所有的经验教训有多么的不同。

What an awful way to see your last weeks or months through, to have consciousness but no ability to communicate. On top of that, to be lying in pain but no one realising or knowing the exact way to ease it. This must happen the world over for sufferers of other diseases too, like stroke or brain injuries. Gosh, what a way to be. It certainly put my own life back into perspective.

> 真的是太可怕了，你在生命的最后几周或者几个月里头脑十分清晰却不能够跟人交流。最主要的是，你极其痛苦地躺在床上，而没有人能够完全地意识到或者知道如何去缓解你的痛。恐怕全世界患有其他疾病的人也都是这样的，比如中风和脑部损伤患者。老天啊，这是怎么啦！

Each day I heard Jude’s speech deteriorating a little more. Some days it was still reasonably good, quite audible. Other days, it was only because we knew each other and I worked intuitively that I was able to follow what she was saying. On days like this, Jude sometimes resorted to using a special computer program she had. Between the eyes of some glasses she wore, made especially for such use, there was a laser that would reach letters on the computer screen. Jude would pause on the letter long enough for it to type the letter, then move onto the next one.

> 每天我都听见朱迪的说话功能一点一点地衰退。有的时候还不错，还听得懂。另外有的时候，由于互相了解对方，我也仅凭着直觉还可以按照她的意思去做。在这些日子里，有时候朱迪采用一种特殊的电脑软件。在她的两个眼睛之间带上一个特别眼镜，里面有个激光器可以抓到字母然后显示在电脑屏幕上。当朱迪利用激光在键盘上的某个字母停留片刻，那个字母就出现在电脑屏幕上，然后她又移动到另外一个字母，又显示出另外的字母。

Then after a couple of letters were written, a choice of words would appear, and so on. It was a slow process for sure, but it at least enabled her to be heard. I silently thanked those who had created such an opportunity for her by developing this program. The time would come soon enough though, when Jude would no longer be able to move her head to even do this.

> 随着几个字母的出现，一个单词就出现了。虽然速度较慢，但是至少我们可以知道她要说什么。我默默地感谢那些发明这种技术的人，使得朱迪有机会跟别人交流。然而，很快的朱迪的头再也不可能移动，这样的沟通机会最后也失去了。

So on the good days, I listened as much as possible while Jude talked. There was much she wanted to convey. Holding a juice to her lips I waited, as she took one slow sip at a time, enabling her to keep talking. One main point in particular she needed to make and did so over and over. “We need to be brave enough to express our feelings,” she said. Very fitting, I thought, considering my own journey so far.

> 所以，在她还比较好的时候，我尽可能的多听听她讲话。她要传达的内容非常的多。我拿着一瓶果汁放在她的嘴唇边等待着，让她慢慢喝一小口，也让她能够继续说话，这其中一个主要原因是她需要这样反复一遍一遍地进行练习。她说，“我们需要足够的勇气表达自己的感受。”联想到目前为止我自己的人生旅途，我觉得这句话跟我的经历太相符了。

Even though she had lost the relationship with her mother by choosing to be with Edward, she was happy knowing she’d at least been brave enough to make that choice, one she’d never regretted. She longed to share her feelings with her mother now though, as her mum had never known Jude as a mother herself. Acknowledging such an opportunity may never come, Jude had already written to her mother some time ago. The letter waited in Edward’s office drawer.

> 为了选择爱德华，即使她失去了跟母亲的关系，她还是很高兴地明白至少她已经拥有了足够的勇气去做这样的选择，她为此从未后悔过。然而，现在她渴望跟她的母亲分享她的所有感受，而她的母亲从未知道她也做了母亲。她承认，可能永远没有这样的机会，不久前她还是给母亲写了一封信。这封信一直放在爱德华办公室的抽屉里。

Jude’s mum knew of the illness. But she was still in a place of stubbornness and un-forgiveness, unable to visit her dying daughter. “We must learn to express our feelings now,” Jude emphasised. “Not when it is too late. None of us ever know when it will be too late. Tell people you love them. Tell them you appreciate them. If they can’t accept your honesty or react in a different way to how you hoped, it doesn’t matter. What matters is you have told them.”

> 朱迪的母亲知道她生病了，但是她还是很倔强和不肯原谅，她不可能来看望她那临近死亡的女儿。“我们现在必须学会表达自己的感受。”朱迪这样强调地说。“何时表达都不会晚。没有一个人会知道什么时候才算最晚。告诉人们你爱他们。告诉他们你谢谢他们。即使他们不肯接受你的诚实或者以与你所希望的方式来回应你，这也都不重要，重要的是你已经告诉了他们你的感受。”

Jude said this was just as important for those who were passing as for those who would remain behind. Those passing need to know everything has been said. It brings peace, she said. If those who are left behind can muster the courage to express their feelings honestly too, then they will not carry this regret to their own passing. Nor will they have to live with the guilt that is carried if someone they loved has passed and things have gone unsaid.

> 她说，表达感受既对那些即将离去的人很重要，也对那些活着的人很重要。她还说，那些临近死亡的人需要知道该说的话都已经说了，带给他们的是平和的离去。如果那些还活着的人能鼓起勇气也诚实地表达他们的感受，那么，他们将来就不会带着他们的遗憾而离去。他们也将不会因如果他们爱过的某个人已经离去而没有说出的话也随之飘散而永远内疚地活下去。

What had made getting this point across even more important for Jude was that she had lost a friend unexpectedly a year earlier. It had shaken her world up immensely. Tracey had been an effervescent woman, the life of any gathering. She was well loved by everyone due to her enormous heart, and held a total lack of judgment towards others.

> 对朱迪而言，促使她觉得这一点越发重要的是一年前，她意外地失去了一位好朋友。这使得她的世界发生了巨大的变化。特雷西曾经是一个充满活力的人，喜欢参加生活中的任何聚会。由于她有一颗博大的胸怀和完全的接纳别人，每个人都很爱她。

“It is too easy to get caught up in life and not spend as much time with people you love, whether that be family or friends. But we really must get back to relationships and honesty. People don’t realise how important this is, until they are dying themselves or living with the guilt after someone else has died,” Jude told me. She said how there is no need for guilt at all if we have truly made our best efforts to express our feelings and to spend time with those we love. But we need to stop thinking those we love will be around forever.

> 朱迪告诉我：“无论是家庭还是朋友，我们太容易被卷入到忙碌的生活之中，没有更多的时间与你爱的人在一起。然而，我们必须回到人际关系的原始状态和诚实当中。人们往往要等到他们自己即将离开人世，或者某个人已经离去之后并带着内疚而活着的时候，才意识到这些有多么的重要。”她认为，如果我们尽了最大努力去表达我们的感受，并有尽可能多的时间与我们爱的人在一起，那就完全没有必要内疚什么。不过，我们需要停止认为我们所爱的人将永远会留在我们的身边。

It is over in a flash, she reminded me. Jude was grateful she’d had time to say her own goodbyes, but emphasised that not everyone receives the blessing of time to express these feelings at the end. In fact millions don’t, as they depart suddenly and unexpectedly. Even though expressing her feelings about the love she felt for Edward had ruined her relationship with her mother, Jude was glad she’d had the courage to be honest.

> 她提醒我，光阴似箭，生命一晃就过去了。朱迪很感恩自己还有时间和机会跟爱的人道别。她强调，不是每个人都会得到恩惠有时间在生命的最后时刻向亲人表达感情。事实上，成干上万的人在突然的或意外离去的时候，他们失去了任何表达的机会。即使她表达了对爱德华的爱意已经毁了她与母亲的关系，朱迪还是很高兴因为她具有勇气成诚实的人。

Not only did it allow her to know the fullness of love she and Edward still shared, she was peaceful knowing she had been true to her own heart. It also showed her how much she was under the control of her parents until then, particularly her mother. If a relationship is based on control she said, how can the other person ever know a truly healthy relationship with that person anyway? If this was the only kind of relationship on offer, she decided she was better off without it.

> 她的勇气和诚实不仅让她懂得了她和爱德华依然分享着他们圆满的爱情，而且她也平静地知道了她已经真实地对待了自己的内心。这也显示出了在那之前，她是多么的受到他的父母尤其是母亲的严厉控制。她说，如果一种人际关系是建立在控制之上，那么，另外那个人怎么可能知道与这个人的关系是真正健康和正常的呢？如果说只有这种关系可以维系的话，她决定最好没有它。

But having tried to communicate with her mother, Jude said she would be dying free of guilt. She’d had the courage to express herself. Thankfully, the same had been the case for her with her friend Tracey. Jude had always been very honest and though the shock of losing Tracey had been enormous, she was again guilt-free. Only a few days before losing her friend, they’d had lunch together. When they hugged goodbye, Jude had told Tracey how much she loved her and valued their friendship.

> 因为已经尽力尝试了与她的母亲沟通，朱迪说她将可以没有任何内疚地离开人世。她已经有了勇气表白自己。谢天谢地，她这样做跟她当年对待她的好朋友特雷西是同样的情形。朱迪总是非常的直率，尽管失去特雷西给了她巨大的震惊，但她依然没有内疚感。因就在失去这个朋友几天前，他们一起共进了午餐。当他们互相拥抱道别的时候，朱迪告诉了特雷西她有多么的爱她，她们的友情是多么的有价值。

It was not the case for most of her friend’s family or other friends though. Tracey had been such a bright person that it was hard to ever imagine she would not be around. Then her life was taken suddenly in a car crash. The ripples of shock and guilt still continued just as strongly in Jude’s circle of friends, a year later.

> 大多数特雷西的家人和其他的朋友都没有这种机会。特雷西曾经是非常快乐的人，很难令人想象她会永远离去。她是在车祸中去世的。出事之后一年多，那惊骇和内疚的余波一直强烈持续在朱迪的朋友圈。

“She had changed people’s lives and they had never told her so. Tracey wasn’t the sort to need the confirmation, no. But people have to live with their own lack of effort afterwards and I have seen this guilt turn toxic in people since, as they battle with how differently they could have done things.” I could understand this of course. “Also,” Jude said, “Although Tracey didn’t need the validation, she would have loved to have heard that encouragement from others. She was so open and beautiful. And now she is gone.”

> “是她改变了大家的生活，而他们从未这样告诉她。特雷西不是那种需要别人肯定的人，她不是。可是，人们往往在事后才不得不带着内疚而过日子，他们后悔当初缺乏努力说出自己内心的真实感受。我见过这样的内疚感从此转变成了某种毒素存留于人们的心中，他们一直在跟自己挣扎，如果事发之前他们能够这样做了，一切都将是怎样的不同。”我当然能够理解这一点。“还有，”朱迪继续说：“虽然特雷西不需要这种认同，但是，她肯定会很乐意听到别人给她的鼓励。她非常的开明和美丽，可惜现在她走了。”

I naturally agreed with her that sharing feelings and honesty was important. Life was certainly throwing those lessons at me already, even more so now as Jude and I talked. She was a beautiful woman, still naturally elegant, despite not being able to hold herself well anymore.

> 自然的，我很同意她的这些观点，分享感受和真诚非常重要。生活已经给我了这些教训，如今我和朱迪谈话之时，我更加体会到了这一点。她是一个漂亮的女人，尽管再也不能好好地掌控自己，她仍然流露出那种自然的优雅。

Her mouth drooled at times and her clothing had to be more practical than stylish. But her spirit and remnants of who she once was, still remained with its own radiance. Smiling at her and in agreement with her opinion, I shared my thoughts. “Yes. So much is held back by pride, apathy, or fear of reprisal or humiliation. But it also takes a lot of courage sometimes Jude and we are not always strong enough to do this.”

> 她的嘴角经常流口水，身穿的衣服只是讲究舒适，谈不上什么风格。但是，她的精神状态和部分外表仍然散发着她自身的光芒。我向她微笑以表明同意她的观点，我也跟她讲述我的想法：“是的，太多的骄傲、自尊、冷漠或者害怕报复、怕丢脸阻碍了我们。朱迪，有的时候，这的确需要有巨大的勇气，我们不是经常有足够强大的勇气来这样做。”

“Yes, it takes courage Bronnie,” Jude continued. “That’s the point I am trying to make. It takes courage to express your feelings, particularly if you are not doing OK and need assistance, or if you’ve never expressed honest feelings to someone you love and don’t know how it will be received. But the more you practice sharing your feelings, whatever they are, the better things become. Pride is such a waste of time. Honestly, look at me now. I can’t even wipe my own bum. What does it matter? We are all human. We are allowed to be vulnerable too. It is a part of the process.”

> “是的，邦妮，的确需要鼓起勇气，”她继续地说：“那就是我要说的重点。表达你的感受是需要勇气的，尤其是如果你的一切状况不是很好和需要一些协助的时候，或者你以前从未坦诚地对你爱的人表达感受以及不知道将有怎样的回复的时候更加需要勇气。你越是做到了分享你的感受，无论是什么样的感受，事情越会变得更好。骄傲和自尊完全是浪费时间。老实说，看看我现在，我都甚至不能擦自己的屁股。但那有什么关系？我们都是人。

Leading up to the time at Jude and Edward’s, life had been particularly hard for me. I decided to share some of this with Jude, as it was relevant to how hard it can be at times to share our feelings. The palliative work had slowed down for a while. It often came in those waves of all or nothing. This didn’t bother me, as my creative work benefitted then instead.

> 我们也可以是脆弱的。这是人类生老病死过程的一部分。”随着在朱迪和爱德华家里的时间渐渐增多，我感觉生活尤其艰难。我决定跟朱迪说出我的一些想法，主要是关系到很多时候我们之间如何的难以分享我们的感受。有一阵子姑息护理方面的工作放慢了。时常是在完全有或者无之间波动。这对我并没有干扰，我的创作工作所带来的利益可以替代护理工作。

After almost two months of hardly any work though, things were starting to get a bit difficult and there was no work on the horizon. Any money I earned was usually invested back into my creative work in some way, so there was not a lot to fall back on. But having survived it before, I was never too perturbed about this.

> 如果两个月几乎没有工作之后，我的生活就会开始出现困难，然而目前还没有工作机会出现。我所挣的任何钱通常都被我以某种方式拿去投资了我的创作工作，所以，还没有得到很多的回报。不过，以前都是这样生存下来的，我也就不为此焦虑太多。

House-sits likewise came and went. Sometimes I would have very little notice of where I was going next, only knowing when the owners were due to return. Usually though, a house would present itself at the last minute. During stronger times, I did enjoy this risk and excitement to a degree. The adrenalin certainly flowed. It would happen relatively often, where someone would call me in a panic and ask if I could look after their home, starting tomorrow for example, as they had just been called away suddenly. The relief that came from those calls always brought huge sighs and smiles. Such occasions would save us both.

> 看守房子的工作同样也是时有时无。有的时候我很少在意下一步我会去哪里，我只知道什么时候房主要该回来了。通常，新的房主会在最后一刻出现。在我比较坚强的期间，我会享受这种危机，某种程度上还感到兴奋。我体内的肾上腺素分泌旺盛。相对的，有一些状况时有发生，比如，某个人会慌张地打电话给我，问我是否明天开始可以为他照看房子，他们要突然离开。那些电话让我立刻得到放松，总是带给我一声大大的叹气和微笑。如此的偶然性解救了我们双方。

Sometimes clients worked in with other friends in the house-sit network to ensure they wouldn’t miss out on me being available. So they would plan to go on holidays the same day their friends were coming back, knowing I would then be free. On these occasions, I was sometimes booked for months in advance. Naturally, I liked this. It made life much easier.

> 有的时候，客户们把需要照看房子圈内的其他朋友介绍给我，并安排日程确定他们不会错过我的时间。所以，我的客户会特意计划他们休假离开的那一天正好是他们的朋友回来的日子，这样我可以接着工作。在这种情况下，有时我提前几个月就安排好了我的看房子工作。自然的，我很喜欢这样，这使得我的生活更加容易。

There were times though, when I was unable to find anywhere to look after for a few days, or a week or two, in between scheduled house-sits. So I would either leave town and visit someone in the country, enjoying the break. Or if I had a specific client who I didn’t want to leave, I would crash out in a friend’s spare room or on their couch temporarily.

> 也有的时候，在我的照看房子排期之间我也有几天或者一至两周的空余时间。那时，我可能会离开城镇去乡下拜访某个人，享受这段休息时间。或者如果我有一个我不愿意离开的特别客户，我就会晚上到我朋友家过夜，或是暂时睡在他们的沙发上。

Initially this was easy enough. But after a few years of this pattern, I began to dread asking and felt like I was wearing out my welcome. My friends said I wasn’t. They supported me and understood me well enough to know it wasn’t forever. When I had settled home bases years earlier, my homes always had visitors, but for me, learning to receive was so much harder than giving.

> 起初还比较顺利。可是这样几年之后，我开始畏惧向朋友提出这样的要求，感觉好像我不会受欢迎。虽然我的朋友说了我是受欢迎的，因为他们想要支持我和非常理解我，知道我不会永远是这样的状况。

Having to ask friends repeatedly if I could stay left me feeling absolutely hopeless. While I had worked through a lot of my past wounds, enabling me to have compassion for others, it was still taking me a lot of work and pain to transform my thoughts towards myself. Decades of negative patterns were being undone and it was a slow process, to change my thinking completely. New and positive seeds had been planted and in many ways, were sprouting in my life. But I was yet to eradicate all of the old seeds, so they would still sometimes surface.

> 然而，不得不反复请求朋友我能否临时入住让我产生无望感。通过以前经历的很多伤痛，让我容易对别人有恻隐之心，我依然需要花很多功夫在痛苦中转变自己的想法。几十年的负面模式并没有完全消除，还需一个缓慢的过程才能完全改变我的思维方式。虽然新的正面种子已经种植下去，在我的生活中正以许多的方式生长发芽，但是，我还没有根除所有的旧种子，因此，它们有时候还是会冒出来兴风作浪。

On this particular occasion the work had stopped ages ago, the money had pretty much run out and I was feeling hopeless all over again. I rang my closest friend and asked if I could stay. But she was going through something herself and it was just not possible. This wasn’t about me at all. It was her stuff and her life. But because of my thinking and emotional state at the time, I took it as a total rejection and felt even worse for having put her in the position of having to say no.

> 在这段特殊时期，工作也停止了很长一段时间，钱也渐渐花光了，我又感觉到无望和惨淡。我打电话给我最亲密的朋友，询问我是否可以住几天。可她自己也正在解决一些事情，我的入住没有可能性，完全不是我的原因，是她个人的事情，是她的生活出了问题。然而，由于我那时的思想和情绪状态，我把朋友的状况完全理解她在拒绝和排斥我，以为她故意对我说不行，我的感觉更加糟糕。

I reluctantly called a few other friends but sorry, full house with interstate visitors, another away, and another consumed in a work project that needed total concentration. I didn’t have the money to leave town and get back without having to borrow some, which would have just left me feeling more hopeless anyway. So I accepted I would be sleeping in my car.

> 我又勉强地给其他朋友打电话，都说对不起没有空位，都被外州来的客人占满了，又一次机会没有了，又一次需要全神贯注打算今后的生活。我没有离开这个城镇和返回的路费，除非向别人借，这样会让我感觉更加的绝望。所以，我接受了现实，我不得不睡在我的车上。

This wasn’t an issue years earlier when I had the jeep and was on the road travelling. In fact, there was nowhere I would have preferred sleeping than in the back of that old car, on the comfy bed in there. But not in the rice bubble, a car so small I couldn’t even straighten out my legs when I tried to lie down.

> 几年前，当我还有一辆吉普车到处跑的时候这根本不是问题。事实上，我宁愿睡在我的那辆老式车后面，里面有舒适的床。但不是我现在这辆“水稻泡”，它太小了，当我试图躺下的时候，我的腿都不能伸直。

It was also without curtains or privacy and was the middle of winter. I couldn’t think of anyone I could call though without feeling even worse by asking. As much as I was a little scared to be sleeping on the city streets so exposed, I was sort of resigned to it, that this is what a hopeless person has to do sometimes.

> 它也没有窗帘或者遮挡的，那是隆冬时节。我不想再给任何人打电话了，请求别人会给我带来更坏的感觉。现在我将睡在街上是无疑的了，我差不多有些害怕，我也只好听天由命吧，有时候一个走投无路的人也不得不这样做。

Driving around before dark, I checked out a few options of places that looked relatively safe and suitable. I also needed to consider I might need to go to the toilet. Freaking people out by piddling on their front lawn in the middle of the night was not the kind of attention I needed, on top of my existing emotions.

> 在天黑之前，我开着车在附近转了转，察看几个看起来比较安全和合适的地方以供选择。我还需要考虑的是要停在有厕所的地方。除了我现有的各种情绪之外，一些奇怪的人半夜在他们的前草坪撒尿的地方不是我需要考虑的。

The days are long when you are homeless and trying to stay out of visibility. You have to be up and out of the way at sunrise and cannot settle in until everyone else has gone home and settled in themselves. In the meantime of course, you are homeless so you can’t go home and wait. Yes, these were long days and the nights were very uncomfortable, achingly cold, and lonely.

> 当你无家可归、露宿街头又尽量不要让人看见的时候，你会觉得日子多么的漫长。你必须在太阳升起的时候爬起来，然后离开，你也只有等待人们都回家入睡的时候才可以躺下。在这个过程中，因为你无家可归，你不能回家，只有等待。是啊，伴随你的是漫长的白天、煎熬的夜晚、穿心的寒冷以及苍凉的孤独。

One night I went to a café where I heard some music, staying as late as possible on my one cup of tea. I felt like the old man in the Ralph McTell song, ‘Streets of London’, trying to keep his one cup of tea going all night to enable him to stay indoors. How ironic, I thought, that this had been one of the very first songs I ever learned on the guitar.

> 有天晚上，我走进了一家咖啡店，在里面，我要了一杯茶，聆听着音乐，可以在那里尽可能呆到打烊的时候。我感觉就好像是拉尔夫•麦克泰尔歌曲“伦敦的街道”中的老人，试图整晚坐在那里慢慢品尝他的一杯茶，以便能够让他呆在室内。我想，这是多么讽刺啊，这首歌是我学习吉他时弹唱的第一首歌。

Turning up at the council toilets near the beach at sunrise, I would wait for them to open. Then I would have a wash, clean my teeth, and use the toilet, all the while enduring the scowls of the council worker who had unlocked the door. I think he saw me as a camper, freeloading, or something. But there was nothing he could have thought of me that was any worse than what I was already thinking of myself.

> 太阳出来的时候，我来到海滩附近政府理事会的厕所，等待他们来开门。然后，我在里面洗脸、刷牙和上厕所，整个过程我都忍受着那个把厕所门大打开，皱着眉头的工人。我想他肯定把我当成了露营者、爱占小便宜或者其他什么人。然而，无论他把我当作什么样的人，都没有比我已把自己想象的那么糟糕。

So I really didn’t care. And one of the blessings already in place from my time with the dying was that by now, I genuinely didn’t care what others thought of me anymore. As it was, I had enough to deal with just with own head. Another night I went to the Hare Krishna’s ‘Feed the Hungry’ program. Whenever I had money, I was always generous with it.

> 所以，我真的不在乎他怎样想。现在，我有这样心态是我在垂死挣扎之时的福分之一，我真的再也不在乎别人怎么看我。事实上，我只需要战胜我自己。另外一个晚上，我去了哈瑞奎师那的“饥饿供食”计划点。以前我有钱的时候，我总是慷慨捐钱到这里。

Queuing up now, I found more irony in my situation as I had often thrown ten or twenty dollars into their bucket for this very program, if I ever saw them trying to raise funds. I liked the Hare Krishnas. They were vegetarians, played happy music, and fed hungry people. That was enough for me. But now I was a recipient of their goodwill and it was rather humbling.

> 现在我跟着人群排着队等待食物，我发现更多讽刺的事情发生在我的身上，以前每当我看到他们为了这个计划尽量在筹备基金的时候，我时常会放十块或者二十块钱到他们的木桶里。我喜欢哈瑞奎师那。他们都是素食者，演奏着快乐的音乐，为饥饿者提供食物。如今，我成为了他们施舍的对象。这是多么的令人惭愧啊。

Then one morning, I sat on a rock by the harbour, praying for strength, endurance, and a miracle. Just then a pod of dolphins came by and one flipped itself out of the water in play. My life felt so serious until that moment, so it gave me a little bit of hope again. I then thought of some friends who lived further out and decided to call them and ask if I could stay.

> 随后的一天早上，我坐在海湾的一个石头上，祈祷着老天给我力量、耐力和奇迹。正在此刻，一群海豚游了过来，一只海豚在水面上快速翻滚着玩耍起来。本来我感到很沉重的心情在这一刻突然变得轻松起来，它们为我再次点燃一束希望之光。然后我又想到了一些住在远一点的朋友，决定给她们打电话询问我是否可以暂时留住。

They were always lovely people. But my sense of unworthiness and hopelessness had not enabled me to ask any more people for help, or even think of anyone else I could ask. I had not had the courage to express my feelings even though I could have very honestly just said to these sweet people, “Look, I’m feeling crap. But may I please come and stay for a little while?”

> 他们都是很可爱的人。然而可悲的是，我的自卑感和无望感已经不能够让我再去向别人请求帮助，甚至连思索下有哪些可以请求帮助的人都不可以。我已经没有勇气表达自己的感受，即使我可以非常坦诚地对那些善良的人说：“看看我，我感觉自己是废物。可不可以让我进去呆上一会儿。”

So with a brighter resolve, I went for a walk around the harbour. Before I had the opportunity to call my friends though, my phone rang and it was Edward, asking if I was free to come and be Jude’s carer and could I please begin immediately? There was also a beautiful apartment on the property available for me if I needed.

> 然而，现在最好的解决办法就是在港湾周围散散步，调整一下心情。就在我找机会给我朋友打电话之前，我的电话铃声响了，是爱德华打来的，问我是否有时间做朱迪的护理员，并能否立刻开始。如果我需要的话，他们也有一间漂亮的房间供我使用。

That night I lay with legs fully outstretched again, no longer aching with cramps and cold. A snug duvet kept me warm after my nurturing bath. I’d eaten a healthy meal with three delightful people and I was earning money again. How quickly life can change!

> 那一晚，我的双腿又可以完全伸直躺着睡觉，再也不为抽筋和寒冷而头痛了。洗完滋润的热水澡后，一床舒适的羽绒被温暖着我。我既跟他们三个讨人喜欢的人一起吃着健康的食物，又还可以再次挣到钱。生活转变得如此之快啊！

I could look at this time and say it was because the work dried up, or the house-sits did. That’s what happened physically anyway. But it was a situation I had created through my own lack of self-worth and by nurturing old seeds that no longer served me. Obviously there were new seeds being sewn too, as at other times I was starting to live an abundant, amazing lifestyle. Learning to undo these old patterns in my head was taking time though and I had made it harder on myself by not being able to ask for help.

> 审视这个时期我的经历，我可以说，那都是因为没有任何工作机会，是躯体上的问题。同时，也是由于自己缺乏自我价值感而滋长了我的那些旧种子生长并制造出来的种种状况。显然的，新的种子也开始发芽，如同前些时候一样，我又开始了丰富而惊人的生活方式。学会废除我头脑里的那些陈旧的观念和模式需要花费时间，通过不去求人给自己制造困境使得我更难做到废除旧思想。

When another lull in house-sitting appeared at a later date, the first thing I did was to phone the friends I had thought of that morning with the dolphins. They welcomed me into their spare room with loving joy and excitement. Allowing goodness in was possible for me again. I was still learning how to express my feelings, but I was getting there.

> 当后来另外短暂的看房子工作机会出现的时候，我做的第一件事就是打电话给我的朋友，告诉他们我想到了那天早上跟海豚在一起所发生的一切。他们欢迎我入住他们的客房跟他们一起享受爱的喜悦和狂欢。对我而言，接受别人对我的任何善意行为再次成为了可能性，我现在完全做到了。

I told Jude how openness was a learning curve for me, as I had become so shut down in the past. So I appreciated her opinion and the opportunity to discuss this all so honestly. “We all need the reminder Bronnie. Everyone holds things in that need to be said, whether it is what people want to hear or not. We must express our feelings in order to grow. It helps everyone in one way or another, even if they don’t realise it. Above all else, honesty works.”

> 我告诉朱迪，由于我在过去自我非常的封闭，如今，学会弯曲身段向他人低头正我打开新局面。所以，我很欣赏她的观点，并感谢有机会跟她这么坦诚地讨论这些问题。“邦妮，我们每个人需要有人提醒。无论人们愿意或者不愿意听，每个人都需要把自己的经验教训说出来。为了自己的成长，每个人必须表达自己的感受。尽管他们没有意识到，事实上，说出内心感受会以这样或者那样的方式帮助到别人。其中，坦诚高于一切。”

Smiling, I looked out at the boats on the harbour as the full moon shone beautifully on the water. It was a magnificent setting. Jude returned to the subject of guilt and how we have a choice to not create it by honestly expressing our feelings as they surface. Then it is never too late, especially if someone we love passes unexpectedly. It also enables us to be free of constraints, as we once were as children. We should never feel guilty for expressing our feelings and we should never make someone else feel guilty if they have found the courage to do so.

> 我微笑了，望着窗外的港湾，一轮美丽的明月照耀在水中，各种船只在那里游荡。这是多么华丽的景色。朱迪又把话题转到了内疚：我们如何通过坦诚，表达已经出现的我们内心的感受以选择不要留下内疚感。表达内心感受永远要尽早，尤其是如果我们所爱的人可能会意外的去世。在我们曾经还是孩子的时候，我们就能够无拘无束地表达人的情感。我们不应该为表达自己的感受而感到内疚，如果别人找到了勇气来表达自己的感受，我们也不应该使他们感到内疚。

After a couple of months with Jude, her deterioration became so severe she was admitted into a palliative care hospice. Work was flowing for me again back at the agency and a decent length house-sit had presented itself. I dropped into the hospice to see Jude, pleased to be able to catch up with Edward and Layla at the same time. Sitting on the other side of the bed was a lady I’d never met, but it took no time to see the likeness between Jude and her mother.

> 照顾朱迪几个月之后，她的全身衰退变得非常严重，她被允许进入姑息照顾安养院。我又回到职业介绍所等待另外的工作机会，一个相当长时间的照看房子的工作机会自己找到了我。一天，我到那个安养院去看望了朱迪，很高兴同一时间在那里碰到了爱德华和莱拉。坐在床的另一边是一位我从未见过的女士，但是，很快我就看出了朱迪与她母亲长得很像。

Edward had taken it on his own initiative to deliver Jude’s letter to her mother, before his beloved Jude passed over. By now she was unable to talk, but it had all been said in the letter. Jude had told her mother she had loved her, and still did. She wrote of happy memories she held dear and of the positive things she had learned from her mum. The letter contained nothing negative, as Jude hated guilt and wanted her mother to know she was loved, despite the sadness of their relationship.

> 在他心爱的朱迪去世之前，爱德华主动地把朱迪的信交给了她的母亲。如今朱迪不能够讲话，可是她要对母亲说的话全都写在那封信上。朱迪已经告诉她的母亲她过去爱过她，至今依然爱她。她写了许多愉快的回忆，她从母亲那里学到了一些可爱和正面的东西。这封信里没有任何负面内容，朱迪讨厌内疚，尽管他们的关系是不愉快的，她还是想要她的母亲知道女儿爱她。

Jude’s mother had turned up unexpectedly a few days later and had been back every day since, holding Jude’s hand, watching as her daughter’s life was coming to an end. Kissing Jude on the cheek after talking to her for a while, I said my final goodbye and thanked her for everything. “See you when I get there, Jude,” I said through tears and a smile. She grunted back and her eyes smiled, even though her mouth no longer could.

> 几天之后，朱迪的母亲突然出现了，从此以后，每天都会来看望朱迪，并握着朱迪的手，陪着她直到她生命逐渐走向尽头。跟朱迪聊了一会儿，我在朱迪的脸颊上吻了一下，向她表示了最后的告别，感谢她给我的一切。“朱迪，我们在另外一个世界再相见吧。”我含着眼泪和带着微笑对她说了这番话。她咕噜着回复了我，即使她的嘴巴再也不能动，她仍以眼神对我微笑了。

Edward and Layla walked me out to the rice bubble, both of them holding one of my hands each. We were all tearful. But love flowed so honestly that tears didn’t matter. He told me that Jude’s mum had been talking a lot to her and tears had fallen down Jude’s cheeks, particularly when she heard her mother say she loved her. Her mother apologised for having been so judgmental. She admitted to being secretly jealous of her daughter, and of Jude’s courage to step out of the opinions of society, something that had robbed herself of true happiness.

> 爱德华和莱拉送我到我的车旁，他们分别握着我的一只手。我们三个都哭了。没有关系，那是真诚和爱的眼泪。爱德华告诉我，朱迪的母亲已经跟朱迪聊了很多，尤其当她听到母亲说她爱她的时候，朱迪也流下了眼泪。她的母亲为这些年这么执着的偏见向她表示了道歉。她承认自己私下是嫉妒她的女儿，嫉妒女儿有勇气走出社会世俗观念，这些观念有的时候已经掠夺了她自己真正的幸福。

After hugging both Edward and Layla goodbye, I wished them all the very best in life ahead. I thought of beautiful Jude lying there with her mother sitting at her side, and how powerful the force of love really is. My heart was aching but it was also joyful. An email came a couple of years later from Edward, which was a lovely surprise.

> 在拥抱中告别了爱德华和菜拉之后，我祝福他们俩将来生活幸福。我想象着朱迪躺在那里，她的母亲坐在她的旁边，爱的力量真的是如此的伟大。我的心在隐隐作痛，同时也感到喜悦。几年之后我收到了爱德华的电子邮件，带给我了一个惊喜。

Layla and her grandmother had enjoyed some cheerful months of getting to know each other before the older lady had died. He said she was a different woman by then and reminded him of his beautiful Jude at times. When the estates were settled, Edward and Layla decided to leave the city and move up to the mountains, closer to his father and where the air was cleaner. He had met a new lady about a year ago and Layla now had a little sister on the way.

> 在朱迪的母亲去世之前，莱拉和她的外婆在一起住了几个月，享受了一些快乐时光，也增进了双方的了解。他说，朱迪的母亲是一个与众不同的女人，那个时候，她常常让他想起了他那美丽的朱迪。当房产的问题处理好之后，爱德华和莱拉决定离开城市搬迁到山区，这样离他的父亲比较近，空气也更加清新。大约一年以后，他遇到了一位新的女朋友，莱拉现在马上快有一个妹妹了。

My reply included my best of wishes to them all. I was also happy to share with him the things I remembered about Jude: her smile, her patience with the illness, her acceptance, and her determination to get her points across. Guilt is toxic. Expressing our feelings is a necessity for a happy life. I can still remember sitting beside her bed as the full moon shone on the water, with Jude determined to be heard for as long as her voice would allow. She’d made her point and I now know the joy of expressing my feelings, as honestly as that dolphin showed its joy when it flipped up out of the water.

> 我给爱德华回复了邮件，包括我对他们全家幸福的祝福。我也很高兴地告诉了爱德华，我依然记得朱迪，记得她的微笑、她身患疾病的耐心、她的包容，以及表明她的观点的决心——内疚是毒素。为了幸福的生活，表达我们自己的感受是非常有必要的。我至今依然记得我坐在她的床边，满月照耀着水面，只要她的声音还会发出，她就尽力阐明自己的观点。她的观点没有白费，如今我明白了表达自己感受的快乐，当海豚在向上翻转跳出海面的时候，它们是在诚实地展现它们的喜悦。
