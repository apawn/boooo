# Products of our environment · 环境的产物

Anthony was only in his late thirties when we first met one Saturday afternoon. He had dark-blonde curly hair and despite being ill, had a natural look of mischief about him. It was a big change for me to be caring for someone younger. Striking up a friendship was a breeze and despite the circumstances, we enjoyed an element of humour from the start.

> 当我们在一个周六下午第一次见面的时候，安东尼只有三十七八岁。他有着暗金色的卷发，尽管他是病号，却有一张自然流露出来的调皮面孔。照顾一些年轻的病人，对我来讲是一种很大的改变。尽管他是病人，我是看护者，要建立一种友谊还是一件轻而易举的事，因为从一开始我们就被幽默的元素围绕着。

With a younger brother, four younger sisters, and a high profile family in the business world, he had been very indulged throughout his life. Whatever Anthony wanted was his and he used it to his advantage as a younger man. But he also had big shoes to fill due to the financial success of his family. This pressure worked in reverse and despite intelligence and opportunities, he had very low self-esteem. He masked this quite well beneath his humour and mischief. Anthony could not be what his family wanted him to be and as the oldest son in the family, this created a lot of pressure within him.

> 他有一个弟弟、四个妹妹和一个在商业圈中活跃高调的家庭。在生活中他一直被极度地溺爱，无论安东尼想要什么，他都能得到。作一个年轻男人，他完全习惯了他的优越家庭。然而，由于他的家庭在经济上的成就，需要他有足够的能力将来可以去接替这个家族企业。可是，这种压力却起到了反作用。尽管他聪慧又有发展机会，但他的自尊是很低的。他以幽默和调皮把他的所有弱点掩盖得很好。安东尼根本不可能成为他的家庭所希望的那种人，作这个家的长子，他身上的压力太大了。

His years as a young adult were spent driving fast cars, being chased by police, hiring the most expensive working girls, and causing havoc to anyone who crossed his path. This was territorial land between the young men of the wealthy suburbs. Some of the actions Anthony had employed in his past were hardly endearing. But due to being a man of little self worth, he also lived quite recklessly, challenging life to some dangerous levels. One such act left him in hospital with damaged organs and limbs, with the potential of losing his health forever, and the freedom that comes from such health.

> 在刚成年的那几年里，他开高级跑车、被警察追赶、花大钱包养女孩，以及攻击任何超车到他前面的路人。这就是郊外富人区年轻男人们的生活方式。安东尼以前的行为几乎没有一点让人喜欢的。由于极低的自我价值感，他的生活是相当的肆无忌惮及莽撞，经常挑战生活到了某种危险的地步。一次过急行动使得他严重伤害了自己的某些器官和肢体而住进了医院，并潜在性永远失去健康和自由。

The doctors were doing what they could to return his freedom to him but things were not looking hopeful. Anthony was pretty resigned to it though. Already realising he had probably done some permanent damage, he asked the doctors for the next operation to go ahead as soon as possible, so he could know either way. A couple of surgical procedures were done.

> 医生们正在努力恢复他的自由行动的能力，但目前看来希望不大。安东尼也接受了听天由命的结局。他已经意识到可能自己给自己造成了永久性的伤害，他请求医生们尽快计划给他做下一个手术，以至于他能知道每个手术的情况。几个手术就这样完成了。

Then painkillers kept him sleeping for the first week or so, as I sat beside his bed in the hospital room. After that it was a case of wait and see, hopefully allowing for a gradual recovery. We fell into the habit of me reading to him. It started one evening when he asked me what I was reading. After spending that time living in the Middle East previously, I wanted to spend more time there.

> 随后，当我守在他的医院病房床边的时候，术后的止痛药使得他整个星期或者更长的时间一直处于睡眠状态。接下来就是等待和观察，希望能逐渐恢复健康。我们俩都习惯了由我他朗读书籍。那是从一个下午当他问我正在看什么书的时候开始的。自从以前在中东生活过一段时间之后，我希望回到那里再多待些日子。

The book I was reading was an intelligent, unbiased look at the way of life in that region and the history. While I was not in denial about the subjugation of women in some of these countries, or the extent that some of the extremists from these nations go to in the name of religion (just as extremists from any religion lose sight of the teachings of kindness that are common in all religions), I had also seen a side of this culture that is unfortunately never portrayed in the media. These warm-hearted people were wonderfully family orientated, and some of the most hospitable hosts that I had come across. Beautiful hearts were opened and they welcomed me without hesitation.

> 那里的人很热情，非常注重家庭，我在那里遇到过一些最为热情好客的东道主。他们敞开美好的胸怀，没有任何敌意地欢迎我。

This has also been the same with people from that region I have since come to know in Australia. We have lost so much of the family connection in the West, particularly where the older generation is concerned. I was seeing this first hand by the amount of lonely people in nursing homes, during the random shifts I sometimes had to do in them.

> 我在澳大利亚认识的从中东来的人也都是这样的。在西方，我们失去了太多的家庭纽带，这些尤其是老一代人所担心的。在养老院里，我亲眼目睹许多孤独的老人，我在随机轮换照顾病人期间，有时候不免会遇见这些老人。

Other cultures and how differently we all choose to live fascinate me. So do the culinary delights to be discovered through other cultures. Yet we are all very much alike in other ways too. Racism is something I will never understand. The majority of us are the same, in that we just want to be happy. And on some level, we all have hearts that suffer.

> 异域的文化以及跟我们截然不同的生活方式使我着迷。因为，在不同的文化中，你会发现各式美味佳肴。然而，除此之外，东西方文化还是有相当多的相似之处。种族主义是我永远搞不明白的东西。不过，我们大多数人的梦想都是一样的，都只想成为快乐的人。但在某些层面上，我们每个人都有伤心事。

Anthony was keen to hear more of what I was learning. So after making us a pot of herbal tea, its aroma wafting gently through the room, I brought him up to date on the book so far. The reading then continued, but now out loud. We would share an hour or two like this every day and it became a time we both enjoyed. Because we had quite a few weeks of this, I was able to introduce Anthony to books he would have never come across otherwise. Offering him a choice of topics, he always insisted he would be happy with whatever I read.

> 安东尼渴望更多地聆听我正在阅读的书。所以，当煮好了一壶我们的草本茶之后，伴随着茶香轻轻地在房间里飘荡，我继续他朗读，而今天的声音比较大些。我们每天都会像这样度过一到两个小时，这是段我们两个都觉得很享受的时光。由于我们这样朗读已经进行了相当一段时间，我可以向安东尼介绍几本书了，不然的话，他绝对不会去看那些书的。我列出了书的分类让他去选，他总是坚持无论我给他读什么书他都很开心。

So I introduced some spiritual classics to him. Books came to be shared about life, philosophy and thinking outside of the square. Discussion would flow on naturally afterwards as I tended to his needs; lifting up an arm that was not working, another in a plaster cast, dressing the wound on a leg that was not working, then feeding him, combing his hair, and attending to other personal grooming needs.

> 所以，我介绍了一些有关精神方面的经典书籍给他。书的内容都是关于生活、哲学和思考外面的世界的。每当我护理完了他的需求之后，比如帮他举起一支他不能举的手臂（另外一支还在石膏内）、包扎一条大腿的伤口、喂他吃东西、给他梳头以及其他个人梳洗等需求，我们就会自然流畅地讨论起书中的内容。

In the end though, the physical damage resulting from his actions meant the operations were not wholly successful. Some things were fixed. But some parts of him were damaged for life. So he was unable to go home, due to life ahead now requiring assistance with permanent personal care. It was then decided he would go into a nursing home, one of the best in the city, at least according to the brochure and the price.

> 他的过激行为给他的身体机能造成了极大的伤害，到最后所有手术都没法达到预想的效果。有些功能得到了修复，但有些功能是终生的损害。由于他以后的生活需要终生被人照顾，因此，他不能够回家。医生决定让他住进城里最好的一家疗养院，至少根据该院的宣传册子和价格来看，可以认为它是最好的。

Anthony was a young man now surrounded by drab coloured walls, and dying, elderly people. It was an awful environment and I longed to take to the walls with brighter coloured paint. Initially, he was happy enough though. It brought him peace that the pressure had been lifted from his family, as they knew he was being cared for. He was also able to bring good cheer to the older residents and they loved him.

> 安东尼本是一个年轻人，可现在却生活在一个周围都是单调灰暗的墙壁、临终的病人和老年人的环境里。这个地方太可怕了，我恨不得把所有墙壁粉刷成光亮的颜色。他刚进去的时候基本上还是快乐的，因为这里没有来自家庭的压力。他的家人知道有专人照顾他，也不常来打扰他，这样也给他带来了平静。在这里，他也能够为那些老年人带来欢乐，他们很喜欢他。

Over time though, his light grew dull and the shortage of outside stimulus numbed his intelligence from lack of use. He started to become a product of his environment. We are all fairly malleable, bendable creatures really. While we have the choice to think for ourselves and have free will to live the way our hearts guide us, our environment has a huge effect on us all, particularly until we start choosing life from a more conscious perspective.

> 然而，一段时间过后，他那炯炯有神的眼睛变得迟钝，由于缺乏外界的刺激，也很少动用脑子，他的智力也变得比往常低下。他开始成为环境的产物。我们每个人都具备一定的可塑性和能屈能伸的特质。在选择力自己着想，有自由意志地按照自己内心引导的方向去生活的同时，我们的环境无处不在地对我们产生极大的影响。只有到了我们开始用更加理性的洞察力来选择自己生活的时候，受环境的影响才会变得越来越少。

Another example of being influenced by the surrounding environment is watching down-to-earth and already happy people get caught up in the chase for more, more, more after a job promotion. The desire to keep up with new friends on the new level of income often sees people change within to suit their environment. The area they were once happy living in is no longer good enough, so they move to somewhere more suitable, for example. Sometimes this will bring happiness, sure, but not always.

> 另外一种受到周围环境影响的情况是，原本那些很朴实和拥有快乐的人，当他们的职位获得晋升后，身不由己地卷入到不断地追逐更多、更多的利益之中。时常可以见到，渴望在新的收入水平相当的环境里与新的朋友齐肩并进，人们开始改变自己去适应他们的环境，例如，以前曾经给他们带来快乐的居住地方已经再也不能满足他们的欲望，因此，他们搬迁到更适合他们身份的地区去居住。某些时候，这样的改变的确会带来快乐和幸福，但并非总是如此。

Many country people also adapt to city life and become affected by city fashions and busier lifestyles. Not that there isn’t fashion in the country. There definitely is. But again, it is a case of being influenced by where you live. Some people raised in cities also adapt to country life and slow their lifestyles down, ditching their labels and finding happiness in jeans and gumboots, as they plod around on their acreage. Wherever we are, our environment influences us enormously if we hang around long enough.

> 许多乡村人也很适应城市化生活，城市的时尚和忙碌的生活方式影响着他们。不是说在乡村就没有时尚，那里肯定是有的。我只是在强调，这是我们正在受着其生活环境影响的一个方面。有些人虽然在城市里长大，却也适应乡村那般的生活方式，放慢他们的生活节奏，甩掉他们的头衔，当他们辛勤培植和打理他们的住家花园时，他们在牛仔裤和长筒胶靴中享受其快乐。无论我们住在哪里，一旦时间久了，我们的环境都会彻底地影响我们。

During my mid twenties I was having a lot of fun. The start of that decade had been hard for me. By the time I was nineteen years old I was engaged to be married, and had a serious life complete with mortgage commitments. It was an unhealthy relationship for the most part. Somehow I survived that time though. Looking back, I have no idea how. An excess of mental abuse, psychological games, and being exposed to various states of rage expressed by my partner, continuously diminished my confidence.

> 在我二十五岁期间，我的生活充满乐趣。我在那个十年开始的时候，真的很难。我十九岁的时候就跟别人订了婚，双方的房屋贷款按揭协议，使我们的生活非常艰难，关系难以维持。我们的同居关系大部分来说是不健康的。尽管如此，不知什么原因，我还是生存下来了。回头来看，我真的不清楚那些日子我是怎样过来的。太多的自我精神虐待、心理游戏以及被我的同居者时常愤怒的吼叫持续性地削减着我的自信心。

It all became too much around the same time as I scored a new job, not surprisingly, in a bank. The team at work was fantastic and I found myself starting to enjoy life again. Having steady work also allowed me to dream of a life beyond my current situation and I moved out. Before too long, I transferred to the north coast with my job, for a fresh start.

> 当我顺利的在银行找到一份新的工作期间，上述那些生活状态变得更为恶劣。在工作上，同事们的团队合作真是太棒了，我发现自己又开始在享受生活。有份稳定的工作使得我对现有的状况以外的真正的生活产生了梦想，最后，我搬出了我和男朋友同居的地方。不久，我随工作调动迁移到了北岸地区，我的新生活从此开始了。

In no time at all dancing and frivolity were given free license and became a happy, carefree part of my life. There were also a lot of drugs around me. By now I knew drinking wasn’t for me and although I hadn’t yet reached the point where I would give up alcohol forever, drinking was not a big part of my life.

> 我立刻参与了不需要许可证的跳舞和轻松娱乐场所之中，我变得快乐和自由自在，这成为了我生活的一部分。我的生活圈子里面也有许多的毒品。现在我知道了，饮酒不适合我，虽然我还没有达到完全戒掉酒精的地步，但是，饮酒不是我生活的重要部分。

There was plenty of other stuff on offer though and within a year I experimented with most of it. These were the days before synthetic drugs like ice and others I don’t even know the street names of. Homegrown pot was common in my circle of friends and when another friend gave me the opportunity to try opium, I did.

> 这里有太多其他东西为你提供，一年之内，我尝试了其中的大部分。有些日子，我服用过冰状的毒品原料和另外一些我甚至都叫不出名字的东西。在我的朋友圈内，吸大麻很普遍，当另外的朋友拿鸦片让我尝试的时候，我也吸了。

I was in a space where I felt I could try new things but was clear enough in myself to let most of it go after one experience, though I never tempted that theory with heroin, thank goodness. I never went near it. Thankfully only one experiment was the case with opium, magic mushrooms, LSD, and cocaine, all of which I tried within that twelve month period but never again. There was a need in me for some recklessness I think, after the confines of my upbringing and the previous relationship. But underneath all of this, on an unconscious level, was a total lack of self worth that had become a part of me and was still being nurtured.

> 在那自由的空间里，我感觉到我可以尝试所有的新东西，但是，我也让自己保持清醒。大多数东西我尝试一次之后便不再碰它们，谢天谢地，我从未被海洛因所诱惑。我从来没有靠近海洛因。感谢上帝，我也仅仅用过一次鸦片，一年之内我试用过致幻蘑菇、摇头丸、可卡因等，但以后就再也没有用过。我想，在我结束我的成长教育规范和以前的男女朋友关系之后，我需要一些不顾一切的突破，但是，所有这一切之所以存在，完全是因为我的潜意识里缺乏自我价值感，这成了自我的一部分，我还是需要一直有人来照顾，需要依赖别人。

The life of excessive drug indulgence wasn’t for me though. I knew that immediately and while I was happy to try some things, I told myself it was more out of a desire to experience life than the need to be ‘off my face’. Consciously, it didn’t take me long to work out I preferred a healthier life. Unconsciously though, there was still so much to be undone after decades of allowing the opinions of others to dominate my belief system. Happiness was still very dependent on external forces.

> 过度的药物放纵生活不属于我，我很快就知道。在我愉快地尝试某些事情的同时，我也告诫自己，打消体验生活的愿望远比仅仅让自己在家人面前完全消失要好得多。从意识层面上，我很快就发现自己更喜欢健康的生活方式。然而，从潜意识层面上，允许别人的意见来主宰我的信念体系已长达数十年之久，还是有太多的这种依靠他人的状况需要消除。那时我的快乐和幸福仍然依赖外界的压力。

A few years afterwards, following the island stint too, I was living in England pulling beers in the village pub. Speed was around a lot. After doing a couple of lines, the local lads would come into the pub with huge dilated pupils, whilst grinding their teeth all night. Their regular routines were the same year in, year out. So when someone scored speed, it altered their realities enough to give them a different view of the same scene. It was merely boredom they were trying to escape from and watching them on the days after, with the melancholy and exhaustion that followed, I had to wonder if the price was worth it.

> 在这几年之后，由于岛上生活也有限制，我就前往英格兰去生活，并在一家乡镇酒吧为客人开啤酒。周围有许多的安非他命（一种毒品兴奋剂）。使用这些兴奋剂之后，当地的一些伙伴们会晕晕沉沉地来到我们这个酒吧，同时，整个晚上都躺在这里，牙齿磨得嘎嘎的响。他们年复一年的那种规律性的作息生活没有一点变化。当有人在分配安非他命的时候，足以改变他们对现实的认知，让他们在同样的地方有着不同的感受。这仅仅是他们试图逃离白天那种令人厌倦的生活的一种手段。眼看着他们在以后的日子里还是忧郁和疲劳，我不免纳闷他们这样花大把钱值得吗？

There were a few occasions where my partner and I decided to join in. But it didn’t take us long to realise it wasn’t our thing. The comedown from speed was awful and I hated myself for doing this to my body. Yet about a month or so later, I found myself experiencing a life changing time, again being influenced by my environment and the lack of will and conscious choice to live a better life.

> 偶尔有几次机会，我的同居人和我决定加入进去，但是，很快地我们就意识到我们不属于这里。服用安非他命后的失落感真是太可怕了，我恨自己这样去糟蹋自己的身体。大约一个月或者更久，我发现自己正在经历生活方式的改变，再一次的被环境所影响，缺乏志向和清醒的意识去选择一种更好的生活方式。

Dean was working all weekend, so I joined the ranks of the other village lads and jumped on a train up to London for the night. Despite being in my late-twenties, I had never been to a rave, simply because they didn’t play my sort of music. But rather than see me at home on my own, the lads convinced me to join them, promising me the time of my life. They were all my mates so off I went.

> 整个周末迪安都在工作，所以，我加入了其他乡村年轻人的行列，并跳上一辆火车到伦敦玩一晚。尽管我都快30岁了，却还从未参加过一场狂欢晚会，因为很简单，那里没有演奏过我个人喜爱的音乐类型。然而，他们看见我一个人在家，费尽心思说服我加入到他们队伍之中，并向我保证这是在享受生活。他们都是我的人生旅伴，我毫不犹豫地跟着他们上了火车。

A previous experience on ecstasy, the only occasion I had tried it, had been OK. I’d had a silly night and survived the come down, although definitely not enjoyably. My stomach felt awful and my energy was incredibly low for days. It had felt like enough of an experience though, and I had declined any offers of it since. I also lived with self-loathing afterwards and I could do without any more of that. There were enough of those thoughts in me already. Yet here I was on the train to London with eight guys convincing me to have an ecstasy pill.

> 我以前也仅尝试过一次狂喜的经历，感觉还可以。虽然明知绝对不好玩，但是我还是度过了一个无聊的夜晚，并幸存了下来。好几天我的胃都感觉很不舒服，同时，难以置信的是我极度的疲劳。这种感觉刻骨铭心，有一次就足够了，从此，我拒绝所有的这种邀约。在这之后，我依然生活在自我厌恶之中，发誓不能再有这种事情发生。尽管那些想法已经在我的大脑里形成了，但是，此时此刻，我还是跟着八位伙伴坐上去伦敦的火车，他们劝我服一颗摇头丸。

Those in the city scene were dropping several pills every week, so what was the big deal about me having one little one? I don’t blame the guys at all though, not in the slightest.
