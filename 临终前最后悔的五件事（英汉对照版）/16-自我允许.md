# Allow yourself · 自我允许

As far as work went, looking after Harry was the easiest time I ever had. Not only was he a wonderful person, but his family insisted on doing everything. Three of Harry’s five daughters lived in the same suburb and would bring his main meals over most days and one of his sons insisted on caring for his dad himself. Questioning the need for me to be there at all, the daughters and other sons assured me they definitely wanted me there.

> 据目前的工作情形，照顾哈里是我做过的最轻松的工作。不仅因为他是一位很好的人，还因为他的家人也坚持参与照顾他。他五个女儿中有三个都住在同一个郊区，大多数时间她们会带哈里的主食过来，而他其中一个儿子也坚持亲自照顾父亲。问题来了，我在这里完全有必要吗，不过他的女儿们和其他的儿子非常肯定我在这里绝对有必要。

It meant though, that much of my time was spent reading or writing. There is only so much housework to be done in an already clean and tidy home, with its only tenant bedridden. I did create a couple of delicious soup recipes in his kitchen though.

> 这意味着我有很多的时间阅读和写作。在这个已经很干净和整齐的家庭里，仅有的那么一点家务事就是围绕这位卧床不起的老人。我在他的厨房里研发了几种新的美味汤肴。

Harry had bushy eyebrows, hairy ears, a red face and an honest laugh. We liked each other immediately. Within the first minute of meeting, we had both cracked a joke with each other. So it was an easy, natural association from the start.

> 哈里有着浓密的眉毛，耳廓多毛，一张红色的脸和坦诚的笑声。我们很快就喜欢上了对方。在我们见面的第一分钟里，我们双方都相互开着玩笑。因此，从一开始我们就容易又自然地互动起来。

His son, Brian, was a different story though. He was very highly strung. Harry and Brian had fallen out years ago and while contact had remained between them, their bond was never quite the same. The rest of the family explained it as being Brian’s fault. I wasn’t there all those years ago though, nor had I walked in either Harry or Brian’s shoes, so I don’t know. It didn’t matter to me either way. But it was obvious that Brian was now trying to make up for lost time by insisting he be his father’s primary carer.

> 他的儿子布赖恩有着不同寻常的传奇故事。他是一位神经非常敏感的人。许多年前，哈里和布赖恩曾经闹翻过，但仍有些联系，不过他们俩从来都没有完全达成默契关系。这个家庭的其他成员跟我解释说这都是布赖恩的错。之前的那些年我又不住在这里，没有办法设身处地从哈里或者布赖恩的角度看问题，所以，我不知道谁对谁错。无论如何这与我没有任何关系。不过，明显看得出来，现在的布赖恩想通过坚持亲自担当照顾父亲的基本护理员来尽量弥补过去失去的亲情。

Brian interrupted any attempt I made to help Harry. By now, I was very good at finding the right position for a client’s comfort. It was an intuitive thing and one many clients commented on. But family often rearranged pillows and supports out of kindness, not realising how sensitive a person’s body is at this time, and how the slightest adjustment can undo the only tiny bit of comfort they have.

> 布赖恩阻止我任何有关帮助哈里的想法和行为。如今，我对于照顾病人的工作驾轻就熟，有能力为客户的舒适找到自己正确的位置和责任。这是一种直觉的问题，是许多客户共同要求的问题。不过，家庭成员时常重新调整枕头和出于好意的帮我搭把手都是帮倒忙，他们没有意识到在这种病危时刻，一个人的身体有多么的敏感，以及一点点轻微的调整就能扰乱病人仅有的微乎其微的舒适感。

When his son reluctantly went off to work every day for a few hours, the first thing I would do was to get Harry comfortable again. If there was a tiny moment of time during the day when I could attend to him without being hounded out by his son, literally, the first thing Harry would ask was for me to quickly adjust his pillows.

> 每天当他的儿子很不情愿地离开去上班的几个小时里，我做的第一件事是让哈里重新感到舒服些。在一天之中，当我不被他的儿子撵走并能够直接照顾他的时候，哪怕就只有一丁点时间，毫不夸张地说，哈里都会请求我的第一时间尽快地帮他调整好枕头的位置。

Each afternoon though, we had those few hours together before the family arrived back en-masse for dinner, even though by now their dad was hardly eating. These hours were wonderful and what Harry affectionately called ‘the peace hours’. While I attended to his physical needs, we would chat and laugh. This was usually followed by a cup of tea and some more chat.

> 每天傍晚，他们家人都一窝蜂回到家里用晚餐，即使他们的父亲现在几乎不能进食，他们依然习惯回家吃饭。在他们回来之前，每个下午我和哈里也有几个小时在一起。这段时间是我们的美好时光，哈里亲切地称这几个小时为“和平钟点”。每当我参与他的基本需求照顾的时候，我们都会边聊天边大笑。通常是在喝完一杯茶后，我们聊得更加起劲。

Harry had lost his wife twenty years earlier but had come to continue living his life well. He had enjoyed his work, though became even busier on retiring, joining a couple of sporting and social clubs. While his illness was terminal, prior to this he had experienced amazing health for his whole life.

> 哈里二十年前就已经失去了妻子，然而，他的生活依然继续下去而且过得很好。他以前很享受他的工作，退休后反而变得更加忙碌，他加入了一些体育活动和社交俱乐部。虽然现在他的病情进入了晚期，但在这次生病之前，他一生中总体来讲身体都一直非常的健康。

“I respected the gift of health I was given,” Harry told me, “by staying active and not believing some quota of years saying I should be acting this way or that. People make themselves old before their time, you know”. Despite being a dying person, Harry was the healthiest man of eighty years I had ever seen. The illness was certainly starting to wear him down, but evidence of his prior fitness was still apparent. Massaging his legs for example, muscle tone from all of his walking could still be seen.

> “我尊重我所拥有的健康。”他告诉我说，“靠不停的运动和不相信一些年龄定额学说，我应当参与各式各样的活动。你知道的，人们往往把自己弄得未老先衰。”尽管哈里是一个快要离开人世的人，但他是我见过的八十岁“老男人”中最健康的。肯定的，这种疾病开始耗尽他的体力，但是，他以前的健壮体魄依然很明显就可以看出来。比如，我在给他做腿部按摩时，还是可以感觉得到他过去经常走路运动练成的肌肉张力。

“When you’re retired and your children are raising their own children, the need for friends is even more important,” Harry said. “So when my wife died, God rest her soul, I joined the rowing club. Then I joined a bushwalking club. I don’t know how I ever had time to work!” Harry very much believed in the importance of extended family, that grandparents are an integral part of children’s lives and should be given plenty of opportunity to spend time with them.

> “当你退休了，你的孩子们也有了自己的孩子的时候，朋友的需求就变得更加重要。”哈里感叹地说，“所以，当我的太太去世时，上帝保佑她的灵魂，让她好好安息。我加入了划船俱乐部。然后，我又加入了一个丛林徒步旅行俱乐部。我都不知道以前怎么还有时间去上班工作！”哈里非常坚信大家庭对人生的重要性，祖父母是孙子生活中不可缺少的组成部分，应当有更多的机会跟孙子们在一起共度时光。

It was obvious in his relationships with his grandchildren who visited him daily that he had a very positive and loving influence on them all. “My family comes first but you need people your own age too. If it weren’t for the friends I’ve made through the clubs, I would have been a very lonely old person. I wouldn’t have been lonely for company, as I have my children and grandchildren, but lonely for like-minded company of people my own age.”

> 从他的孙子们每天来看望他就明显看出了他与孙子们的关系有多么的亲密以及他那非常乐观的正能量个性和爱心影响了孙子们。“虽然我把家庭放在第一位，但我也需要同龄的朋友。如果不是为了与朋友在一起，我不会加入那些俱乐部，那么我就会成为一个非常孤单的老人。虽然我有这么多孩子和孙子，不乏缺少有人陪伴，我不会孤单，但是，如果没有了相同年纪志同道合的朋友陪伴就会感到孤独。”

Hours were spent chatting in his room, until the late afternoon sun would warn us of the peace hours being almost over. The family would soon be descending on us again, but Harry always talked for as long as he could. He said he didn’t understand why people left it too late to realise the importance of friends. Also, while it was beautiful when elderly people still managed to hold a loving and respected position within their family, he grew frustrated that so many of them hadn’t left time for friendship along the way too.

> 在他的房间里我们聊了很久，直到傍晚太阳西下才提醒了我们和平钟点时间快要结束了。很快的他的家人们会突然再次出现在我们面前，而哈里总是非常健谈，只要有可能就说个不停。他说他不明白为什么人们到了最后时刻才意识到朋友的重要性。另外，他力老年人在自己的家庭里虽然一直得到爱和尊敬感到生活很美好，但是他们在晚年期间没有友谊的生活而感到不满。

“They’ll realise it too late,” he insisted. “But it’s not just my generation. I look at the younger ones too, who get so caught up and busy, not taking just a little bit of time out for themselves now and then, to do things that make them happy on an individual basis. They lose who they really are completely. A little time with friends reminds them of who they are when they are not mum, dad, grandma, or grandfather. Do you understand what I am saying?”

> 他坚持自己的见解说：“等他们意识到这一点会太晚了。”“不仅我们这一代人是这样的，我看到年轻人同样也是陷入工作的忙碌之中，有时只抽出一点点时间花在自己身上。即使这样，他们都是在个人基础上独自享受快乐。他们完全失去真实的自我。与朋友在一起的那点时间提醒了他们不再是父亲、母亲、爷爷或者奶奶，他们是真实的他们自己。

Agreeing that I had seen plenty of people go down that road, I also said I had seen others who had maintained just a slight bit of time out for themselves and were much happier people. They were also much better company to be around. “Exactly!” He laughed, slapping the bed in agreement. “Good friendships stimulate us. The beauty of friendship is that these people take us as we are, on the things we have in common. Friendship is about being accepted as who you are, not as who someone else wants you to be, like a partner or family. We must maintain our friendships, my dear girl.”

> 你明白我在说什么吗？”我同意说我也见过许多人也那样生活，我还对他说，曾经见过那些维持很少时间为自己生活的其他人也是非常快乐的，他们的周围也有更好的陪伴者。“绝对正确！”他笑起来，并用手拍打着床以示同意。“良好的友谊激励着我们。友谊的美在于朋友看重真实的我们，我们大家都有共同之处。友谊是接受实实在在的你，而不是像你的伴侣或者家人那样要求你成为他们所希望的那种人。我亲爱的女孩，我们必须维系我们的友谊。”

By the stream of visitors that came to visit Harry regularly, it was obvious this man walked his talk. His friends were all happy, jovial people and brought great joy with them. Equally respectful of his illness though, they accepted that sometimes he was resting and could not be disturbed. On another afternoon, Harry asked me about my own friendships. So I filled him in on my close friends and explained how some of my other friendships were changing lately, as was I.

> 通过一波一波的探访者定期地来看望哈里，明显看得出这个男人很爱交谈。他的朋友们都是快活、平易近人和给人带来极大愉快的人。同样的，他们也很尊重哈里的病情，他们承认有的时候他需要休息，不能打扰他。另外一个下午，哈里问了我有关我自己的朋友关系问题。因此，我跟他讲述了我的几个亲密朋友，并说明了随着我自身的改变，我的某些其他友谊又发生了怎样的改变。

“Well, that’s natural too,” he said. “Friends will come and go throughout life. That’s why we should value them while they are here. Sometimes, you simply finish learning or sharing what you were meant to through each other. But others will stay the distance, and that history and understanding is a comforting thing when you’re at the end of the road.” During these talks we both agreed that women approach friendships much differently to men.

> “是的，那也是自然的。”他说。“我们一生中，朋友将来来去去。那就是为什么当朋友都还健在的时候我们应当重视他们。有的时候，很简单，你必须通过朋友间的互动才能够完成个人的成长或者分享你的人生经历。而其余的人都将跟你保持一定距离。所以，真正的朋友与其他认识的人有根本的区别。

Women value friendships in a stronger way emotionally, that is, the friendships grow closer with a lot of talk about emotional things. Men need friendships for talking too, he said. But they do this best when they are doing things together, like playing tennis, cycling, or doing something active. Men enjoy friendships where they can work things out, resolve problems, whether physical or emotional, and this often happens best when they are active. “Like building a fence around a paddock together,” I suggested.

> 当你走向人生的尽头，与朋友交往的经历和他们对你的理解会给你带来安慰。”在谈话中我们双方都同意女人和男人在结交朋友的方式上有很多的差异。女人在情感方面以强烈的方式看重友谊，亲近的友情关系就是相互交谈大量的情感问题。他说，男人也需要友谊也需要交谈。但是，他们的交谈都是当他们在一起做某件事情的时候进行，比如一起打网球、骑单车运动，或者做什么活动等等。无论是身体或者是情感方面，男人是在一起制订计划，解决问题中享受友谊，这种友谊都最好是在他们做事情的时候发生。“就像一起在一个围场周围搭建围栏。”我暗示地说。

Harry cracked up laughing. “My, my. You can take the girl out of the country, but you can’t take the country out of the girl. Yes, a very rural example Bronnie, but exactly. Building a fence or doing something manual together is a bonding thing for men.” He carried on laughing and said if I ever wanted to bond with a good-looking man, all I had to do was to help him to build a fence. I told him I’d keep it in mind.

> 哈里跟着哈哈大笑起来。“哎呀，哎呀。你可以把一个女孩带出农村，但你不可以把农村从一个女孩身上抹掉。是的，邦妮，很乡士的例子，但很贴场，男人和女人的特性天生决定，不能更改。对男人来说，在一起手工搭建围栏或者做某件事就是一种黏胶，把他们黏合在一起。”他继续又大笑地说，我是否曾经希望跟某个外表长得好看的男人黏合一起。我明白，现在我唯一必须要做的是帮他搭建一个围栏。我告诉他我会考虑他的建议。

Sharing some of his favourite stories about mate-ship with me, Harry reinforced the blessings of the friendships still existing. Every day lovely friends visited him. They were now operating a roster amongst themselves though, so as to not exhaust him. This way, everyone still had a chance to spend time with Harry. It was loyal and wonderful.

> 哈里跟我分享了他最喜欢的有关他哥们儿的故事，他强调说友谊的福音在他身上一直存在。每天，他的好朋友都会来看他。为了不想让他感到人多容易疲惫，他们现在制定出了一个朋友群的花名册，这样可以分期分批地每个人都有机会来看望他。真是一群忠实又优秀的朋友。

We both admitted that through these peace hours, we were each receiving a new friendship into our lives too, each other. It frustrated him, he said, to know I was out in another part of the house for the rest of the day, just reading or writing, when I could be in his room chatting. In total agreement, I laughed.

> 我们俩都承认，通过这些和平钟点时间，在我们彼此的生活中，我们双方也都获得了新的友谊。他说，当他得知在一天的其他时间里，我只能呆在他家另外的地方阅读和写作的时候感到很不高兴，而这个时段本来我可以在他的房间跟他聊天。我笑了，完全赞同他的说法。

But he understood, as did I, Brian’s need to make amends and the desire to assist his father. Harry didn’t want Brian to be carrying any guilt, though he was sure he still would, unfortunately. So he was happy to go with it and allow his son to feel needed during their last weeks together. “Even if he can’t arrange the pillows well,” he sighed.

> 然而，我们俩都明白，布赖恩需要弥补过去，他希望帮助他的父亲。哈里不想布赖恩有任何的内疚，不幸的是，哈里确信布赖恩仍然会这样。所以，在他们在一起的最后几个星期的时间里，他乐意布赖恩来照顾他，允许他的儿子去感受他的这个需求。他叹气地说：“即使他不能把我的枕头调整好。”

Harry was philosophical about his illness and what was to come. He had lived his life to the fullest, he said, and was ready to see what lay beyond. While we did speak about his approaching passing sometimes, he still steered many of the conversations to the subject of friends: the memories, the value of them, and the necessity of them for happiness and acceptance. He also encouraged me to share some of my favourite memories with him about my friendships so far. “Start with one from your childhood. Let’s hear where you’re coming from,” he said, then laughed in delight when my story began in a rural setting, a paddock of wheat.

> 对于自己的病情，哈里很冷静、沉着。他说，他的生活已经过得很充实，准备好了看看后面还会有什么花样。有的时候，在我们谈论有关他接近死亡的话题时，他依然把我们许多交谈的话题转到朋友问题上：比如，他们的记忆、他们的价值、他们的幸福以及被接受的必要性。他也鼓励我跟他分享我最喜欢的到目前止有关友谊的记忆。他说：“从你的童年开始。让我们听听你从哪里来。”当我的故事从农村的一个小麦围场开始的时候，他愉快地大笑起来。

By the time I was twelve, we had moved from a cattle and lucerne growing farm, to a sheep and wheat farm. It was miles from town, under a magnificently huge sky. About a year later my first dog disappeared suddenly, when she was seven. We think it may have been a snake’s bite, as we never found her.

> 我十二岁的时候，我们家从一个养牛和种植紫花苜蓿的农场迁移到了一个养羊和种植小麦的农场。该农场远离城镇，在辽阔的天空之下。大约一年之后，我第一次养育的小狗突然不见了，它只有七岁。我们再也没有找到它，我们想它也许是被蛇咬了。

This wasn’t surprising as the farm was so large. It was devastating for me though. A few months later my folks bought me a new dog. She was a little white Maltese Terrier, who neglected to realise that she was supposed to be a housedog. Instead, she spent her days chasing the sheep dogs, the border collies and kelpies, over paddocks near and far.

> 农场太大，发生这样的事并不奇怪。然而，还是给了我很大的打击。几个月后，我的亲戚送给我了一条新狗。它是一条很小的白色马耳他梗狗，大家没有意识到它应该是看家狗。相反的，它却整天都在围场里忽近忽远地追逐着牧羊犬、边境牧羊犬和澳洲犬。

My closest friend through my high school years and for a long time after was Fiona. Although she lived in town, much of our time was spent out at the farm. I stayed in town at her parent’s place a bit too, particularly as we grew a little older and there were boys to be kissed.

> 我在整个高中时期及以后很长一段时间最亲密的朋友是菲奥纳。她虽然住在城镇，但是很多的时间是在我们家农场度过的。尤其是我们俩都长大一些，有男孩子亲吻我们的时候，我也到过城里她父母家里住过。

One of the main things that linked Fiona and I over the years though was our love of walking. I cannot think how many miles we ended up walking together over the decades of our friendship: beaches, rainforests, city streets, foreign countries, bush tracks, you name it. It all started with walking those paddocks of wheat.

> 那些年，能够让我和菲奥纳紧密联系在一起的主要原因之一是我们俩都酷爱徒步。我想不起了在那几十年我们的友谊之中我们一起最终到底走了多少英里：海滩、雨林、城市街道、国外、“布什轨道”。而所有的徒步都是从行走那些小麦围场开始的。

As usual, my dog and a couple of the other dogs came along with us. It was not particularly strange to turn around and see a cat or two also following. While we girls stuck to the track that led to the furthest paddocks away, the dogs would run through the wheat. This was fine while the wheat was low but as it grew, my little dog became invisible. Fiona and I were given the most beautiful comedy sketch this day.

> 通常我自己的狗和几条其他的狗会追过来跟我们一块行走。有时，我们走着走着回头一看，一只或者两只猫也跟着我们。在我们那个地方，这一点都不特别的奇怪。当我们女孩子在铁轨上卡住了而距离农场已经很远之时，那些狗狗会穿过麦田奔跑过来。如果麦子还是低的时候没有关系，但一旦麦子长高了，我的小狗就看不见了。这一天里，菲奥纳和我就像看到最滑稽的喜剧小品一样非常快乐。

Following the big dogs, which we could see clearly above the tallest sections of crop, was a trail of movement in the wheat behind as my little dog ran blindly after the big dogs. Then every now and then the movement would stop. A little white head would pop up and look around like a submarine telescope coming out of the water, until it spotted the other dogs. It then disappeared below again into the wheat, blazing another trail of movement in the new direction.

> 站在高于农作物的位子，我们可以清楚地看到，奔跑的大狗后面出现一条麦田运动形成的小道，我的小狗也盲目地跟着大狗跑。它那可爱的小白头会突然冒出来，然后向周围望一望，就好像潜水艇的望远镜出现在水面，直到它发现到其他的大狗之后又开始低头跑。随后，它的身体又被掩埋在麦田之中，开拓出一条新方向的麦田运动小道。

Then the movement would stop, up would pop the little white head, spot the target again, disappear below and run on. This went on for ages and by the end of it, every time we saw the little white head pop up and look around, Fiona and I would break into hysterical teenage giggling all over again. Our cheeks ached from laughing and as tears ran down our faces, we leaned on each other, holding the other up before spotting the dog popping up yet again, and doubling over in laughter even more. In the end, we could hardly even stand up.

> 接下来，这种麦田运动会停止，那小白头再次冒出来寻找目标，又低下去，消失了，又开始跑。这样反复很久才结束。每次看到那小白头冒出来，巡视周围的时候，我和菲奥纳都会爆发出歇斯底里的青少年那种咯咯的傻笑。我们的腮帮子会笑得发酸，泪水直往下流，在那条小狗还没有冒出白头，巡视周围之前，我们俩会相互倚靠着对方，等待着下一次小白头冒出，之后，我们的笑声更加不可收拾。直到最后，我们笑得几乎都不能站起来。

Sharing this simple but precious memory took me back to the value of friendship in an instant. Harry and I laughed together, as I missed that innocence of youth and the carefree, uninhibited laughter I used to share with Fiona. “Where is she now?” Harry asked. I went onto explain that she was living in another country and we had lost touch. Life had moved on, I said, and there were now others in my life who I was closer to.

> 与哈里分享这个简单而又珍贵的回忆，斯须之间把我带回到那有价值的友谊之中。哈里和我一起大笑，仿佛我又寻找到往日习惯于跟菲奥纳分享的那种年幼的天真无瑕、无忧无虑和尽情的笑声。哈里问我：“现在她在哪里？”我向他说明她居住在另外的乡镇，我们已经失去了联系。我说，生活发生了变化，如今在我的生活中又有了其他亲密的朋友。

Other factors had also affected our friendship, other people, but also other tastes and gradual differences in lifestyles. Harry agreed that you can’t go back, but maybe life would bring our paths around again. Having already observed many cycles in life, I agreed it was possible. But either way it didn’t matter. I valued the memories and wished Fiona well; silently thanking her for the learning and friendship we had once shared.

> 也有另外的因素影响了我们的友谊，不仅是有了其他人的原因，而且还有不同的饮食口味以及逐渐形成的生活方式的差异等等。哈里赞同地说：“你不可能回到从前，可是生活也许会将我们绕回原来的生活轨迹。”鉴于曾经观察过很多这样的生命周期，我同意有这个可能性。但无论如何都没有关系，我看重那些美好的记忆，也祝愿非奥纳一切都好，同时，我默默地感谢她在学习上给予我的帮助和我们曾经拥有的友谊。

Many of my best memories of friendships were of walking, talking and laughing. Over the next week or two, I shared with Harry stories from some of these other friendships. He had also been a keen walker and shared some of his own with me, of places he’d walked and of friends with whom he had shared the experiences with. I could imagine any group being brightened by Harry’s laughter as they walked. It made me smile at the thought and when he asked what I was smiling at, I was happy to tell him. Harry agreed they had always shared some great laughs while walking.

> 我许多最好的友谊记忆都是关于步行、说话和欢笑。接下来的一两个星期里，我跟哈里分享了与其他人的友情故事。他曾经也非常喜欢步行，所以也跟我分享了他自己的一些故事，包括他走过哪些地方和跟他一起步行的那些朋友的故事。我可以想象得到，当他们步行的时候，任何一群人都会被哈里的笑声所感染，其步行活动也会增光添彩。想到这一点，我忍不住微笑了，他问我笑什么，我很高兴地告诉了他。哈里同意这一点，他说在步行的时候他们总是分享一些很好笑的笑话。

As it was, I was to leave Harry the following week to go on a long walk myself. I hadn’t been sure if he would still be alive when I had booked into it. So while I was looking very forward to getting out of the city, I was also a little sad to be leaving him, not sure if he would still be here when I returned. When I told Harry what I was doing though, he wholeheartedly agreed with enthusiasm and said he would be there with me in spirit whether he was alive or not.

> 事情就这么巧，下周我自己也要离开哈里去参加一次长途步行活动。我不敢确定当我结束旅程的时候他是否还活着。因此，在我非常期待走出城参加野外活动的同时，又要离开他而有点伤感，不确定当我回来的时候他是否还会在这里。当我告诉哈里我将打算外出徒步的时候，他很热情地真心诚意赞同我的计划，并表示无论他是活着还是离开了，他的灵魂和精神都将伴随着我。

The walk was in a remote area and was held each year, always ending at the same lake. Each time a different tributary was followed though. This particular year it was starting on some farms where the very mouth of a river began. We would follow that river, much of it now just dry riverbeds, and eventually end up at the lake.

> 那个步行活动是在一个偏远地区进行，每年都举行，其终点是在同一个湖边。每次我们选择不同的河水支流作为步行路线。今年比较特殊，我们开始于一条河流的最远端的某个农场，然后沿着河流，最终到达那个湖，现在河流几乎没有水，只是干枯的河床。

The concept of the walk was to give the participants the opportunity to heal with the Earth, as we walked the paths trodden by ancient civilisations. Rivers were like highways then, thoroughfares at least, where tribes would live and walk along its banks, from one place to the next. An Aboriginal Elder blessed us as we all partook in a cleansing smoke ceremony, then off we went, walking for six days.

> 这次步行活动的目的是给参加者疗愈地球的机会，我们走过的路径留有古老文明的脚印。那些河流就好像当年的“高速路”，是古代人的通行大道，那里是各族部落生活的地方，人们从一个地方到另一个地方都是沿着河岸行走。当我们参加完传统的点烟火仪式后，一位澳大利亚原住民长老我们祈福，随后，我们又继续赶路，一共有六天的行程。

We all fell into our own rhythm. There were about a dozen of us. Some walked in groups and talked all the way. Others meandered in and out of conversation. Some stopped and took photos of everything, and some of us walked more alone. Each night, a couple of volunteers would turn up with the trailer carrying our gear and we’d establish a camp. Then around a peaceful campfire a communal dinner was prepared, while beautiful friendships formed under a magnificent blanket of stars.

> 我们都进入了自己的行走节奏。我们大约有十二个人，有的结伴行走，一路上聊个不停；有的慢慢行走，一声不吭；有的走走停停，把什么都要拍摄下来；我们几个人走在比较前面。每天傍晚，几个志愿者会打开装有各式装备的拖车，我们搭建了一个露营篷。然后大家围坐在平和的篝火旁，开始一顿公共晚餐，在绚丽的满天星斗之下，美丽的友情就这样形成了。

With every footstep, the connection with the Earth increased. While I enjoyed conversations when we stopped for a break, I found more enjoyment in walking alone and my pace ensured this anyway. Having done so much walking in the past, my natural rhythm carried me ahead of the main group. One other walker, the wise and loving soul who originally started these walks, was always ahead of me, also walking to his own rhythm.

> 随着一步一个脚印，与地球的联结越来越多。当我们暂停休息的时候，我喜欢大家之间的交谈，但是我发现我更加享受独自行走，不管如何，我的步伐确定了这一点。过去我已经走了太多的路，我的自然节奏把我带到队伍的最前面。另外一名同伴，是这个步行活动的元老，是一位有智慧和爱心的人，他总是走在我的前面，也是按照自己的节律独自前行。

The time alone, just walking and walking, was also great for finding clarity within myself again. During this time I realised I didn’t want to keep house-sitting for much longer. Something in me was starting to think about having my own kitchen again. All of the moving I had once loved was starting to exhaust me. A new seed was planted, not with big fanfare, just a quiet acceptance within that some things were changing. I walked on peacefully.

> 这种单纯的不停行走的独处时间对我而言也是很棒的机会，可以让我再次从自己内心里找到清晰的思路。在边走边思索期间，我意识到了我再也不想做太久的看房子工作。我开始思考我要重新拥有自己的厨房。经过这些年的不断迁移，以往曾经热爱的生活方式开始让我精疲力竭。一颗新的种子已经植入到我的心田，不需要大张旗鼓地宣称，只需悄悄地接受，在我的内心某些事正在发生着变化。我踏着宁静的脚步继续往前走。

It is rare to be able to walk so far in modern times as land is now separated by ownership. Thankfully, this was all approved of beforehand so we walked across farm after farm without any problem. In the modern rush of life, it is very easy to not be aware of the Earth beneath our feet. Sure, most of us feel a connection with the Earth when we stop and absorb the beauty of nature. To be able to walk for six days unhindered though, left me feeling a connection with the Earth I hadn’t known was missing, despite all of my time previously spent in blissful appreciation of the planet.

> 在当今社会里，由于土地被农场主们隔离开来，到目前为止，像这样的行走是难以办到的。谢天谢地，好在这次活动，我们事先都得到了批准，所以，我们穿越一个个农场没有任何的问题。在当代忙碌的生活中，人们很容易忽视我们脚下的地球。是的，我们大多数人直到停下忙碌的脚步，吸收美丽的大自然的时候才感觉到与地球是多么的亲近。能够尽情地行走六天让我感觉到与地球更加紧密相连，尽管以前很多的时间都在幸福的欣赏这个星球中度过，却不知道自己曾经与地球失去了这种联系。

Along the way, we discovered carvings from ancient people and marvelled at magnificent red gums, trees hundreds of years old. There were intricate carvings, as well as indentations where canoes had been crafted from their bark. This evidence of past people, whose tribes were now long lost, was heartbreaking and inspiring at the same time. The energy in certain places was incredibly strong too and I understood why it was a walk intended for healing.

> 沿途中，我们发现了很多古代人留下来的雕刻、惊奇宏伟的红色树脂和几百年的老树。从错综复杂的雕刻以及凹痕中可以看出，那些独木舟原来都是用树皮制作的。那些过去已经消失了很久的部落人的证据既令人心碎，同时也令人振奋。在某个地方，也让人感觉那种能量有如此不可思议的强大，我理解到了为什么这是用于心灵疗伤的步行。

On top of this, much of the farmland we crossed reminded me of where I had grown up. Even the smell of sheep poo brought a flood of memories rushing back and I loved being in the dry, dusty climate again, even if only temporarily. With every footstep, my fitness improved and I dreamed of returning to a world where walking was the main means of transport. It just made more sense to me than all of the rush and bustle of modern life.

> 最重要的是，我们穿越的很多农场让我想起了我生长的地方。甚至那羊屎的臭味也让往日的回忆如潮水般席卷而来，我重新喜爱上干燥、尘土飞扬的气候，即使仅有短暂时刻也很珍惜。每增加一脚步，我的体魄就得到了进一步提升。我梦想着回到从前徒步是我的主要交通手段的那个世界。对我而言，这比忙碌而喧闹的现代生活更加有意义。

Finding a cooling waterhole to have a swim in was a welcome relief one day, when I had somehow lost the group briefly. Stripping off and swimming in this clean, refreshing water was rejuvenating, as the water cleansed my spirit as much as it did my body. Every moment of this week was a spiritual blessing, as the connection with nature grew more enhanced.

> 有一天，当我暂时与徒步团队失去联系的时候，我发现了一个冰凉的水潭，在里面游泳肯定是难得的放松。于是我脱光衣服，跳进这干净而清爽的水中游泳，这让我重新恢复了活力。那清澈而神圣的凉水不仅清洗着我的身体，也一样清洗着我的灵魂。这一星期的每个时刻都是灵性的祝福，我与大自然的连接逐渐地增强了。

The landscape changed constantly as we walked from about eight in the morning until five or so each afternoon, then set up camp. Other signs of previous life dotted our path too. An old cart that had once become bogged in the river was now a part of the dry landscape and possibly had been for over a hundred years. A stone cabin with no roof told of river dwellers in other times too. The best though was when we saw the carvings and realised what a unique history lesson we were all blessed with, confirming the lives of those ancient people whose footsteps we were now following.

> 我们每天的行走大约从早上八点开始直到下午五点左右结束扎寨，沿途的风景不断有变化。其他前世生活的痕迹也点缀在我们的行径途中。曾经掉入河里的一个老式手推车如今是干燥景观的一部分，它很可能已经超过百年历史。一个没有房顶的石头小屋也述说了其他时期河边居民的故事。最为震撼的是我们看到了那些雕刻，我们知道这是多么独特的一堂历史文化课程，为此，我们大家都感到是一种幸福，如今我们追逐着古人的脚印，确认着他们的种种生活。

After six full days of walking and about eighty kilometres covered, we arrived weary but exhilarated. It was with great sadness I said farewell to the other walkers, but with even greater sadness that the walk had finished. The following day I walked for another five hours, around the dry lake itself, as I just couldn’t get out of walking mode. A small music festival followed a few days later, which was organised with equal reverence as the walk. So I hung around for that and then headed back to Melbourne.

> 整整六天、大约八十公里的步行之后，我们既疲惫又兴奋地到达了终点。我带着极大的悲伤和依依不舍与每位同伴们道别，而让我更加伤感的是这次的步行活动已经结束了。第二天，因为我不能够平息步行的兴奋心情，又沿着干枯的湖边走了五个钟头。几天之后，一个小型音乐节在那里举行，该活动跟步行一样受到人们的喜爱。因此，我在那里呆了一阵，随后出发回墨尔本。

Thankfully Harry had not yet passed, so I was able to spend a little more time with him. During the ten days I was away though, the disease had taken over his body and I found him looking quite emaciated. All of his tone had left his once muscular legs, and his big round face was now gaunt with loose skin. He was still Harry though, still a delightful, beautiful man.

> 感谢上帝，哈利还没有去世，这样我又可以跟他多呆一些时间。我离开的这十天，病魔已经散发到他的全身，我发觉他看起来相当的消瘦和憔悴。他腿部的所有肉都已经失去了张力，他的大而圆的脸如今也消瘦得只剩下松弛的皮肤。然而，他依然是哈里，依然是让人愉快和英俊的男人。

But the intensity of Brian’s desperation to look after his father had increased enormously. He was even more controlling than ever and would only leave the house for an hour maximum each afternoon. I was grateful that both Harry and I had already appreciated those peace hours before I went away, as they were hardly on offer now. In addition to Brian’s obsessive behaviour, Harry was sleeping much more anyway.

> 布赖恩不顾一切拼命照顾他父亲的力度更加增强了。他甚至比以前更加控制一切，每天下午他最多只离开他父亲一个小时。我感谢在我暂时离开去步行之前哈里和我都已经拥有了那些和平钟点，如今，几乎很难再有那种机会。另外，对于布赖恩的强迫行为，不管如何，哈里就睡得更多，以避免与他对撞。

As life would have it though, Brian was called away unexpectedly one morning and had to reluctantly hand the care over to me. Thankfully it was when Harry was at his brightest – not that his brightest was bright anymore. But he was awake and capable of talking a little at least. At his request, I told him all about the walk and the insights I’d had into myself while I was away. He asked about the other walkers as well and any positive changes they’d noticed in themselves or that I had noticed. There was much to share.

> 然而，生活有时候就会这样离奇，一天上午，布赖恩突然被一通电话叫走，他不得不很勉强地把照顾哈里的工作交给了我。谢天谢地，现在哈里的精神状态还处在相对好的时候，当然再也不比从前。可是，他醒着的时候，至少还可以说一会儿话。应他的要求，我告诉了他所有这次步行的情况以及我离开他的这段时间所获得的自我领悟或者洞察力。他也问了有关其他步行者的情况，他们是否有任何自己已经注意到或者我观察到的正面性变化。我有太多的事情要跟他讲述。

“And what are you doing this week in regards to friends, Bronnie?” he enquired in his weakening voice. “What time are you giving of your week to spend with good quality friends? That’s what I want to know.” I laughed at his persistence on the subject, and said there would be plenty of time to catch up with other friends later. Right now I wanted to enjoy my time with him, Harry, who was also my friend.

> “邦妮，关于朋友方面，这个星期里你做了些什么呢？”他以微弱的声音向我打听。“这周里，你在什么时候跟你高品质的朋友在一起？那都是我想知道的。”我因他一再坚持这个话题而发笑了。我说，稍后，我会花很多时间去联系我的其他朋友，而现在，我希望享受跟他在一起的时间。哈里，现在也是我的朋友了。“我亲爱的女孩，这还不够。你在做别人做的事。

“It’s not enough my dear girl. You are doing what others do. Surely you have learned by now that you must take time out for you too. Find some balance and make time for your friends regularly. Do it for yourself even more than for them. We need our friends.” Harry looked at me sternly with a look of warning. But we both knew there was love behind his insistence.

> 我敢确定，你现在学到的是你也必须把时间花在自己身上。寻找一些平衡，定期跟你的朋友相处一些时间。跟朋友在一起不是为了他们，更多的是为了你自己。我们需要我们的朋友。”哈里以一种严厉的警告眼神看着我。然而，我们双方都明白在他坚持的后面是一种爱。

He was right. I needed to maintain some time out with friends regularly, rather than be working all of these twelve-hour shifts and catching up with them all later. As much as I loved this work and sometimes shared some wonderful laughter with the clients and their families, it was a pretty serious world I was living in. Being around dying people and the sadness of their families needed to be balanced with some lightness that only friends could provide. Joy was missing from my life and it was only now I was able to truly acknowledge this to myself.

> 他是对的。我需要定期花一些时间跟朋友在一起，而不是总在十二小时的轮班中拼命工作，只在晚些时候才去找他们。我差不多很爱这种工作，有些时候与客户们和他们的家人分享某些很棒的欢笑声是我生活世界里非同小可的一部分。整天置身于临终的人和他们家属的悲伤之中是需要一些轻快的元素来平衡自己，而那些元素只有朋友能够提供。我的生活没有了喜悦，而现在只有靠自己能够真正的承认这一点才能得到改变。

“You’re right, Harry,” I admitted. He smiled and put his arms up for a hug. I leant down on the bed and embraced him, smiling. “It’s not just about staying in touch with your friends, my dear girl. It is about giving yourself the gift of their company too. You understand this, don’t you?” he asked with both words and eyes.

> “你是正确的，哈里。”我承认。他微笑了，伸出他的手臂要拥抱我。我弯下身微笑着紧紧拥抱了他。“我亲爱的女孩，这不仅仅是保持与朋友联系。这也是给你自己的一种有人相伴的礼物。你懂得这一点，是吗？”他用话语和眼神来问我这个问题。

Nodding with conviction I replied, “Yes Harry, I do”. Leaving him to his rest a little while later, I appreciated the point he had made and the direct honesty shared. Harry was blessed with a smooth passing. He died in his sleep a few nights later.

> 我点点头，确信的回答他：“是的，哈里，我懂了。”稍后，我离开他让他休息一会儿，我欣赏他指出的这个观点，并感激他很直接且坦诚地跟我分享他的思想。哈里很幸运，在安详中离去。他在几天后的晚间睡觉中去世的。

Calling to let me know, his daughter thanked me sincerely. But as I said to her, Harry gave a lot to me too. It had been my pleasure to know him. “Allow yourself time with your friends,” I still hear him say. The words of that dear man with the bushy eyebrows, red face, and big smile live on still.

> 他的女儿打电话给我让我知道了这个消息，并诚挚地感谢我。而我也对她说，哈里也给了我很多，能够认识他是我的荣幸。“允许你自己有时间跟朋友在一起。”我仿佛依然听见他对我这样说话。那位有着浓眉、红脸和满面笑容的可爱男人的话还至今在我耳边回响。
