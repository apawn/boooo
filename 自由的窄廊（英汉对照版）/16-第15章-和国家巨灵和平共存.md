# Living with the Leviathan · 第15章　和国家巨灵和平共存

## Hayek’s Mistake · 海耶克的错误

In the middle of World War II, the director of the London School of Economics (LSE), William Beveridge, led a team of civil servants to produce a government report titled Social Insurance and Allied Services. This document, now known as the Beveridge Report, became the foundation of the expansion of the welfare state in the UK. Its key recommendations included a significant expansion of National Insurance, the program that provides unemployment benefits, sick pay, and pensions, creating universal free healthcare in the guise of the National Health Service and implementing a minimum wage. The report was hugely popular among the British public. The postwar Labour minister of national insurance, James Griffiths, wrote in his memoirs, “In one of the darkest hours of the war [the report] fell like manna from heaven.”

> 二次大战期间，伦敦政经学院院长威廉．贝佛里奇（William Beveridge）领导一群公务员，提出一份题为《社会保险与联合服务》的政府报告。这份报告如今以《贝佛里奇报告》为人所知，后来变成英国扩大福利国家的基础。该文件的主要建议包括大幅扩张国民保险方案，以便提供失业救济、疾病给付和退休金，并以国民健康服务之名，创造全面免费医疗保健，实施最低工资。这份报告备受英国大众欢迎，战后工党国民保险部长詹姆斯．葛里菲斯（James Griffiths）在回忆录中写道：「在战争最黑暗的一刻，〔这份报告〕犹如天降甘露。」

Some of the report’s recommendations were implemented during the war, with an expansion of infant, child, and maternity services, a program providing fuel and subsidized milk to mothers and families with children under the age of five, and free school meals for children. In 1945, the Labour Party swept to power based on its promise to implement the Beveridge Report, and proceeded to enact a number of iconic legislations making the report’s plans a reality. These included the Family Allowances Act of 1945, the National Insurance Act of 1946, the National Assistance Act of 1948, and the National Health Service Act of 1946.

> 报告中的若干建议在战时即已实施，包括扩大婴儿、儿童与产前护理，对妈妈和有五岁以下子女的家庭，提供燃料和获有补贴的牛奶，以及学童免费营养午餐。一九四五年，工党靠着承诺实施《贝佛里奇报告》的政见上台执政后，推动制定若干代表性的立法，使该报告化为实际，其中包括一九四五年的《家庭津贴法》、一九四六年的《国民保险法》一九四八年的《国民济助法》和一九四六年的《国民健康服务法》。

A brilliant émigré from Vienna, then teaching at the LSE, was alarmed. Friedrich von Hayek’s main concern was the rise of the totalitarian state, and he saw Nazism, from which he had fled several years earlier, as one extreme form of this totalitarian state. Hayek was particularly concerned about “socialist” state planning and administrative regulation of the economy morphing into a type of totalitarianism. He first expressed his ideas about the dangers of growing state administration in the economy in a memo written to William Beveridge. This memo grew into a magazine article, and then into a book, The Road to Serfdom, that has since become one of the most influential works of social science of the twentieth century.

> 当时在伦敦政经学院任教的维也纳杰出移民海耶克深感震惊。海耶克主要担心的是极权主义国家的崛起，认为几年前他所逃避的纳粹主义，正是这种极权主义国家的一种极端形式。海耶克特别担心「社会主义式」的国家计划和经济行政管理，会演变成一种极权主义的形式。他首先在写给贝佛里奇的备忘录中，表达他对经济行政管理日增会有危险的看法，这份备忘录扩写为一篇杂志文章，然后变成名作《到奴役之路》（The Road to Serfdom ），变成二十世纪社会科学中最有影响力的一本著作。

Hayek was not against all government intervention or social insurance. He wrote, “Probably nothing has done so much harm to the liberal cause as the wooden insistence of some liberals on certain rough rules of thumb, above all the principle of laissez faire,” and added “there can be no doubt that some minimum of food, shelter, and clothing, sufficient to preserve health and the capacity to work, can be assured to everybody.” But he was worried about the state playing a defining role in influencing wages and the allocation of resources.

> 海耶克并非反对所有的政府干预或社会保险，他写道：「很可能没有什么事情，会像若干自由派僵固坚持特定通则，尤其是坚持自由放任原则那样，对自由主义大业造成这么大的伤害。」他继续指出：「确保每一个人都能获得某种最低限度的食衣住供应，以便维护健康和工作能力，是毫无疑问的事情。」但是，他担心国家机器在影响工资和资源分配上，扮演决定性的角色。

This, he thought, might be the direction that many countries were moving toward, partly because of the influence of socialist ideas, and his book was meant to be a corrective. In the foreword to the 1956 U.S. edition, Hayek, having seen the Labour government’s policies, wrote: That hodgepodge of ill-assembled and often inconsistent ideals which under the name of the Welfare State has largely replaced socialism as the goal of the reformers needs very careful sorting-out if its results are not to be very similar to those of full-fledged socialism.

> 他认为，这样可能是受到社会主义理念的部分影响，变成很多国家正在前进的方向。他的大作意在力挽狂澜。在看到英国工党政府推动的政策后，他在一九五六年版《到奴役之路》的序言中写道：以福利国家为名，不当凑合而且经常不连贯的理念大杂烩，大致已经取代社会主义，变成了改革派的目标。我们必须慎重厘清这些目标，因为我们不希望产生非常类似于成熟社会主义所造成的结果。

This is not to say that some of its aims are not both practicable and laudable. But there are many ways in which we can work toward the same goal, and in the present state of opinion there is some danger that our impatience for quick results may lead us to choose instruments which, though perhaps more efficient for achieving the particular ends, are not compatible with the preservation of a free society. He continued:

> 这不是说其中若干目标不可行、不值得称赞；但是，要达成同样的目标，有很多方法可以应用。在目前舆论的氛围中，有一些我们急于见到成果的危险，可能导致我们选择或许能够更有效地达成特定目标、却跟维护自由社会相左的工具。他继续写道：

Of course, six years of socialist government in England have not produced anything resembling a totalitarian state. But those who argue that this has disproved the thesis of The Road to Serfdom have really missed one of its main points: that the most important change which extensive government control produces is a psychological change, an alteration in the character of the people. This is necessarily a slow affair, a process which extends not over a few years but perhaps over one or two generations.

> 英国社会主义政府执政六年，当然没有产生类似极权主义国家的结果，但主张这种情形可以驳斥《到奴役之路》论点的人，其实错失了书中的一个重点，就是政府全面控制造成的最重要变化是心理变化，是人民性格的变化。这种变化一定是缓慢的过程，不单是延续几年，而是可能延续个一、两代。

The important point is that the political ideals of the people and its attitude toward authority are as much the effect as the cause of the political institutions under which it lives. This means, among other things, that even a strong tradition of political liberty is no safeguard if the danger is precisely that new institutions and policies will gradually undermine and destroy that spirit.

> 重点是人民的政治理念和对权威的态度既是人民所过政治制度的因，也是这种政治制度的果。先不提其他，这至少表示即便拥有强大的政治自由传统，也无法保证能够持续维持，因为新制度和新政策有着逐渐破坏和摧毁自由精神的危险。

Hayek’s “psychological change” here is similar to what we have called the dominance of the state over society. Viewed from this perspective, Hayek’s concern was that the increased power of the British state would emasculate society and pave the road to despotism. As Hayek himself argued elsewhere and even in the quote above, some of the goals may be “practicable and laudable.”

> 海耶克在这里说的「心理变化」类似我们所说国家对社会的宰割。从这个角度来看，海耶克担忧的事情，是英国国家机器力量增加后，会削弱社会，为专制主义奠定坦途。海耶克本人在别的地方，甚至在上述引文中，认为其中若干目标可能「可行、值得称赞」。

But that wasn’t enough, because the character of state-society relations might be endangered by this increased power of the state. That’s what he was afraid of. In fact, the defense Hayek envisaged against this potential problem is in line with our thesis. He wrote: “The consequences can of course be averted if that spirit reasserts itself in time and the people not only throw out the party which has been leading them further and further in the dangerous direction but also recognize the nature of the danger and resolutely change their course.”

> 但是，这样还不够，因为国家与社会的关系本质可能遭到国家机器能力增加危害，这点正是他害怕的事情。事实上，海耶克为这种可能问题设想的防御之道，正好符合我们的论点。他写道：「如果那种精神能够及时自行重新伸张，人民不但扬弃领导他们愈来愈深入危险方向的政党，还认清这种危险的本质，坚决改变自己的方向的话，这种结果当然可以避免。」

In other words, Hayek recognized that the only way to prevent the Despotic Leviathan from emerging is for society to reassert itself against the state’s power and dominance. So far so good. But Hayek’s astute analysis misses a vital force—the Red Queen effect. The only option of society against expanding state capacity is not to rein it back completely. It can alternatively increase its own capacity, its own checks over the state. That is what happened in Britain and in most of Europe in the decades following World War II. And as we saw in Chapter 10, some of these dynamics played out in the United States as well.

> 换句话说，海耶克承认，要防止专制国家出现，唯一的方法是社会重新伸张自己，对抗国家的权力和宰割。目前为止听起来都很好，但是海耶克在敏锐的分析中，错过了一种极为重要的力量，就是错过了红皇后效应。社会在对抗国家机器扩张权力时，唯一的选项不是彻底抑制国家机器，而是可以采用别的方法，提高自己约束国家机器的能力。二次大战后的几十年里，英国和大部分欧洲国家正是透过红皇后效应，同时提高国家与社会的力量。而正如我们在第十章所见，红皇后效应在美国也展现了部分的效果。

In fact, a lot of human progress depends on the state’s role and capacity advancing to meet new challenges while society also becomes more powerful and vigilant. Nipping greater state capacity in the bud would preclude such human progress. It is particularly important for the state to expand its remit during moments of economic or social crisis. In Britain the Beveridge report was a response to such a crisis.

> 事实上，人类的很多进步要取决于国家角色和能力的推进，同时社会也变得更有力、更警觉，才能因应新的挑战。在国家能力萌芽之际，就摘除其根芽，会阻止人类的进步。当国家面临经济或社会危机时，国家扩大其职权范围就特别重要。英国的《贝佛里奇报告》就是为了因应这种危机。

So Hayek’s mistake was twofold. First, he did not foresee the power of the Red Queen and recognize that it could keep the Shackled Leviathan inside the corridor. Second, perhaps unsurprisingly, he did not see what is now much more evident—the need for the state to play a role in redistribution, creating a social safety net and regulating the increasingly complex economy that had already emerged in the first half of the twentieth century. Staying in the corridor is not automatic, especially in the face of new challenges.

> 因此，海耶克犯了双重错误：第一是没有预测到红皇后的力量，不知道红皇后可以把受到制约的国家巨灵，继续留在自由窄廊里。第二个错误可能不足为奇，就是没有看出现在已经更加明显的事实：人们需要国家机器发挥作用，以便重新分配、创造社会安全网、规范二十世纪上半叶时已经日趋复杂的经济体系。国家不会自动停留在这条窄廊中，面对挑战时尤其如此。

We saw in Chapter 13 how countries fall out of the corridor when the Red Queen turns zero-sum. Hayek was concerned about an even more basic challenge to liberty—the increasing power of the administrative state ushering in a new type of “serfdom.” But the Red Queen effect, provided that it doesn’t turn zero-sum, can also be a powerful force helping a society stay in the corridor while developing new capabilities and institutional arrangements for keeping in check an expanding state. There is perhaps no better example than the founding of the Swedish welfare state in the midst of the Great Depression to illustrate how the Red Queen can play this role and how her mobilization often necessitates new coalitions.

> 我们在第十三章中，已经看到红皇后变成零和状态时，国家会跌出这条窄廊的现象。海耶克担心自由碰到一种更根本的挑战──国家行政能力提高──会带来新形态的「奴役」，但是只要红皇后效应不变成零和游戏，也可以成为强而有力的力量，协助社会留在自由窄廊中，同时发展新的能力和制度，继续约束不断扩大的国家机器。可能没有一个例子，比瑞典在大萧条期间建立福利国家的事例，更能说明红皇后怎么扮演这种角色，以及红皇后的动员经常需要靠新联盟推动的原因。

## The Cow Trade · 乳牛交易

The Great Depression created a crisis for state and society everywhere in the West. The economic crisis begot a political crisis, but one that played out very differently in different countries. While Germany succumbed to Nazism and swiftly left the corridor, and the United States scrambled to deal with these problems within its own constraints, Sweden embarked upon what has become an iconic example of the simultaneous expansion of the state’s and society’s capacities powered by the Red Queen effect. In the interwar years Sweden introduced universal male suffrage, a more competitive electoral landscape emerged, and new coalitions not only kept Sweden inside the corridor but significantly increased the state’s capacity to regulate the labor market and influence the distribution of income. Several factors prepared the ground for these changes.

> 大萧条为西方每一个国家和社会创造危机。经济危机引发政治危机，但不同国家发生的政治危机大不相同：德国屈服在纳粹主义之下，迅速脱离自由窄廊；美国忙着在自己所受到的限制内，手忙脚乱地应付这些问题；瑞典则开始在红皇后效应的推动下，同时扩大国家和社会能力。瑞典在两次大战之间的战间期，推动男性的全面投票权，更具竞争性的选举版图因此出现。新联盟不但让瑞典继续留在窄廊里，也大幅提高了国家机器的能力，以便规范劳工市场、影响所得分配；好几个因素为这些变化奠定了基础。

The Swedish economy was largely rural at the turn of the century, with as much as half of the population still working in agriculture. Sweden had a long history of parliamentary representation, including for peasants, as we saw in Chapter 6, and by the nineteenth century the landed aristocracy had lost much of its wealth and power. Nevertheless, the extent of the franchise was limited and its second, aristocratic chamber still exercised considerable influence over politics.

> 十九、二十世纪之交，瑞典大致上属于农业经济，多达半数人口，仍然以务农为生。我们在第六章里已经看到，瑞典的议会代议制度历史悠久，连农民都包括在内；到了十九世纪，土地贵族虽然已经失去大部分的财富和权力，不过参政权的范围仍然有限，由贵族组成的第一议院（First Chamber）在政治上仍然能够发挥相当大的影响力。

Universal male suffrage was introduced in 1909 for the Second Chamber and in 1918 for all elections, and the power of the monarchy was curtailed in 1918, paving the way for parliamentary democracy with competitive elections. The Swedish Workers Party (SAP) played a defining role in these institutional changes. In contrast to many other European socialist parties, the SAP had shed its Marxist roots quite thoroughly before it started contesting power.

> 一九○九年，瑞典引入第二议院（Second Chamber）的男性全面普选，并在一九一八年将男性普选推行至所有选举中。同一年里，国王的权力遭到削弱，为国会民主制度和竞争性选举奠定坦途。在这些制度性改革中，瑞典社会民主工人党（简称瑞典社民党，SAP）扮演了决定性的角色。和欧洲其他社会主义政党相比，瑞典社民党开始争取政权前，已经相当彻底地抛弃了本身马克思主义的根源。

One of the architects of this transformation was its influential early-twentieth-century leader Hjalmar Branting. While other socialist parties on the continent were holding out for a revolution led by the proletariat and undermining their electoral prospects with bitter ideological quarrels, Branting was busy looking for coalition partners to turn the SAP into a veritable electoral force. He argued in 1886, “In a backward land like Sweden we cannot close our eyes to the fact that the middle class increasingly plays a very important role. The working class needs the help it can get from this direction just like the middle class for its part needs the workers behind it . . .”

> 推动这种转型的建筑师之一，是二十世纪初期很有影响力的领袖亚尔马．布兰廷（Hjalmar Branting）。欧洲大陆其他社会主义政党坚持无产阶级领导的革命，并让激烈的意识形态之争破坏自己在选举中胜出的希望时，布兰廷却忙于寻找联盟伙伴，好把瑞典社民党变成真正的选举力量；一八八六年时，他曾经主张：「在瑞典这样落后的地方，我们不能闭上双眼，无视中产阶级扮演的角色愈来愈重要的事实。工人阶级需要协助时，可以像需要工人做后盾的中产阶级一样，从这个方向去寻找，得到协助……。」

This quest was helped by the fact that a major objective of the SAP at the beginning of the century was to secure universal male suffrage for both chambers of parliament, which meant a program committed to a strategy of empowering all those who did not have representation, including farmers and peasants. When the Great Depression hit, Sweden was not spared. As in many other countries, the government tried to defend the value of its currency, the krona, and the resulting policies led to deflation and a deeper crisis as unemployment soared. Even though Sweden partially reversed course and abandoned the gold standard after the British devalued in 1931, conditions did not improve.

> 二十世纪之初，瑞典社民党的主要目标，是争取男性在国会两院选举时的普选权，也就是决心为包括农民和小农等没有代表权的族群，推动争取权利的策略，这个目标有助于瑞典社民党寻找盟友。大萧条来袭时，瑞典并没有幸免，瑞典政府像许多国家一样，试图捍卫瑞典货币克朗（krona）币值。政府采取的政策造成通货紧缩，失业人口飞跃上升，危机更趋严重。虽然瑞典在一九三一年英镑贬值后，改变部分路线，放弃金本位，情势却没有改善。

It was in this context that the SAP’s search for coalition partners began transforming Swedish politics. The SAP at this point turned from the middle class to farmers and peasants as coalition partners. It was an uphill struggle. The SAP was allied with trade unions, whose main priorities were to preserve unemployment compensation, maintain high wage levels, and create jobs in the industrial sector using public works and government spending. They stood against any policy that would increase food prices for workers. Farmers, on the other hand, were opposed to high wages for workers and instead sought price supports via marketing boards and other methods to prop up agricultural prices.

> 社民党就是在这种情况下寻找盟友，开始改变瑞典政治。这时，社民党要找的盟友从中产阶级，变成农民和小农，这样做是一场艰苦的奋斗。社民党跟工会结盟，工会的优先目标是维护失业救济，维持高水准的薪资，以及利用公共工程和政府支出在产业部门创造就业；凡是会提高工人购买食品价格的政策，工会一概反对。相反地，农民反对工人的高工资，反而利用行销协会和其他方法寻求价格支撑，以便提高农产品价格。

But this did not stop the SAP’s quest. In advance of the 1932 elections, its leader, Per Albin Hansson, presented the SAP as the “people’s home,” open to all Swedes. He explained that its most important task [is] working with all energy to help all groups suffering from unprovoked effects of the economic crisis . . . The party does not aim to support and help [one] working class at the expense of the others. It does not differentiate in its work for the future between the industrial working class and the agricultural class or between workers of the hand and workers of the brain.

> 但是，这种情形没有阻止社民党的追求。一九三二年选举前，该党领袖佩尔．韩森（Per Albin Hansson）把社民党比拟为「人民的家园」，对所有的瑞典人开放，也说明该党最重要的任务是：尽全力协助所有平白无故受到经济危机打击的族群……该党的目标不是协助〔一种〕工作阶级、牺牲其他阶级，该党推动本身未来的行动方针时，也不会在产业工人阶级和农业阶级之间，或在手工工人和脑力工作者之间有所区分。

The leaders of the SAP presented this strategy as a response to the dire conditions created by the economic crisis, and the party’s main selling point for the electorate was its willingness to experiment with activism to counter the adverse effects of the Depression. Its manifesto in the 1932 election made this clear, stating that the country was engulfed by a crisis developing which claims victims in all sectors of society . . . [the party] strives for measures for lasting improvement of the situation [and] devotes its efforts towards inducing the state to bring effective help to the innocent victims of the crisis.

> 社民党领袖提出这种策略，以便因应经济危机造成的严峻情势。该党竞选时主打乐于实验的行动主义，以便对抗大萧条的不利影响。该党在一九三二年选举中的竞选宣言说得很清楚，宣称国家遭到侵害：社会所有阶层都成为此一发展中危机的受害者……〔本党〕会努力推出能够永久改善这种情势的措施，〔而且〕会致力引导国家，为危机的无辜受害者提供有效的协助。

It worked. The party massively increased its vote share compared to the 1928 election, capturing 41.7 percent of the total vote, an historic success, even if not enough for a majority. It was at this point that Hansson’s efforts to court farmers bore fruit and the SAP arranged the “cow trade,” entering into an alliance with the Agrarian Party to form a government. The SAP accepted protectionist measures to increase agricultural prices, and in return received a mandate to implement a crisis package aimed at increasing government spending and wages in the industrial sector.

> 这种策略产生了功效，和一九二八年相比，该党的得票比率巨幅提高，在全部票数中，得到百分之四一．七的选票；仍然不足以构成多数，却是空前的成就，代表韩森努力追求农民支持的做法开花结果。于是社民党安排「乳牛交易」（cow trade），和农业党结盟[〔注1〕](#note-1) ，组成政府，接受保护主义措施，以便提高农产品价格，并因此获得授权，可以实施意在增加政府支出、提高产业部门薪资的危机方案。

This package was initially opposed by powerful banking interests such as the Wallenbergs and the largest and most assertive segment of the business community, which was producing for the export market and was thus concerned about higher labor costs eroding its competitiveness. But this changed after the 1936 election, which witnessed the electorate lining up behind the SAP in greater numbers. This broad support led to a meeting between representatives of business interests, trade unions, and farmers as well as the government, in the small resort town of Saltsjöbaden in 1938. The meeting resulted in an expansion of the “social democratic” coalition to include businesses, which acquiesced to the new government programs, the welfare state, and the resulting high wages in return for more cooperative labor relations and reduced strike activity.

> 这个方案起初遭到华伦堡（Wallenberg）家族等金融利益团体反对，也遭到最大、最独断的企业部门反对，因为企业部门主要是为出口市场生产，担心劳工成本升高会侵蚀他们的竞争力。但是，在一九三六年的选举后，情势改变，选民大量集结，作为社民党的后盾。这种普遍支持促使企业界、工会、农民和政府代表在一九三八年，在休闲小镇萨尔特舍巴登（Saltsjöbaden）集会，得出扩大「社会民主」联盟、纳入企业界的结论，企业界默认政府的新计划、福利国家和因此而生的高工资，换取劳工的合作和减少罢工。

This model was further developed after World War II. The social consensus came to form around the idea that the Swedish state should support both equity and growth. Out of this consensus emerged the corporatist model, wherein the state provided generous benefits to workers while also encouraging moderation in wage setting and facilitating greater fluidity in the labor market via active labor market policies (for example, helping workers find jobs by retraining them). In line with the agreement at Saltsjöbaden, this strategy benefited businesses as well.

> 二次大战后，这种模式进一步发展，开始根据瑞典国家机器应该支持平等和成长的观念，凝聚社会共识，形成社团主义模式。在这种模式中，国家要提供工人丰厚的福祉，同时也要鼓励适度的设定工资，利用积极的劳工市场政策，促进劳工市场更大的流动性（例如利用职业再培训，协助工人找工作）。此一策略符合萨尔特舍巴登协议，也为企业界带来好处。

A bulwark of the system was the Rehn-Meidner model of centralized wage setting in which a social bargain would fix industry-level wages for all firms. This not only created a more equal distribution of earnings among workers via “wage compression” (all workers doing the same job being paid the same amount) but also implied that the more productive firms would not have to pay higher wages. This was a huge profit opportunity for high-productivity firms that would pay the same wage as the rest of the industry. By the same logic, the system encouraged firms to invest, innovate, and reorganize to increase their productivity, since they could get to keep all of the increase in productivity as additional profits.

> 这种制度的重要支柱是工资订定集中化的雷恩—梅德诺模式（Rehn-Meidner model），根据这种模式进行的社会谈判，会订出所有企业都适用的产业薪资水准，这样不但透过「薪资压缩」（同工同酬），创造比较平等的盈余分配，也暗示比较有生产力的公司不必发放比较高的薪资，因此对生产力较高、却只需要发放跟同业水准相同薪资的公司来说，这样是惊人的获利机会。根据同样的逻辑，这种制度会鼓励企业投资、创新和整顿，以便提高生产力，因为他们可以把提高的所有生产力保留下来，变成额外的利润。

Meanwhile, the Swedish welfare state continued to expand and develop. Sweden not only established more generous social benefits, but also provided them at similar levels to all citizens following the universal template of the Beveridge Report. Generous unemployment benefits and health insurance were followed by pioneering maternity and child benefits and an egalitarian high-quality education system, which came out of an effort to “democratize the Swedish school system.” These programs enabled the country to be at the forefront of reducing poverty, a huge achievement during the Great Depression when sky-high poverty rates not only created an environment of fear and uncertainty but also threatened the democratic political system, as in Germany.

> 同时，瑞典式的福利国家继续扩大、继续发展。瑞典不但建立了比较慷慨的社会福利，也根据《贝佛里奇报告》的通用范本，对所有公民提供类似水准的福利。瑞典率先推出生育与孩童福利后，接着推出丰厚的失业救济和健康保险，以及高品质的公平教育制度，致力追求「瑞典学校制度的民主化」。这些计划使瑞典成为推动铲除贫穷的先驱，也让瑞典在大萧条期间创造了惊人的成就，没有陷入贫穷比率一飞冲天、恐惧与不确定心理弥漫，威胁诸如德国等国家民主政治制度的情况。

From the viewpoint of our framework, the crucial point isn’t just the major expansion of the role and capacity of the Swedish state, but the way this took place together with a deepening of democracy and societal control; society’s capacity increased at the same time as the state’s. There were several facets to this process. First, a primary concern with any expansion in the role of the state is the possibility of “elite capture,” turning state involvement into a tool for a few businesses or some narrow interests to benefit at society’s expense.

> 从我们的理论架构的角度来看，关键不仅是瑞典国家机器的角色和能力大幅扩张，也在于民主与社会控制同时强化、社会和国家能力同时提高的方式。这种过程包括好几个面向：第一个面向是国家机器角色扩张的主要忧虑，是国家机器可能遭到「菁英俘掳」，把国家的参与变成造福若干企业或狭隘利益团体的工具，却牺牲了社会。

The fact that this was all happening under the leadership of the SAP and the pivotal role that trade unions came to play as partners in this process and in monitoring and administrating the system was a major obstacle to this type of hijacking of state institutions. The universal nature of the Swedish welfare programs precluded the possibility that these could become tools of patronage in the hands of the elite, and further helped create social cohesion and a sense of ownership in the population, contributing to society’s mobilization to support them.

> 这一切在社民党的领导下，以及在工会以伙伴身分，对这种过程和制度的严密监督和管理的情况下发生，正是一种重大阻碍，足以防止国家制度遭到劫持。瑞典的福利计划具有普遍性，可以排除福利计划落在菁英手中，变成施惠工具的可能性，进一步协助社会团结和凝聚人民是主人翁的意识，强化人民对社会动员的支持。

Second, a major danger related to Hayek’s worries was that the greater role of the state in the economy could come at the expense of business in general, for example, with nationalizations and expropriation of capital. Sweden contained this possibility by engaging the business community in the social democratic coalition after Saltsjöbaden. It is telling in this context that the SAP had consistently refused to partner with Communists and stayed away from nationalization or overt expropriation of profits or capital.

> 第二，和海耶克忧心忡忡有关的重大危险是：国家机器在经济体系中的角色扩大，可能会牺牲整体企业，例如，可能使企业遭到国有化，资本遭到没收。瑞典在萨尔特舍巴登会议后，借着拉拢企业界参与社会民主同盟，消除了这种可能性。社民党显然在这种情况下，一直拒绝跟共产党人为伍，同时跟国有化或公开没收利润或资本保持距离。

At times, trade unions attempted to push for policies that would further increase wages, but the SAP typically resisted them. One exception, which affirms the extent of societal control, was the reaction against a movement in the trade unions and the SAP in the 1970s to change the terms of the Rehn-Meidner model of centralized wage setting by creating “wage-earner funds” to claw back the “excess profits” accruing to high-productivity firms still paying industry-level wages. As it became clear that the implementation of these funds threatened the very coalition that undergirded Swedish social democracy, opposition to them grew. This forced the SAP to back down and ultimately lose power for the first time in 1976.

> 工会偶尔会试图推动进一步加薪的政策，社民党通常都会拒绝，只有一次例外：就是在一九七○年代内，社民党为了因应工会的行动，改变雷恩—梅德诺集中化制定工资模式的条件，设立「薪资所得者基金」，希望把发放符合企业薪资水准的高生产力企业的「超额利润」收回。结果情势很清楚，设立这种基金将会威胁支撑瑞典社会民主制度联盟的根基，反对声浪增加，迫使社民党退缩，最后还在一九七六年时，首次失去政权。这个结果反而确认了社会控制的程度。

Third, the expansion of the state coincided with a deepening of democracy. As the major parties in Sweden all came to accept the basic tenets of social democracy, the electorate started having a choice between different parties that would implement versions of the social democratic approach, and also if necessary pull back from more extreme policies such as the wage-earner funds proposed by the trade unions and the SAP in the 1970s. Finally, the Swedish bureaucracy and judiciary developed in tandem with these changes, especially in the course of administering and overseeing these programs together with the trade unions. In the process, they gained the competence to implement the social programs and restrain abuses of the system.

> 第三，国家机器扩张和民主制度深化同时发生。随着瑞典主要政党全都接受社会民主制度的基本信念，选举人开始可以在实施不同社会民主模式的不同政党之间选择，而且在必要时，像一九七○年代工会和社民党提倡「薪资所得者基金」时，撤回对比较极端政策的支持。最后，瑞典的官僚体系和司法体系跟这些变革同时并进，在结合工会，共同管理和监督这些计划时尤其如此，行政和司法体系在这种过程中，获得实施社会计划、拒绝滥用整个制度的能力。

In sum, the new necessities and conditions of crisis created by the economic downturn were met by the Swedish state expanding both its role and its capacity. In contrast to Hayek’s fears, this did not pave the way to totalitarianism. On the contrary, because state expansion was carried out by a coalition of workers, farmers, and business interests and because the Red Queen effect triggered societal mobilization to keep the state in check, Swedish democracy, far from weakening, got stronger in the process.

> 总之，瑞典的国家机器借着扩大本身的角色和能力，因应经济下行造成的新需要和危机状况，这种情形跟海耶克的恐惧正好相反，并没有为极权主义奠定坦途。反而因为国家机器的扩张是由工人、农民、企业团体的联盟负责执行，也因为红皇后效应激发了社会动员，对国家机器加以制约，瑞典的民主制度不但没有削弱，反而在这种过程中日益强健。

Though the details of the Swedish experience are unique, its broad outlines parallel what transpired in several other countries. Denmark and Norway built similar welfare states, even if the way in which their coalitions came together differed. Germany too developed a welfare state supported by a high level of state capacity and societal controls after World War II. Equally interesting is the American experience. FDR confronted the same economic and social upheaval that the SAP encountered but also had to grapple with a society that was sharply divided along racial and regional lines and more suspicious of government action.

> 虽然瑞典经验的细节独一无二，大致的轮廓却类似另外好几个国家发生的事例：丹麦和挪威建立了类似的福利国家，只是两国形成联盟的方式各不相同。二次大战后，德国也在高水准的国家能力和社会控制的支持下，发展成福利国家。美国的经验同样有趣，小罗斯福总统和瑞典社民党一样，面对相同的经济与社会动荡，却也必须因应社会中的严重种族和地域分裂，以及对政府更怀疑的人民。

Nevertheless, his early policy initiatives, which included the National Industrial Recovery Act (NIRA) in 1933 and the founding of the Agricultural Adjustment Administration, went in the same direction and expanded the capacity of the state to bolster the safety net and help the economy recover. This program sought to bring both workers and farmers on board as well. Title I of NIRA, for example, had a very similar remit to the industrial policies that the SAP adopted, while support for farmers in the form of higher prices was at the center of the administration’s agricultural policies.

> 然而，他早期的政策作为，包括一九三三年《全国工业复兴法》的立法、农业调整管理局的设立，走的都是相同的方向，都强化了国家加强安全网和帮助经济复苏的能力。这个计划试图招来工人和农民的共同参与，例如，《全国工业复兴法》第一章的条文中，就有非常类似瑞典社民党工业政策的职权范围；同时，还以较高的价格，作为农业调整管理局农业政策的核心，借以表明对农民的支持。

In both cases, FDR’s initial plans involved administrative controls and implementation, just as in Sweden. The U.S. conditions were different from those in Sweden, however; the NIRA faced fierce opposition from businesses and courts, and many of its provisions had to be abandoned or were implemented differently. But even if FDR’s plans were partly watered down and fell into line with the U.S. public-private partnership model, they achieved some of the same objectives as Sweden’s cow trade, and in the process fundamentally transformed the regulation and administration of the U.S. economy.

> 在这两个事例中，小罗斯福总统最初的计划都像瑞典一样，包括行政控制和实施。然而，美国的情形和瑞典不同，《全国工业复兴法》遭到企业和法院的强烈反对，很多条文都必须废弃或用不同的方式实施。但是，即使小罗斯福总统的计划有一部分遭到稀释，必须配合美国的公私合营模式，却也达成了跟瑞典「乳牛交易」相同的目标，而且借此改变了美国经济的管制和管理方式。

## The Leviathan vs. the Market · 国家巨灵和市场之争

A crucial debate in economics and social science concerns the balance between the state and the market. How much should the state intervene in the economy? What is the right scope and extent of regulation? Which activities should be left to markets and which others should be the purview of the state? The textbook economic answer is that the state should intervene only under clearly delineated circumstances.

> 经济学和社会科学中，有一项国家和市场之间平衡的重大争辩：国家对经济的干预应该多深入？正确的管制范围和程度应该怎么订？哪些活动应该由市场自理？哪些活动应该属于国家的管制权限？经济学教科书的答案是：国家只有在明确划定范围的情况下，才能干预。

These include the presence of “externalities,” which arise when actions by individual actors have major consequences for others that are not mediated via markets, paving the way for excessive levels of some activities such as pollution; the provision of “public goods,” which are goods from which everybody benefits, such as infrastructure or national defense; and situations in which there is pervasive “asymmetric information,” meaning that some market participants will not be able to accurately judge the quality of the products and services they are trading. They include as well the presence of monopolies that need to be regulated to prevent them from charging excessively high prices or engaging in predatory activities to drive out their competitors. Critically, government intervention is also needed for social insurance or redistribution to limit inequality.

> 这些情况包括：出现「外部性」，也就是个别行为人的行动对别人产生的影响，无法透过市场调解，为过度污染等若干活动奠定坦途时；其次是「公共财」的供应，也就是提供基础建设和国防之类每个人都能受惠的东西；以及「资讯普遍不均衡」，以至于某些市场参与者、无法精确判断所交易产品与服务品质的状况时；此外，也包括独占企业存在时需要加以管制，以免独占企业收取过高价格，或从事掠夺性活动，驱逐竞争者的状况。至为重要的是，政府在社会保险或限缩不平等的重分配上，也必须加以干预。

An important tenet of the textbook approach is that when working to influence the distribution of income in the economy, the state should minimize its impact on market prices and instead rely on taxes and transfers to achieve its objectives. This is consistent with The Road to Serfdom, where Hayek advocated limits on the scope of the state in the economy because markets are more efficient at allocating resources. But critically, Hayek went further and also argued that the increase in the power and involvement of the state can have adverse political implications.

> 教科书所说的方法有一个重要原则，就是国家在致力影响经济体系中的所得分配时，应该尽量降低这样做对市场价格的冲击，改为依赖租税或移转，以便达成目标。这个原则和《到奴役之路》若合符节。海耶克在这本大作中，主张限制国家在经济体系中的运作范围，因为市场在资源分配上，会比政府更有效率。但重要的是，海耶克也进一步主张国家的权力和介入增加，可能产生不利的政治影响。

Even if some of Hayek’s conclusions were neither fully compelling nor have been borne out by the political developments of the intervening decades, the way he approached the problem broke considerable new ground. Perhaps Hayek’s most brilliant insight is that the balance between the state and the market isn’t just about economics; it is about politics (and we are not saying this just because it is one of the main implications of our conceptual framework as well).

> 即使海耶克的若干结论既没有充分的说服力，也没有得到往后数十年的政治发展证实，他处理这个问题的方法还是开辟了相当多的新天地。海耶克最睿智的远见，可能是国家和市场之间的平衡不只是和经济有关，也和政治有关（我们并不是因为这一点同时也是我们观念架构中的主要涵义所以才这么说）。

The vital challenge is to make sure that the state can increase its capacity to meet society’s needs but still remain shackled. That necessitates new ways in which society is empowered to monitor and control the state and elites. So the diagnosis of beneficial state interventions is not just about economic trade-offs, but also about the interventions’ political implications. It’s not just about state capacity, but about who controls and monitors that capacity and how it will be used.

> 其中的重大挑战是确保国家能够提高能力、满足社会的需要，却仍然受到约束。这样就需要寻找新方法来赋予社会力量，以便监督和控制国家与菁英。因此，判断国家的干预是好是坏时，不仅跟国家的能力有关，也和国家能力由谁控制和监视，以及国家能力的使用方式有关。

In this light, the real institutional innovation in Sweden, and subsequently in other Scandinavian nations, was not just creating a more interventionist, redistributive state but doing so under the auspices of a coalition including businesses and the great majority of workers organized in politically active trade unions, which imposed tight shackles on the state. On the one hand, as we have noted already, the involvement of businesses, including the biggest corporations in Sweden, meant that the Swedish welfare state never went in the direction of wholesale nationalization of industries or abrogation of markets.

> 因此，瑞典等斯堪地那维亚半岛国家的真正创新，不只在于创造了比较偏向干预主义和重分配的国家，而是与此同时也接受一个成员包括企业界和绝大多数工人的联盟监督。这些工人都是由政治上活跃的工会负责组织，会为国家机器加上严密的制约。一方面，就像前面所指出的那样，包括瑞典最大企业在内的企业参与，表示瑞典的福利国家从来没有走上产业全面国有化，或废除市场的方向。

On the other hand, the pivotal role that trade unions played in this process enabled much greater popular participation in politics, stacking the cards against the hijacking of the now more powerful state institutions by elite interests. This coalition and the Red Queen effect enabled the Swedish political system to reorient itself when some of the regulations went too far in the 1970s and then again in the 1990s.

> 另一方面，工会在这种过程中所扮演的重大角色，有助于吸引极大多数的民众参与政治，防止菁英利益团体劫持现在已经比较有力的国家制度。在一九七○年代和后来的一九九○年代，若干管制变得太过极端时，这种联盟和红皇后效应使瑞典的政治制度能够自行调整。

There are three other vital lessons from the Swedish experience for understanding the balance between the state and the market. The first is a corollary of what we have already argued. When conditions necessitate new responsibilities for the state, this expansion must be accompanied by new ways for society to participate in politics, monitor the state and bureaucrats, and pull the carpet from under the new programs if necessary.

> 瑞典经验还有另外三个重要教训，有助于我们了解国家和市场之间的平衡。首先，是我们争论过的推论：情势要求国家负起新的责任时，这种能力扩张必须伴随着社会参与政治、监视国家机器和官僚体系、必要时从新计划中抽腿离开的新方法。

This means that a lot of the debate about the proper scope of markets and governments fails to grapple with the most defining question—even though Hayek long ago identified the critical importance of this question. Can we keep the Leviathan under control even with its new responsibilities and newly acquired powers? Do the costs of introducing new checks on the Leviathan, especially when these will not be automatically forthcoming due to the Red Queen effect, outweigh the benefits from the additional state intervention?

> 这表示有关市场和政府适当范围的辩论，都没有解决最重要的问题──即使海耶克很久以前就看出了这个问题极为重要。那个问题是：当国家机器有了新的责任、获得新的权力，它仍然能够受到我们的控制吗？约束国家机器的成本──尤其是因为红皇后效应，这些成本不会自动显现──是否超过国家额外干预的效益？

From this perspective, the reason why the government should not regulate the prices of most goods isn’t because these prices are set perfectly by markets (or, in the terminology of economics, because there are no externalities, public goods, asymmetric information, or distributional concerns), but because the political costs of the state widening its remit would be too high, either because of the additional precautions this requires or because of the heightened risk of spinning out of the corridor. This line of reasoning implies that the state should intervene only when the benefits from intervention are larger than the political costs of intervention.

> 从这个角度来看，政府不该管制大部分商品价格的原因，不是因为价格已经由市场设定的很完美（或是用经济学的术语来说，是因为其中没有外部性、公共财、资讯不均衡或分配问题），而是因为国家扩大职权范围的成本太高（需要额外的预防措施），或是因为逸出这条窄廊的风险升高。这种推理方式表示只有在干预的效益大于成本时，国家才应该干预。

More important, it also shows that interventions and activities that set in motion powerful (positive-sum) Red Queen effects are much more likely to be socially beneficial. So it is preferable for the state to provide social insurance and broad-based services and coordinate bargaining between employers and employees while getting both trade unions and businesses involved (which is likely to empower the Red Queen, as in the Swedish case) than to engage in specific, often opaque government regulations and corrective actions in narrow areas like sugar or steel tariffs (in the way that Paper Leviathans dealt with social demands, as we saw in Chapter 11).

> 更重要的是，这也显示干预和推动强力红皇后效应的行动，造福社会的可能性大得多（也就是双赢）。因此，国家最好提供社会保险和基础广大的服务，协调雇主和员工之间的谈判，同时促请工会和企业界参与（这样可能像瑞典经验一样，赋予红皇后力量），而非推动经常不透明的特定政府管制（或像第十一章所说纸糊国家处理社会需求那样），在砂糖或钢铁关税等狭隘范围中，采取矫正措施。

The second lesson is that some apparently inefficient aspects of the economy might have a useful social role after all. One such aspect is trade unions themselves, which are often viewed with great suspicion, because one of their main objectives is to push for higher wages for their members even if this makes it harder for non-members to find jobs. Indeed, we have seen that even in the Swedish context, trade unions have at times pushed for excessively high wages.

> 第二个教训是：经济体系中某些显然效率低落的层面，毕竟可能具有某种有用的社会功能。工会本身就是其中一个层面，人们经常用非常怀疑的眼光看待工会，因为工会的主要目标之一是：不顾非会员可能更难找到工作的问题，努力为会员争取比较高的工资。的确如此，我们甚至在瑞典的情况中，都可以看到工会偶尔会争取过高的工资。

This suspicious attitude is more than shared by many policy makers in the United States, who have sought to undermine the power of unions. Partly as a result of these policy attitudes (and partly because of the decline of manufacturing employment), today union membership is much lower in the U.S. economy, especially in the private sector, than it was in the heyday of labor unions in midcentury, after the rights of workers to organize in unions, engage in collective bargaining, and go on strike were recognized with the National Labor Relations (Wagner) Act of 1935. Similar declines in the power of labor unions have been ongoing in other advanced economies.

> 对工会抱持这种怀疑态度的美国人，不仅限于很多希望破坏工会权力的美国决策官员而已。今天一方面是因为这种政策态度（另一方面是因为制造业就业人口减少），美国经济中的工会会员人数，尤其是民间部门的工会会员人数，已经比二十世纪中期工会声势如日中天时少得多；当时工会兴起，是因为一九三五年全国劳资关系法（也叫华格纳法）立法，承认工人组织工会、从事集体谈判、发动罢工的权利。其他先进经济体也有类似工会会员人数减少的现象。

Whether the opposition to trade unions makes sense on purely economic grounds is debatable. But an essential role of trade unions is political; they are central for maintaining a partial balance of power between well-organized business interests and labor. The decline in the power of unions over the past several decades may thus have been one of the factors tilting the balance of power in U.S. society in favor of large corporations. The more important point for our framework is that, in evaluating the role of various policies and institutions, we must take into account the supporting arrangements that seek to create a balance and thus help keep the Leviathan and the elites shackled.

> 从纯经济的角度来看，反对工会是否有道理的问题，还是有辩论余地，但基本上，工会就是政治性的角色，在组织良好的企业利益和劳工维持局部权力均衡上，扮演最核心的角色。因此，过去数十年里，工会力量的降低，可能是美国社会权力均衡偏向有利于大企业的原因之一。我们的架构中比较重要的论点是：我们评估各种政策和制度的角色时，必须考虑寻找能够创造平衡、从而协助约束国家巨灵和菁英的辅助性安排。

The third important lesson is about the form of government intervention. Here we diverge more sharply from Hayek and the textbook answer in economics. They maintain that it is always better to refrain from meddling with market prices, and if the government wishes to create a more equitable division of income, then it should let the market work and use redistributive taxation to move toward the desired distribution. But this way of thinking incorrectly separates economics from politics. For the Leviathan to take market prices and the distribution of income as given and just rely on fiscal redistribution to achieve its objectives might translate into very high levels of taxes and redistribution.

> 第三个重要的教训和政府干预的形式有关，我们在这一点上，跟海耶克和经济学教科书上的答案，有着更严重的分歧──他们认为，避免干预市场价格总是比较好的方法，如果政府希望创造比较公平的所得分配，就应该让市场继续运作，利用具有重分配性质的租税，推动比较合乎理想的分配。但是，这种思维错误地把经济和政治划分开来，如果国家机器要像他们所说的那样，处理市场价格和所得分配问题，却只依靠财政重分配的手段，又希望达成目标的话，就可能必须实施非常高的税率和重分配。

Wouldn’t it be better, especially from the viewpoint of controlling the Leviathan, if market prices could be altered so as to achieve some of these objectives without as much fiscal redistribution? This is exactly what the Swedish welfare state did. The social democratic coalition was built on the corporatist model wherein trade unions and state bureaucracy directly regulated the labor market. This generated higher wages for workers and meant that there was less need for redistribution from the owners of capital and corporations to labor. It also generated wage compression so that the distribution of income among laborers was more equal.

> 如果能够改变市场价格，以便达成上述若干目标，却不必推动这么多的财政重分配，难道不会比较好吗？尤其是从控制国家机器的角度来看，难道不是更好吗？瑞典的福利国家正是这样做，瑞典的社会民主联盟建立在社团主义模式的基础上，工会和国家官僚体系直接管制劳动市场，为工人创造了比较高的工资，这表示比较不需要把资源从资本主和企业手中，重分配到劳工手中；这样做也会产生工资压缩的效果，促使劳工之间的所得分配更为公平，因此会比较不需要重分配的租税做法。

As a result, there was less need for redistributive taxation, even if quite a bit of that also took place in the Swedish economy to finance the generous welfare state. Much of this was not designed or planned in advance. Nevertheless, our framework highlights one reason things came to be organized this way; by ensuring that wages were higher and more compressed, and thus departing from what an unfettered market outcome might have been, the state avoided the need for even greater fiscal redistribution and taxation. With the fiscal role of the state diminished, keeping the state in check became a more feasible objective.

> 即使瑞典经济中，可以看到相当多为了融通丰厚福利国家所需资金、因而开征的这种租税，情况还是一样；大部分租税都不是事先设计或规划的。不过，我们的架构强调事情会这样安排，原因是国家如果借着确保比较高和比较受压缩的工资，摆脱完全自由市场可能产生的结果，就可以不必推动更重大的财政重分配、课征更高的租税。国家的财政功能降低后，约束国家会变成比较可行的目标。

## Unshared Prosperity · 独享的繁荣

Many Western nations, not least the United States, are facing fundamental economic exigencies today. The political response so far has been closer to the zero-sum Red Queen than the type of dynamic we saw in the Swedish cow trade, which involved the development of novel coalitions and institutional architectures to deal with new challenges. But the latter path is open to most countries in the corridor, and the first step in supporting it is to understand what these new challenges are. These challenges are the focus of the next three sections.

> 很多西方国家，尤其是美国，今天在经济上都面临紧要关头。到目前为止，各国在政治上的反应，一直都比较近似零和式的红皇后行动，没有表现出瑞典在乳牛交易中所呈现的那种改革力量，没有发展出新的联盟和制度架构，来因应新的挑战。但是，对身处自由窄廊中的国家来说，瑞典采用的路线一直都开放着。支持这种路线的第一步是了解这些新挑战的内容，这些挑战就是下面三节要探讨的重点。

Two of the most powerful engines of economic prosperity over the last several decades have been economic globalization and the rapid introduction of automation technologies. Economic globalization has increased the volume of trade, and outsourcing and offshoring have enabled the production process to be distributed around the world to take advantage of lower cost of production for certain tasks and goods. Both developing and developed nations have benefited from this globalization process. The spectacular growth of economies such as South Korea and Taiwan in the 1970s, ’80s, and ’90s and of China in the 1990s and 2000s would not have been possible without globalization.

> 过去几十年里，经济繁荣最有力的两具引擎是经济全球化和自动化科技的快速引进。委外和离岸委外使生产程序能够分散到全世界，以便利用某些任务和商品的最低生产成本，开发中和已开发国家都从这种全球化过程中受惠。如果没有全球化，在一九七○年代至一九九○年代期间的南韩和台湾、一九九○年代至二○○○年代期间的中国，就不可能创造惊人的经济成长。

Nor would we have enjoyed the lower prices of hundreds of products ranging from textiles and toys to electronics and computers. We saw in the previous chapter that globalization impacts how narrow the corridor is and the prospects for some of the nations outside the corridor to move into it. But its effects on the economics and politics of developed nations have been more complicated because of how the gains from globalization have been shared, or more to the point, have failed to be.

> 我们应该也不可能享受从纺织品和玩具，到电子产品和电脑等数以百计比较低价的产品。我们在前一章里，已经看到全球化怎么影响这条窄廊的宽度，怎么影响窄廊之外若干国家踏进窄廊的可能性。但是，因为全球化好处怎么分配的缘故（说得更精确一点，是因为这种好处没有好好分配的缘故），以至于全球化对已开发国家经济与政治的影响，一直都复杂得多。

Though much economic policy advice emphasizes how everybody benefits from economic globalization, the reality has been different both in the United States and in Europe, where corporations and the already well-off have seen their incomes rise, while workers have experienced much more limited gains and in some cases lower wages and job losses. This is in fact what economic theory predicts: globalization creates winners and losers, and when it takes the form of integration of an advanced country with a less advanced economy abundant in low-skill, low-wage labor, workers—and especially less skilled workers in the advanced economy—lose out.

> 虽然大部分的经济政策建议都强调，人人都从经济全球化中得到好处，美、欧两地却呈现不同的事实真相，两地企业和已经致富的人所得升高，工人得到的好处却有限得很，有些地方甚至出现工资下降和就业机会丧失的现象。其实，经济理论已经预测到：全球化会产生赢家和输家，而且如果全球化表现在先进国家，和拥有充沛低技术、低工资劳工的比较不先进经济体整合的话，那么先进经济体中的劳工──尤其是比较没有技术的工人──会大输特输。

The other powerful engine of economic prosperity, technological change, has had similar effects. Technological progress increases productivity and expands the range of products available to consumers, and has historically been at the root of sustained economic growth. At times, it has also been the tide that has lifted all (or most) boats. From the 1940s to the mid-1970s in the U.S. economy, there was rapid productivity growth along with growth of earnings of all education groups, all the way from workers with less than high school education to those with postgraduate degrees.

> 经济繁荣的另一个有力引擎科技变化，也有类似的影响。科技进步会提高生产力，扩大消费者所能得到的产品范围，一向都是经济持续成长的根本原因，偶尔也是人们（或大部分人）共存共荣的原因。从一九四○年代至一九七○年代中期，美国经济的生产力快速上升，从教育程度低于中学的工人，一直到研究所程度的劳工，所有教育程度族群的所得跟着成长。

But the bewildering array of new technologies that have transformed workplaces over the past thirty years appears to have had quite different effects. Many of these technologies, including much more powerful computers, numerically controlled and then computerized machines, industrial robots, and more recently artificial intelligence, have automated the production process, allowing machines to take over tasks that were previously performed by workers. By its nature, automation favors capital, which is now used more extensively in the form of the new machines. It also tends to favor skilled workers relative to the less skilled, whose tasks are being taken over by machines. Not surprisingly, therefore, new automation technologies have had sweeping distributional consequences.

> 但是，过去三十年里，改变职场且令人困惑的大量新科技，似乎具有大不相同的影响；其中很多科技，包括功能更加强大的电脑、数值控制和后来改为电脑控制的机器、工业机器人和比较晚近的人工智慧，都促成了生产程序自动化，让机器接管先前由工人负责执行的任务。自动化本质上是对资本有利的事情，因此资本现在更密集的运用在新机器上。自动化通常也对高技术性劳工比较有利，对工作遭到机器取代的较低技术劳工不利。因此，新科技对分配具有广泛的影响。

The combined effects of globalization and automation have led to divergent fortunes. In the United States, the pattern of broad-based wage increases ceased after the late 1970s and was replaced by a widening gap between the workers at the bottom of the earnings distribution and those at the top. For example, while the (inflation-adjusted) earnings of men with postgraduate education increased by almost 60 percent since 1980, those of men with high school education or less declined by more than 20 percent. In the course of the last three and a half decades, the real take-home pay of less skilled workers has fallen precipitously.

> 全球化和自动化的合并影响造成不同的命运，一九七○年代晚期后，美国工资普遍成长的形态消失，取而代之的是底层和顶层劳工所得分配缺口扩大。例如，从一九八○年以来，拥有研究所学历的男性，经过通货膨胀调整后的所得，增加了将近百分之六十，拥有高中以下学历劳工的所得下降了百分之二十多。过去三十五年来，较低技术劳工的实质所得急剧下降。

The same period has also witnessed declining job creation in the American economy. U.S. manufacturing employment fell by about 25 percent from the mid-1990s, while the overall employment-to-population ratio has declined significantly since 2000. Similar trends are visible in several other advanced economies, even if the staggering decline in the real earnings of less educated workers is unique to the U.S. labor market.

> 同期内，美国经济创造的就业机会也减少。从一九九○年代中期开始，美国制造业的就业人口大约减少了百分之二十五，同时从二○○○年起，整体就业人数占人口的比率也大幅下降。类似的趋势在其他先进经济体也很明显，连教育程度较低、劳工实质所得减少惊人的情形，也不是美国劳动市场所独有的现象。

There is general agreement that both automation and globalization have been major contributors to these trends. Employment and earnings losses are concentrated in areas, industries, and occupations that used to specialize in activities that either have been automated or have witnessed a rapid expansion of imports from developing economies, particularly China. Estimates in the literature suggest that imports from China alone may have reduced employment in the U.S. economy by over two million jobs, and the adoption of industrial robots, one salient example of new automation technologies, may have led to the loss of as many as 400,000 jobs. In both cases the majority of the effects were felt by workers at the lower end of the skill distribution.

> 多数人都同意，自动化和全球化是这种趋势的主要成因。就业机会和所得的丧失，集中在过去所从事专业制造活动已经自动化的地区、产业和职业，或是集中在受到从开发中国家进口，尤其是从中国的进口快速扩张影响的领域。文献中的估计显示，光是从中国的进口，可能就使美国的就业机会减少二百万个以上；采用新自动化科技亮点之一的工业机器人，造成的就业机会损失可能多达四十万个。在这两个案例中，承受大部分影响的人，都是技术分类中比较低阶的劳工。

## Wall Street Unhinged · 华尔街的疯潮

Economic globalization and automation are not the only trends contributing to high levels of inequality. The rapid deregulation of several industries in the United States, accompanied by more modest changes in other developed economies, has been a major contributor to inequality as well. Particularly important in this process was financial deregulation. The financial industry in much of the world was highly regulated during the several decades following World War II, so much so that in the United States banking occupations came to be viewed as typical white-collar jobs, and their pay reflected this, typically hovering around the same level that workers would receive in other sectors.

> 不但经济全球化与自动化造成严重不均，美国好几个产业快速解除管制，配合其他先进经济体比较轻微的变化，也是不均的主要原因，在这种过程中，特别重要的是金融业的解除管制。二次大战后的几十年里，世界大部分国家的金融业都受到严密管制，以至于美国人把银行业的工作，当成典型的白领工作，员工薪资反映这种情形，通常都和其他部门的薪资水准相同。

The bedrock of the postwar financial system in the United States was “Regulation Q,” which restricted interest rates on savings accounts, limiting competition between different financial institutions, as well as interstate branching restrictions that prevented banks from competing for deposits in multiple states. These restrictions were augmented by the Glass-Steagall Act, which was enacted in 1933 and separated retail banking (mainly deposit taking and lending) from the riskier business of investment banking (which focused on such things as underwriting, mergers and acquisitions, financial derivatives, and trading).

> 战后美国金融体系的基础是Q管制（Regulation Q），这种管制包括限制储蓄存款的利率、限制不同金融机构之间的竞争，以及限制跨州设立分行，以免银行在很多州里竞争存款。一九三三年立法的葛拉斯—史蒂格尔法（Glass-Steagall Act）强化了这些限制，也把零售银行业务（主要是存放款）和风险较高的投资银行业务（以承销、并购、衍生性金融商品和交易为主）区隔开来。

In this regulated environment, the bureaucratized and comfortable jobs in banking came to be described by the “3-6-3 rule”—take deposits at 3 percent interest rate, lend them at 6 percent interest rate, and hit the golf course by 3 p.m. This started to change in the 1970s, particularly after Regulation Q was abolished in 1986, paving the way to a significant rise in concentration in banking. Together with greater concentration came a huge shift toward riskier activities, such as financial derivatives including interest rate swaps (where one party to the financial contract makes payments to the other depending on whether a benchmark interest rate is below or above a threshold) or credit default swaps (where payments are made depending on whether a debtor defaults).

> 在这种受到管制的环境下，人们用「三六三规则」，形容金融业官僚化的安逸工作──以百分之三的利率吸收存款，再以百分之六的利率放款，然后在下午三点踏上高尔夫球场。到了一九七○年代，这种情况开始改变，一九八六年Q管制废除后，更是如此，这些做法为银行业的集中化奠定基础。集中程度提高后，银行业争相转作衍生性金融商品之类风险较高的业务，如换利合约（如金融合约的一方根据基准利率低于或高于某个门槛，付款给另一方），这种业务也包括信用违约交换（CDS，依据债务人是否违约而付款）。

Even though the financial sector was branching into riskier activities, the rising political power of banks blocked any new regulations and in fact pushed for further deregulation. With greater concentration, fewer regulations, and more aggressive risk-taking came greater revenues and profits. Between 1980 and 2006, the financial sector grew from 4.9 percent of gross domestic product in the United States to 8.3 percent, and its profits rose 800 percent in real terms, more than three times the growth of profits in the nonfinancial sector.

> 虽然金融部门转入风险较高的业务，银行业却用日益升高的政治力量，阻挡任何新的管制，而且实际上还争取进一步的解除管制。在集中程度提高、管制减少、比较积极冒险的情况下，营收和利润提高。一九八○年至二○○六年期间，金融部门产值占美国国内生产毛额的比率，从百分之四．九，成长到占百分之八．三，实质利润跃升八倍，是非金融部门获利成长率的三倍多。

In a powerful feedback cycle, greater size and profits led to increased political power. By 2006, the financial sector was contributing $260 million to political campaigns, up from about $61 million in 1990. The consequence of this was continued and bolder financial deregulation. Other important pillars of post–Great Depression financial regulations were dismantled, beginning with the 1994 Riegle-Neal Interstate Banking and Branching Efficiency Act which relaxed interstate banking regulations and opened the way to a series of mergers leading to the formation of gargantuan banking corporations such as JPMorgan Chase, Citicorp, and Bank of America. In 1999, the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act demolished most of the remaining barriers between commercial and investment banking.

> 规模和获利增加在强而有力的回馈循环中，促使金融部门的政治力量增加，到二○○六年，金融部门的政治献金达到二亿六千万美元，远高于一九九○年的六千一百万美元上下。这种情形促成更积极的持续性金融业解除管制，大萧条后金融管制的其他重要支柱纷纷废除。从一九九四年的《利格尔—尼尔银行跨州经营与设行效率法》（Riegle-Neal Interstate Banking and Branching Efficiency Act）开始，放宽跨州银行业务管制，开启了一系列的并购，形成诸如摩根大通（JPMorgan Chase）、花旗集团（Citicorp）、美国银行（Bank of America）等超大金融业者。一九九九年，《葛兰姆—李奇—布利雷法》（Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act）拆除了商业银行和投资银行之间残存的大部分防火墙。

During the same period, even as complex financial derivatives were spreading, bankers strenuously opposed new regulations. As a result, the huge growth in collateralized debt obligations based on mortgage-backed securities (which created synthetic securities of different risk profiles from large pools of mortgages) and credit default swaps took place almost entirely outside any regulatory framework. This was one of the reasons why an insurance company, the American Insurance Group (AIG), could sell massive amounts of credit default swaps and take on a vast amount of risk. With this wave of deregulation in place, the cycle continued, and profits in finance grew.

> 同期内，虽然更为复杂的衍生性金融商品扩散开来，银行业却坚决反对新的管制，因此，以房贷抵押担保证券（MBS，从庞大房贷组合中汇集不同风险房贷而成的合成证券）为基础的担保债权凭证（CDO）和信用违约交换合约的惊人成长，几乎全都出现在管制架构之外，这是美国国际集团（AIG）这家保险公司可以出售巨量信用违约交换合约、承担惊人巨量风险的原因。这种解除管制浪潮出现后，循环继续发展，金融业的获利继续成长。

Deregulation in finance contributed to inequality. Megaprofits on Wall Street not only added to the more unequal distribution of income between owners of major financial institutions, including hedge funds specializing in risky investments for wealthy clients, but boosted overall inequality because high-level managers and traders in the financial industry started receiving huge pay packages and bonus payments. The earnings of workers and executives in finance, which had tracked those of their counterparts in other sectors until 1990, started diverging sharply thereafter. By 2006, workers in finance were receiving 50 percent more than their counterparts, while executives in the financial industry were receiving a staggering 250 percent more than executives with similar qualifications employed in other industries.

> 金融业的解除管制助长了不均，包括避险基金在内、专门为富有客户从事风险性投资的主要金融机构东主之间，所得分配不但因为华尔街创造的超大利润，而变得更为不均，整体不均也因此提高，原因在于金融业的高阶经理人和交易员，开始得到惊人的薪资方案和奖金。一九九○年前，金融业员工和经理人的所得，一直跟着其他部门的所得亦步亦趋，一九九○年后，两者之间的差距急剧扩大。到二○○六年，金融业员工的所得比其他行业高出百分之五十，金融业经理人所得高出其他行业类似资格经理人所得的幅度，更是惊人，高达百分之二百五十。

An indicator of this aspect of inequality is the share of national income accruing to those in the top 1 percent and top 0.1 percent of the income distribution, which respectively represent the very rich and the very very rich, among whom owners and executives of financial corporations are heavily overrepresented. The top 1 percent of Americans received around 9 percent of national income in the 1970s. By 2015 this number had increased to 22 percent. The increase has been even more striking for the share of the top 0.1 percent, which went up from around 2.5 percent in the 1970s to almost 11 percent of national income in 2015.

> 有一个指标可以说明这种不均的性质，就是国民所得归属前百分之一和千分之一所得分配层级的比率，这两个层级分别代表非常富有和非常、非常富有族群，其中金融业老板和高阶经理人所占比率极高。一九七○年内，归属上层百分之一群体的国民所得，大约为百分之九；到了二○一五年，此一比率增加到百分之二十二；上层千分之一富人的这个比率增幅更惊人，从一九七○年占国民所得的百分之二．五，升高到二○一五年的将近百分之十一。

The second challenge is related to the allocation of resources. Finance, by transferring funds from savers to those with new ideas and investment opportunities, plays a vital role in improving the efficiency of economic activity. But when it becomes concentrated, specialized in risk-taking, and protected by its political power, it can conversely become a source of widespread inefficiencies. By the time of the 2007–2008 crisis, the financial industry had moved far in this direction. Excessive risk-taking was rooted in the unregulated competition in finance that was encouraging many institutions to lend recklessly and take risks in their own trading divisions in order to raise the rates of return they could promise to investors.

> 第二个挑战跟资源的配置有关。金融业负责把资金从存户手中，移转到有新构想和投资机会的人手中，因此在改善经济活动的效能上，扮演重要的角色。但是，金融业变成集中化，又专事冒险时，反而可能变成普遍不均的来源。到二○○八年金融海啸爆发时，金融业已经远远脱离这种方向，不受管制的金融竞争引发过度冒险，鼓励很多金融机构胡乱放贷，同时在自己的交易部门冒险，以便提高他们可以对投资人承诺的报酬率。

It was also fueled by the belief of many in the leading financial institutions that the government and the Federal Reserve Bank would not let them go under even if their investments failed abysmally (and they weren’t proven wrong). It was ultimately the collapse of such risky investments that spurred the financial crisis, which then morphed into a global economic downturn. Though the Dodd-Frank Act of 2010 and tighter regulations by the Federal Reserve System have attempted to limit the extent of such risk-taking and the negative consequences of financial losses on the economy, this has been at best a partial success.

> 很多主要金融机构认为，即使他们的投资惨赔，政府和美国联邦准备理事会也不会让他们倒闭（事实证明他们的想法没有错），这种「大到不能倒」的想法也助长了冒险。最后，就是这种高风险投资崩盘，引爆了金融危机，进而促成全球经济衰退。虽然二○一○年《陶德—法兰克法》和美国联准会加强管制，试图限制这种冒险行为的范围，也限制金融亏损为整体经济带来的不利影响，但这些做法顶多只能说是事倍功半。

The financial industry, relying on its powerful lobbying, has resisted and prevented the full implementation of these regulations and often has sought to dial them back. Meanwhile, the sector has in fact become more concentrated. The share of the five largest banks in the U.S. financial system, which increased from 20 percent in 1990 to 28 percent in 2000, stands above 46 percent in 2019.

> 金融业凭着强大的游说力量，一直抗拒和阻止这些管制的实施，甚至经常设法把时钟倒拨回去。事实上，金融业在同期内，反而变成更为集中，美国金融体系中五大银行的市占率从一九九○年的百分之二十，升到二○○○年的百分之二十八后，到二○一九年，更提高到百分之四十六以上。

## Supersized Firms · 超大企业

Greater concentration has not been confined to finance. Together with general deregulation and new technologies has come a huge increase in economic concentration in many sectors, and especially in online services, communications, and social media. The size of the largest companies relative to the rest of the economy is at an all-time high. The tech giants Alphabet (Google), Amazon, Apple, Facebook, and Microsoft have a combined market value (as measured by their stock market valuations) equivalent to over 17 percent of U.S. gross domestic product. The same number for the five largest companies in 1900, when policy makers and society became alarmed about the power of large corporations, was less than 6 percent.

> 加强集中化并非只限于金融业，随着全面解除管制和新科技而来的是，很多部门出现极为惊人的经济集中化，线上服务、社群和社交媒体尤其如此，最大企业和经济其他部门相比的规模，已经升到空前高峰，科技巨擘字母控股（Alphabet，即原来的Google，谷歌公司）、亚马逊、苹果、脸书和微软的合并总市值（根据他们的股票总市值计算）等于美国国内生产毛额的百分之十七。一九○○年时，美国五大企业的这种比率低于百分之六，却已经使决策官员和社会惊觉大企业的力量。

This huge increase in concentration appears to have several causes. The most important is the nature of the technology of these new companies, which creates what economists call “winner take all” dynamics. Take Google, for instance. Founded in 1998, when there were already several successful search engines for the Internet, Google quickly distinguished itself because of its superior search algorithm. While its competitors, such as Yahoo! and AltaVista, ranked websites by the number of times they included the term being searched for, the founders of Google, Sergei Brin and Larry Page, came up with a much better approach when they were graduate students at Stanford University. This approach, which came to be called the PageRank algorithm, ranked a web page according to its relevance estimated from how many other pages also mentioning the search term linked to this website.

> 集中度这样巨幅提高，原因似乎有好几个，最重要的原因是这些新公司的科技本质，这种性质产生了经济学家所说「赢家通吃」的力量。以Google公司为例，一九九八年该公司创立时，市面上已经有几家成功的网路搜寻引擎，Google能够迅速脱颖而出，是因为该公司优异的搜寻演算法，雅虎、下望（AltaVista）等竞争对手公司，根据网站纳入搜寻字词的次数，为网站排名，Google公司的创办人谢尔盖．布林（Sergei Brin）和拉利．佩吉（Larry Page）还在史丹佛大学当研究生时，却推出高明得多、后来叫做「网页排序」（PageRank）的演算法，根据有多少连结此一网站的其他网页、也提到所搜寻字词的相关性估计，为网页排序。

Because this algorithm was much better at suggesting relevant websites to users, Google’s market share of Internet searches grew quickly. Once it had a large market share, Google could use more data from user searches to refine its algorithm, making it even better and more dominant. These dynamics got stronger once data from Internet searches started being used for artificial intelligence applications, for example, for translation and pattern recognition. Early success also brought more resources to invest in research and development and acquire companies that were developing technology that would be useful to Google’s further expansion.

> 因为这种演算法在对使用者建议相关网站时，远胜过其他演算法，Google的网路搜寻市占率迅速成长。Google一旦占有庞大的市占率后，就可以利用更多的使用者搜寻资料，改善自己的演算法，使演算法更好、更具有主导态势。一旦网路搜寻资料开始用在人工智慧应用、如翻译和形态辨识上时，上述力量会变得愈发强大。初期的成功也带来更多的资源，让Google可以投资在研究发展和并购上，买下拥有正在发展的科技、对Google进一步扩张应该有用的公司。

Winner-take-all effects were also at the root of the meteoric rise of Amazon, whose early growth as an online retailer and platform made it more attractive to sellers and users, and of Facebook, whose popularity as a social media platform critically depends on the users’ expectations that their friends are joining as well. Though the nature of the winner-take-all considerations are somewhat different for Apple and Microsoft, they are no less important, because once again the value of their products depends on their overall popularity and widespread adoption in the population.

> 赢家通吃效应也是亚马逊公司迅速崛起的根本原因。亚马逊线上零售商和平台身分的早期成长，使该公司更能吸引卖家和使用者；脸书公司是同样的例子，脸书这种社交媒体平台会受欢迎，对使用者认为朋友也会连结在一起的期望，有着很高的依赖。虽然赢家通吃性质表现在苹果和微软公司的情形略有不同，但两家公司同样重要，因为他们的产品价值还是取决于整体受欢迎程度，以及产品在人口中普遍采用的程度。

Even if the nature of the technology of the Internet age has been a defining factor in the rise in economic concentration, the inaction of regulatory agencies, particularly in the United States, has been a major factor too. This contrasts with what we have seen in U.S. history at similar junctures. When several companies reached similarly dominant positions at the turn of the twentieth century, administrations influenced by the progressive agenda came to power and started taking action to break them up, as we saw in Chapter 10. Today there are no similar institutional or policy proposals on the agenda.

> 在经济集中度上升方面，网际网路时代科技的性质虽然是决定性因素，主管机关──尤其是美国主管机关的不作为，也是一个重要因素。这一点和我们在美国史上类似重要关头看到的情况相左，正如第十章所述，二十世纪之初，几家公司达到类似的主导地位时，受到进步政治议程影响的政府正好上台执政，开始采取行动，拆解这些公司，今天的政治议程中，却没有类似的制度性或政策建议。

It is of course true that many of these companies have grown rapidly because they have offered new, better, and cheaper products. This does not obviate the concerns over increased concentration, especially with the prospect that companies that dominate markets will at some point exercise their monopoly power, charge higher prices, and start choking off innovation. The rise in economic concentration has been a major factor in the increase in inequality, too, not just because the owners and major shareholders of these corporations have become very rich but also because their employees have seen their wages increase relative to those working in other industries.

> 其中很多公司能够迅速成长，当然是因为能够提供更好、更廉价的新产品，但这样却不能打消众人对集中度提高的疑虑，在主宰市场的公司将来可能利用自己的独占地位，收取较高的价格，开始扼杀创新上，更是如此。经济集中度上升也是不均升高的主因，原因不仅这些企业的东主和大股东变得非常富有，也是因为他们的员工薪资增加幅度超过其他产业的员工。

The economic trends we have briefly described—economic globalization and automation, the growth of finance, and the rise of supersized firms—pose urgent challenges for the United States and several other advanced economies for at least three reasons. The first is their inequality implications, which we have already emphasized. The second is economic efficiency. Some see our epoch as the golden age of technology, and yet income and productivity growth for at least the last two decades have been disappointing despite the spectacular rise of globalization and fascinating new technologies.

> 我们刚刚简单描述的经济趋势──经济全球化与自动化、金融部门的成长和超大企业崛起──对美国和另外几个先进经济体，构成了急迫的挑战。原因至少有三个：第一个原因我们已经强调过，就是它们对不均的影响。第二个原因是经济效能问题。也许会有人认为，我们的时代是科技的黄金时代，因为全球化和美妙新科技出现惊人成长；然而，至少过去二十年来，所得和生产力的提高却令人失望。

The causes of this disappointing productivity growth are not well understood. They may well be related to the trends we have outlined. Globalization and rapid automation bring benefits, but their recent rise may have been at the expense of other technological advances that would have contributed to productivity and prosperity even more. Excessive growth in the financial sector and inefficient risk-taking have probably been quite costly because they have created instability in the economy (think of the financial crisis) and diverted resources that should have gone into other sectors and innovation toward finance (think of the smartest graduates going to hedge funds and investment banking instead of innovation, science, or public service).

> 生产力提高令人失望的原因还不很清楚，可能和我们大致说明的趋势密切相关。全球化和快速自动化带来了好处，但它们近来的崛起，却可能牺牲了更有助于生产力和繁荣的其他科技进步。金融部门的过度成长和无效冒险的代价很可能相当高昂，因为这样已经为经济体系带来不稳定（想一想金融海啸就会知道），还把原本应该用在其他部门和创新上的资源，转用在金融部门中（想一想最聪明的大学毕业生投身避险基金和投资银行业，而非投入创新、科学或公共服务）。

The huge increase in economic concentration has also likely undermined efficiency by both hamstringing competition and distorting the new technologies being adopted and developed. The third challenge is related to trust in institutions. The Shackled Leviathan doesn’t just need a balance of power between state and society. It also needs society to trust institutions. Without trust, citizens won’t protect these institutions from the state and the elite, and the Red Queen becomes much more zero-sum.

> 经济集中度巨幅上升，也可能因为会限制竞争，扭曲人们采用和发展的新科技，因而对效能造成伤害。第三个挑战和对制度的信任有关。受制约的国家机器不仅需要国家和社会之间的平衡，也需要社会对制度的信任。没有信任的话，公民不会保护这些制度，以免制度遭到国家机器和菁英的伤害，而且红皇后效应会呈现极端的零和性质。

Without trust, institutions will not be able to mediate conflicts in society (as in interwar Germany). Rising inequality, sluggish employment growth, the enormous profits in finance, and huge firms that remain unregulated all contribute to the feeling that the economy is rigged and the political system is complicit in this process. This feeling was certainly strengthened by the financial crisis and its aftermath, which witnessed government bailouts of banks partly responsible for the crisis while poor households facing bankruptcy received little help.

> 没有了信任，社会冲突就无法靠制度调解（例如两次大战期间的德国）。贫富不均升高、就业成长低迷、金融业的惊人利润、超大企业仍然不受管制，在在都助长经济遭到操纵、政治制度在这种过程中沆瀣一气的感觉；金融海啸及其后遗症确实强化了这种感觉，因为政府对要负部分金融海啸责任的银行纾困，却放任穷人家庭在几乎孤立无援的情况下，面临破产。

Worse, as in our discussion of Weimar Germany, segments of society that are falling behind economically and losing trust in institutions are prime targets for movements that seek to destabilize the political system and tear down the balance of power between state and society that underpins life in the corridor. Such movements have been, predictably, in the ascendancy of late. Inequality, unemployment, low productivity and income growth, and loss of trust in institutions were among the factors that made the period of the Great Depression such a fertile ground for political instability. Though the crisis engulfing advanced economies today is not as extreme as the Great Depression, given the parallels we cannot afford complacency.

> 更糟的是，就像我们探讨德国威玛共和时说的那样，在经济上落后、对制度失去信心的社会阶层，恰是那些意图破坏政治制度、摧毁国家和社会之间权力均衡的民粹运动最欲争取的目标，最终将使自由窄廊中的日子难以为继。可以预测的是，最近这种运动的优势正在升高。不均、失业、生产力和所得成长率低落、对制度失去信心，都是大萧条变成政治不稳定极为肥沃温床的原因。虽然说今天正在吞噬先进经济体的危机没有大萧条这么极端，其中的相似性却让我们不能轻忽大意、洋洋自得。

## Avoiding the Zero-Sum Red Queen · 避免零和式的红皇后效应

We have seen two diametrically opposed responses to the Great Depression. The first, the collapse of the Weimar Republic in Germany, was an example of a zero-sum Red Queen, where each side competed to undercut the other without any compromise. The second, illustrated by the Swedish response, entailed greater involvement and empowerment of the state, while society also became more capable and better organized to control the state. This societal mobilization was bulwarked by a new coalition supporting the new institutional architecture.

> 我们已经看到可以有两种截然不同的方式来回应大萧条。第一种方式以德国威玛共和为代表，红皇后效应在此变成零和游戏，导致国家与社会无法互相妥协，反而竞相摧毁对方。第二种方式则可以用瑞典的例子来说明。这种方式需要加强国家介入的程度、赋予国家机器更大的力量，同时社会也要更有能力、更能有组织地控制国家机器。这种社会动员受到支持新制度架构的新联盟所保障。

The response of many Western nations today is closer to Weimar Germany’s than Sweden’s, with the elites fighting to defend their advantages and those in the most precarious positions succumbing to the allure of autocrats, and polarization and intransigence becoming the order of the day. Are we doomed to repeat the mistakes of interwar Germany? Or can we prevent the Red Queen from turning completely zero-sum? Can we also heed Hayek’s warnings and avoid “serfdom”?

> 今天有很多西方国家回应经济萧条的方式，比较近似威玛德国而非瑞典，出现菁英奋力捍卫既得利益、处境最危险的族群反而向独裁者诱惑屈服的现象，两极化和坚持己见蔚为风潮。我们是否已经注定会再重蹈德国在战间期的覆辙？我们有可能防止红皇后效应变成彻底的零和游戏吗？我们能不能同时也听从海耶克的警告，免于「奴役」呢？

Let’s start with the good news. As we emphasized in Chapter 13, the Red Queen is more likely to get out of control when the corridor is narrower. Here the United States and many other Western nations are in a better situation because their diversified economies built on manufacturing and services, very limited role of coercion (recall Chapter 14), lack of dominant groups diametrically opposed to democracy (like the Prussian landed elites), and their recent history of uninterrupted democratic politics translate into a wider corridor.

> 我们要先说好消息。就像我们在第十三章中强调的那样，窄廊比较窄的时候，红皇后比较可能失控，现在美国和西方很多国家处在比较好的状况中，因为他们的多元化经济是以制造业和服务业为基础，强制性的角色非常有限（请回头参看第十四章），没有（像普鲁士土地菁英那样）和民主制度完全对立的主导群体；此外，这些国家在晚近历史上的民主政治历程未曾中断，也使他们拥有较宽的窄廊。

But neither the width of the corridor nor stability in the corridor can be taken for granted. The width of the corridor is bolstered by democratic, participatory institutions. If these institutions lose people’s trust, the corridor narrows and the ability of society to handle conflicts is diminished. And the Red Queen can get out of control even in a wide corridor if it turns resolutely zero-sum. Let’s revisit the Swedish experience during the Great Depression to see how to avoid such a zero-sum response. Three pillars of the Swedish response were critical. The first pillar was that the whole project was built on a broad coalition composed of workers, farmers, and businesses. The workers’ movement, represented by the trade unions and the SAP, far from undercutting other interests, attempted to strike a compromise with them.

> 但是，窄廊的宽度和其中的稳定性都不能视为理所当然，窄廊的宽度要由参与式的民主制度支持，如果人民对这种制度丧失信任，窄廊会变窄，社会处理冲突的能力会变小，即使在宽大的窄廊中，如果情势变成零和游戏，红皇后也可能失去控制。我们要回顾瑞典在大萧条期间的经验，看看怎么避免这种零和反应。瑞典的应对之道中，有三大支柱至为重要。第一支支柱是整个计划是以广泛的联盟为基础，联盟成员包括工人、农民和企业，工会和社民党代表的工人运动根本没有和其他利益恶性竞争，反而试着和其他利益达成妥协。

The second pillar was a range of economic responses, both short-term and institutional. These responses involved taking measures to stimulate the economy as well as a series of reforms to redistribute income toward those who were suffering unemployment, loss of earnings, and poverty. They proceeded to institutionalize these measures by developing a social democratic model in which the state would mediate the negotiations between employers and workers in order to ensure industrial peace. They also founded a generous welfare state for making prosperity more equally shared.

> 第二支支柱是一系列短期和制度性的经济反应，这些反应包括采取刺激经济的措施，推动一系列的所得重分配改革，造福失业、丧失所得和贫困的人民。瑞典的做法是推动这些措施的制度化，发展出社会民主模式，由国家居中调解员工和雇主的谈判，确保产业界的和平，瑞典人也建立慷慨的福利国家，让国民更平等的分享繁荣。

The third pillar was political. Deepening state capacity was embedded in a political system in which there were powerful societal controls on both the state’s activities and the relationship between the political and economic elites. These controls were helped by the universal nature of the programs, which strengthened the social democratic coalition, by the fact that the administrative capacity of the state developed rapidly in the process of managing the welfare state, and by the direct involvement of the trade unions in the operation of key programs. All of this was in turn undergirded by the earlier political reforms that had significantly democratized Swedish politics.

> 第三支支柱是政治，国家能力的深化植根在政治制度中，政治制度中含有强力的社会控制，能够控制国家的活动，也能够控制政治与经济菁英之间的关系。整个计划具有普遍性，有助于推动这些控制，计划又获得社会民主联盟的强化，表现在外的事实是：国家在管理福利国家的过程中，快速发展出行政能力，也表现在工会直接介入关键方案的运作上，先前瑞典推动的大力民主化政治改革，现在变成这一切发展的支持力量。

The first lesson from Sweden is obvious: Engineer compromise and find ways of building a broad coalition to support the Shackled Leviathan and the new policies. This of course may be much harder once politics becomes highly polarized, as we have witnessed in the German case. The hope is to find some common ground before it’s too late. In this context it’s important that both the right and the left in the United States and many Western nations today agree that the trends we have highlighted here—rising inequality, disappearing jobs, the dominance of Wall Street, and economic concentration—are problems.

> 从瑞典学到的第一个教训很明显，就是打造妥协，设法建立广泛的联盟，以便支持受到制约的国家机器。一旦政治变成高度两极化，要这样做当然可能会更加困难，德国的情形就是这样。我们希望能在为时已晚前，找到彼此的共识。在这种情况下，重点是今天美国和很多西方国家的左、右两派，都必须同意本书强调的趋势，即贫富不均升高、就业机会消失、华尔街的主导力量和经济的集中度等等，在在都是问题。

The challenge is that there is less agreement about solutions. But this is not unusual. New coalitions often necessitate new ideas, perspectives, and institutional innovations. We now discuss where these might come from, focusing on the U.S. case for specificity. Let’s start with coalition building. The challenge is similar to the one that the Federalists faced. The compromises they came up with, even if costly in some dimensions, as we emphasized in Chapter 10, may be useful again.

> 其中的挑战是各国对解决之道比较没有共识，但这一点没有什么不寻常，新联盟经常都需要新观念、新视界和制度上的创新；我们现在就要以美国的特异状况为重点，探讨创新可能从何而来，我们要先谈联盟的建立。这个挑战类似联邦党人面临的挑战，他们达成的妥协虽然像第十章中所强调的那样，在某些层面上代价高昂，却也可能很有用。

One leg of this compromise was to transfer significant powers to the states (so that local communities would have some say in the process). Given the differences in the economic and political problems and tolerance for government involvement across states, the same compromise may be necessary today too. Another leg was the public-private partnership. This had the virtue of getting the private sector involved and reassured as the state’s capacity expanded.

> 这种妥协中的一个面向是把若干大权交给各州（以便地方拥有若干发言权）。鉴于经济与政治问题和各州对联邦政府介入的容忍程度不同，今天的美国可能也需要相同的妥协。另一个面向是公私合营的伙伴关系，随着国家的能力扩大，公私合营制度具有促使民间部门参与和放心的优点。

Though a similar compromise is necessary in the current U.S. context, there may be need for the institutional architecture to move beyond the current version of the public-private partnership model, as we’ll discuss momentarily. Finally, incorporating Hayek’s concerns from the beginning would help. This would mean that any social compact entailing greater state involvement and a stronger social safety net should reaffirm a significant increase in the ability of society to monitor the state. Even though Swedish society was less suspicious of state interventions, this is exactly what happened in Sweden in the 1930s.

> 虽然以美国的现况来说，美国现在需要类似的妥协，却也可能需要制度性的架构，以便进入超越现有公私合营模式的境界，这一点我们片刻之后就会讨论。最后，如果从一开始就把海耶克的忧虑纳入考量，应该会有帮助。这表示凡是需要国家加强参与、需要比较强大社会安全网的社会契约，都应该重申社会监控国家机器的能力必须大幅增加。虽然瑞典社会比较不怀疑国家的介入，但这种情形正是瑞典在一九三○年代发生的事情。

On the economic front, the nature of the challenges makes it evident that a multipronged expansion in the responsibilities and the capacity of the state is necessary. The responsibilities that the state, especially the American state, must begin shouldering include designing and operating a more generous and comprehensive social safety net, which will protect individuals who are not benefiting from the major economic changes. Policies aimed at improving the social safety net need to be complemented by others that improve job creation and the earnings of workers and help them transition into new jobs. One example of this is the U.S. earned income tax credit, which effectively subsidizes low-wage workers by making their earnings less heavily taxed.

> 在经济方面，这种挑战的性质清楚显示，美国必须从多方面扩大国家的责任和能力。国家，尤其是美国国家机器，必须开始承担的责任包括设计和经营比较慷慨、比较全面的社会安全网，保护没有从重大经济变化中得到好处的个人。以改善社会安全网为目标的政策，必须辅以改善创造就业、改善劳工所得和协助劳工转职的其他政策，美国的劳动所得税扣抵额就是一个例子，这种扣抵可以借着降低低薪工人的税负，有效地补贴低薪工人。

It will have to involve a rethink of the U.S. educational system, which has become outmoded not only because it has not kept up with the needs of the changing economic landscape, but also because it has come to reflect the inequities in society, failing to provide a level playing field for most Americans. It will need to develop tighter, bolder, and more comprehensive regulations for many businesses, including the financial industry and the tech sector.

> 政府的责任也包括重新思考教育制度，美国的教育制度已经过时，过时的原因一则是不能跟上经济景象不断变化的脚步，二则是因为教育制度已经变成了社会贫富不均的缩影，不能为大多数美国人提供公平的竞争场地。另外，美国还需要为很多产业，包括为金融业和科技部门，发展更严格、更大胆、更全面性的管制。

To top it off, the recent U.S. experience highlights the fact that excessive reliance on the public-private partnership model is a drawback when building a modern welfare state. The successful operation of welfare and social insurance programs necessitates a deepening of the administrative capacity of the state. This doesn’t mean no role for the private sector, but it requires a more autonomous, capable, and authoritative civil service. Another lesson from the Swedish experience (confirmed by the Danish, Norwegian, and British cases) is to move welfare programs in a more universal direction, encouraging society at large to embrace them and be involved in their monitoring.

> 最重要的是，美国最近的经验突显一个事实，就是在建立现代福利国家时，过度依赖公私合营制度是一大缺点；要成功地经营福利和社会保险计划，必须依赖国家的行政能力，这并不表示民间部门不必发挥功能，而是表示需要更自主、更有能力、更有权威的公共部门。瑞典经验（获得丹麦、挪威和英国证实）提供的另一个教训是：要从比较普遍的方向，推动福利计划，要鼓励整个社会拥抱这种计划，并参与监督这种计划。

Subsidies to specific industries or specific types of workers do not typically achieve this objective. Times of major economic and social change offer an especially fertile ground for the introduction of universal benefits, because they require broad-based programs, which can then forge a popular coalition in their support. Possible reforms to deal with the adverse effects of economic globalization, automation, and other economic changes, as well as investments in education to enable more effective and equitable use of these opportunities, must be similarly broad-based and could be designed to furnish their own powerful coalitions.

> 仅补贴特定产业或特殊类型的工人，通常不能达成这种目标。重大经济与社会变化出现时，是特别适于推出全面福利的绝佳时机，因为这种时刻需要基础广泛的计划，从而打造受到民众欢迎和支持的联盟。因应经济全球化、自动化和其他经济变化不利影响的可能改革，以及让人民能够更有效、更公平利用这种机会的教育投资，同样必须建立在广泛的基础上，也可以设计成建立本身强而有力联盟的样子。

Once again building on the Swedish experience, we can argue that it would be a mistake to rely just on the tax policy and direct redistribution to pursue these objectives. Rather, it would be preferable to design labor market institutions that directly move the economy toward a more equally shared distribution of the gains from economic growth, such as better opportunities for workers to engage in collective agreements, minimum-wage legislation, and other policies to increase pay. Such policies would both reduce the burden on the state (and thus make it easier to control it) and also contribute to a broader coalition in favor of maintaining these programs.

> 我们可以再度根据瑞典经验，主张只依靠租税政策和直接的重分配，追求达成这些目标的做法，应该是错误的做法。比较好的方法应该是设计能够直接推动经济体系，朝向比较能够公平分配经济成长好处方向前进的劳动市场制度，好让劳工能有更有机会，参与集体协议的订定，同时得到最低工资法和其他提高薪资政策的保障。这种政策应该可以减轻国家的负担（从而使国家更容易控制这种政策），同时有助于形成基础比较广泛，又便于维持这些计划的联盟。

The same factors suggest that it may be necessary to redirect the path of technological change. Technology’s path and impact on the economy are not predetermined. The anemic rate of productivity growth today signals that all may not be well on this front. One problem is the declining support of the U.S. government for fundamental research and corporate R&D since the end of the Cold War. Reversing this decline would certainly be an important step in encouraging faster productivity growth.

> 同样的因素显示，国家可能必须重新引导科技变化的道路。科技的方向和对经济的影响并非事前可以决定，今天生产力提高速度乏善可呈的情况，可能代表这方面诸事不顺。问题之一是冷战结束后，美国政府对基础研究和企业研究发展的支持减少，在鼓励生产力加速提高方面，扭转这种下降趋势一定是其中重要的一步。

Moreover, the emphasis over the last several decades has been on rapid cost-saving, which has encouraged greater automation. It’s not far-fetched to think that this focus on automation has not generated sufficient productivity growth. A social consensus on making the gains from economic growth more equally shared may motivate investments in technologies that will not simply automate existing tasks but also generate new opportunities for workers with different skills to contribute to production. If this can be achieved, the result will not only be a more equitable distribution of income and jobs and less need for fiscal redistribution, but also higher productivity as human skills are better utilized.

> 此外，过去几十年内，人们都把重点放在快速节省成本上，因而鼓励更高的自动化程度，认为注重自动化未能创造足够生产力成长的想法，并非牵强附会。促进更公平分享生产力提高的社会共识，可能促进科技投资流入不但可能加强现有工作自动化，还可能为拥有不同技术、可以献身生产的工人创造新机会，如果这种目标能够达成，结果不只是所得和就业可以更公平的分配、降低财政重分配的需要，也会因为工人的技术获得更妥善的利用，可以创造更高的生产力。

In politics, the challenges are no less formidable. Beyond securing a coalition around the economic reforms and institutions, it is imperative to rein in the excessive influence of private interests via campaign contributions and lobbying, which have reached astronomical proportions over the past two decades. Hence the concern that a larger state will do the bidding of economic elites is not a distant threat for the U.S. political system; it is already here. Yet the enormity of this problem means that both the left and the right in the United States agree that such capture is a problem (even if their favorite solutions differ).

> 政治方面的挑战同样艰巨，除了确保能够建立以经济和制度改革为中心的联盟之外，美国也必须限制民间利益团体，不让他们利用二十年来比例达到天文数字的政治献金和游说，取得过大影响力。因此，在美国的政治体系中，比较大的政府听命于菁英的忧虑并非遥不可及，而是已经发生的事情。但是，这个问题已经大到美国左右两派都同意这种宰制是大问题（只是他们的解决方法不同）。

Our short list of political reforms to counter these threats include the following. The first is to curtail campaign contributions and limit the impact of lobbying. Specific measures to bring greater transparency to the relationship between firms, lobbyists, and politicians may be particularly important, since accounts of how politicians become faithful servants of certain industries or interests often involve meetings hidden from the public eye and poorly monitored revolving-door arrangements in which regulators and politicians are later hired by the private sector at very attractive salaries.

> 为了对抗这种威胁，我们拟定了简短的政治改革清单，其中包括下列事项，头等大事就是减少政治献金和限制游说的影响。提高企业、游说团体和政客之间关系的透明度的特定措施，可能特别重要，因为政客如何成为某些产业或利益团体的忠仆，旋转门的安排监管不力，主管官员和政客后来以非常优渥薪资，受雇于民间部门的说法，都涉及避开公众耳目的私下聚会。

A second reform is to increase the autonomy of the civil service. Ending the cozy relationship between lobbyists and the state is an obvious first step. But more important, more fundamental reforms reducing the ability of new administrations to make political appointments for all high positions in government agencies would increase the autonomy of the civil service and help prevent its political capture. Other necessary reforms include actions to reverse several trends that have reduced the representativeness of the U.S. political system, in particular via redistricting, which since the early 2000s has created scores of electoral districts that are safe for one of the two major parties.

> 第二项改革是提高公务员的自主性，结束关说和国家机器之间融洽的关系显然是第一步，但缩减新政府政治任命所有政府高官的权力，是更重要、更基本的改革，这样应该可以提高公务员的自主性，协助防止公务员遭到政治控制。其他必要改革包括采取行动扭转美国政治体系中代表性下降的几种趋势，尤其是透过选区重划；从二○○○年代初期以来，现行选区划分已经造成很多选区，成为一、两个大党的禁脔。

Even more important than specific policy reforms is a general increase in the mobilization of society, and here too there is fairly broad agreement in the United States. One of the features of nineteenth-century American society that most fascinated Tocqueville was people’s willingness to organize and form associations outside the government. This not only enabled them to solve specific societal problems but also created popular pressure on political decision making. The decline of these types of associations has been much emphasized in recent years. Though the extent and exact causes of this decline are debated and not all organizations have an important political role, a new vigor in the type of associations that are capable of keeping the state and powerful elites in check is essential.

> 比特定政策改革更重要的是，全面提高社会动员力量。美国对这一点也有相当普遍的共识。十九世纪美国社会最吸引托克维尔的特点是人民乐于在政府之外，组织和组成社团。这样不但可以让人民解决特定社会问题，也可以为政治决定带来民意压力。近年来，这种形态的结社减少深受重视，虽然减少的程度和正确原因尚有争论，而且并非所有社团都扮演重要的政治角色。但是，能够抑制国家和有权有势菁英的社团，却必须具有这种新活力。

This is even more the case because labor organizations, which often counteract the influence of economic elites, have become much weaker over the last several decades. This decline highlights the need for alternate forms of organizations that can enable new pathways into politics for both industrial workers and other citizens. An open question is whether (and how) such organizations can effectively shoulder the role that trade unions played in the past. We return to this question at the end of the chapter. Learning from the Swedish success in building a diverse coalition to support and monitor the expansion of the state’s capacity should not be read as a recommendation that the United States or other Western countries blindly emulate and copy the details of what Sweden started doing more than eighty years ago.

> 现在的情势更是如此，因为过去几十年来，经常抗衡经济菁英影响力的劳工组织已经大为式微，这种式微突显需要能够让产业劳工和其他公民，用新的途径打进政治的其他形式组织，但这种组织能否（或如何）有效的承担工会过去的角色，还是悬而未决的问题；我们要在本章结束时，回头探讨这个问题。从瑞典的成就中，学习建立多元化的联盟，支持和监督国家能力扩张的说法，不应该解读为建议美国或其他西方国家，盲目模仿和复制八十年前瑞典做法的细节。

To start with, the coalitions that can undergird positive Red Queen dynamics in the United States will have to be very different from the coalitions of workers and peasants in the Swedish cow trade. They will have to involve different regions, different ideological groups, and different ethnic groups. Because the United States is still the world’s most innovative country in a range of leading industries, such as software, artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and high-tech engineering, it must pursue a different organization from what Sweden pursued in the 1930s.

> 第一个原因是：能够支持美国红皇后正面力量的联盟，一定和瑞典乳牛交易中的工农联盟大不相同，必须牵涉不同的区域、意识形态团体和种族团体。美国在软体、人工智慧生物科技和高科技工程等一系列领导产业中，仍然是全世界最善于创新的国家，因此要利用的组织，必定和一九三○年代瑞典所利用的组织不同。

But securing opportunities and incentives for business dynamism and innovation does not contradict creating a better safety net and welfare state. It does not contradict helping mobilize society to keep the state in check. It certainly does not contradict developing a capable state either, especially since the involvement of the American state in science and research has been a mainstay of the innovative energy of the American economy. This can be seen from the activities of the U.S. government as a major purchaser of high-tech equipment and also as the primary financier of research via organizations such as the National Science Foundation and with generous tax credits for research expenditures. The question then is how the United States and other Western nations can redirect economic activity toward creating a more equitable division of resources, while keeping the state shackled. Some clues about an answer are provided by considering the problem of monitoring the state when it is dealing with security threats.

> 但是，确保企业活力、确保创新的机会与诱因，和创造更好的安全网与福利国家并不冲突，和协助动员社会、以便控制国家机器也不冲突，和发展能力高强的国家机器一定也不冲突；尤其是因为美国国家机器参与科学研究，一直是美国经济创新动力的主力，这一点从以下美国政府是高科技设备的大买家，也是透过国家科学基金会资助研究的大金主，研究支出可以获得慷慨的租税抵减的事实中，可以清楚地看出来。因此，现在的问题是美国和其他西方国家要怎么引导经济活动，创造比较公平的资源分配，同时维持对国家机器的控制；思考如何监督国家机器处理安全威胁的做法，可以提供和答案有关的一些线索。

## Leviathan’s War on Terror · 国家巨灵的反恐战争

The template of how the state can expand its capacity to deal with new problems while remaining shackled also applies to non-economic challenges. Some of the most critical demands that citizens make of their states are about security. Indeed one powerful incentive to engage in state building is the search for a centralized authority to enforce laws, resolve conflicts, and guarantee security. But as the world changes, so does the nature of security challenges.

> 国家如何扩大因应新问题的能力，同时仍然受到社会控制的模式，也适用在非经济的挑战上。公民对国家所提出的最严苛要求中，其中之一是和安全有关。建立国家时，一个有力的诱因是寻找中央化的权威，以便执行法律、解决纷争和保证安全，但随着世界变化，安全挑战的性质也跟着变化。

That became abundantly clear to most Westerners on the morning of September 11, 2001, when nineteen hijackers from the terrorist organization Al-Qaeda took control of four U.S. commercial flights and crashed two of them into the towers of the World Trade Center in New York, while another one was flown into the Pentagon building in Washington, D.C., and the final one was brought down in a field in Pennsylvania as passengers fought the hijackers. The overall death toll was 2,996, with over 6,000 people injured. Though of course the world had seen its fair share of murderous terrorist attacks and hijackings before September 11 and Western states had dealt with the various security challenges of the Cold War for some decades, the scale and audacity of these attacks shocked the public.

> 对大多数西方国家来说，这一点在二○○一年九月十一日早晨，变得十分清楚。这天早上，盖达恐怖组织的十九位劫机犯，控制了美国的四架民航班机，让其中两架撞进纽约世界贸易中心的两座高楼，另一架飞进华府的五角大厦建筑，最后一架在乘客和劫机犯的搏斗中坠毁。这次事件总共造成二千九百九十六人死亡，六千多人受伤。世界各国在「九一一」攻击前，虽然遭遇过相当高比率的谋杀式恐怖攻击和劫机，而且西方国家在因应冷战的各种安全挑战上，已经有几十年的经验，但「九一一」攻击仍然震惊大众。

Most citizens and government agencies interpreted this as the beginning of a new world of security threats that had to be confronted, with great urgency. Even if further large-scale attacks have been averted in the intervening eighteen years, this diagnosis has largely been proven right, as there have been many smaller-scale attacks and several foiled attempts engineered by similar organizations, most notably the so-called Islamic State. We thus have a clear example of society calling for an increase in state capacity and activism to confront new challenges.

> 大部分公民和政府机构都把这次事件视为安全威胁的新局面，必须极为迫切的应对。虽然随后的十九年里，进一步的大规模攻击得以避免，事实证明这种判断正确无误，因为这段期间里，发生过由类似组织、主要是所谓的伊斯兰国发动的多次规模较小的攻击，以及几次失败的攻击意图。因此，我们有一个明确的例子，说明社会呼吁加强国家能力与行动，以便因应新挑战。

These calls have been answered, and U.S. security agencies have grown massively and expanded their responsibilities. But as we already pointed out in Chapter 10, this hasn’t happened under the control of society, as became painfully clear in June 2013 when the media began to report some of the secret documents released by Edward Snowden revealing the existence and functions of the classified surveillance programs of the federal government. The first program to be revealed was PRISM, which allowed for direct access to Americans’ Google, Yahoo!, Microsoft, Facebook, YouTube, and Skype accounts.

> 这些呼吁已经获得回应，美国安全机构已经巨幅成长、已经扩大职权范围。但是，就像我们在第十章中所指出的那样，这一切发展并没有受到社会控制；二○一三年六月，媒体开始报导史诺登释出的若干秘密文件中，揭发美国政府秘密侦监计划的存在和活动时，大众才痛苦地知道这项事实。第一个遭到揭发的是「棱镜计划」（PRISM），这个计划容许安全机构直接侵入美国人的谷歌、雅虎、微软、脸书、YouTube和Skype帐户。

We also learned about a secret court order requiring Verizon to hand over millions of Americans’ phone records to the National Security Agency (NSA); about Boundless Informant, a data-mining program collecting metadata information on billions of e-mails and phone calls; and about XKeyscore, a computer system that allows for collection of “almost anything done on the internet.” Snowden revealed that the NSA was harvesting millions of e-mail and instant-messaging contact lists, searching e-mail content, tracking and mapping the location of cell phones, and undermining attempts at encryption.

> 我们也知道法院发出秘密命令，要求威讯无线公司（Verizon），把数百万美国人的电话纪录，交给美国国家安全局。我们还知道，美国国家安全局利用「无边界线民」（Boundless Informant）计划的资料采矿方法，针对几十亿封电子邮件和电话，搜集大数据资讯；美国国家安全局还利用全球风控查询电脑系统（XKeyscore），「搜集人们在网际网路上所做的几乎每一件事情」。史诺登揭发美国国家安全局搜集千百万封电子邮件和即时简讯联络人名单，搜寻电子邮件内容，针对行动电话进行追踪和定位，破坏别人的加密意图。

As Snowden put it, “I, sitting at my desk [could] wiretap anyone, from you or your accountant, to a federal judge or even the president, if I had a personal e-mail.” After Snowden’s revelations, Daniel Ellsberg, famous for leaking the Pentagon Papers, argued: Snowden’s disclosures are a true constitutional moment . . . Edward Snowden has done more for our Constitution in terms of the Fourth and First Amendment than anyone else I know.

> 正如史诺登所说：「如果我有一个个人电子邮件，我坐在桌前，〔可以〕监听任何人，从你或你的会计师开始，一直监听到联邦法官，甚至可以监听总统。」史诺登揭发这些秘密后，以泄露「五角大厦文件」[〔注2〕](#note-2) 闻名的丹尼尔．艾尔斯柏格（Daniel Ellsberg）主张：史诺登的揭露是真正的宪法时刻……史诺登在宪法第四和第一修正案上所做的贡献，超过我所认识的任何其他人。

Perhaps all of this is a tempest in a teacup. Maybe it’s unavoidable that in fighting serious terrorist threats security agencies must act secretly, collect massive amounts of data, and ignore privacy concerns, and let some in the media complain loudly. Maybe. Let’s turn to the Danish experience to gain a perspective. In 2006 the European Union issued a Data Retention Directive concerning “the retention of data generated or processed in connection with the provision of publicly available electronic communications services or of public communications networks.”

> 或许这一切只是茶壶里的风暴，或许在对抗恐怖主义威胁时，无法避免安全机构必须秘密行动，搜集大量资料，忽略隐私问题，容许媒体中人大声抱怨。或许是这样吧。我们看看丹麦的经验，看看是否能够得到启发。二○○六年时，欧盟发布一项「资料保留指令」（Data Retention Directive），内容涉及「保留公众所用电子通讯服务或公共通讯网路产生或处理的资料」。

The Danish government decided to expand on the directive and published a law going far beyond what was slated, including an obligation for “session logging” which required providers to store information on users’ source and destination IP addresses, port numbers, session type, and time stamp. In response, Privacy International, a nonprofit organization that monitors and defends privacy around the world, downgraded the country to a score of 2.0 (extensive surveillance societies) from its previous level of 2.5 (systemic failure to uphold safeguards). This places Denmark 34th among the 45 countries included in the study.

> 丹麦政府决定扩大运用，公布远超过这项指令所规定的法律，包括「会话纪录」（session logging）的义务，要求供应者储存使用者的来源端与目的地网路位址、通讯埠号码、会话形态和时间标记。监视和捍卫全世界隐私权的非营利机构隐私国际组织（Privacy International），为了这件事而把丹麦的评等从原来的二．五分水准（系统性地疏于维持保障措施），降级为两分（广泛地监视社会），以至于丹麦在这项纳入四十五国的研究中，排名跌到第三十四名。

Most Danes don’t seem bothered, however. They trust that the Danish government will not use their IP addresses, port numbers, session types, and time stamps to snoop on them, suppress their freedom of speech, or imprison them because of their political views. In April 2015 the European Court of Justice concluded that Danish data retention practices were “a particularly serious interference with fundamental rights,” but the Danish public was not up in arms and did not demand the cessation of the data retention practices.

> 然而，大部分丹麦人似乎不觉得困扰，他们信任丹麦政府不会利用他们的网路位址、通讯埠号码、会话形态和时间标记，来窥探他们，压制他们的言论自由，或因为他们的政治观点而监禁他们。二○一五年四月，欧洲法院断定丹麦的资料保留做法是「对基本权利特别严重的干预」，但丹麦民众没有因此强烈反对，也没有要求政府停止采用保留资料的措施。

The difference between the Danish and U.S. responses is not that the Danish government did not aggressively deal with similar security threats. It was that it did this while maintaining the trust of Danish society. This turned on two critical factors. First, while the U.S. program was secret and kept expanding without oversight, the Danish data retention policies were clearly announced to the public and were not subject to mission creep. Second, the Danes started with basic trust in their institutions and believed that their government would not use the information against them or for extraordinary rendition and torture, like the CIA did in the aftermath of September 11.

> 丹麦和美国的反应不同，原因不是丹麦政府没有积极因应类似的安全威胁，而是丹麦政府这样做时，能够维持丹麦社会的信任。这点取决于两个重要因素：第一，美国采用秘密行动方案，而且在不受监督情况下又不断扩大，丹麦却对大众清楚宣布这项资料保留政策，其说词没有受到「任务偏离效应」（mission creep）[〔注3〕](#note-3) 的影响。第二，丹麦从国民对制度的基本信任开始做起，相信政府不会利用这种资讯对付他们，或是像中情局在「九一一」攻击后那样，被离奇地诠释后用在酷刑上。

Both of these factors suggest that the Danes believe, for good reason, that the data collected by their government would not threaten the shackled nature of their state. Not so with the American public, precisely because the CIA, the FBI, and the NSA have a habit of acting in unchecked, sometimes unscrupulous ways. There is therefore a strong parallel between how the Shackled Leviathan might have to deal with new security threats while remaining shackled and how it may have to respond to new economic challenges. This parallel is rooted in the critical importance of institutional and other restraints keeping the Leviathan in the corridor.

> 这两个因素都显示丹麦人无可置疑地认为，他们政府搜集的资讯不会威胁国家机器受到约束的本质；美国大众却不是这样，原因正是中情局、联邦调查局和美国国家安全局习于不受约束，甚至偶尔会以不道德的方式，采取行动所致。因此，受到制约的国家机器可能怎么因应新的安全威胁，同时继续受到约束的问题，和国家机器可能如何因应新的经济挑战之间，有着非常类似的地方。这种相似性出自保持国家留在自由窄廊中的制度性和其他限制，是其中至为重要的因素。

Society’s trust in the state is a reflection of these restraints. From this perspective what was problematic in the response by the NSA and the CIA to new security threats wasn’t their expanding responsibilities and activities, but the secret and unmonitored ways in which they went about organizing them. The programs revealed by Snowden were supposed to be monitored by the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Courts, but the courts functioned secretly as well and were often no more than a rubber stamp. Not the right way to keep the shackles on or build trust.

> 社会对国家机器的信任是这些限制的反映。从这个角度来看，美国国家安全局和中情局对新安全威胁的反应会有问题，不是因为他们扩大职权和活动，而是因为他们安排这种活动时秘密进行、又不受监督的方式。史诺登揭发的行动计划理当受到外国情报监视法院的监督，但这种法院的运作也是秘密进行，而且经常只不过是橡皮图章，因而不是维持约束或建立信任的正确方法。

We started in Chapter 1 with some famous predictions about how most countries will move toward liberal democracy, anarchy, or dictatorships. Yuval Noah Harari’s warning that digital dictatorship was on the horizon for much of humanity was perhaps the most ominous, and the Chinese “social credit” system and the NSA’s aggressive surveillance programs add credence to Harari’s predictions. But as we have argued, there is no reason to expect that all or most countries will inexorably move toward the same type of political or economic system. It will be the prevailing balance between state and society that determines their paths. The Danish alternative to the same security threats underscores this point.

> 我们在第一章开始时，曾经针对大部分国家会走向自由民主制度、无政府状态或走向独裁的问题，提出过一些著名的预测。哈拉瑞所说大部分人类未来会面对数位独裁政权的警告，可能是其中最不祥的预测；中国的「社会信用制度」和美国国家安全局侵略性的监视行动，提高了哈拉瑞预测的可信度，但就像我们所主张的那样，没有理由预测所有或大部分国家，会向着相同的政治或经济制度前进，不受阻挡。决定大部分国家前进路线的，一定是国家和社会当前的平衡。丹麦针对恐怖主义威胁采取的不同因应之道，强化了我们的这个论点。

When security threats are met with expanding and unmonitored powers of the state, abuses are much more likely to occur and the danger of a digital dictatorship increases. When the same actions are taken in plain sight and society can monitor whether these powers are being misused, the balance of power underpinning the corridor is reaffirmed. This reaffirmation encourages the use of new technologies in a way that is much more consistent with the principles of the Shackled Leviathan, even if these technologies could compromise privacy. The way new technologies will be deployed and whether they will disrupt the balance of power is not preordained. It is our choice.

> 用持续扩大和不受监督的国家权力来因应安全威胁时，滥权的可能性会大大增加，数位独裁政权的危险也会提高。在众目睽睽之下，采取社会可以监督权力是否遭到滥用的相同行动，可以重新肯定支持自由窄廊的权力均衡，这样的重新肯定会鼓励人们，以极力配合国家受到制约原则的方式，利用新科技，即使这些科技可能伤害隐私亦然。新科技的运用方式，以及新科技是否会扰乱权力均衡，不是预先注定的事情，而是取决于我们的选择。

## Rights in Action: The Niemöller Principle · 行动的权利：倪慕乐原则（Niemöller Principle）

It is not just difficult to create a Shackled Leviathan. It also takes hard work to live with it. We have suggested a few specific ways in which society can be strengthened in the presence of a growing Leviathan. The most critical idea is to leverage society’s mobilization. But how do you achieve that in practice?

> 创建受到制约国家机器不仅困难，要和国家机器共同生活也需要努力奋斗。我们建议过几个特别的方法，让社会可以在国家机器不断成长的情况下自我强化，其中最重要的概念是利用社会动员的力量。但是，实际上你要怎么达成这个目标呢？

Are there organizational avenues to help society expand its capabilities and control over the state and elites? We believe the answer is yes. It relates to the ideas from the previous chapter—to build on the protection for the rights of citizens against all threats, including those from the state, the elites, and other citizens.

> 有没有什么有组织的方法，帮助社会扩大本身能力、控制国家机器和菁英呢？我们认为答案是肯定的。答案和前一章谈到的观念有关，建立在保护公民权利的基础上，对抗所有包括来自国家机器、菁英和其他公民的威胁。

Rights are intimately connected to our notion of liberty as protection of individuals from fear, violence, and dominance. Though fear and violence have been the main drivers of people fleeing their homes, dominance—the inability of individuals to make choices and pursue their lives according to their own values—is often as stifling. Rights are fundamentally ways for society to encode in its laws and norms the capacity of all individuals to make such choices in their lives.

> 权利和保护个人免于恐惧、暴力和宰割自由的观念密切相关。虽然恐惧和暴力一直是人民逃离家园的主因，个人无法根据自己的价值观、选择和追求本身生活的宰割，却经常一样让人窒息。「权利」基本上就是社会在法律和规范中，赋予每一个人都可以在生活中做出这种选择的能力。

The emphasis on rights extends back at least to John Locke as well as to Thomas Jefferson’s statement concerning “rights inherent and inalienable, among which are the preservation of life, and liberty, and the pursuit of happiness” in the Declaration of Independence, and the French Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen of 1789. Our modern conception is shaped by the Universal Declaration of Rights of the United Nations adopted in 1948. William Beveridge anticipated these ideas in a 1945 pamphlet, Why I Am a Liberal, in which he wrote:

> 强调权利的传统至少可以回溯到洛克，回溯到杰佛逊在美国《独立宣言》中有关「若干不可剥夺的权利，其中包括生命权、自由权和追求幸福的权利」的声明，以及一七八九年的法国《人权和公民权宣言》。现代的权利观念则由一九四八年的联合国《世界人权宣言》所塑造，但贝佛里奇已经在一九四五年出版的《为什么我是自由主义者》的小册子里，预料到这些理念：

Liberty means more than freedom from the arbitrary power of Governments. It means freedom from economic servitude to Want and Squalor and other social evils; it means freedom from arbitrary power in any form. A starving man is not free. The Universal Declaration similarly asserts: Whereas disregard and contempt for human rights have resulted in barbarous acts which have outraged the conscience of mankind, and the advent of a world in which human beings shall enjoy freedom of speech and belief and freedom from fear and want has been proclaimed as the highest aspiration of the common people.

> 自由不仅表示免于政府独断权力的左右，也表示在经济上，免于匮乏、贫穷和其他社会弊病的奴役，表示免于任何形式的独断权力侵扰。〔因此〕饥饿的人没有自由。联合国《世界人权宣言》同样主张：鉴于对人权的无视和侮蔑已发展为野蛮暴行，这些暴行玷污了人类的良心，而一个人人享有言论和信仰自由，并免于恐惧和匮乏的世界来临，已被宣布为普通人民的最高愿望

Article 23 proceeds: Everyone has the right to work, to free choice of employment, to just and favorable conditions of work and to protection against unemployment. Everyone, without any discrimination, has the right to equal pay for equal work. Everyone who works has the right to just and favorable remuneration ensuring for himself and his family an existence worthy of human dignity, and supplemented, if necessary, by other means of social protection.

> 《世界人权宣言》第二十三条继续指出：一、人人有权工作、自由选择职业、享受公正和合适的工作条件，并享受免于失业的保障。二、人人有同工同酬的权利，不受任何歧视。三、每一个工作的人，有权享受公正和合适的报酬，保证使他本人和家属有一个符合尊严的生活条件，必要时并辅以其他方式的社会保障。

Everyone has the right to form and to join trade unions for the protection of his interests. FDR was also articulating similar notions. In 1940 and 1941, he emphasized “four essential freedoms”: freedom of speech, freedom of religion, freedom from want, and freedom from fear. In his State of the Union address of 1944 he went further and stated: We have come to a clear realization of the fact that true individual freedom cannot exist without economic security and independence.

> 四、人人有为维护其利益而组织和参加工会的权利。美国的小罗斯福总统也曾经表达类似的理念，他在一九四○年和一九四一年间，强调过四大自由：言论自由、宗教信仰自由、免于匮乏的自由，和免于恐惧的自由。他在一九四四年的国情咨文演说中，进一步声明：我们已经清楚地了解，没有经济安全和独立，真正的个人自由不可能存在。

“Necessitous men are not free men.” People who are hungry and out of a job are the stuff of which dictatorships are made. He then listed the essential rights as “the right to a useful and renumerative job”; “the right to earn enough to provide adequate food and clothing and recreation”; “the right of every businessman, large and small, to trade in an atmosphere of freedom from unfair competition and domination by monopolies at home or abroad”; “the right of every family to a decent home”; “the right to adequate protection from the economic fears of old age, sickness, accident, and unemployment”; and “the right to a good education.”

> 「贫穷的人不是自由人」，饥饿和失业的人是独裁的温床。接着，他列出的基本权利包括「获得有用与有薪工作的权利」、「赚到足够提供适当食物、衣着和娱乐所需钱财的权利」、「每个企业家无论大小，都有在免于国内外不公平竞争与垄断宰割的自由气氛中交易的权利」、「每个家庭都有合宜住家的权利」、「在经济上，得到适当保护，免于老病、意外和失业恐惧的权利」，以及「接受良好教育的权利」。

FDR had in the past been willing to abridge some of these rights and freedoms, for example, with the internment of Japanese citizens from 1942 to 1945, and he had worked with Jim Crow laws in the South. (One African American’s reaction to FDR’s four freedoms is telling: “White folks talking about the Four Freedoms, and we ain’t got none.”) His conversion to the importance of rights signals how the mood was changing on both sides of the Atlantic.

> 小罗斯福总统过去曾经乐于缩减上述若干权利和自由，例如在一九四二年至一九四五年间扣留日本公民，而且曾经和导致美国南方实施种族隔离的吉姆．克劳法合作（一位非裔美国人对小罗斯福总统四大自由的反应说得很清楚：「白人谈论四种自由，我们却连一样都没有。」）小罗斯福皈依权利的重要性显示，大西洋两岸的气氛都在改变。

What is remarkable in these statements are two tenets of the conception of rights: they are universal and general (and in this they go far beyond the Declaration of Independence, which did not cover slaves and was unclear on women), and they recognize the importance of individuals being able to realize their choices. Thus threats of violence and restrictions on freedom of thought or speech against any group are violations of rights, and so is preventing people from exercising their religious activities (or lack thereof) or sexual preferences.

> 在这些声明中，权利观念中全面和普遍这两项原则值得注意（在这方面，这种声明远胜过美国《独立宣言》，因为《独立宣言》没有涵盖奴隶，在女权方面则是含糊其词），而且这些声明承认个人实现自身选择能力的重要性。因此，暴力威胁、限制任何团体的思想或言论自由，都违反人权，阻止别人进行宗教活动（或不进行宗教活动）或性偏好也一样违反人权。

But equally important, taking away the means of earning a decent living is a violation as well, because this too will create a form of dominance. This latter dominance is rooted not just in the fact that abject poverty would make it impossible for people to pursue a meaningful life, but also in the realization that under such circumstances, employers can demand work in unpleasant, demeaning, or highly disempowering conditions (remember the Dalit manual scavengers in Chapter 8). This conception of rights is crucial for the liberty not just of men and the majority; but of women; of religious, ethnic, and sexual minorities; and of people with disabilities and impairments.

> 但同样重要的是，剥夺他人赚取合宜生活的手段，也违反人权，因为这样也会创造一种宰割的形式，这种宰割不但起源于赤贫、不但让人不可能追求有意义的生活，还会让人意识到，雇主在这种情况下，可以要求员工在令人难过、卑下和信心十分低落的状况下工作（请回想第八章中当清洁工的达利特人）。这种权利观念不但攸关男性和多数族群的自由，也攸关女性、宗教、种族、性少数、残障或受伤人士的自由。

Enshrining these rights creates clear limits on what the state and powerful elites in society can and cannot do. Taking away the ability of people to organize, argue their case, or pursue their way of life is beyond the limits of what anybody can do when these rights are clearly protected. So is creating conditions that force people to be economically subservient and dominated.

> 揭橥这些权利，会为国家和社会上有权力的菁英，明订什么事情可以做、什么事情不可以做的限制。当人们创立组织、申明主张或追求其生活方式的权利受到明确保护时，任何人再试图剥夺这些权利都会逾越红线；创造某些条件，迫使别人在经济上，沦落到受屈辱或受宰割的行为，也一样违反人权。

Herein lie the beginnings of a transformative power for society. If clearly delineated boundaries on what the state cannot do are recognized universally, encroachments of these boundaries can create the spark for a broad-based societal mobilization to stop the state’s creeping overreach. Recognizing minority rights as universal is crucial, because without such recognition, only the specific minorities whose rights are currently being violated will complain and protest—with no mobilization or response, as in the disorganized, fragmented societies in India (Chapter 8) and Latin America and Africa (Chapter 11). Universal recognition of rights creates the basis for broad coalitions.

> 这是社会力量转型的开始，如果说明国家可以做什么的限制，获得人们普遍的承认，那么侵犯这些界限，可能引爆广泛社会动员的火花，阻止国家过分行为的蔓延。承认少数族群普遍的权利至为重要，因为如果没有这种承认，就只会有目前权利遭到侵犯的特定少数族群，会在没有动员或反应的情况下，表示不满和提出抗议，就像印度（参见第八章）、拉丁美洲和非洲（参见第十一章）支离破碎、没有组织的社会那样。普遍承认权利，会为广泛的联盟创造基础。

The importance of this idea was anticipated by the German Lutheran pastor Martin Niemöller, who in a poem composed in the 1950s pithily captured why it was so easy for the Nazi state to quickly dominate German society. The most well-known version of the poem, engraved at many of the Holocaust memorial museums and often recited at remembrance events, reads: First they came for the Socialists, and I did not speak out because I was not a Socialist. Then they came for the trade unionists, and I did not speak out because I was not a trade unionist.

> 德国信义会牧师马丁．倪慕乐（Martin Niemöller）曾经预示到这种观念的重要性，他在一九五○年代写作的一首诗里，简洁地描述为什么纳粹国家机器能够这么轻松地迅速宰割德国社会，这首诗最著名的版本刻在很多大屠杀纪念博物馆中，而且经常在纪念会中朗诵。诗文如下：起初，他们来抓社会主义者，我没有说话，因为我不是社会主义者；接着，他们来抓工会会员，我没有说话，因为我不是工会会员；

Then they came for the Jews, and I did not speak out because I was not a Jew. Then they came for me and there was no one left to speak for me. So in Niemöller’s account it was the lack of universal recognition of very basic rights that was at the root of the inability of German society to rise up to the Nazis, who could deal with and eliminate each group separately, without mobilizing a broad coalition within German society to stand up to them. This turned out to be a particularly bad way of defending the corridor.

> 然后，他们来抓犹太人，我没有说话，因为我不是犹太人；最后，他们来抓我，已经没有人能为我说话了。因此，根据倪慕乐的解释，缺少对非常基本权利的普遍认识，是德国社会无力起来反抗纳粹党的根本原因，因为纳粹党可以分别对付和个个击破每一个族群，却不至于在德国社会内部，引发广泛的联盟动员起来对抗纳粹党。事实证明，「无法团结」正是捍卫自由窄廊却不得其法的原因。

These ideas too were to some degree anticipated by FDR, who in the same State of the Union address of 1944 emphasized the importance of a diverse set of rights for everybody, and for good measure quoted Benjamin Franklin’s statement from 1776, “We must all hang together, or assuredly we shall all hang separately.” Turning this logic on its head, to the extent that society can make a broad set of (reasonable) rights more universal, it will be better placed to organize and match the state’s expanding power.

> 美国的小罗斯福在一九四四年的同一篇国情咨文演说中，多少已经预见到这些观念，他在这篇演讲中，强调一套人人适用的人权很重要，他理由充分地引述富兰克林一七七六年的声明：「我们若不团结，必然各无死所。」从这种逻辑的反面来说，如果社会能够把一整套（合理的）权利变得更普遍，在推动组织、抗衡国家权力的扩张上，就会占有比较有利的地位。

It is noteworthy that these rights as expressed by the Universal Declaration include access to gainful employment, because this creates room and incentives for different parts of society, motivated by economic considerations and grievances, to come together in a broad coalition and organize to resist despotism. These challenges may be particularly vital in the future as the labor movement may never regain its previous influence, as we have already discussed. A (civil) society organized around rights is one alternative.

> 值得注意的是，《世界人权宣言》中陈述的权利中，包括获得有薪工作的权利，因为这样能替社会上受经济考量和不满所驱动的不同人群，创造空间和诱因，让他们组织成广泛的联盟，用有组织的方式，对抗专制主义。将来这些挑战可能变得特别重要，因为我们已经讨论过，劳工运动可能永远无法恢复过去的影响力，以权利为中心而组织的（公民）社会可能是一种新的替代方案。

Many of us living in democratic countries with assertive societies and high-capacity states are immensely fortunate compared to those suffering under the yoke of a Despotic Leviathan or surviving under fear, violence, and dominance without any state institutions to protect them. Nevertheless, living with the (shackled) Leviathan is a work in progress. Our argument has been that the key to making this more stable and less likely to spin out of the corridor is to seek to create and re-create the balance of power between state and society, between those who are powerful and those who are not. The Red Queen effect is there to help us, but ultimately society’s power is about society’s organization and mobilization.

> 我们很多人有幸能生长在民主国家里，拥有坚定自信的社会与具有高强能力的国家。相形之下，有很多人在专制国家的枷锁下受苦受难，或是在恐惧、暴力和宰割之下苟延残喘，得不到任何国家制度的保护。然而，和（受到制约的）国家巨灵一起生活是一种现在进行式。我们一直主张，要使这种情况更稳定、更不可能脱离自由窄廊的关键，是设法在国家和社会之间、在有权有势和无钱无势的人之间，创造和再造权力均衡。红皇后效应会在场帮助我们，但社会的力量最后还是取决于社会的组织和动员。

In October 2017, women started speaking up about the sexual harassment and assault they had been subjected to by men with power over them. It began with the allegations against the towering movie mogul Harvey Weinstein. On October 5, actress Ashley Judd added to the accusations. On October 17, actress Alyssa Milano adopted a term coined by activist Tarana Burke in 2006 and tweeted, “If you’ve been sexually harassed or assaulted, write ‘me too’ as a reply to this tweet.” An avalanche of tweets followed and a social movement was born.

> 二○一七年十月，许多女性开始大声疾呼，揭发她们受到有权掌控她们的男性性骚扰和性侵害。高高在上的电影大亨哈维．韦恩斯坦（Harvey Weinstein）遭到指控，替这件事拉开序幕。十月五日，女演员艾希莉．贾德（Ashley Judd）加入指控。到了十月十七日，女演员艾莉莎．米兰诺（Alyssa Milano）采用激进主义分子塔拉纳．伯克（Tarana Burke）二○○六年创造的一个名词，她在推特（Twitter）发文说：「如果你曾经遭到性骚扰或性侵，请在回复这篇推文时，写下『我也是』（me too），作为回答。」回复推文如排山倒海般涌入，一场社会运动就此诞生。

Even if we are nowhere near full equality and protection for women around the world, because people have rallied against the violations of these most basic rights, it has become quite a bit more difficult for the powerful to harass, demean, and assault women in government, companies, and schools. Laws started to change in response, for example, with New York State’s new sexual harassment prevention law. Human progress depends on the expansion of the state’s capacity to meet new challenges and combat all dominances, old and new, but that won’t happen unless society demands it and mobilizes to defend everybody’s rights. There is nothing easy or automatic about that, but it can and does happen.

> 虽然我们离保护全世界女性的全面平等还很遥远，但因为有人们团结起来，对抗违反这种最基本权利的行为，现在政府、企业和学校里的有力人士，想要骚扰、贬抑和侵害女性，已经变得困难许多。法律为因应新局，也开始改变，例如，纽约州就已经制定了防止性骚扰的新法律。人类的进步取决于能否扩大国家能力来因应新挑战，对抗无论新旧的所有宰割。但是，除非社会提出要求，并且动员起来捍卫每一个人的权利，否则这种进步不会发生。这件事一点都不容易，也非必然。但改变与进步是有可能的，而且也实实在在地发生了。
