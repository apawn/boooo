# Wahhab’s Children · 第12章　瓦哈比的子女

## The Tactician’s Dream · 战术家的美梦

In the West, the Middle East, especially Saudi Arabia, has come to epitomize lack of liberty, with personal freedom abridged by custom, religion, and despotism. What explains the perfect storm of unchecked despotism and a stifling cage of norms? Such an outcome is surprising given Muhammad’s successful state-building drive, which originated in part of Saudi Arabia, and the spectacular civilization that Islamic empires built in the following centuries. What happened? Why did the cage of norms become so fervent and suffocating in the Middle East, when we have seen many other state-building drives relax rather than intensify the cage’s restrictions?

> 西方已经把中东，尤其是把沙乌地阿拉伯，看成是缺少自由的象征。此地的个人自由遭到习俗、宗教和专制主义扼杀，不受约束的专制主义，结合了令人窒息的规范牢笼。我们应该怎么解释这种坏到不能再坏的局面呢？想到沙乌地阿拉伯孕育出了穆罕默德建国成功的故事，想到伊斯兰帝国在随后的几个世纪里所建立的璀璨文明，这样的结果确实让人惊讶。到底发生了什么事？鉴于其他国家建国过程中都是放宽牢笼，而非强化其限制，中东的规范牢笼为什么会变得如此严苛、如此令人窒息？

As we saw in Chapter 3, Muhammad’s state spread rapidly through much of the Middle East and North Africa. But not everywhere. Arabia is divided into several distinct regions (as can be seen in Map 4 in Chapter 3). Medina and Mecca are in the Hijaz, nestled in the Sarawat Mountains, which run along the western side of the peninsula bordering the Red Sea. On the eastern side the mountains end and the great vastness of the Arabian desert interior, the Najd, begins. Field Marshal Erwin Rommel, who headed the German Afrika Korps during World War II, called the desert “a tactician’s dream and a quartermaster’s nightmare.” Such was the Najd.

> 我们在第三章里看到，穆罕默德的国家迅速扩张到中东大部分地方和北非，却不是拓展到每一个地方。（我们从第三章的地图四中可以看出）。阿拉伯分为好几个不同的区域，麦地那和麦加所在的汉志（Hijaz）地区位在沙捞越山脉（Sarawat Mountains）中，这座山脉位在濒临红海的阿拉伯半岛西侧，大致呈纵贯走势，山脉东侧尽头、广大无垠阿拉伯内陆沙漠出现的地方，就是内志（Najd）地区的起点。二次大战期间，统率德意志非洲军团的陆军元帅隆美尔，把沙漠叫做「战术家的美梦、军需官的恶梦」，内志区域就是这样的地方。

Rather than taking on this logistical nightmare, the Islamic empires after Muhammad spread up toward Damascus and Baghdad, and then west into Egypt and North Africa, where they could conquer already centralized states. Even later, after the various early caliphates collapsed and the seat of Islamic power shifted to Constantinople and the Ottoman Empire, the Najd resisted integration. The Ottomans controlled the Hijaz and the Muslim holy cities as well as Mesopotamia between the Tigris and Euphrates, but they left the interior of Arabia more or less alone.

> 穆罕默德死后，伊斯兰帝国没有承担这种后勤补给恶梦，而是进军大马士革和巴格达，然后西征埃及和北非，征服已经设立中央政府的国家。更久之后，甚至在多个早期的伊斯兰王国崩溃，伊斯兰政权中心移到康士坦丁堡和奥图曼帝国后，内志区域仍然抗拒合并。奥图曼帝国控制了汉志、穆斯林的圣域，以及底格里斯河和幼发拉底河之间的两河流域（美索不达米亚），大致上却不大理会阿拉伯内陆地区。

Though the Bedouin of the Najd converted to Islam, they avoided political centralization. All great religions are flexible and open to multiple interpretations and implementations (otherwise how could they spread so successfully?). Though Muhammad was intent on building a powerful state and spelled out the nature of centralized authority in the Constitution of Medina, the Quran was less specific on this issue. In fact there are only two surahs (chapters) that directly address constitutional issues. In one, obedience to authority is emphasized. Another calls for consultation, the norm among the Bedouin tribes of the desert. There was plenty of wiggle room there to assuage the Arabs’ skepticism of centralized authority.

> 内志地区的贝多因人虽然皈依伊斯兰教，却避免政治上的中央化。所有伟大宗教都很有弹性，放任多种解释和实践（否则如何传播？）穆罕默德虽然建立强大的国家，还在《麦地那宪章》中阐明中央权威的性质，《古兰经》对这一点的说明却比较不明确。事实上，《古兰经》中只有两章直接触及宪法问题，其中一章强调必须服从权威，另一章要求咨商，而咨商正是沙漠贝多因人部落奉行的规范，其中有很大的回旋余地，足以缓解阿拉伯人对中央集权的怀疑。

Perhaps most important, there would be no elaborate hierarchical church structure with priests, bishops, and popes in Islam. Instead, each Muslim could connect directly to Allah with no intermediaries (not unlike many Protestant denominations). This made it harder for a centralized authority to imprint its wishes on local communities and made the new religion more palatable to the Bedouin tribes. It also implied that the tribal structure in areas such as the Najd was more likely to persist and fuse with the teachings of Islam.

> 或许最重要的是，伊斯兰教没有由教士、主教和教宗构成的复杂教会结构，每位穆斯林反而可以直接和阿拉联系，不必透过中间人（和新教很多教派不同）。这点使中央权威比较难以把自己的愿望，打进当地社区里，也使贝多因人更乐于接受新宗教。这点也表示内志之类地区的部落结构比较可能延续下去，并与伊斯兰教义融合。

So the Najd remained divided into competing, autonomous tribes ruled by sheikhs or emirs well into the eighteenth century. Competition sometimes turned violent. At the oasis of al-Diriya, just outside Riyadh, the modern capital of Saudi Arabia, a series of assassinations led to the emergence of a new emir, Muhammad ibn Saud, who assumed power either in 1726 or 1727. It was Saud’s descendants who in 1932 were to give their name to the as yet unimagined kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Our earliest existing sources of information about Saud come from early in the nineteenth century and all agree on the centrality of one event to Saud’s and his kingdom’s destiny—his first meeting with Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab.

> 因此，内志地区一直到十八世纪开始后很久，仍然分裂为由酋长或王公统治、彼此互相竞争的自治部落。竞争偶尔会变得很暴力，在现代沙乌地阿拉伯首都利雅德近郊的德拉伊耶（al-Diriya）绿洲，一系列的暗杀促成新王公绍德（Muhammad ibn Saud）崛起。绍德在一七二六年或一七二七年掌握政权。绍德的后代一直到一九三二年，才把绍德的名字，加在还无法想像的沙乌地阿拉伯王国的国名中。现存跟绍德有关的资讯来源出自十九世纪初，人们都同意，绍德和穆罕默德．瓦哈比（Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab）的第一次见面，对绍德和他的王国的命运，都是至为重要的事情。

Al-Wahhab came from Uyaina, an oasis town about twenty miles north of al-Diriya. His family was deeply steeped in Islamic teaching and his father was a qadi, a judge appointed by the local emir to resolve cases using the Islamic law, Sharia. This law had emerged along with Muhammad and the early Islamic empires. Its most basic elements were the holy book, the Quran, and the hadiths, a large collection of sayings and actions attributed to Muhammad and written down by his followers.

> 瓦哈比出身绿洲城镇优雅伊耶（Uyaina），这里离南边的德拉伊耶大约二十英里，他的家庭笃信伊斯兰教义，父亲是当地王公任命的穆斯林法官（qadi），任务是利用伊斯兰教法判决案子。伊斯兰教法是跟着穆罕默德和早期伊斯兰帝国一起出现的法律，最基本的要素是《古兰经》，和门徒记录下来的穆罕默德言行实录《圣训》（hadith ）。

During the early Middle Ages various schools of thought, most prominently the Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi’i, Hanbali, and Jafari, had started debating about what constituted Sharia. Though they all agreed on the centrality of the Quran and the hadiths, they disagreed on the extent to which judges could create precedents or make rulings based on analogies. Of these schools, the Hanbalis were the most conservative. They rejected all such evolution of the law, and their interpretation of Sharia dominated the Najd.

> 中世纪初期，各种思想流派──包括最著名的哈纳菲（Hanafi）、玛里克（Maliki）、莎菲懿（Shafi’i）、罕百里（Hanbali）和贾法里（Jafari）等法学派──开始辩论伊斯兰教法的内涵。虽然他们都同意《古兰经》和《圣训》的绝对重要性，却对那些法官可以创造判例，或根据类推裁决的程度，产生歧见。其中罕百里法学派最保守，拒斥伊斯兰教法的所有这种演变，他们对伊斯兰教法的阐释却在内志地区赢得主导权。

Al-Wahhab had memorized the Quran by the age of ten and began traveling in Iraq, Syria, and Iran, returning to the Najd to begin preaching in the early 1730s. In the absence of a religious hierarchy, anyone with knowledge of the Quran and the hadiths could be recognized as an ulama, a religious teacher who could issue fatwas, which were rulings articulating particular interpretations of the Islamic scriptures, usually in the context of some contemporary problem or debate.

> 瓦哈比十岁就会背诵《古兰经》，而且开始游历伊拉克、叙利亚和伊朗，回到内志地区后，从一七三○年代初期开始传教。在没有宗教阶级的地方，人们可能承认通晓《古兰经》和《圣训》的人是乌理玛（ulama），也就是可以发布伊斯兰教令（fatwa）的宗教导师（乌理玛）。教令通常是针对若干当代问题或论辩，阐明宗教导师对伊斯兰经文的特殊解释。

Sometime during his travels, al-Wahhab formulated a distinct interpretation of Islam and what he decided were its failings in Arabia. Following the Hanbali school, he thought that the people had strayed from the real religion by worshipping idols. Remember that prior to Muhammad’s revelation in Mecca, the Kaaba had been a shrine of different pre-Islamic gods. Worship of these gods persisted, and people sanctified saints and Muhammad’s tomb at Medina. For al-Wahhab this was all idolatry. He preached that holy war, jihad, could be waged against anyone engaged in such idolatry. These interpretations turned out to be very useful for Saud, who was an astute tactician.

> 瓦哈比游历期间，对伊斯兰教形成了不同的解释，认定伊斯兰教在阿拉伯的传播事功失败，他遵循罕百里法学派的思潮，认为人们崇拜偶像，偏离了真正的宗教。请记住，穆罕默德在麦加获得启示前，天房是伊斯兰教之前不同宗教神祇的圣殿，人们对这些神祇的崇拜仍然持续进行，人们也把圣徒和穆罕默德在麦地那的坟墓神圣化。瓦哈比认为这一切都是偶像崇拜，鼓吹可以对从事这种偶像崇拜的人发动圣战。结果这种解释对精明的战术家绍德非常有用。

But first al-Wahhab had to develop his doctrine and build a following. Two of his ideas turned out to be particularly attractive to rulers: people must obey the authorities and they must pay zakat, the obligatory religious tax stipulated in the Quran. Zakat was not popular among the Bedouins of the Najd and seems to have been seldom paid in this period. It was supposed to be used for charity and religious activities, but presumably sheikhs and emirs might get a cut too. The cornerstone of what became Wahhabism, however, was the idea that anyone who didn’t pay zakat was an infidel.

> 但是，瓦哈比首先必须发展自己的主张、建立追随他的群众；他的两种概念特别能够吸引统治者，一是人民必须服从权威，二是人民必须缴纳《古兰经》规定的强制性宗教税「天课」（zakat）。这段期间内，天课在内志地区的贝多因人中不受欢迎，而且很少缴纳。天课理当用在慈善和宗教活动中，但酋长和王公可能也可以分一杯羹。然而，凡是不缴纳天课的人都是异教徒的说法，后来却变成瓦哈比派的基础。

Al-Wahhab began to put his new doctrine into action in Uyaina. He cut down a sacred tree with his own hands. There was to be no worship of trees. He destroyed the grave of Zaid ibn al-Kattab, a companion of the Prophet, which had turned into a place of pilgrimage. There was to be no worship of tombs, no pilgrimages except the hadj, the annual pilgrimage to Mecca. When a woman from Uyaina was found guilty of fornication, al-Wahhab applied a strict interpretation of Sharia and ordered her to be stoned to death.

> 瓦哈比开始在优雅伊耶实施他的理念：他砍倒一棵圣树，意指不可崇拜树木；他摧毁先知的伙伴萨伊德．哈塔卜（Zaid ibn al-Kattab）已经变成朝圣地点的坟墓，意指不得崇拜坟墓；除了一年一度的麦加朝圣外，别无其他朝圣之旅。优雅伊耶一位妇女犯了通奸罪时，瓦哈比从严解释伊斯兰教法，下令用石头把她砸死。

This was a step too far, and in the face of opposition from local ulama who disliked his radical new teachings, al-Wahhab was forced to flee from Uyaina to al-Diriya. There he and Saud had a meeting of minds. No firsthand account of the fateful meeting exists, but al-Wahhab wanted military support for the jihad he planned to wage in order to spread his new doctrines. Saud saw the potential for Wahhabism to be a powerful tool of military expansion and social control. Saud asked al-Wahhab to stay in al-Diriya and support his planned military campaign into the Najd. He also demanded that al-Wahhab agree that he could raise regular taxes on local harvests.

> 这一步太过分了，不喜欢他的激进新教义的本地乌理玛起而反对。于是瓦哈比被迫从优雅伊耶逃到德拉伊耶，和绍德初次见面，两人一拍即合。这次决定性的会面没有第一手纪录存在。但是，瓦哈比需要军事支持，以便发动他计划中的圣战，传播他的新教义。绍德看出瓦哈比派教义深具潜力，是军事扩张和社会控制的有力工具，于是要求瓦哈比留在德拉伊耶，支持他计划中在内志地区发动的军事行动；他也要求瓦哈比，同意他把本地收成的例行税负提高。

Al-Wahhab accepted the first, but not the second. He instead granted that Saud could take one-fifth of all the booty from jihad and pointed out that this would be far more money than taxing harvests. The deal was sealed; Saud relied on Wahhabism, and Wahhabism relied on Saud. The marriage was to be fabulously successful. The Sauds expanded out of a little oasis, taking over first the Najd, and by 1803 had captured Mecca and the Hijaz from the Ottoman state.

> 瓦哈比接受他的第一项要求，却不接受第二项要求，而是同意绍德可以从圣战的所有战利品中，拿走五分之一。瓦哈比还指出，这样收到的钱会远比对收成课税多得多。因此，双方达成协议，绍德和瓦哈比派教义是焦不离孟，孟不离焦，这种天作之合极为成功。绍德家族从小小的绿洲向外扩张，首先取得内志地区；到一八○三年，已经从奥图曼帝国手中，夺取麦加和汉志地区。

One account records how Saud seized al-Hasa, to the east of the Najd. When morning came and Saud went on his way after the prayer, when they [the Wahhabis] rode camels and horses and shot their hand-guns at once, the sky darkened, the earth shuddered, puffs of smoke rose in the air and many pregnant women suffered miscarriages. Then all the people of al-Hasa came to Saud, throwing themselves on his mercy.

> 有一段话记载绍德夺占内志东边阿赫萨（al-Hasa）地方的经过：拂晓来临时，绍德祈祷结束后出发。他们（瓦哈比派信徒）骑着骆驼和马匹，同时发射手枪时，天空为之昏暗，大地为之震动，浓烟升到空中，很多孕妇小产。然后，阿赫萨人民出来，向绍德投降，听任他的摆布。

He ordered all of them to appear before him and so they did. He stayed there for some months, killing, exiling and jailing everybody he wanted to, confiscating property, destroying houses and erecting fortresses. He demanded 100,000 dirhams from them and received that sum . . . Some people . . . toured the markets and caught those who had led a dissolute life . . . Some people were killed in the oasis, others were taken to the camp and their heads chopped off in front of Saud’s tent until they were all destroyed.

> 他下令所有人在他面前现身，所有的人都照办无误。他住在那里好几个月，随心所欲地杀害、流放和关押人民，没收财产，摧毁房舍，建立堡垒。他向他们索取十万迪拉姆（dirham），也如数取得……有些人……巡查市场，抓住过着放荡生活的人……有些人在绿洲上遭到杀害，有些人被带到营地来，在绍德的帐篷前砍头，到所有这种人斩尽杀绝为止。

For the first time ever the Arabian peninsula was more or less unified under one state, though what is now Yemen and Oman in the south remained independent. None of this would have surprised Ibn Khaldun; the desert people who had asabiyyah were conquering the urban areas under the banner of Islam. The political system that developed in the Najd after the 1740s and eventually gelled in Saudi Arabia was very different from what existed before. At the time, tribal sheikhs had to consult other notables in councils called majlis.

> 除了今天的叶门和南方的阿曼仍然独立外，阿拉伯半岛首次大致大一统，成为一个国家。赫勒敦对这种事情应该不会意外，奉行部族主义的沙漠人民高举伊斯兰的大旗，征服了都会地区。一七四○年代后，在内志地区发展出来的政治制度最后把沙乌地阿拉伯，凝结成跟过去的国家大不相同的国度。当时部落酋长必须在人称「协商会议」（majli）的聚会中，征询其他名人的意见。

The English writer and explorer Charles Dougherty noted that this was still the case in the 1860s and 1870s. The principle was “Let him speak here who will, the voice of the least is heard among them; he is a tribesmen.” In fact, sheikhs were elected and potentially any Bedouin could rise to the position, even if it was typically monopolized by notable families. As the Swiss traveler Johann Ludwig Burckhardt noted earlier in the century: The sheikh has no actual authority over the individuals of his tribe.

> 英国作家兼探险家查尔斯．杜格蒂（Charles Dougherty）指出，到一八六○年代和一八七○年代，情形还是这样，原则是「让他在这里说话，至少大家会听到他的声音，他是部落人士」。事实上，酋长是大家选任的，任何贝多因人都可能升到这个职位，不过酋长大致上是由著名家族独占。十九世纪初瑞士旅行家约翰．伯克哈特（Johann Ludwig Burckhardt）曾经指出：酋长对部落中的个人没有实际权威。

Just a garden-variety Absent Leviathan. When Muhammad ibn Saud died in 1765, he was succeeded by his son Abd al-Aziz Muhammad ibn Saud, who still needed to gain legitimacy by being elected by the people of al-Diriya. But this balance between state and society was soon toppled. Abd al-Aziz carried on the conquests of his father, using conversion to Wahhabism as a pretext for military expansion and annexing territory in the region. A copy of a letter, which was read out to those about to be conquered, states:

> 这个国家只是老套巨灵并不存在的国家。一七六五年，绍德去世时，由他儿子阿不都阿齐兹（Abdal-Aziz Muhammad ibn Saud）继承时，仍然需要由德拉伊耶人民选举，才能得到合法性；但是，国家和社会之间的这种平衡很快就会遭到颠覆，阿不都阿齐兹继续从事父亲留下的征服大业，利用皈依瓦哈比派教义，作为军事扩张和兼并这个区域领土的借口。他的军队曾经对即将征服的某个国家，宣读下面这封信函：

Abd al-Aziz to the Arabs of the tribe of ***. Hail! Your duty is to believe in the book I send you. Do not be like the idolatrous Turks, who give God a human intermediary. If you are true believers, you shall be saved; otherwise, I shall wage war upon you until death.

> 阿不都阿齐兹致书某某部落：欢迎！你们的责任是相信我送给你们的书，不要像崇拜偶像的土耳其人一样，为真主制造一个人类的中间人。如果你们是真正的信徒，你们会得救，否则我会对你们发动战争，到你们死亡为止。

When an oasis was conquered, Wahhabi ulama were sent to preach. Saud replaced local emirs and sheikhs with people he handpicked. He appointed Wahhabi qadis to implement their strict version of Sharia. He also appointed an official known as a muhtasib. The muhtasib had various administrative roles, such as overseeing commerce and weights and measures, and also ensured that key Islamic practices, such as prayers, were observed. Ibn Saud also built quite an elaborate bureaucracy of tax collectors to raise the zakat, supposedly sending out seventy teams of collectors each year, each consisting of seven collectors.

> 一处绿洲遭到征服后，瓦哈比派宗教导师会进驻、传教，阿不都阿齐兹用他亲手挑选的人，取代当地王公和酋长，任命瓦哈比派穆斯林法官，实施他们的严格伊斯兰教法，他也会任命一位「监察使」（muhtasib）。监察使担任多种行政职务，例如监督商业与度量衡、确保人们遵守祈祷等伊斯兰教的重要做法。阿不都阿齐兹也建立了相当复杂的税务机关，以便课征天课。据说他每年会派出七十队税务人员，每一队由七位税务员组成。

He started to settle tribal disputes too. In 1788 al-Wahhab issued a fatwa declaring that the Sauds were the hereditary emirs and all Wahhabis had to swear allegiance to the ruling Saud. The despotism of the Sauds was fusing with the Wahhabi cage of norms. The initial triumph of the Sauds and Wahhabis over the Ottomans did not last long. The Ottoman sultan tasked Muhammad Ali, the general of Albanian origin who had made himself de facto independent ruler of Egypt, to confront the Wahhabi threat.

> 他也开始解决部落中的争端。一七八八年，瓦哈比发出一份教令，宣布绍德家族是世袭王公，所有瓦哈比派教徒都必须对统治的绍德国王，宣誓效忠。绍德家族的专制主义和瓦哈比派的规范牢笼融合为一。绍德家族和瓦哈比派对奥图曼帝国初期的胜利并不持久，奥图曼苏丹派出出身阿尔及利亚、后来自立为埃及实质独立统治者的大将穆罕默德．阿里（Muhammad Ali）对抗瓦哈比派的威胁。

Muhammad Ali and later his son Ibrahim Pasha invaded the Hijaz and destroyed the aspiring Saudi state in 1818. Yet it was difficult to govern the Najd. In 1824 a second Saudi state formed, but with the Ottomans more securely in charge of the Hijaz, it never had the authority or scope of the initial state. It collapsed in 1891, defeated by a rival Najd family, and the Sauds went into exile in Kuwait. But not for long.

> 阿里和他儿子易卜拉欣．帕夏（Ibrahim Pasha）先后侵入汉志，在一八一八年消灭新兴的绍德国家。但是，要统治内志地区很难。一八二四年，绍德家族建立第二个国家。在奥图曼人更稳稳控制汉志的情况下，绍德家族建立的第二个国家无论是在领土抑或权威上，都不如第一个国家；这个国家遭到内志地区一个对手家族击败，在一八九一年灭亡，绍德家族流亡科威特，但时间并不长。

## Domesticating the Ulama · 制服宗教导师乌理玛

In 1902 the Sauds were back, led by Abd al-Aziz bin Saud, a great-great-great-grandson of Muhammad ibn Saud. Abd al-Aziz trekked across the desert from Kuwait and captured Riyadh, al-Diriya having been abandoned after being destroyed by Ibrahim Pasha in 1818. Saud had a new version of his ancestor’s religious secret weapon—the Ikhwan.

> 一九○二年，绍德家族在阿不都阿齐兹（Abdal-Aziz bin Saud）的领导下班师回朝。阿不都阿齐兹是绍德国王的六世孙，他从科威特横越沙漠，占领利雅德。德拉伊耶在一八一八年遭到帕夏摧毁后，已经废弃，变成废墟。阿不都阿齐兹拥有新版的祖先秘密宗教武器，就是兄弟会。

The Ikhwan, meaning literally “brothers,” was a religious organization started by the qadi of Riyadh, a member of the al-Shaikh family, descendants of al-Wahhab. They formed settlements devoted to a strict version of Islam, shunned foreigners, adopted a rigid code of conduct, and developed strong norms of cooperation and mutual support. They also inherited the Wahhabi habit of declaring jihad on those who did not adhere to their rules. Muhammad ibn Saud had seen the potential of Wahhabism in his state-building efforts and used the Wahhabi fighters to attack his rivals. So did Abd al-Aziz with the Ikhwan.

> 兄弟会是利雅德穆斯林法官创立的宗教组织，这位穆斯林法官是瓦哈比后代的阿尔夏克（al-Shaikh）家族成员，他们组成奉行严格伊斯兰教规定的居住地，回避外国人，采行严谨的行为准则，发展强力的合作与互助规范。他们也继承瓦哈比对不奉行他们规定的人发动圣战的习惯。阿不都阿齐兹看出瓦哈比派教义在他建国大业中的潜力，利用瓦哈比派战士攻击对手，也用相同的手法利用兄弟会。

The first Ikhwan settlement dates back to 1913 at al-Artawiya, to the northwest of Riyadh. Soon Abd al-Aziz was giving them money and seed and helping build mosques and schools. This assistance was followed by guns and ammunition, even if the Ikhwan seemed to have preferred traditional weapons like swords. He encouraged and built more settlements, inducing the ulama of Riyadh to issue a fatwa promoting settled life and agriculture. He managed to get the right to appoint qadis to the settlements, typically from the al-Shaikh family, which bolstered the Saud-Wahhab alliance.

> 兄弟会的第一个定居地点阿塔维亚（al-Artawiya）位在利雅德西北，定居时间可以回溯到一九一三年。阿不都阿齐兹很快就送给他们资金和种子，协助他们建立清真寺和学校，接着再送他们枪枝和弹药。不过兄弟会似乎比较喜欢刀剑等传统武器。他鼓励人们建立更多定居地点，诱使利雅德乌理玛发布伊斯兰教令，宣扬定居生活和农业，设法取得任命定居地点穆斯林法官的权力，法官人选通常出自阿尔夏克家族，因而进一步提升绍德和瓦哈比两大家族的联盟。

The Ikhwan were then subjected to a military draft and became Abd al-Aziz’s shock troops. (A surviving photo of the Ikhwan from this period is shown in the photography section.) They fought for jihad, he for a kingdom. It was an uneasy balance, but for a while these goals coalesced around the despotism–cage of norms axis. As early as 1914, however, he had to induce an ulama to issue another fatwa appealing for tolerance to reign the Ikhwan in.

> 随后，兄弟会接受征兵，成为阿不都阿齐兹的突击部队（插页相片集中刊有此一期间留传下来的兄弟会照片）。他们为圣战而战，他则为建立王国而战，这种平衡并不稳定，却可以依附在专制主义和规范牢笼的轴心上，共存共荣。然而，早到一九一四年，他就必须诱使一位乌理玛，发布另一份伊斯兰教令，呼吁大家容忍兄弟会接受治理的事情。

Things came to a head when the Ottoman Empire, still in control of the Hijaz and the holy cities, entered World War I on the side of Germany. The British, Lawrence of Arabia included, encouraged the emir of Mecca, Hussein, to lead the famous Arab revolt of 1916 by promising him that after the Ottomans had been defeated they would guarantee the emergence of an Arab state “in the limits and boundaries proposed by the Sherif of Mecca,” but excluding “portions of Syria” which lay to the west of “the districts of Damascus, Homs, Hama and Aleppo.” By 1918 the Ottoman army had collapsed, and the British took advantage of the ambiguities of the agreement with Hussein to restrict his state to the Hijaz. Together with the French they carved up the rest of the former Ottoman Empire with the exception of part of Turkey.

> 仍然统治汉志和圣域的奥图曼帝国加入德国阵营，参与第一次世界大战时，情势到达紧要关头，包括阿拉伯的劳伦斯在内的英国人，鼓励麦加王公胡赛因，领导著名的一九一六年阿拉伯起义，承诺在打败奥图曼帝国后，保证「会在麦加王公提倡的限制和疆界中」，成立一个阿拉伯国家，但不含大马士革、荷姆斯（Homs）、哈玛（Hama）和阿勒坡以西的叙利亚」。一九一八年，奥图曼帝国土崩瓦解，英国利用和胡赛因所订协议中的含混不明的地方，把胡赛因的国家限制在汉志地区，还和法国人合作，瓜分了旧奥图曼帝国的其他部分，只有土耳其例外。

Hussein was livid at this betrayal and refused to sign the Treaty of Versailles in 1919. In the meantime, Abd al-Aziz and the Ikhwan consolidated their dominance over the Najd. At first they had no desire to confront the Ottoman troops. Nor did they wish to fight Hussein, ruling over the Hijaz with the backing of the British. But Hussein’s antagonism to Britain’s postwar plans, particularly those involving Palestine, led Britain to switch its support to Abd al-Aziz in 1924. Emboldened, he invaded the Hijaz. Mecca fell in October of that year and Medina was captured in December 1925.

> 遭到背叛的胡赛因勃然大怒，拒绝签署一九一九年的凡尔赛和约。同时，阿不都阿齐兹和兄弟会巩固自己在内志地区的主宰，他们起初无意对抗奥图曼的军队，也不希望和统治汉志、受到英国人支持的胡赛因作战。但是，因为胡赛因反对英国人的战后计划，尤其反对跟巴勒斯坦有关的计划，导致英国人在一九二四年，转而支持阿不都阿齐兹。阿不都阿齐兹受到鼓励，侵入汉志，这一年十月，麦加沦陷。一九二五年十二月，阿不都阿齐兹夺下麦地那。

Abd al-Aziz became the king of the Najd and Hijaz thanks to the Ikhwan. He had what he wanted, but the Ikhwan didn’t. They were on a jihad against apostates, and not just those confined to the Arabian peninsula. They started launching attacks on the British protectorate of Transjordan, only to be beaten back by the British air force. Abd al-Aziz decided that the Ikhwan had done their job and were more of a nuisance than an asset. He turned on them, defeated them at the battle of Sabilla, and rounded up and killed their leaders. In 1932 he unified the Hijaz and Najd into the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

> 阿不都阿齐兹在兄弟会的支持下，如愿以偿，出任内志和汉志地区国王。兄弟会却还没有完成心愿，他们正在对叛教的人发动圣战，而且不仅限于对付阿拉伯半岛的叛教者，还开始攻击约旦河东岸的英国保护国，却遭到英国空军击退。阿不都阿齐兹认定兄弟会已经完成任务，现在比较像是麻烦而非资产，于是对兄弟会发动战争，并在萨比拉战役中，击败他们，围捕并杀害他们的领袖。一九三二年，他统一汉志和内志地区，建立沙乌地阿拉伯王国。

The defeat of the Ikhwan was a powerful message that in the coalition of the Sauds and Wahhabism, the Sauds called the shots. But it took some time for the Saudi kings to institutionalize this in its modern form. A pivotal moment came following the death of Abd al-Aziz in 1953. He was succeeded by his son Saud, but there was intense rivalry with other brothers who wanted the throne, particularly his half brother Faisal.

> 击败兄弟会释出强力的讯息，表示在绍德家族和瓦哈比教派的联盟中，绍德家族才是发号施令的人。但是，沙乌地国王还需要花一点时间，才能把这一点纳入现代制度中。一九五三年，阿不都阿齐兹去世后，关键时刻来临，他的儿子绍德继承王位，却和希望继承大宝的其他兄弟，爆发激烈竞争，跟同父异母兄弟费瑟的竞争尤其激烈。

Faisal turned out to be politically far more astute. As Saud was struggling with health problems, Faisal gradually took control of many policy portfolios and built a coalition of supporters within the royal family. Finally, confident of his power, Faisal convened the ulama to consider the issue of excluding Saud from the affairs of state. They dutifully issued a fatwa on March 29, 1964, emphasizing two main points: first, “Saud was the country’s sovereign and must be respected and revered by all”; second, “As prime minister, Prince Faisal could freely manage the kingdom’s internal and external affairs without consulting the king.” It was in effect a coup d’état, sanctioned by religious authority.

> 事实证明，费瑟在政治上精明得多。绍德国王卧病在床之际，费瑟逐渐掌控了很多政策职位，在王族中建立了支持者的联盟。最后，费瑟对自己的力量深具信心，就召集宗教导师会议，考虑把绍德国王排除在政治事务之外的问题，并在一九六四年三月二十九日，忠实地发布伊斯兰教令，强调两大重点：第一，「绍德是国家主权所在，必须受到所有人的尊敬和尊崇」。第二，「费瑟王子身为总理，可以自由管理国家内政、外交事务，无须和国王咨商」。

Except that there was no precedent for this decision in the Quran or any relevant scripture; the ulama just recognized where power lay. But even this wasn’t enough for Faisal and his supporters. They decided they needed to get rid of Saud completely. In October 1964 Faisal convened the ulama again, now to find a way to justify deposing King Saud. Action was necessary in order to preserve the unity of the community and of the Islamic state. The ulama had to support the decision of the royal house. Shaykh Muhammad therefore decided to gather together the ulama at his home . . . After a brief discussion of the country’s situation, they concluded that it was necessary to confirm the royal house’s choice.

> 曾经多次接触穆罕默德酋长……希望说服他，发布废除绍德国王的伊斯兰教令……为了维护社会和这个伊斯兰国家的统一，乌理玛确实必须采取这项行动，必须支持王族的决定。穆罕默德酋长因此决定召集所有乌理玛到他家里……他们简短讨论国家大势后，断定必须肯定王室的抉择。

Sheikh Muhammad was the Grand Mufti, the most important ulama in the country. The language here is striking. Though historically al-Wahhab and his descendants probably had a large amount of autonomy from the Sauds, it is clear here that by 1964 the ulama did what they were told by whatever faction of the Sauds was most powerful, and they agreed to depose the king after only a “brief discussion.” The fatwa deposing Saud was duly issued on November 1.

> 穆罕默德酋长是大穆夫提（Grand Mufti），是国家最高宗教权威、最重要的乌理玛。上面这段话的文字令人震惊。虽然在历史上，瓦哈比和他的后裔很可能从绍德家族手中，取得大量的自主权，但到一九六四年时，乌理玛们对于绍德家族中最有权力的一派，显然已经必须听命行事。因此，他们只经过「简短讨论」，就同意罢黜国王。于是罢黜国王的伊斯兰教令正式在十一月一日发布。

Faisal became king. Prior to his reign the relationship between the royal family and the ulama was informal. Faisal started to change this and forged an institutional structure he could control more directly. He announced a series of reforms that included creating “an advisory council consisting of twenty-two members who will be chosen amongst leading jurists and scholars.” They were charged to “issue rulings and give advice on questions of interest to members of the Muslim community.”

> 费瑟登基为王，在他统治前，王族和乌理玛之间，只有非正式关系，费瑟开始改变这一点，形成他比较可以直接控制的制度性结构；他宣布一系列改革，包括创立「由二十二位委员组成的顾问委员会」。「委员要从领导法官和学者中挑选出来，负责发布裁决，并为穆斯林社会成员感兴趣的问题，提供建议。」

It took until 1971 to finally create this Committee of the Grand Ulama, apparently because of the opposition from the Grand Mufti Sheikh Muhammad. After he died in 1969, Faisal abolished the post of Grand Mufti (it was later reintroduced). The Committee of the Grand Ulama has various subcommittees to consider different types of problems and issue fatwas relating to different areas of Islamic law. But they are only able to hear questions that are authorized by the royal cabinet, which can modify their agendas at will. The committee thus became a tool for the domestication of the ulama.

> 这个最高乌理玛委员会一直到一九七一年，才终于创立，原因显然是出于大穆夫提穆罕默德酋长的反对；一九六九年他去世后，费瑟废除了大穆夫提的职位（但后来又恢复设立）。最高乌理玛委员会有多个小组委员会，以便考虑不同形态的问题，发布跟伊斯兰法律不同领域有关的伊斯兰教令；但是，他们只能听取王室内阁授权他们听取的问题，王室内阁又可以随意修改他们的工作目标，因此这个委员会变成了驯化宗教导师的工具。

The Quran and the Prophet’s Sunna (activities and statements) have indicated the need to be ready and take precautions before it is too late. The Sunna refers to the set of practices, norms, and beliefs of the Islamic community drawn from the Quran and the hadiths. ## Intensifying the Cage of Norms · 强化规范牢笼

The story of Saudi Arabia exemplifies the cage of norms, and its intensification. Norms of societies without centralized authority often evolve to constrain behavior in a multitude of ways, both to regulate conflicts and prevent the destabilization of the status quo. These norms take root in the customs, beliefs, and practices of the people, and they become ingrained in religion and religious practice. So they did with Islam, despite Muhammad’s strong drive to build centralized authority in Medina and beyond.

> 沙乌地阿拉伯的故事是规范牢笼强化的例证，社会如果没有中央权威，通常会演变出很多限制行为的方法，目的是为了管制冲突，同时预防现状陷入不稳定状态。这些规范起源于习俗、信念和人民的习惯，而且会根深柢固，融入宗教和宗教做法中。虽然穆罕默德强力推动在麦地那和其他地方，建立中央化的权威，伊斯兰教还是变成这种情形。

With the efforts of the Hanbali legal school and Wahhabism, and their emphasis on tradition and opposition to innovation, these norms powerfully reproduced themselves. Then came the deal with ibn Saud, who exploited the Wahhabis’ zeal for military expansion, and he and his successors bought into the mainstream Wahhabi norms and restrictions as part of this quid pro quo. A small price to pay for a kingdom.

> 在罕百里法学派和瓦哈比派教义的努力下，再加上他们把重点放在传统和反对创新上，造成这些规范自行强力繁殖，然后跟绍德的交易出现。绍德和其继承人利用瓦哈比的军事扩张热情，把瓦哈比派的主流规范和限制，纳为交易中的交换条件，对创建王国而言，这样只是小小的代价。

But in the hands of ibn Saud and Abd al-Aziz, the Wahhabi ideas and restrictions started having an impact well beyond the oasis of al-Diriya. Other would-be despots in the Middle East began using the same ideas and strategies, similarly stiffening the cage of norms to support despotic power. Three interrelated factors explain the popularity of this strategy in the region. One follows from Islam’s institutional structure. As we have noted, in Islam, especially Sunni Islam, there is no church hierarchy, no priests intermediating between an individual and the deity. The ulama, who are learned in religion, can give people guidance on the interpretation of the scriptures and issue fatwas.

> 但是，在绍德和阿不都阿齐兹手里，瓦哈比派的理念和限制所产生的影响，远超出德拉伊耶绿洲，中东其他未来的暴君开始利用相同的理念和策略、类似的僵化规范牢笼，以便支持专制政权。这种策略会在这个地区流行，有三个环环相扣的原因。其中一个原因出自伊斯兰教的制度性结构。就像前面所指出的那样，伊斯兰教里，尤其是逊尼派伊斯兰教中，没有教会结构，没有教士介于个人和神祇之间，精熟宗教的宗教导师可以在解释经文、发布教令上，指导一般人。

On the one hand, this means that anybody with sufficient knowledge of the Quran and the hadiths can play this role and interpret Islam and its teachings (a dynamic whose implications we’ll discuss in a little bit). On the other hand, this organizational structure opened the door for the takeover of the ulama and the supply of fatwas to shore up the Saudi regime. There was nothing like the hierarchy of the Catholic Church to act as a counterweight to Saudi machinations.

> 另一方面，这表示凡是具有足够的《古兰经》和《圣训》知识的人，都可以扮演这种角色，解释伊斯兰教及其教义（我们稍后再会略加探讨）。另一方面，这种组织结构开启了宗教导师接掌大权，提供伊斯兰教令，支撑沙乌地政权的大门。沙乌地的制度里，没有像天主教会等阶级，作为反制绍德家族阴谋诡计的筹码。

Other despotic regimes in the Middle East have done the same. A second factor is related to the fact that, as noted above, the Quran is not a constitutional document, and is open to interpretation on the degree of power vested in rulers. For example, because the Quran and the Constitution of Medina are silent on who should take part in councils and consult with a ruler, there was a lot of leeway for the Sauds to sideline existing councils, the majlis of Bedouin tribes, and to limit their role to local affairs and completely control who gets to sit in the larger Majlis Ash-Shura, or Shura Council.

> 中东其他专制政权都效法沙国的做法。第二个因素跟下述事实有关，就是前面已经指出的：《古兰经》不是宪法文件，例如，归属统治者的权力是多是少，可以随意解释，因为《古兰经》和麦地那宪法对于谁应该参与委员会议、谁可以指正统治者等事情，都没有任何规定，因此绍德家族有很大的回旋空间，可以避开现有的部落会议、贝多因人的部落集会，把人民的角色限制在地方性事务上，彻底控制人民参加比较大型协商会议，或参与咨询理事会的资格。

A third factor is a Hobbesian view of state-society relations that developed and became ingrained during the reign of the despotic Islamic empires. For example, as the famous tenth-century philosopher al-Ghazali noted: The tyranny of a sultan for a hundred years causes less damage than one year’s tyranny exercised by the subjects against another . . . Revolt was justified only against a ruler who clearly went against a command of God or His prophet.

> 第三个因素是专制伊斯兰帝国统治期间，从国家社会关系中发展出来，又融入其中的霍布斯式看法，例如，就像著名的十世纪哲学家安萨里（al-Ghazali）所指出的那样：一百年的苏丹暴政造成的损害，低于臣民彼此相残一年所形成的暴政……只有在统治者显然违反真主或先知命令时，人民起义才算合法。

Thus Warre was much worse than despotism, and as long as a despot stuck to Sharia, he was to be tolerated. So this interpretation of Islam provided an alluring set of principles for a potential despot (and as we’ll see, the history of the Middle East meant that there were plenty of those around). It seemed easy to manipulate, it held no strong inclination toward democracy or any other type of political accountability, and it preached submission, as long as Sharia was followed.

> 战争状态远比专制主义糟糕得多，只要专制暴君坚持伊斯兰教法，人民就可以容忍。因此，这样解释伊斯兰教义，就为潜在的暴君，提供了一套有吸引力的原则（我们接着会看到，中东历史上有很多这种暴君）。这种情形看来容易操纵，却没有追求民主或其他形式政治责任的强烈倾向，而且只要遵守伊斯兰教法，伊斯兰教义就会宣扬臣服之道。

But of course Islam was much more than this, it was a whole system of beliefs about how one should live one’s life in accord with God’s laws. Many of these principles were encoded in the 620s in Arabia and reflected the norms of the region and the period. Groups like the Hanbalis or Wahhabis emphasized a strict traditionalist interpretation of Sharia and reified a very restrictive cage of norms, quite anomalous in the modern world.

> 但是，伊斯兰教义当然远远超过这种情形，是一整套信念系统，是个人应该如何根据真主的律法过日子的体系，其中很多原则是在西元六二○年代，在阿拉伯制定的，反映这个地区当时的规范。罕百里派或瓦哈比派都强调：要严格遵守传统主义的方式，解释伊斯兰教法，还要具体实施限制非常严格的规范牢笼，这种情形在现代世界算是相当反常的事情。

This cage of norms was not simply a powerful tool in the hands of the Sauds. It was also the price they had to pay for their coalition with the Wahhabis. For instance, Abd al-Aziz had founded a commercial court in 1926 with seven magistrates, only one of whom was a religious figure. He was trying to minimally modernize economic relations. In 1955 the ulama persuaded King Saud to abolish the court completely. In 1967 Faisal resuscitated it, creating three commercial courts, one in Riyadh, and one each in the major port cities of Jeddah and Damman. But now one-half of the magistrates had to be ulama.

> 在绍德家族手中，这种规范牢笼不仅仅是强而有力的工具而已，也是他们为了要和瓦哈比派结盟，必须支付的代价。例如，一九二六年，阿不都阿齐兹曾经成立一个商业法院，派任七位法官，其中只有一位是宗教界人士，他的目的是要推动最低程度的经济关系现代化。到一九五五年，宗教导师说服绍德国王，完全废除商业法院。到一九六七年，费瑟国王重新设立三个商业法院，一座设在首都利雅德，另外两座分别设在主要港口城市吉达和达曼，但现在却有一半的法官，必须由宗教导师担任；到一九六九年，更变成三分之二的法官必须由宗教导师担任。

By 1969, two-thirds had to be ulama. Sharia trumped any attempt to introduce modern civil commercial codes. One might have thought that after the defeat of the Ikhwan and the domestication of the ulama, the Saudi royal family would have the upper hand and start relaxing parts of the cage of norms that did not suit their political or economic interests. However, as King Fahd’s fatwa on the defense of the nation illustrates, in a nakedly despotic system without any consultation with society there is often demand for religious authorities to reaffirm Saudi policies and practices. This dynamic got much worse after two events in 1978 and ’79.

> 引进现代商法的所有企图，都遭到伊斯兰教法击败，让人不由自主地想到，沙乌地王族打败兄弟会和驯化宗教导师后，应该已经掌握优势，应该开始放宽一些不符合他们政经利益的规范牢笼才对。然而，就像法德国王发布保卫国家的伊斯兰教令时的情势那样，清楚显示在赤裸裸的专制制度里，政府完全不需要跟社会协商，却经常需要宗教权威，重新肯定沙乌地的政策和做法。经历一九七八年和一九七九年的两件大事后，这种动态严重恶化。

First came the Iranian Islamic Revolution, which threatened the Saudi claim to be the standard-bearer of Islam in the region. Second, more ominously for the Saud family, hundreds of insurgents (nobody knows the exact number) led by a man called Juhayman al-Otaybi stormed the Grand Mosque in Mecca. Al-Otaybi came from a settlement established by King Abd al-Aziz to house the Ikhwan, and his father and many of his relatives were active members of the brotherhood and had been part of the conflict with the king.

> 第一件大事是伊朗爆发革命，威胁沙乌地阿拉伯自命这个区域伊斯兰教领袖的地位。第二件大事对绍德家族更为不祥，就是有几百位叛乱分子（没有人知道确切数字），在一位名叫朱海曼．欧太比（Juhayman al-Otaybi）的人领导下，攻占麦加的禁寺大清真寺。欧太比出身国王阿不都阿齐兹安置兄弟会人士的城镇，父亲和很多亲戚都是兄弟会的活跃分子，曾经参与兄弟会和国王的冲突。

His and the insurgents’ grievances had Wahhabi roots. They argued that the House of Saud had deviated from Muhammad’s teachings by becoming westernized, and called for a return to a more traditional interpretation of Islam. They (rightly) noted that the ulama had been captured by the Sauds and had lost their legitimacy. The reaction by the Saudi ruling family, after the long siege was defeated with the help of Pakistani and French special forces and al-Otaybi and his captured followers were beheaded, was to double down on Wahhabism. The interpretation and the teaching of the religion became more strict, especially as a way of indoctrinating Saudi youth in schools. The cage of norms tightened yet again.

> 他和叛乱分子的不满起源于瓦哈比派的教义，他们认为，绍德家族已经西化，背离了穆罕默德的教导，因此他们呼吁沙国回归伊斯兰教比较传统的诠释。他们（正确的）指出，宗教导师已经受到绍德家族的控制，失去合法性。沙国政府在巴基斯坦和法国特种部队的协助下，击破他们长期占领禁寺的行动，欧太比和被捕的追随者遭到斩首后，统治沙国的绍德家族的反应是：加紧向瓦哈比派教义靠拢，宗教的解释和教导变得更为严格，学校里教育年轻学子的方式尤其如此，规范牢笼进一步收紧。

## The Untouchables of Saudi Arabia · 沙乌地阿拉伯的贱民

In 1955 King Saud announced that there would be public education for girls. But four years later, in the face of resistance from the ulama, Saudi policy changed and put this education under the supervision of the ulama. The Grand Mufti and ulama maintained this control until 2002. It is not only women’s education; every aspect of women’s treatment in Saudi society is constricted by the cage of norms forged by the Saud-Wahhab pact.

> 一九五五年，绍德国王宣布，女童要接受公共教育；但四年后，在宗教导师的反对下，沙乌地改变政策，把女童教育交给宗教导师负责监督，大穆夫提和宗教导师维持这方面的控制，一直到二○○二年才结束。不仅止于女性的教育，沙乌地社会中，妇女待遇中的每一个层面，都受到绍德和瓦哈比派协议打造的规范牢笼限制。

A key enforcer of the cage in Saudi Arabia is the Committee for the Promotion of Virtue and the Prevention of Vice, members of whom are known as the mutaween, typically translated to English as the religious police. The mutaween are charged with compelling people to adhere to Sharia and Islamic norms, such as the strict dress code for women. They take this seriously, very seriously—so seriously that in March 2002 when a fire broke out in a girls’ school in Mecca, they tried to stop girls who were inappropriately dressed—without headscarves and abayas (the black robes required by the Kingdom’s traditional interpretation of Islam)—from leaving the burning building. Fifteen girls died. A rescue worker was quoted as saying:

> 沙乌地牢笼的主要执行者是「劝善惩恶协会」，协会成员的名称通常翻译为「宗教警察」，负责强迫人民遵守伊斯兰教法和伊斯兰规范，包括女性严格的服装规范。宗教警察很重视、非常重视这种事情，重视到二○○二年三月麦加一所女校爆发火灾时，他们居然试图阻止服装不整的女童逃出火场，而所谓的服装不整只是没有戴头巾和穿罩袍（abaya，沙乌地王国伊斯兰教传统解释中规定的黑袍），结果十五位女童烧死在建筑物里。下面引述一位救援人员的话：

Whenever the girls got out through the main gate, these people forced them to return via another. Instead of extending a helping hand for the rescue work, they were using their hands to beat us. You might think that the Committee for the Promotion of Virtue and the Prevention of Vice was an ancient Islamic institution, but it isn’t.

> 女童从大门出来，这些人就强迫她们从另一扇门回去。他们不但没有伸出援手救人，反而伸手打我们。你可能认为，劝善惩恶协会是古代伊斯兰教的制度；其实不是。

It’s true, as we saw, that as the Saudi state expanded, it appointed muhtasibs to capture oases, whose duties included enforcing religious norms and laws, and the roots of this office extend back to the Abbasid Caliphate in the early Middle Ages. But “the Committee” was a new institution, whose progenitor, “the Company” for the Promotion of Virtue and the Prevention of Vice, was created by Abd al-Aziz in 1926 after he had completed his conquest of the Hijaz. The company turned into the committee in 1928. As the Saudi state consolidated its power, the cage of norms tightened. This was partly because of the concessions to the Wahhabi ulama. But it was also because it was quite useful for shoring up despotism. The cage helped keep people under control even as the economy and life modernized; hence the creation of the committee.

> 就我们所知，实际情形是随着沙乌地国家机器扩张，政府任命检查官，负责控制各个绿洲；检查官的职责包括执行宗教规范和法律，这个职位的起源可以回溯到中世纪的阿拔斯王朝（Abbasid Caliphate）。但是，这个协会是新设的机构，协会的前身劝善惩恶「局」是阿不都阿齐兹在一九二六年、攻占整个汉志地区后设立的机关；一九二八年，「局」变成协会。随着沙乌地国家机器巩固大权，规范牢笼跟着紧缩，原因之一是对瓦哈比派的宗教导师让步，但也是为了这种制度相当有助于支撑专制主义；这种牢笼在经济和生活现代化之际，有助于控制人民，因此才会创设这个协会。

The brunt of the cage of norms in Saudi Arabia is borne by women. In 2014 a female student at King Saud University in Riyadh died because male paramedics were not permitted to treat her. Men who are not close relatives are not allowed to touch women, not even to politely shake hands, let alone give essential medical care. In Saudi Arabia, women are the untouchables.

> 沙乌地阿拉伯规范牢笼首当其冲的人是妇女。二○一四年，利雅德绍德国王大学有一位女学生死亡，死因是男性急救医护人员不准处理她的病痛。非近亲男性不准碰触女性，连礼貌客气地握手都不行，更不用说进行基本的医护了。女性在沙国是贱不可触的人。

The dress code, the no-touching stipulation, and the web of regulations placing women under the control of men come from particular interpretations of the Quran. Surah 4, verse 34 says that “men are the protectors and maintainers of women, because God has given the one more [strength] than the other, and because they support them from their means.” This is interpreted in Saudi Arabia to mean that women are under the control of men, like children, and this reading is thought to be consistent with the one clause, number 41, of Muhammad’s Constitution of Medina, formulated in 622, that mentions women and says, “A woman shall only be given protection with the consent of her family.”

> 服装规定、不准碰触规定、把女性放在男性控制之下的管制网，都起源于对《古兰经》的特定解释。《古兰经》第四章第三十四节说：「男人是维护妇女的，因为真主使他们［的力量］比她们更优越，又因为他们所费的财产。」这句话在沙乌地阿拉伯的解释是女性像儿童一样，要由男性控制。而且人们认为，这种解读符合西元六二二年、穆罕默德所制定麦地那宪章中第四十一条所说：「只有在得到女性的家人同意后，才襄助属于受害者。」

So women are under the control of their family (read “men”). In Saudi Arabia the dominance of men over women is institutionalized via the guardianship system. Every woman must have a male guardian from whom she needs to get permission to do many things, like travel. The guardian can be her father, her husband, or even her son.

> 因此，女性要受他们的家人（意思是「男人」）控制。沙乌地阿拉伯男性对女性的宰割，已经透过监护人制度变成制度化，每一位女性都必须有一个男性监护人，女性想做的很多事情──例如出门──都必须得到男性监护人的允准。监护人可能是她的父亲、丈夫，甚至可能是她的儿子。

If a woman travels without a guardian, she must carry a yellow card that documents the number of trips and for how many days her guardian has approved travel for. Permission is also needed to open a bank account, rent an apartment, start a business, or apply for a passport. The government’s electronic portal stipulates that a male guardian has to fill out the application for a woman’s passport. A woman even needs a man’s permission to get out of prison when her sentence is over!

> 如果女性在无人监护的情况下出门旅行，就必须带着一张黄牌，记载她的旅行次数和监护人批准的旅行天数。要开立银行帐户、租公寓、创业或申请护照，也需要监护人准许。政府的电子入口网站规定，男性监护人必须填写女性护照的申请表格，女性刑期届满时，甚至必须得到一位男性的允准，才能出狱！

Until recently, permission was required to get a job too. Though this has changed, Saudi law requires segregated workplaces with separate areas for men and women which significantly disincentivizes hiring women. If a woman wants to study abroad, she must be accompanied by a male relative. Women cannot eat at a restaurant that doesn’t have a separate walled off “family” section and a separate entrance for women. A fatwa from the Grand Council states that “a woman should not leave her house, except with her husband’s permission.”

> 一直到最近，女性要得到工作，也需要男性的许可，虽然这一点已经改变，沙乌地的法律仍然规定工作场所必须隔离，男女分区工作，这样会严重妨碍雇用女性的意愿。如果女性想出国留学，必须由男性亲戚陪伴；如果餐厅没有设立用墙壁隔开的「家人区」和独立的女性入口，女性不得进入用餐。最高乌理玛委员的一份伊斯兰教令规定，「除非获丈夫准许，否则女性不得离家」。

Of course, there is no equality before the law. In legal cases the testimony of women is worth one half of men’s. Similarly, under Sharia, women inherit half the amount that men inherit. Women have trouble filing a case or being heard in court without a legal guardian intervening.

> 法律之前男女当然没有平等。女性的证词在法律案件中的价值，只有男性证词的一半。同样地，根据伊斯兰教法，女性可以继承的金额只有男性的一半。如果没有合法的监护人介入，女性连要提出告诉或到庭陈述都会碰到困难。

Courts, presided over by male judges, generally refuse to accept the testimony of a woman as a witness in criminal cases. Two women told Human Rights Watch researchers that judges had refused to allow them to speak in the courtroom because they deemed their voices to be “shameful.” Judges allowed their guardian to speak on their behalf. But what happens when women are abused by their guardians or husbands?

> 由男性主持的法院在刑事案件中，通常会拒绝女性证人的证词。两位妇女告诉「人权观察组织」说，法官拒绝让她们在庭上说话，因为法官认为她们的声音「可耻」，却同意她们的监护人代表她们说话。但是，如果妇女遭到监护人或丈夫凌虐时，该怎么办？

One ranking of gender parity by the World Economic Forum puts Saudi Arabia 141 out of 149 countries (the United Arab Emirates, despite its gender equality awards we saw in the Preface, ranks only a little above Saudi Arabia, 121). This ranking combines many things; one is labor force participation, which stands at just 22 percent in Saudi Arabia compared to 56 percent in the United States.

> 世界经济论坛在性别平等排名中，在一百四十九个国家里，把沙乌地阿拉伯排在第一百四十一名（序言中提到的阿拉伯联合大公国虽然颁发性别平等奖，排名却只比沙乌地阿拉伯略高，排名第一百二十一名）。这项排名结合很多因素，其中一项是劳动参与率，沙乌地阿拉伯的劳动参与率为百分之二十二，相形之下，美国的劳动参与率为百分之五十六。

The guardianship system and systematic discrimination against women have been consistently upheld by religious authorities. In the 1990s when the Grand Ulama was asked to make a ruling about the appropriateness of a woman’s delaying marriage to finish her university education, it issued a fatwa decreeing that for a woman to progress through university education, which is something we have no need for, is an issue that needs examination. What I see [to be correct] is that if a woman finishes elementary school and is able to read and write, and so she is able to benefit by reading the Book of God, its commentaries, and Prophetic hadith, that is sufficient for her.

> 监护人制度和对女性有系统地歧视，一直得到宗教主管机关的支持。一九九○年代内，最高乌理玛应邀对女性为了完成大学教育、因而延后结婚的适当性，做出裁定，并发布伊斯兰教令，规定：女性透过大学教育追求进步，是我们不需要的东西，却是我们需要检视的问题。我认为〔正确的是〕如果女性完成国小教育，能够看书写字，能够从阅读「真主之书」、其注解和先知的《圣训》中受益，对她而言就够了。

When asked about women’s employment, it ruled: God Almighty . . . commended women to remain in their homes. Their presence in the public is the main contributing factor to the spread of fitna [strife]. Yes, the Shari’ah permits women to leave their home only when necessary, provided that they wear hijab and avoid all suspicious situations. However, the general rule is that they should remain at home.

> 问到女性就业问题时，最高乌理玛的裁定是：全能的真主……称赞女性留在家里，她们出现在公共场合是冲突的主要原因。不错，伊斯兰教法只准许女性在有必要时，可以离开家，但是必须戴着头巾，避免所有可疑状况。然而，一般的规定是她们应该留在家里。

And definitely no touching. No leading either. Another fatwa by the same body stated that women could not fill positions of leadership over men due to their “deficient reasoning and rationality, in addition to their passion that prevails over their thinking.” The typical defense of these rules by officials of the Saudi government is that Saudi Arabia is a conservative society and the rules reflect the way the people think.

> 而且绝对不能碰触，也不能领导。同一个机构发布的另一项伊斯兰教令表示，女性不能担任领导男性的职位，因为她们「除了热情压倒思维外，还缺少推理能力和理性」。沙乌地政府官员为这些规则辩护时，常说沙乌地的社会保守，这种统治反映人民的思考方式。

But this claim receives no support from the available evidence. A recent study by Leonardo Bursztyn, Alessandra González, and David Yanagizawa-Drott asked men in Riyadh whether or not they agreed with the simple statement “In my opinion, women should be allowed to work outside of the home.” Eighty-seven percent agreed with this statement. But reaffirming the cage of norms, many men still were not comfortable for their wives to be working outside the home because of how they thought others would react. In particular, they reasoned that others would be less favorable toward outside work by women and only 63 percent of them thought that other men from their neighborhood would agree with the same statement.

> 但是，既有的证据毫不支持这种说法，李奥纳多．柏之庭（Leonardo Bursztyn）、亚历山德拉．龚萨雷斯（Alessandra González）和大卫．柳泽．杜洛（David Yanagizawa-Drott）在最近的一项研究中，询问利雅德的男性，是否同意下述简单的陈述：「我认为应该容许女性走出家门工作。」结果百分之八十七的男性同意这句话。但是，重申规范牢笼时，很多男性对自己的妻子外出工作这件事，仍然觉得不安，因为他们会想到别人对这种事情会有什么反应；尤其是他们会认为，别人应该会比较不赞成女性出门工作；而且只有百分之六十三的男性认为，他们邻居中的男性会同意同样的陈述。

So in the cage of norms, everybody is afraid of what others would say about the most basic empowerment of women. Women’s work becomes stigmatized and the cage of norms is reinforced. Though scholars can cite the Quran and hadiths in support of the control of women by men, everything is subject to political imperatives. As recently as 1996 the Grand Ulama issued a fatwa that categorically stated that women were not allowed to drive according to Sharia law.

> 因此，在这种规范牢笼中，每一个人都害怕别人对赋予女性最基本权利的事情，会有什么不同看法；女性出门工作遭到污名化，规范牢笼则遭到强化。学者虽然可以引述《古兰经》和《圣训》，来支持男性控制女性这件事，但一切都要遵照政治上的要求办理。近在一九九六年，最高乌理玛才发布伊斯兰教令，明确指出根据伊斯兰教法，女性不准开车。

There is no doubt that such [driving] is not allowed. Women driving lead to many evils and negative consequences. Included among these is her mixing with men without her being on her guard. It also leads to the evil sins due to which such an action is forbidden. There were obviously no cars during Muhammad’s lifetime. A fatwa like this is no more than a speculative interpretation of what basic Islamic principles would imply for women driving, had there been cars in the 620s.

> 毫无疑问的是，这种事情（驾驶）不获允许，女性开车会导致很多罪恶和不利影响，包括女性在没有警觉的情况下，和男性混在一起。这件事也会造成邪恶的罪行，因此必须禁止这种行为。穆罕默德活着时，显然没有汽车，像这样的伊斯兰教令顶多是假设西元六二○年代已经有汽车，揣测伊斯兰基本原则对女性驾车的意义应该会有什么看法。

But the fact that women were not allowed to drive in Saudi Arabia became a more and more embarrassing facet of the regime, remorselessly referred to in international media. In 2017 it was announced that this would be changed under the reform agenda of Crown Prince Mohammad bin Salman. But hang on, in 1996 the Grand Council had announced that it was definitely against Sharia to allow women to drive. No problem; the Sauds just got them to issue a new fatwa declaring that it was perfectly Islamic after all.

> 但是，沙乌地阿拉伯不准女性开车的事实，已经变成让这个政权愈来愈尴尬、国际媒体又毫不留情提起的一个面向。二○一七年，沙国宣布，根据王储沙尔曼（Mohammad bin Salman）的改革计划，这件事会改变。但是，请等一等，一九九六年时，沙乌地阿拉伯的最高理事会不是宣布过：容许女性驾车绝对违反伊斯兰教法吗？没有问题的啦，沙乌地人只要促请他们，发布新的伊斯兰教令，宣布这样完全符合伊斯兰教，不就一切都解决了。

## Nebuchadnezzar Rides Again · 巴比伦国王尼布甲尼撒再度奋起

Even if Saudi Arabia is the poster child for the intensification of the cage of norms, other despotic regimes in the region have followed the same playbook. Take the government of Saddam Hussein in Iraq. Iraq emerged as a British mandate out of the same shady plan that Emir Hussein of Mecca felt had cheated the Arabs of their true rewards for fighting the Ottoman Empire. To sweeten the pill, the British made one of Hussein’s sons, Faisal, king of Iraq. Iraq was a colonial creation, an amalgamation of three Ottoman provinces, Mosul, Baghdad, and Basra.

> 沙乌地阿拉伯是收紧规范牢笼的典型代表，但中东其他专制政体也遵循同样的剧本运作。看看已故伊拉克前总统海珊的政府。伊拉克是英国人在某一个计划中创造出来的领土，当年阿拉伯人跟奥图曼帝国作战时卓有贡献，英国为了酬谢阿拉伯人，才订出这个计划；麦加王公胡赛因（Emir Hussein）却觉得这个计划可疑，欺骗了阿拉伯人。英国人为了增加计划的吸引力，扶植胡赛因的儿子费瑟，出任伊拉克国王。伊拉克是英国人创造的殖民地，是由奥图曼帝国的摩苏尔、巴格达和巴斯拉三个省份合并而成。

Putting Faisal in charge of it was a bizarre piece of colonial politics; when he was crowned, the band played the British national anthem, “God Save the King,” because Iraq didn’t have its own yet. The monarchy didn’t last. Iraq became independent in 1931 and the first coup happened in 1936, followed by two decades of intense political instability until members of the Free Officers, a group led by Brigadier Abd al-Karim Qasim, finally overthrew the monarchy in 1958. In the first hours of the coup, Qasim’s men summarily executed the king and his family. Qasim imposed state control over the ulama and tried to secularize the state.

> 扶植费瑟统治是怪异的殖民政治手法，费瑟加冕时，乐队演奏的是英国国歌《天佑吾王》，因为伊拉克还没有自己的国歌。伊拉克在一九三一年独立，一九三六年发生第一次政变，随后的二十年里，政治极为动荡，一直到一九五八年，由阿卜杜勒．卡塞姆（Abd al-Karim Qasim）准将领导的自由军官团，终于推翻伊拉克王室为止。卡塞姆的手下在政变后的最初几小时内，迅速处决国王及其家人，卡塞姆实施以国家控制乌理玛的政策，并且设法让国家世俗化。

But his attempts were short lived. He was himself murdered in 1963 by the military putschists who sympathized with the Baath Party. Founded in 1947 in Syria, the Baath Party had an ideology rooted in pan-Arabism, anticolonialism, and socialism. Even though the Baathists were secular, they had no problem using Islam to subjugate society and intensify the cage of norms. This process started in 1968, when the Baathists definitively took control in another coup, and escalated after 1979 when Saddam Hussein grabbed personal power.

> 但是，他的尝试为时相当短暂，他自己在一九六三年，遭到同情复兴党的军事盲动分子杀害。复兴党一九四七年在叙利亚成立，秉持泛阿拉伯主义、反殖民主义和社会主义意识形态。复兴党徒虽然属于世俗派，却毫不迟疑地利用伊斯兰教，来压制社会、强化规范牢笼。这种过程始于一九六八年，当时复兴党徒明确控制了另一场政变；一九七九年，海珊个人掌握权利后，这种过程开始加速。

Saddam was not a military man, but had ruthlessly worked his way up through the party. He took his chance when it came. To consolidate his hold on power, Saddam held family members of one-third of the members of the Revolutionary Command Council hostage. Then he really got going. He orchestrated and filmed a confession from Muhyi Abdel-Hussein, the secretary of the Revolutionary Command Council (Saddam was vice chairman), which was shown to party members from all over the country. According to a version recorded by a historian:

> 海珊不是军人，却残忍无情，善于掌握机会，因此能够在党内扶摇直上。为了巩固自己的权力，海珊把革命指导委员会三分之一成员的家人变成人质，就此开始真正的发迹。这时他担任复兴党副主席，（在总统兼党主席辞职后，出任党主席），却策画并拍摄革命指导委员会秘书长阿布达尔—胡塞因（Muhyi Abdel- Hussein）的自白，影片还对全国的复兴党员播放。根据一位历史学家的记载：

A grief-stricken Saddam addressed the meeting with tears running down his cheeks. He filled in the gaps in [Abdel-Hussein’s] testimony and dramatically fingered his former colleagues. Guards dragged people out of the proceedings and then Saddam called upon the country’s top ministers and party leaders to themselves form the actual firing squads. It seems that some five hundred high-ranking Baathists were executed by August 1, 1979. Saddam was now in full control. He made the ulama salaried employees of the state and subservient to him. He constructed an elaborate ideology to legitimize his rule, claiming a line of descent from the great Babylonian king Nebuchadnezzar from the fifth century BCE.

> 哀伤满面的海珊声泪俱下，在〔党中央〕会议上对众人讲话，他填补了〔阿布达尔—胡塞因〕供词中的空白，并戏剧化地指控过去的众多同事。警卫把人从司法程序中拖出来，然后海珊邀请伊拉克的最高级部会首长和党领袖，自行组成实际行刑队。到一九七九年八月一日，似乎大约有五百位高阶复兴党徒遭到处决。海珊现在完全掌控伊拉克，他把乌理玛变成领取国家薪水的公务员，命令他们屈从他的命令。他建构了一套详尽的意识形态，宣称自己继承西元前五世纪伟大的巴比伦国王尼布甲尼撒（Nebuchadnezzar）的世系，好让自己的统治合法化。

Nebuchadnezzar was not a Muslim, of course, but as the rule of Saddam became more and more precarious and less and less legitimate, he tried to shore up his power not just by extensive appeals to Islam but by any means he could think of. The year after taking power Saddam invaded Iran, launching the disastrous Iran-Iraq War. He had hoped to exploit the weakness of the Iranian regime after the overthrow of the shah in 1979 and grab their oilfields. Instead, there was a bloody stalemate that lasted eight years.

> 尼布甲尼撒当然不是穆斯林，但随着海珊的统治愈来愈危殆、愈来愈不合法，他不只极力借重伊斯兰教，还用尽他所能想到的所有方法，来支撑自己的政权。海珊夺权后的隔年，就挥军入侵伊朗，发动惨烈的两伊战争。他原本希望利用伊朗国王巴勒维一九七九年遭到推翻后的弱势，夺取伊朗的油田，结果却造成长达八年之久的血腥僵局。

By 1982 Saddam had completely cast aside his secularist roots. He was talking of jihad and finishing his speeches with religious phrases such as “God will defend and protect you and lead you on the road to victory.” In a 1984 celebration of the Prophet’s birthday, Saddam was lauded along with “our historical, ingenious, Jihad-launching leadership, fighting for the future of our Iraqi people and elevating our monotheistic Islamic religion, guided by the Message of Eternal Islam.” By the time he invaded Kuwait six years later, Saddam was claiming “it was God that showed us [read: me] the path . . . God has blessed us.”

> 到一九八二年，海珊已经完全抛弃自己的世俗根源，谈论圣战，演说结束时，总是以宗教性的词句，如「真主会捍卫和保护你们，领导你们走上胜利之路」作为结语。他在一九八四年庆祝先知穆罕默德诞辰的庆典上，获得的赞语是：「在永恒的伊斯兰教义领导下，发动圣战，为我们伊拉克人民的未来、提升伊斯兰一神教地位而奋战的天才横溢历史性领袖。」六年后，海珊侵入科威特时，宣称：「真主对我们显示〔对我读出〕真道……真主保佑我们。」

His picture kneeling down for prayer began adorning public spaces, as shown in our photography section. After the complete defeat at the hands of the (mostly) U.S. military in Operation Desert Storm in January 1991, Saddam intensified his appeals to Islam. He initiated a massive program of Islamic education, doubling or even tripling the amount of time spent studying the Quran and the hadiths in school. Adults, even cabinet ministers, were forced to take classes on the Quran, and he started the Saddam Center for the Reading of the Quran and the Saddam University for Islamic Studies.

> 他跪着祈祷的相片如我们在插页照片集中所示，开始在公共空间出现。一九九一年一月，海珊在沙漠风暴行动中，遭到以美军为主的军队击溃后，加强依赖伊斯兰教，启动庞大的伊斯兰教育方案，把学校中研习《古兰经》和《圣训》的时间加倍，甚至增加三倍；连包括内阁部会首长在内的成人，都被迫上《古兰经》的课程，而且他还创设了海珊研读《古兰经》中心和海珊伊斯兰研究大学。

Teachers’ knowledge of the scriptures was tested and prisoners could have their sentences reduced if they managed to successfully memorize key passages of religious texts. In 1992 Saddam insisted that the words Allahu Akbar (God is Great) be added to the Iraqi flag and he announced in public that the flag had become the banner of jihad and faith . . . against the infidel horde.

> 教师对经文的知识要经过测验，囚犯如果能够记住宗教经文的重要段落，刑罚可以减轻。到一九九二年，海珊坚持要在伊拉克国旗上，加上「真主至大」（Allahu Akbar）的文字，还公开宣布伊拉克国旗已经变成：圣战和信仰……对抗异教徒众的大旗。

Saddam was now styling himself “the Commander of the Congregation of the Believers” and burnishing his religious bona fides by doubling down on the cage of norms. In 1994, Decree No. 59 brought the first of a host of Sharia-inspired laws that would transform the Iraqi legal code. For robbery and car theft, punishment would be amputation of the hand at the wrist. For a second offense, the left foot would be amputated at the ankle. Soon unauthorized money changers got the same punishments, as did “profiteering bankers.” These measures had been preceded in 1990 by laws that introduced “tribal customs” into the penal codes, for instance making it legal for relatives of an adulterous woman to kill her.

> 海珊现在自称是「信众的指挥官」，还借着加倍强化规范牢笼，擦亮自己的宗教信仰诚意。一九九四年，第五十九号法令推出受伊斯兰教法启发、必然会改变伊拉克法典的第一套法律，其中，对抢劫和偷车的惩罚是从手腕处砍断手掌，再犯的话，左脚要从脚踝处砍断。很快地，黑市汇兑商人和「牟取暴利的银行家」，也变成要遭到类似的处罚。不过，伊拉克采取这些措施前，已经在一九九○年，把「部落习俗」纳入刑法的法条中，把亲属杀害通奸妇女的罪行视为合法，就是其中的一个例子。

Saddam continued to intensify the cage of norms, decreeing measures similar to Saudi Arabia’s male guardianship system. Women were not allowed to travel abroad unless they were in the company of a male relative. He announced that women should give up employment and stay at home, but did not enforce this decree, apparently deeming that it would be too unpopular. It was left to the new U.S.-installed government in 2003 to fire all women judges in Iraq on the basis that their employment was un-Islamic.

> 海珊继续强化规范牢笼，发布类似沙乌地阿拉伯的男性监护制度，包括女性没有男性亲属陪同、不得到国外旅行的规定。他还宣布女性应该放弃工作、留在家里的命令，却显然因为这道命令太不得民心，因而没有执行。要等到二○○三年美国扶植的政府成立后，才把伊拉克的所有女性法官解除职务，理由是雇用她们违反伊斯兰教义。

The Saudi strategy of marrying unchecked despotism with an intense (and intensifying) cage of norms was attractive not just for Saddam but for many other regimes in the Middle East. The region was fertile ground for this unholy alliance for several reasons. The first enticement was the history of despotic rule. The Islamic empires evolved into a rigidly despotic direction for the reasons Ibn Khaldun identified. This despotic evolution was continued and if anything aggravated by Ottoman rule.

> 沙乌地阿拉伯把不受约束的专制主义，跟严密（和不断强化）的规范牢笼合而为一的策略，不但吸引海珊，而且吸引中东许多政权。中东地区是这种不神圣结合的温床，原因有很多，第一个诱惑是专制统治的历史，多个伊斯兰帝国都因为赫勒敦所指出的原因，变成严苛的专制政权，在奥图曼帝国的统治下，这种专制体系继续演变，甚至更形加强。

There were few pathways for society to have a say in political decision making or any type of accountability for rulers save for rebellion. After World War I, European colonial powers replaced the Ottomans. Aspirations for self-governance and independence that had built up over the last several decades were quashed and a patchwork of artificial client states was soon created. They had little in common with existing political structures and boundaries, except for their penchant for despotism.

> 社会除了反叛外，没有什么其他方式，能够对政治决策或统治者担负任何责任的议题，表达任何意见。一次大战后，欧洲殖民政权取代了奥图曼帝国，过去几十年发展出来的自治和独立愿望遭到镇压，人为制造的拼凑附庸国家迅速出现。这些附庸国除了偏爱专制主义外，和现有的政治结构与疆界几乎毫无共通之处。

Then came oil, which would be the biggest export for the region, even if quite unequally distributed across the countries of the region. Natural resources that create great rewards for those who control political power tend to give a boost to despotism, and the Middle East’s recent history hasn’t been an exception. Then came the founding of the state of Israel and the incessant Arab-Israeli conflicts that followed. The scene was set for the exploitation of religion and the cage of norms to create and re-create despotism throughout the region.

> 随后，石油出现，虽然石油蕴藏在中东的分布极不平均，却变成中东地区最大的出口产品。天然资源为掌控政权的人带来极大报酬，通常也会大为提振专制主义，因此中东最近的历史不是例外。然后以色列建国，造成以阿之间无止境的冲突，利用宗教和规范牢笼，为整个中东地区创造和再造专制主义，铺下了坦途。

## The Seeds of 9/11 · 「九一一」恐怖攻击的种子

We’ve seen that it is not a coincidence that despotic states in the Middle East are associated with an intensified cages of norms. There’s no doubt that Saudi Arabia is the most extreme case; there is no other Muslim country that features such aggressive separation of men and women in the workplace, for example. But all of these states have played the same game of exploiting the decentralized structure of Islam to bolster their political authority. In Egypt a fatwa was issued in 1962 by Al-Azhar University, the most authoritative voice of Sunni Islam, declaring peace with Israel to be anti-Islamic.

> 我们已经看到，中东专制国家和强化规范牢笼结合的情况并非巧合，沙乌地阿拉伯无疑是其中最极端的例子。例如，没有那个穆斯林国家在职场上，这么严格地隔离男女，但所有这些国家都上演相同的戏码，利用伊斯兰的分权结构，提升自己的政治权威。一九六二年，伊斯兰教逊尼派最权威的代言人埃及艾兹哈尔大学（Al-Azhar University）发布一份伊斯兰教令，宣布和以色列签订的和平协议违反伊斯兰教义。

Yet when President Anwar Sadat signed the Camp David Accords with Israel’s prime minister Menachem Begin in 1979, the sheikh of Al-Azhar issued a fatwa citing the Quran and the treaties that Muhammad had made to show that peace with Israel was indeed consistent with Islamic principles. When the Egyptian military wanted to make peace with Israel, they could count on the ulama to come to the rescue. The economist Jean-Philippe Platteau has pointed out one more implication of this symbiotic relationship between the ulama, or at least part of it, and Middle Eastern despotic states.

> 但是，到一九七九年，埃及总统沙达特和以色列总理比金签署大卫营协定后，艾兹哈尔的领袖发布伊斯兰教令，引述《古兰经》和穆罕默德签订的条约，说明以埃和约确实符合伊斯兰教的原则。埃及军方希望跟以色列谈和时，可以依靠宗教导师乌理玛出面救援。经济学家约翰．菲立普．卜拉图（Jean-Philippe Platteau）指出，全部或部分乌理玛和中东专制国家之间的共生关系中，具有另一种意义。

Recall that when al-Wahhab became an ulama nobody appointed him, he just started teaching and became recognized by the people as a religious authority and learned man. He made rulings, and people started to listen. So while the Saudi state can have their Great Council of Ulama and tell them what fatwas to issue, they can’t really prevent someone else from setting himself up as an ulama and issuing contradictory fatwas. Much of bin Laden’s fatwa is an anti-American tirade, but he was also highlighting his view of the real problem in Saudi Arabia, “the ruling regime,” and was calling for a jihad against it. The political strategy of Middle Eastern states not only extinguishes liberty by tightening the cage of norms. It also sows the seeds of violence, instability, and terrorism. Every society’s cage restricts freedoms by regulating both behavior and discourse—what people talk about and how they talk about it.

> 宾拉登的伊斯兰教令大致上是激烈的反美抨击，却也突显他对沙乌地阿拉伯「执政政权」真正问题的看法，还呼吁对沙国发动圣战。中东国家不但借着收紧规范牢笼来扼杀自由，还种下暴力、不稳定和恐怖主义的种子。每一个社会的牢笼都借着管制行为和言论──人民的谈话和谈话内容，来限制自由。

The Middle Eastern cage of norms makes it very difficult to develop a discourse that criticizes the despot, because the despot claims to represent religion. Criticize him and you are criticizing Islam. This generates a natural tendency to couch and develop criticisms by pointing out that the despot is not sufficiently religious and you are more devoted to the faith. In Platteau’s words: When despots use religion to legitimize themselves in a highly contested environment they may provoke a counter-move in the form of religious backlash in which the ruler and his opponents compete to demonstrate their superior fidelity to the faith.

> 中东的规范牢笼使人们很难发出批评暴君的论述，因为暴君宣称自己代表宗教，批评他就是批评伊斯兰教。这样自然产生一种倾向，就是借着指出暴君的宗教信仰不虔诚、你对信仰比他还虔诚的批评，表达和发展你的抨击。用卜拉图的话来说：暴君在高度竞争的环境中，用宗教让自己合法化时，可能引发以宗教形式表现的反动，造成统治者和对手竞相表现自己对信仰的超级忠诚。

This is exactly what bin Laden was doing. His fatwa went on to note the “Suspension of the Islamic Sharia law and exchanging it with statutory laws and embarking upon bloody confrontation with devoted scholars and righteous youths.” The Sauds may have captured most of the ulama, but there remained the “devoted scholars,” like bin Laden. Indeed, though the Sauds tried, bin Laden couldn’t be captured. He forged a social movement and a radical, violent agenda, not just around his hatred of the West and the United States, but also around his hatred and contempt for the Sauds and “the oppressive and illegitimate behavior and measures of the ruling regime.”

> 宾拉登就是这样做，他的伊斯兰教令继续指出：「废止伊斯兰教法，用成文法取代，而且要用虔诚的学者和正义的青年，发动血腥的对抗。」绍德家族可能掌握了大部分的乌理玛，像宾拉登之流的「虔诚学者」仍然继续存在。确实如此，绍德家族虽然尝试过，却抓不到宾拉登。宾拉登不仅以他对西方和美国的仇恨为主轴，也以对绍德家族和「执政政权不合法的镇压行为和措施」的仇恨和轻视为主轴，发动一场社会运动，推动一项激烈、暴力的大业。

The fact that the strategy of manipulating the ulama for the goals of the Despotic Leviathan has been used most intensively and successfully in Saudi Arabia helps explain how Osama bin Laden was forged in the Saudi crucible and why fifteen of the nineteen hijackers who crashed planes into buildings in the United States on September 11, 2001, were Saudi citizens. The concoction of Despotic Leviathans and the institutional structure of Islam doesn’t just intensify the cage of norms, it creates terrorism, violence, and instability.
