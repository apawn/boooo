# Bibliographic Essay · 参考文献

The main arguments in this book are related to many areas of research, and we cannot do justice to all these ideas in this brief essay. We thus focus on some of the most related research here and refer the reader to Acemoglu and Robinson (2016, 2019) for a discussion of the broader literature and our connections to and differences from it. Most centrally, we build on our previous work on the importance of balance between state and society in Acemoglu (2005) and Acemoglu and Robinson (2016, 2017). We also build on a large literature on the role of institutions (Acemoglu, Johnson, and Robinson, 2001, 2002, 2005a, 2005b; Acemoglu, Gallego, and Robinson, 2014; North, Wallis, and Weingast, 2011; Besley and Pearson, 2011; Acemoglu and Robinson, 2012).

> 本书的论点和很多研究领域有关，我们在这篇短文中无法适当处理所有这些理念，因此我们把重点放在一些最相关的研究上，建议读者参考 Acemoglu and Robinson（2016, 2019），得知比较广泛的文献探讨，以及我们跟这些文献的关系和差异。最重要的是，我们的前一本拙作植基于Acemoglu（2005）和Acemoglu and Robinson（2016, 2017）所申论的国家和社会之间平衡的重要性。我们探讨制度的角色时，也根据Acemoglu, Johnson and Robinson（2001, 2002, 2005a, 2005b）, Acemoglu, Gallego, and Robinson（2014）, North, Wallis, and Weingast（2011）, Besley and Pearson（2011）, Acemoglu and Robinson（2012）等大量文献。

Our book is centrally about the development of state capacity, which has been studied by many social scientists. We sharply differ from the modal emphasis in this literature, which is on the importance of the state setting up its control over society and over violence as a precursor to the development of democratic institutions, civil society, and political rights (e.g., Huntington, 1968; Tilly, 1992; Fukuyama, 2011, 2014; and also Besley and Persson, 2011). Rather, we argue and document that society’s mobilization and contestation of power is critical for the development of democratic and participatory institutions and in fact a capable state. This perspective in turn builds on Acemoglu and Robinson (2000, 2006) as well as Therborn (1977) and Rueschemeyer, Stephens, and Stephens (1992).

> 本书的重点放在国家能力的发展上，很多社会科学家研究过这一点，我们的观点和这些文献中的典型重点截然不同。这些文献强调发展民主制度、公民社会和政治权利前，国家必须先控制社会和暴力〔例如，亨廷顿（Huntington, 1968）、堤利（Tilly, 1992）、法兰西斯．福山（2011, 2014）、Besley and Persso（2011）〕。我们反而主张与证明要发展民主与参与式制度、建立相当能干的国家机器，社会的动员和竞争权力至为重要。这种观点依次植基于Acemoglu and Robinson（2000, 2006）, Therborn（1977）, Rueschemeyer, Stephens, and Stephens（1992）。

But our argument here is much broader because it brings in the organization of society and associations (inspired by Tocqueville, 2002, and Dahl, 1970); because it stresses the role of norms in this power struggle (partly borrowing from the anthropological literature, such as Bohannan, 1958, as well as Scott, 2010); because, inspired by Migdal (1988, 2001), it recognizes that “weak states” will result when these norms are too strong and prevent the emergence of political hierarchy and autonomous state institutions; and because it also incorporates how the agenda over which political contestation takes place changes, potentially strengthening society, as state institutions develop (as proposed by Tilly, 1995, and Acemoglu, Robinson, and Torvik, 2016). Finally, our overall approach is also inspired by several important academic works. These include: Mann’s (1986) definition of the despotic power of the state (similar to ours as the state not being accountable to society); Moore’s (1966) approach to linking the origins of different political regimes and types of state-society relations to historical economic and political circumstances, such as presence or absence of labor coercion, and the resulting social coalition; North and Thomas’s (1973) thesis about the “rise of the West”; Engerman and Sokoloff’s (2011) work on the historical roots of comparative development in the Americas; Pincus’s (2011) analysis of the Glorious Revolution; Bates’s (1981) theory of the comparative political economy in Africa; Flannery and Marcus’s (2014) synthesis of archaeological and ethnographic evidence and their account of the emergence of complex society; and Brenner’s (1976) emphasis on the role of power relations between landowners and peasants in the transition from feudalism to capitalism.

> 但是，本书论证涵盖的范围广大得多，因为我们受到Tocqueville（2002）和Dahl（1970）的启发，纳入集会结社因素，又因为本书强调在这种权力斗争中规范所扮演的角色〔参考Bohannan（1958）和Scott（2010）的部分人类学大作〕；还因为受到Migdal（1988, 2001）的影响，认为规范太有力，以至于妨碍政治阶级和自主性国家制度出现时，会形成「弱势的国家」；而且把国家制度发展时，和政治竞争有关的议程如何出现会改变社会、而且可能强化社会的因素纳入考虑〔如同Tilly（1995）和Acemoglu, Robinson, and Torvik（2016）所说〕。最后，我们的整体处理方法也受多篇重要学术著作启发，包括Mann（1986）针对国家专制权力的定义（类似我们所说国家机器不对社会负责的状况）；Moore（1966）把不同政治制度的起源和国家社会关系的型态，跟历史性经济和政治情势结合为一— 包括纳入有无强制性劳工和因此而产生的社会联盟等因素的方法、North and Thomas（1973）和「西方崛起」有关的论文、Engerman and Sokoloff（2011）有关南北美洲发展起源历史比较的大作、Pincus（2011）针对光荣革命所做的分析与Bates（1981）所提倡非洲比较政治经济学的理论、Flannery and Marcus（2014）综合考古学与人种学证据，对复杂社会出现的纪录、Brenner（1976）针对封建主义过渡到资本主义期间，强调地主和农民权力关系所扮演角色的大作。
